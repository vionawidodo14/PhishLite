								I have to admit, I started playing just for giggles, but it actually has the bones of being a fun game. There are a few issues at the moment, though I know it's in alpha. The biggest issue that really bothers me is that while the game promises that your breeder and get it on with any of the Nephelym, they fail to point out that this only applies to only interactions that involve females or futas. M/M action in this game is completely off the table, and that severely disappointed me. It also made it irritatingly frustrating to obtain male nephelyms since you can't sex them into submission if you are a male breeder. The second point of contention is that the options for how nephelyms appear are pretty limited. Males are always fursonas, females/futas are usually mostly human with animal ears/tails/feet. I'd like to have seen more diversity in this regard, as it would be nice to see males as mostly human with animal ears/tails/feet and some females/futas with more furry features.<br><br>There are a few other things this game does need, like a bit of hand holding to explain how things work. There's no map, so it took me a lot of time to find things, and the NPCs are pretty vague when explaining where some things are, like the Sultry Plateau. The game also doesn't explain your Lust Bar, which was pretty frustrating to me when literally after I'm done with the first conversation after starting the game, I die and am getting humped back to life by a weird grim reaper. I dunno what that was even about, but it completely drained my Lust Bar, which I found out you really need Lust in order to start some of the major interactions with some of the NPCs like Fern and Neela. The other thing is harvesting byproducts off your nephelyms - it doesn't explain how to do this, and took me forever to even figure out the process to do it. That's even if I was capable of harvesting from the particular nephelyms - I as a male breeder can only harvest milk from females, I can't harvest anything else, which made collecting materials for sale or recruiting wild nephelyms frustrating.<br><br>As I said, this game is weird, but it has the bones to be an entertaining game, it just needs to get over it's M/M hangups so that it can present a fully fleshed out game that has the same kind of player experience no matter what gender your player character is. The other things I presume will get corrected or added as the game moves through apha. For now, I'll have to give it a "No" review because of the M/M hangups, that impeded gameplay pretty badly for me, and as someone who enjoys M/M interactions, was a major letdown.			</div>
