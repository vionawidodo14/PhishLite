						<p><b>Minimum opening deposit</b></p>
<p>Open with a $25 initial deposit. </p>
<p><b>Monthly service fee</b></p>
<p>Avoid the $10 monthly service fee on your Everyday Checking account with one of the following each fee period:</p>
<ul class="disc">
<li>$1,500 minimum daily balance. <strong>Effective with the fee period beginning after October 8, 2020, this option will be lowered to a $500 minimum daily balance.</strong></li>
<li>Qualifying direct deposits totaling $500 or more</li>
<li>10 or more posted debit card transactions (any combination of posted debit card purchases or posted debit card payments of bills) from this checking account. This does not include transactions at an ATM. <strong>Effective with the fee period beginning after October 8, 2020, this will no longer be an option to avoid the monthly service fee.</strong></li>
<li>Linked to a Wells Fargo Campus ATM or Campus Debit Card</li>
<li>Primary account owner is 17 through 24 years old. (On the primary account owner’s 25th birthday, the account will automatically be subject to the then current monthly service fee unless you meet one of the other options to avoid the monthly service fee.)</li>
</ul>
<p class="last">See the <a href="https://www.wellsfargo.com/fetch-pdf?formNumber=CNS2016ED&amp;subProductCode=WRC" target="_blank" class="red">
common fees for this account (PDF).</a></p>
