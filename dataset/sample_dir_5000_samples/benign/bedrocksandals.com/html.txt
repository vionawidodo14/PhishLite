<h5 class="center">Sizing Chart</h5>
<img alt="" src="//cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/1132/3434/files/BedrockSandal_12_23_18_Bydlon_HiRes--94_30b233e3-fdc2-4c77-b6c2-fe1dd523b66a_2048x2048.jpg?v=1548284988" width="100%"/>
<p><strong>Find Your Unique Bedrock Sandals Size</strong></p>
<p>Our sizing is <strong>unisex</strong> (e.g. size 9/10 is equivalent to size 9 for men, and size 10 for women.) Some Bedrockers find our sizes run slightly small, especially in our smallest sizes. To find your Bedrock Size please print our size outlines or consult or sizing chart below.</p>
<p> </p>
<p><img alt="" src="//cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/1132/3434/files/BRS_SIZEGUIDELINES_2018_6e2d9c5f-ed78-4388-8d0b-a35481653ac8_1024x1024.jpg?v=1528142217" width="80%"/></p>
<meta charset="utf-8"/>
<ul>
<li>Print off the size outlines for the <a href="https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/1132/3434/files/Cairn_Template_2018.pdf?3238355579814526865" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Cairn Sandals</a> or <a href="https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/1132/3434/files/Classic_Tracings_2018.pdf?17891310204828937607" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Classic Sandals</a> to find your ideal fit. Please note that you must change Printer Settings to "100% Scale". Double check this by verifying the ruler on each outline. Give yourself ~1 cm of space inside of the outline to ensure a good fit! </li>
</ul>
<ul>
<li>Wondering which size would fit best? E-mail us a photo of your foot on a printed size outline to get our expert opinion. We'll get back to you quickly! Email: support@bedrocksandals.com.                                                             </li>
<li>Don't have access to a printer? Consult our sizing chart above. We advise measuring the length of your feet and ordering a size that is 1-2 cm longer. </li>
</ul>
