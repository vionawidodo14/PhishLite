<div class="bb-center"><img alt="[​IMG]" class="bbCodeImage LbImage" data-url="https://www.hiveworkshop.com/media/hivecup_night.127474/full" src="https://www.hiveworkshop.com/media/hivecup_night.127474/full" style=""/><br/>

<br/>
<b><span style="color: #ffffff"><span style="font-size: 22px">We are excited to announce the first ever The Hive Workshop pro-tournament for Warcraft 3.</span></span></b><br/>
The Hive Workshop Cup is a competitive 1v1 tournament where members of our, and the greater Warcraft 3, community will compete for a prize of <b><span style="color: #ffffff">€555</span></b>.<br/>
</div><br/>
Today, we're excited to have the opportunity, thanks to the sponsorship of <a class="username" data-user="204498, @Nightmare2077" href="https://www.hiveworkshop.com/members/204498/">@Nightmare2077</a>, to run a competitive 1v1 Warcraft 3 tournament for our community, and Warcraft 3 as a whole. We are also working with <a class="externalLink" href="https://www.back2warcraft.com/" rel="nofollow" target="_blank">Back2Warcraft</a> to organize and cast this event.<br/>
<br/>
The Hive Workshop has been a dedicated community creating custom experiences in Warcraft 3 for many years. And it's important for us to have the tournament to reflect this. This is why we'll be using community maps for the map pool.<br/>
<br/>
<div class="bb-center"><b><span style="font-size: 22px"><span style="color: #ffffff">A contest for our community tournament!</span></span></b></div><br/>
We're hosting a melee map contest where the top 4 maps get a spot in the contest. You're allowed to submit 1 of your maps - new or old - for this contest. Either create a new map, polish an existing one, or submit an approved map you've previously made.<br/>
<br/>
<div class="bb-center"><span style="font-size: 15px"><b><a class="internalLink" href="https://www.hiveworkshop.com/threads/hive-cup-melee-mapping-contest.318961/">Check out the melee map contest's forum topic here!</a></b></span></div><br/>
<b><span style="font-size: 15px"><span style="color: #ffffff">Tournament schedule:</span></span></b><br/>
<ul>
<li>The tournament map pool contest will be conducted from now until October 6th, 2019. The top 4 maps will be selected for the map pool.<br/>
</li>
<li>The Hive Tournament will have 2 open qualifiers from October 12-14, 2019. The top competitors of this group will move on.<br/>
</li>
<li>Following this, the Hive qualifiers will take place October 15th, 2019. Exclusive to members of the Hive community (prior to September 20th, 2019), the top 2 competitors of this group will move on.<br/>
</li>
<li>The Playoffs and Finals will take place October 18th and 19th, 2019. The top players of all 3 qualifiers will compete for the grand prize of €555.</li>
</ul><br/>
Details on registration and format will be announced soon.

<br/>
<b>Do <span style="text-decoration: underline">not </span>create a new account.</b><br/>
<br/>
I have deleted the passwords for around 60,000 accounts on the site because they were leaked. I am sorry if this is an inconvenience to you.<br/>
<br/>
If you used your password and email from 2016 on any other site, I highly recommend you change it. Go to <a class="externalLink" href="https://haveibeenpwned.com/" rel="nofollow" target="_blank">this site</a> to get a quick overview of the status of whether any of your emails or passwords are in the hands of someone else.<br/>
<br/>
I also recommend using <a class="externalLink" href="https://lastpass.com/" rel="nofollow" target="_blank">LastPass</a> for storing randomized passwords for each site instead of re-using the same old ones.
