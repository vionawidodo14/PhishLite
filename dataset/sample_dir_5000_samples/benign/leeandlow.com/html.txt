<h3 class="low-margin-top">About Us</h3>
<p></p><p><strong>LEE &amp; LOW BOOKS is the largest multicultural children's book publisher in the country.</strong> We are also one of the few minority-owned publishing companies in the United States.</p>
<p>Founded in 1991, we have always been family-owned and independent, which means we publish what we want and aren't afraid to take risks. Our books have won major awards including the Coretta Scott King Award, the Pura Belpré Award, the NAACP Image Award, and many more.</p>
<p>LEE &amp; LOW BOOKS is committed to publishing diverse books that are about everyone, for everyone. We are dedicated to cultural authenticity, and we make a special effort to work with <a href="https://www.leeandlow.com/writing-contests">new authors and illustrators of color</a>.</p>
<p>We publish multicultural children's books for every age, including <a href="https://www.leeandlow.com/leveled-reading">leveled books</a> for beginning readers, <a href="https://www.leeandlow.com/imprints/lee-low-books">picture books</a>, <a href="https://www.leeandlow.com/imprints/dive-into-reading">early chapter books</a>, <a href="https://www.leeandlow.com/middle-school">middle grade</a>, and <a href="https://www.leeandlow.com/high-school">young adult</a> books. We also publish a wide selection of <a href="https://www.leeandlow.com/prek-grade-2/spanish-and-bilingual-">bilingual and Spanish</a> language books.</p>
<p>Visit our <a href="https://www.leeandlow.com/about-us/testimonials">testimonials</a> page to see some of the people and organizations who support our work!</p>
