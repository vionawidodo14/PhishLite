<h2 class="large-text white-text">About Us</h2>
<p class="white-text">We are the nation's largest funder of health and care research and provide the people, facilities and technology that enables research to thrive. Working in partnership with the <a href="https://www.nhs.uk/">NHS</a>, universities, local government, other research funders, patients and the public, we deliver and enable world-class research that transforms people's lives, promotes economic growth and advances science.</p>
<p class="white-text">We are primarily funded by the <a href="https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/department-of-health-and-social-care">Department of Health and Social Care</a>, but also receive UK Aid funding to support research for people in low- and middle-income countries.</p>
</div>
<div class="about-us-landing-block-item padding-bottom-2rem">
<a href="/about-us/our-mission/our-mission-and-strategic-workstreams.htm"><img class="padding-bottom-2rem padding-top-2rem img-fluid" src="/layout/img/motifs/corporate/radiating.png"/></a>
<a href="/about-us/our-mission/our-mission-and-strategic-workstreams.htm">Our Mission</a>
<p>NIHR's mission is to improve the health and wealth of the nation through research. We deliver against this mission through five strategic workstreams.</p>
</div>
<div class="about-us-landing-block-item padding-bottom-2rem">
<a href="/about-us/our-governance/"><img class="padding-bottom-2rem padding-top-2rem img-fluid" src="/layout/img/motifs/corporate/coral-and-aqua-leaf.png"/></a>
<a href="/about-us/our-governance/">Our Governance</a>
<p>The NIHR's work is directed by leaders at Department of Health and Social Care working in partnership with the directors of our seven coordinating centres.</p>
</div>
<div class="about-us-landing-block-item padding-bottom-2rem">
<a href="/about-us/our-contribution-to-research/"><img class="padding-bottom-2rem padding-top-2rem" src="/layout/img/motifs/corporate/coral-and-aqua-bullseye.png"/></a>
<a href="/about-us/our-contribution-to-research/">Our Contribution to Research</a>
<p>The NIHR is a central part of the UK research landscape, collaborating in national activities to improve research and supporting NHS research performance.</p>
