<div class="panel-heading">
<h4 class="panel-title">
<a aria-expanded="false" class="accordion-toggle tab-feature-title collapsed" data-toggle="collapse" href="#collapse-homepage-os">Does It Matter If My Computer Is Windows, Mac or Linux?</a>
</h4>
</div>
<div aria-expanded="true" class="panel-collapse collapse" id="collapse-homepage-os" style="">
<div class="feature-content fade tab-feature-content panel-body fw-previous-tab-pane active in" id="homepage-os" role="tabpanel">
<p>You may have noticed that we offer both <a href="https://www.a2hosting.com/windows-hosting">Windows</a> and <a href="https://www.a2hosting.com/linux-hosting">Linux</a> Hosting service options. Which one of these options should you choose depending on your computer's operating system? You may be relieved to learn that It actually doesn't matter what your computer's operating system is. You <strong>can choose Linux or Windows </strong>Hosting whether your computer's operating system is Windows, Linux, Mac or another option. <strong>Your hosting account's operating system is NOT dependent on your computer's operating system.</strong> That means if you a Windows operating system for your computer, you can choose either Windows or Linux Hosting for your A2 Hosting account.</p>
<p>Now that you know that you can host with either Linux or Windows regardless of what your computer's operating system is, you may now be wondering<strong> which OS option you should choose </strong>for your A2 Hosting account. What we recommend doing is <strong>looking at the requirements for the software</strong> you are looking to run on your hosted account. This is one of the best ways to decide which operating system you should select.</p>
<ul>
<li>If the software you want to use requires solutions like PHP, MySQL, Apache, Perl, Python or PostgreSQL, you'll most likely want to choose Linux for your A2 Hosting account operating system.</li>
<li>If the software you want to use requires solutions like ASP, ASP.NET, MSSQL, Microsoft SQL Server, Windows Server or .NET Core, you'll want to choose our Windows Hosting selection.</li>
</ul>
</div>
</div>
</div>
<div class="panel panel-default tab-feature">
<div class="panel-heading">
<h4 class="panel-title">
<a aria-expanded="false" class="accordion-toggle tab-feature-title collapsed" data-toggle="collapse" href="#collapse-homepage-beginner">I've Never Hosted A Site Or Used A2 Hosting. How Do I Get Started?</a>
</h4>
</div>
<div aria-expanded="true" class="panel-collapse collapse" id="collapse-homepage-beginner" style="">

<p>Whether this is the first site you've ever hosted or the one-hundredth site, our website hosting services are loaded with tools to make your experience as easy as possible.Your first step to get started with A2 Hosting is to <strong>choose your hosting solution </strong>and your domain name. If this is your first site or if you are a beginner, we recommend our <a href="https://www.a2hosting.com/web-hosting">Shared Hosting</a> solutions. These are affordable plans loaded with intuitive tools to help you get your site up and off the ground quickly and easily. Essentially Shared Hosting is a perfect way for you to dabble with our web hosting services at an extremely affordable price. Visit our <a href="https://www.a2hosting.com/compare">Service Comparison</a> page to see a complete breakdown of our Shared, Reseller, VPS and Dedicated server options. We also offer a comprehensive <a href="https://www.a2hosting.com/kb/getting-started-guide" target="_blank">Getting Started guide</a> to help you along the way!</p>
<p>One of the reasons that we're confident you'll have an easy time hosting your site with us is because your A2 Hosting account includes a <strong>user-friendly control panel</strong>. Your control panel provides you with all the tools you'll need to manage your software, websites, email, databases and more. You'll receive an email providing you with login details explaining how to access your control panel. If you sign up for a Linux Hosting account, you'll receive access to the <a href="https://www.a2hosting.com/cpanel-hosting">cPanel</a> control panel and if you choose one of our Windows web hosting services, you will receive access to the Plesk control panel. <p>The next step to getting started is selecting what software you would like to use to power your site. Are you running a content-based site? This may sound complicated, but we make it easy at A2 Hosting with our <strong>1-click software installers</strong>! cPanel users get access to the 1-click software installer <a href="https://www.a2hosting.com/kb/installable-applications/softaculous" target="_blank">Softaculous</a> in their control panel while Plesk users get the <a href="https://www.a2hosting.com/kb/plesk/getting-started-with-plesk/installing-applications-in-plesk" target="_blank">Applications</a> option.Use these tools to install software solutions to your account in a matter of minutes! Best of all, you'll have hundreds of software options to choose to add to your account from within these 1-click installers!</p>
<p>Of course if you still have questions about getting started, the A2 Hosting Guru Crew Support team is standing by to help! Just <a href="https://www.a2hosting.com/contact">contact us</a> via live chat, email us or call us. We're available around the click to help!</p>

<div class="feature-content fade tab-feature-content panel-body fw-previous-tab-pane active in" id="homepage-speed" role="tabpanel">
