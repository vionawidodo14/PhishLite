<p><br/>
DocuWiki.net serves as an index of <a href="/index.php?title=Documentaries" title="Documentaries">documentary films</a> on the <a href="/index.php?title=Ed2k" title="Ed2k">Edonkey Network</a>.
</p><p>(If you came here looking for the Documentation Wiki Software instead head for <a class="external text" href="/extlink.php?http://dokuwiki.org" rel="nofollow" target="_blank" title="http://dokuwiki.org">DokuWiki.org</a>)
</p><p>DocuWiki started as an offspring of the <a class="external text" href="/extlink.php?http://forums.mvgroup.org" rel="nofollow" target="_blank" title="http://forums.mvgroup.org">MVGroup</a> documentary forum in order to make the contents easier to find and index as well as browsing documentaries by subject or finding related entries.
</p><p>If a documentary you have is missing on this site, please do a favour to everyone and add it to this wiki.<br/>
A list of documentaries known to be missing in this wiki is listed <a class="external text" href="http://docuwiki.net/postbot/missingwikipages.php" title="http://docuwiki.net/postbot/missingwikipages.php">here</a>.<br/>
Refer to <a href="/index.php?title=DocuWiki:About" title="DocuWiki:About">Contribute</a> for more information and/or contact <a class="external text" href="http://forums.mvgroup.org/index.php?act=Msg&amp;CODE=4&amp;MID=3649" title="http://forums.mvgroup.org/index.php?act=Msg&amp;CODE=4&amp;MID=3649">JumpinS</a>.
</p><p>To stay updated on new additions you can subscribe to the newsfeed:
<a class="external text" href="http://docuwiki.net/index.php?title=Special:Newpages&amp;feed=rss" title="http://docuwiki.net/index.php?title=Special:Newpages&amp;feed=rss">RSS</a> - <a class="external text" href="http://docuwiki.net/index.php?title=Special:Newpages&amp;feed=atom" title="http://docuwiki.net/index.php?title=Special:Newpages&amp;feed=atom">Atom</a>
</p><p><a href="/index.php?title=Frequently_Asked_Questions" title="Frequently Asked Questions">Frequently Asked Questions</a>
</p>
