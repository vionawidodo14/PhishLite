<h1 class="post-title">
<a class="post-link" href="https://www.macstories.net/reviews/arcade-highlights-card-of-darkness/" rel="bookmark">Arcade Highlights: Card of Darkness</a> </h1>
<div class="post-author">
<a class="post-author-link" href="https://www.macstories.net/author/ryanchristoffel/" rel="author">By Ryan Christoffel</a> </div>
</header>
<div class="post-content">
<div class="media-wrapper"><img alt="" src="https://2672686a4cf38e8c2458-2712e00ea34e3076747650c92426bbb5.ssl.cf1.rackcdn.com/2019-10-15-20-18-16.png"/><p class="image-caption"><a class="view-full-size" href="https://2672686a4cf38e8c2458-2712e00ea34e3076747650c92426bbb5.ssl.cf1.rackcdn.com/2019-10-15-20-18-16.png" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank"><svg viewbox="0 0 120 120" xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg"><path d="M71.2 48.8c1.6 1.6 4.1 1.6 5.6 0L112 13.6V32c0 2.4 2.2 4.4 4.7 3.9 2-.3 3.3-2.2 3.3-4.2V4c0-2.2-1.8-4-4-4H88.2c-2 0-3.8 1.4-4.2 3.3-.4 2.5 1.6 4.7 4 4.7h18.4L71.2 43.2c-1.6 1.6-1.6 4.1 0 5.6zm20.8-.9V104c0 4.4-3.6 8-8 8H16c-4.4 0-8-3.6-8-8V36c0-4.4 3.6-8 8-8h56.1c1.1 0 2.1-.4 2.8-1.2l5.1-5.1c.6-.6.2-1.7-.7-1.7H12C5.4 20 0 25.4 0 32v76c0 6.6 5.4 12 12 12h76c6.6 0 12-5.4 12-12V40.6c0-.9-1.1-1.3-1.7-.7L93.2 45c-.8.8-1.2 1.8-1.2 2.9z"></path></svg></a></p></div>
<p id="p2">Do you like fantasy-themed games? How about Zach Gage’s work – <a href="https://apps.apple.com/us/app/flipflop-solitaire/id1256860418?uo=4" rel="noopener noreferrer">Flipflop Solitaire</a>, <a href="https://apps.apple.com/us/app/really-bad-chess/id1109751921?uo=4" rel="noopener noreferrer">Really Bad Chess</a>, <a href="https://apps.apple.com/us/app/typeshift/id1084762137?uo=4" rel="noopener noreferrer">Typeshift</a>, <a href="https://apps.apple.com/us/app/spelltower/id476500832?uo=4" rel="noopener noreferrer">SpellTower</a>, etc.? Would a mobile game brought to life by the art of Adventure Time creator Pendleton Ward be of interest to you? If the answer to any of these questions is yes, then <a href="https://apps.apple.com/us/app/card-of-darkness/id1364257627?uo=4" rel="noopener noreferrer">Card of Darkness</a> should be one of the first games you download from Apple Arcade.</p>
<p id="p3">If you answered no to all three questions, you should probably play Card of Darkness anyways.</p>
<p id="p4">Card of Darkness is a <a href="https://en.m.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roguelike" rel="noopener noreferrer">roguelike</a> game where each stage contains a grid of stacked cards, with each stack holding a random mix of monsters to defeat, weapons to equip, and potions and treasure to find. There are also magic spell scrolls that help you more easily navigate what can be a treacherous quest to get from the start of the grid to its end. You don’t have to clear every card stack to complete a level, but you do need to forge an open path to the finish line, and every stack you take even a single card from will need to be finished. As you clear each card stack, more stacks further into the grid will be revealed, slowly reducing the amount of cards that stand between you and the end of the grid.</p>
