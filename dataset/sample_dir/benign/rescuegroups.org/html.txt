<h3 style="text-align: center;">Save Time with the <a href="https://www.rescuegroups.org/services/pet-adoption-portal/" title="Pet Adoption Portal"><strong>Pet Adoption Portal</strong></a>,<br/>
<a href="https://www.rescuegroups.org/services/data-management-service/" title="Data Management Service"><strong>Data Management Service</strong></a>, <a href="https://www.rescuegroups.org/services/website-service/" title="Website Service"><strong>Website Service</strong></a>, and more services designed for Animal Welfare Organizations</h3>
<p></p><div class="clearfade"></div><br/>
<div class="ezcol ezcol-one-quarter"><img alt="Free uploads to the major adoption listing websites with the Pet Adoption Portal, Data Management Service and Website Service" class="size-full wp-image-1416 alignnone" height="64" src="https://www.rescuegroups.org/wp-content/uploads/2013/02/arrow-up.png" title="Free uploads to the major adoption listing websites with the Pet Adoption Portal, Data Management Service and Website Service" width="64"/><p></p>
<p><strong>Exports to Hundreds of Adoption Websites and Services - Free!</strong></p>
<p>When you post your adoptable pets with us, we can send them out to<strong><a href="https://www.rescuegroups.org/adoption-listing-websites/" title="Adoption Listing Websites"> over 200 pet adoption listing websites</a>.</strong> You have full control of where your pets appear, and <strong>it's all done for free.</strong></p></div><div class="ezcol ezcol-one-quarter"><img alt="RescueGroups.org is a non-profit organization" class="size-full wp-image-1425 alignnone" height="64" src="https://www.rescuegroups.org/wp-content/uploads/2013/02/circle-love1.png" title="RescueGroups.org is a non-profit organization" width="64"/><p></p>
<p><strong>We are a Non-Profit</strong></p>
<p>That means that our one-and-only mission is to help the community and no one is profiting personally from what we are doing. You won't find any ulterior motives in what we do.</p></div><div class="ezcol ezcol-one-quarter"><img alt="RescueGroups.org has over 3,500 organizations using our services." class="size-full wp-image-1417 alignnone" height="64" src="https://www.rescuegroups.org/wp-content/uploads/2013/02/chat-quote.png" title="RescueGroups.org has over 3,500 organizations using our services." width="64"/><p></p>
<p><strong>Over 6,200 Partner Organizations</strong></p>
<p>We receive many <a href="/testimonials" title="Testimonials"><strong>testimonials from organizations</strong></a> who want to let others know of their great experiences using RescueGroups.org services.</p></div><div class="ezcol ezcol-one-quarter ezcol-last"><img alt="RescueGroups.org takes security very seriously. We use SSL to protect your account." class="size-full wp-image-1419 alignnone" height="64" src="https://www.rescuegroups.org/wp-content/uploads/2013/02/lock-closed.png" title="RescueGroups.org takes security very seriously. We use SSL to protect your account." width="64"/><p></p>
<p><strong>Secure, Web-based Services</strong></p>
<p>We take security very seriously. Our data management website uses SSL to protect your account information, and we require every user to have their own user name and password.</p></div><div class="ezcol-divider"></div><br/>
<div class="clearfade"></div><br/>
Most volunteers don't have time to update more than one or two websites. Our online animal management software tools such as the <strong>Pet Adoption Portal</strong><strong>,</strong> <strong>Data Management Service</strong>, and<strong> Website Service</strong> can automatically send your animal data and pictures to a <strong><a href="https://www.rescuegroups.org/adoption-listing-websites">long list of websites</a>. </strong>You update only one pet list, and the other websites get updated!<p></p>
<p></p><div class="clearfade"></div><br/>
<div class="ezcol ezcol-one-third">
<h3><a href="https://www.rescuegroups.org/services/pet-adoption-portal/" title="Pet Adoption Portal">Pet Adoption Portal</a></h3>

No matter what level of service you choose, you'll get the <strong>best services and support</strong> available so you can <strong>spend less time in front of a computer</strong> and more time doing what you love - helping the animals!</div>
