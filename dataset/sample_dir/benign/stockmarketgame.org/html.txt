<section id="get-involved">
<h1>Get Involved.</h1>
<ul class="list-unstyled circles-list clearfix">
<li>
<a class="connector-line-vertical" href="http://www.sifma.org/foundation">
<img alt="SIFMA Foundation" src="img/SIFMA_logo.png"/>
</a>
<h5>The SIFMA Foundation</h5>
<p>
                An independent 501(c)3 educational organization dedicated to fostering knowledge of the financial markets,
                serving 17 million students since 1977.
            </p>
</li>
<li>
<a class="connector-line" href="http://www.sifma.org/foundation">
<img alt="Stock Market Game" src="img/smg_circlelogo.png"/>
</a>
<h5>Stock Market Game<sup>™</sup></h5>
<p>
                An online simulation of the global capital markets that engages students grades 4-12 in the world of
                economics, investing and personal finance and that has prepared 17 million students for financially independent futures.
            </p>
</li>
<li>
<a class="connector-line" href="http://www.investwrite.org">
<img alt="INVESTwrite" src="img/INVESTwrite_logo.png"/>
</a>
<h5>InvestWrite<sup>®</sup></h5>
<p>
                A national essay competition that serves as a culminating activity for Stock Market
                Game<sup>™</sup> students.
            </p>
</li>
<li>
<a class="connector-line" href="capitol-hill-challenge.html">
<img alt="Capitol Hill Challenge" src="img/CHC_logo.png"/>
</a>
<h5>Capitol Hill Challenge<sup>™</sup></h5>
<p>
                A national financial education competition for junior high and high schools that reaches all 50
                U.S. states and all members of Congress.
            </p>
</li>
<li>
<a href="https://investitforward.sifma.org/">
<img alt="Invest it Forward" src="img/INVEST_logo.png"/>
</a>
<h5>Invest It Forward<sup>™</sup></h5>
<p>
                An industry-wide financial education and capital markets literacy campaign convening
                volunteers from hundreds of financial firms.
            </p>
</li>
</ul>
