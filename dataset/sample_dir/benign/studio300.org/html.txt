<h1 class="entry-title"><a href="https://studio300.org/2019/10/18/a-diy-halloween-village/" rel="bookmark">A DIY Halloween Village</a></h1>
<div class="entry-meta">
			Posted on <a href="https://studio300.org/2019/10/18/a-diy-halloween-village/" rel="bookmark" title="3:15 pm"><time class="entry-date" datetime="2019-10-18T15:15:59+00:00" pubdate="">October 18, 2019</time></a><span class="byline"> by <span class="author vcard"><a class="url fn n" href="https://studio300.org/author/agillespie84/" rel="author" title="View all posts by agillespie84">agillespie84</a></span></span> </div><!-- .entry-meta -->

<div class="entry-content">
<p>It’s the time of year for spooky ghosts, creepy monsters and creative DIYs. The popularity of computerized cutters prompted Studio 300 to add the Silhouette Cameo 3. This cutter can handle card stock, chipboard, fabric and more. Feeling inspired by the season — and sheets of cute card stock — we created a Halloween village. It includes one large mansion, a few little houses, card stock trees, and fences. We also used our 3D printer to add cute pumpkins and scary monsters.</p>
<p>Using the Silhouette, the mansion came from a free project found at <a href="https://paperglitterglue.com">Paper Glitter Glue</a> and converted using Adobe Illustrator. If you want to make your own, watch our <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wVVc6dQi5iE">video</a> with all the steps. Studio 300 has the project files for the houses if you want to cut your own, too. And you can see the finished project in the Studio 300 display case now through Halloween.<br/>

One week later, here’s my perspective. Having played D1 since its launch, I’m not new to the game. For the past five years, I have played through each and every expansion, the good, bad, and horrid grinds. Destiny 2 is now entering into its third year, and after the split from Activision early this year, fans of the game were very interested to see what Bungie would do specifically with Shadowkeep — its first solo expansion. Bungie’s decision to make Destiny 2 free to play up to Shadowkeep, as well as the cross save functions, was also a decision that would impact playability of this franchise. But is it everything we hoped and dreamed?</p>
<p>On first impression the campaign is not what I expected, though I still found myself engaged from beginning to end. I would have liked a stronger narrative like Forsaken or Taken King but I was not displeased. I do hope that the path we are seeing will bring us to new places and, dare I say?, even encounter new enemies. Bungie has a long history of recycling enemies, maps, and even missions. The stroll through memory lane as you explore the Moon which has now become infested with nightmares is not altogether disappointing. There are definitely some fun activities and challenges you face through the campaign as well as in open world. It is my hope that we do not find ourselves mired on the Moon for too long. <a class="more-link" href="https://studio300.org/2019/10/09/destiny-2-shadowkeep-week-one/#more-5694">Continue reading <span class="meta-nav">→</span></a></p>

Many of us love to see the twinkling light of the stars shining above us and take an active interest in capturing images of those beautiful pinpricks. Astrophotography is an old branch of photography that has developed into a passion for many astronomers and photographers alike. Nothing will test your imaging skills like astrophotography. 			Posted on <a href="https://studio300.org/2019/07/09/film-clubs-july-2019/" rel="bookmark" title="7:44 pm"><time class="entry-date" datetime="2019-07-09T19:44:39+00:00" pubdate="">July 9, 2019</time></a><span class="byline"> by <span class="author vcard"><a class="url fn n" href="https://studio300.org/author/aalvarez77/" rel="author" title="View all posts by aalvarez77">aalvarez77</a></span></span> </div><!-- .entry-meta -->
</header><!-- .entry-header -->
<div class="entry-content">
<p>Think book club, but with movies instead. And that describes our two newest clubs for film fans. The next meeting is Tuesday, July 30th where we meet and discuss two <i>films.</i></p>
<p><a href="https://communico.fountaindale.org/event/2157968" rel="noopener" target="_blank"><strong>Film Club</strong></a> meets July 30 at 6:30 p.m., to take a close look at <em>The Lost City of Z</em> (2016), available on Blu-ray, DVD and <a href="https://www.hoopladigital.com/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Hoopla</a>.</p>
<p><img alt="large_large_ik3ebv7J18fs6cHkmu91oxz7EGt" class="wp-image-5624 alignright" data-attachment-id="5624" data-comments-opened="0" data-image-description="" data-image-meta='{"aperture":"0","credit":"","camera":"","caption":"","created_timestamp":"0","copyright":"","focal_length":"0","iso":"0","shutter_speed":"0","title":"","orientation":"0"}' data-image-title="large_large_ik3ebv7J18fs6cHkmu91oxz7EGt" data-large-file="https://fplstudio300.files.wordpress.com/2019/07/large_large_ik3ebv7j18fs6chkmu91oxz7egt.jpg?w=400" data-medium-file="https://fplstudio300.files.wordpress.com/2019/07/large_large_ik3ebv7j18fs6chkmu91oxz7egt.jpg?w=200" data-orig-file="https://fplstudio300.files.wordpress.com/2019/07/large_large_ik3ebv7j18fs6chkmu91oxz7egt.jpg" data-orig-size="400,600" data-permalink="https://studio300.org/2019/07/09/film-clubs-july-2019/large_large_ik3ebv7j18fs6chkmu91oxz7egt/#main" height="272" sizes="(max-width: 181px) 100vw, 181px" src="https://fplstudio300.files.wordpress.com/2019/07/large_large_ik3ebv7j18fs6chkmu91oxz7egt.jpg?w=181&amp;h=272" srcset="https://fplstudio300.files.wordpress.com/2019/07/large_large_ik3ebv7j18fs6chkmu91oxz7egt.jpg?w=181&amp;h=272 181w, https://fplstudio300.files.wordpress.com/2019/07/large_large_ik3ebv7j18fs6chkmu91oxz7egt.jpg?w=362&amp;h=544 362w, https://fplstudio300.files.wordpress.com/2019/07/large_large_ik3ebv7j18fs6chkmu91oxz7egt.jpg?w=100&amp;h=150 100w, https://fplstudio300.files.wordpress.com/2019/07/large_large_ik3ebv7j18fs6chkmu91oxz7egt.jpg?w=200&amp;h=300 200w" width="181"/></p>
<p>This epically scaled tale of courage and passion, told in writer/director James Gray’s classic filmmaking style,<em> The Lost City of Z</em> is a stirring tribute <span style="color:var(--color-text);">to the exploratory spirit and a conflicted adventurer driven to the verge of obsession.</span></p>
<p>The <a href="https://communico.fountaindale.org/event/2157981" rel="noopener" target="_blank"><strong>Classic Reels</strong></a> club meets July 30 at 7:30 p.m<a href="https://communico.fountaindale.org/event/2157980" rel="noopener" target="_blank">.</a> to breakdown and discuss the classic movie: <em>Clue </em>(1985), available on <a href="https://fountaindale.kanopy.com/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Kanopy</a>, Blu-ray and DVD.</p>
<p><img alt="913DShsadYL._SY445_.jpg" class="wp-image-5625 alignleft" data-attachment-id="5625" data-comments-opened="0" data-image-description="" data-image-meta='{"aperture":"0","credit":"","camera":"","caption":"","created_timestamp":"0","copyright":"","focal_length":"0","iso":"0","shutter_speed":"0","title":"","orientation":"0"}' data-image-title="913DShsadYL._SY445_" data-large-file="https://fplstudio300.files.wordpress.com/2019/07/913dshsadyl._sy445_.jpg?w=315" data-medium-file="https://fplstudio300.files.wordpress.com/2019/07/913dshsadyl._sy445_.jpg?w=212" data-orig-file="https://fplstudio300.files.wordpress.com/2019/07/913dshsadyl._sy445_.jpg" data-orig-size="315,445" data-permalink="https://studio300.org/2019/07/09/film-clubs-july-2019/913dshsadyl-_sy445_/#main" height="283" sizes="(max-width: 200px) 100vw, 200px" src="https://fplstudio300.files.wordpress.com/2019/07/913dshsadyl._sy445_.jpg?w=200&amp;h=283" srcset="https://fplstudio300.files.wordpress.com/2019/07/913dshsadyl._sy445_.jpg?w=200&amp;h=283 200w, https://fplstudio300.files.wordpress.com/2019/07/913dshsadyl._sy445_.jpg?w=106&amp;h=150 106w, https://fplstudio300.files.wordpress.com/2019/07/913dshsadyl._sy445_.jpg?w=212&amp;h=300 212w, https://fplstudio300.files.wordpress.com/2019/07/913dshsadyl._sy445_.jpg 315w" width="200"/></p>
<p>Tim Curry, Madeline Kahn and Christopher Lloyd lead an all-star cast in this murderously funny cult classic based on the world-famous <em>Clue</em> board game.</p>
<p>Watch the films on your own, then<strong> join us</strong> for snacks and a breakdown discussion about the films. (Both are available in multiple formats with your library card.)</p>
