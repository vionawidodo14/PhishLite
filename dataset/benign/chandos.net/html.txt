<div class="review-author">BBC Music magazine Awards 2012</div>
<div class="review-stars stars0"></div>

<div class="review">
<div class="review-img"><a href="/products/reviews/CHAn_20141"><img src="/catalogueImages/CHAn20141.jpg"/></a></div>
<div class="review-text showmore"><p>“Ambitious, attractive works blend post-modernism and touches of Prokofiev… The first of the Three Romances for viola, strings and harp creates a rustling accompaniment for the soloist to unfold its carefree song over, while the following ones are of a sterner hue in spite of deceivingly innocent beginnings. The last one features the harp more prominently, and all three include important material for different sections to accompany the solo viola. Elvira Misbakhova is an eloquent soloist throughout, her singing tone just right for these melancholy musings. Ichmouratov’s Octet is heard in the composer’s own arrangement for string orchestra… it is the most musically ambitious piece and receives an intense reading that rounds off this attractive programme.”</p>
</div>
