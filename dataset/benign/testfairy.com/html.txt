<h2 class="feature-title">Enterprise Security</h2>
<div class="bulleted-item">
<div class="bullet"></div>
<div>Run TestFairy on a private cloud (Single Tenant) in a separate dedicated environment, behind firewall, SAML SSO and VPN access.</div>
</div>
<div class="bulleted-item">
<div class="bullet"></div>
<div>End-to-end encryption: Encrypt data on the SDK using your public key and decrypt on browser with your private key so nobody but you can see your sessions.</div>
</div>
<div class="bulleted-item">
<div class="bullet"></div>
<div>Choose where to host your data: North America, Europe or Asia.</div>
</div>
<div class="bulleted-item">
<div class="bullet"></div>
<div>Run TestFairy On-Premise in your corporate data center.</div>
</div>
<div class="bulleted-item">
<div class="bullet"></div>
<div>Comply with GDPR, HIPAA and other standards, laws and regulations (contact us for details)</div>
</div>
<div class="bulleted-item">
<div class="bullet"></div>
<div>TestFairy is fully compliant with ISO-27001:2013</div>
