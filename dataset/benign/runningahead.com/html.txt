<h2>Comprehensive Workout Logging</h2>
<p>RunningAHEAD's running log is <b>completely free.</b>
            It is simple and intuitive to use yet allows you to record all
            aspects of your training.  Track your shoe mileage
            and map your running routes.  You can create custom activities
            and workout types.</p>
<p>You can enter your data manually, upload your workouts directly from
            your Garmin Forerunner GPS or data files.</p>
<p>Analyze your running data and display your statistics and maps on your blog.</p>
</div><div class="Tab" id="ctl00_ctl00_ctl00_SiteContent_PageContent_MainContent_m_plan">
<img alt="Map your running routes in seconds" class="FloatRight" src="/images/ScreenShots/plan.jpg" style="margin-left:0.5em"/>
<h2>Training Plan Designer</h2>
<p>Whether you just started running or training for your next marathon,
            a training plan keeps you focused and motivated.</p>
<p>The RunningAHEAD training plan designer is easy to use like Excel and it is fully
            integrated with your running log.  Create a full marathon plan in a few
            minutes and view scheduled and completed workouts on your calendar.</p>
<p>Reuse an existing training plan for your next training cycle by making
            a copy of it.  Similarly, you can copy another user's plan and modify it
            to your needs.</p>
</div><div class="Tab" id="ctl00_ctl00_ctl00_SiteContent_PageContent_MainContent_m_runningRoutes">
<a href="/maps"><img alt="Map your running routes in seconds" class="FloatLeft" src="/images/ScreenShots/map.jpg" style="margin-right:0.5em"/></a>
<h2><a href="/maps">Map Your Running Routes</a></h2>
<p>Create your running route maps in a few clicks using the auto follow
              road mode.  Running in the trails?  Switch to the manual mode to
              trace the route using satellite map view.</p>
<p>The elevation profile is generated automatically as you map your
              running route.  Place the cursor on the elevation profile to see
              the altitude of a specific location.</p>
<p>Share your running routes with your friends or display them on your
            website!  <a href="/maps/find">Browse the database</a>
            to find running routes in your area.</p>
</div><div class="Tab" id="ctl00_ctl00_ctl00_SiteContent_PageContent_MainContent_m_analysis">
<img alt="Create beautiful graphs of your data" class="FloatLeft" src="/images/ScreenShots/graph.png" style="margin-right:0.5em"/>
<h2>Analyses and Reports</h2>
<p>Traditional training logs provides only a few graphing and reporting 
                options only.  RunningAHEAD's dynamic reporting technology lets you
                do your own data analysis.  Create custom graphs to visualize your
                progress.  Search for data using your own criteria.</p>
<p>You can save the generated graphs and reports for future use.
                Embed the graphs of the data on your website to show off your
                progress to your friends.</p>

<h1>Your training starts here</h1>
<p>RunningAHEAD offers a collection of free tools, including the best online running log, to help you
      train effectively.</p>
<p>There is more to a running log than just entering statistics.
      It should be an integral part of your training, whether you are new to running
      or qualified for the Boston Marathon.</p>
<p>At a minimum, it should give you a broad overview of your
      training at a quick glance. Ideally, your running log can help you identify areas
      of weakness, or act as an affirmation of your achievements.
    </p>
<p>You lead an active life, and need a running log that keeps up with you.  You can
      record all your workouts with RunningAHEAD, so you can go back to doing what you
      love quicker.</p>
