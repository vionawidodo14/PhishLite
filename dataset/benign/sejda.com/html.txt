<div class="post">
<h3>How to edit PDF files</h3>
<ol class="with-numbers">
<li><p>Open a file in the <a href="./pdf-editor">Online PDF editor</a></p></li>
<li><p>Click on the Text tool in the top menu</p></li>
<li><p>Add text on the PDF page. Change text by clicking on existing text to start editing</p></li>
<li><p>Add images to the page. Click and drag to move, resize or rotate the image</p></li>
<li><p>Fill out PDF forms and add signatures. Draw, type or upload an image of your signature</p></li>
<li><p>Annotate PDF pages, highlight text and mark changes with strikethrough</p></li>
<li><p>Add new links to web URLs or pages in the document. Easily edit existing hyperlinks in the PDF</p></li>
<li><p>Easily find and replace all occurrences of words in a PDF. Whiteout parts of the page. Add shapes</p></li>
<li><p>Click Apply changes and download an edited document</p></li>
</ol>
<a href="./pdf-editor">Edit PDF files <i class="fal fa-arrow-right"></i> </a>
</div>
<div class="post">
<h3>How to convert a PDF to Word</h3>
<ol class="with-squares">
<li><p>Open your document with the <a href="./pdf-to-word">PDF to Word converter</a></p></li>
<li><p>Click Convert and download a Word document</p></li>
</ol>
<a href="./pdf-to-word">PDF to DOC converter <i class="fal fa-arrow-right"></i> </a>
</div>
<div class="post">
<h3>How to combine PDF files</h3>
<ol class="with-numbers">
<li><p>Open the <a href="./merge-pdf">Merge PDF files tool</a></p></li>
<li><p>Drag to reorder the files, or sort them alphabetically</p></li>
<li><p>Optionally select only a collection of pages to pick from each file</p></li>
<li><p>Select your documents, or just drag and drop them all to the page</p></li>
<li><p>Generate bookmarks or an outline based on the inputs</p></li>
<li><p>Add file names to the footer of each page</p></li>
<li><p>Generate a nice table of contents based on the documents being merged</p></li>
<li><p>Optionally specify a cover / title page(s)</p></li>
<li><p>Merge Acroforms or flatten form fields</p></li>
</ol>
<a href="./merge-pdf">Merge PDF files <i class="fal fa-arrow-right"></i> </a>
</div>
<div class="post">
<h3>How to convert PDF to JPG</h3>
<ol class="with-squares">
<li><p>Open your PDF with the <a href="./pdf-to-jpg">PDF to JPG, PNG and TIFF converter</a></p></li>
<li><p>Convert all pages or select only pages you want converted</p></li>
<li><p>Select desired image format, PNG, TIFF or JPG</p></li>
<li><p>Choose a higher resolution, 150 or 220</p></li>
<li><p>Quickly convert a PDF page to PNG or JPG</p></li>
<li><p>Download an archive with all the converted images</p></li>
</ol>
<a href="./pdf-to-jpg">PDF to image converter <i class="fal fa-arrow-right"></i> </a>

<a href="./delete-pdf-pages">Delete PDF Pages <i class="fal fa-arrow-right"></i> </a>
