<h3 class="has-subheading">There's more to Megger than you know</h3>
<div class="has-columns">
<div style="font-size:115%">
<p><strong>Understanding every aspect of the electricity network, from generation and transmission to distribution and consumption, is what it takes to be the world leader in cable test, diagnostic and fault location solutions. This requires a huge investment in information gathering, which has historically been part of Megger’s development ethos and continues today. </strong></p>
</div>

This arrangement helps build invaluable profiles of the world’s energy markets and the changes taking place in the segments within them.
