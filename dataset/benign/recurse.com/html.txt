<h1 class="public-header__rc" data-prefix="The ">Recurse Center</h1>
<div class="public-header__columns">
<div class="p-r-2">
<p>
<strong>What we do.</strong> The Recurse Center is a self-directed, community-driven educational retreat for programmers in New York City.
      </p>
<p>
<strong>Our philosophy.</strong> We believe people learn best when they take control of their own education and are free to explore what they’re interested in. RC is heavily influenced by unschooling.
      </p>
<p>
<strong>Never graduate.</strong> RC doesn’t end after you leave. We have a diverse, active, and engaged alumni community of over 1,500 smart, enthusiastic, helpful programmers all over the world.
      </p>
<p>
<strong>Build a career.</strong> We’re here to support you at every stage of your career, whether you’re looking for a new job or not, and whether you just finished RC or attended many years ago.
      </p>
<p>
<a class="display-flex align-items-center m-y-3" href="/about">
<i class="fa fa-chevron-circle-right font-size-large m-r-1"></i> Learn more...
</a> </p>
<p>
<strong>Hire great programmers.</strong> RC has an integrated recruiting agency. We help companies of all sizes hire our alumni. If you care about building productive, diverse teams, <a href="/hire">get in touch</a>.
      </p>
</div>
