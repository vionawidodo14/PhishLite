<h2 class="tip-tittle"><a href="/faq.htm#25">Stay Connected To Your Remote Computers Always Via RemotePC!</a></h2>
<p class="qDate">October 22, 2019</p>
<p>Imagine a scenario – you have to deliver a presentation and while running final checks on the document you find some important data is missing. The data is saved on your home computer but you do not have time to rush home, email the file to yourself and return to the office before the presentation. Time is of the essence in such situations and remote desktop access solutions like RemotePC help save the day!</p>
<p>If you have the RemotePC application installed on your home computer and it is configured for Always-ON Remote Access, you can access it from your office in no time. Remotely control and access the home computer, using any PC, Mac, mobile device or even web browser, from your office. Drag and drop the file from your home computer on to your office computer and you are good to go for the presentation! </p>
<h3 class="newBluTxt">Learn how RemotePC provides a seamless experience with these features:</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong><a href="/faq.htm#3">Instant Access</a></strong><br/>
				Collaborate with your associates sitting at a different branch by sharing a unique one-time Access ID and Key with your associates to provide them temporary instant access to your computer.</li>
<li><strong><a href="/web-viewer.htm">Access Via Web</a></strong><br/>
				Access more than one computer at a time without installing any software on the local computer via RemotePC Viewer Lite.</li>
<li><strong><a href="/faq.htm#1">Platform Independent</a></strong><br/>
				The RemotePC application is platform independent and lets you access your PCs and Macs via any iOS or Android devices anytime.</li>
<li><strong><a href="/faq_security.htm#1">Security</a></strong><br/>
				RemotePC uses TLS v 1.2/AES-256 encryption to offer secure and optimized solution for accessing your faraway computers. A unique Personal Key set for each computer, acts as an extra layer of protection.</li>
</ul>
<h3 class="newBluTxt">What More?</h3>
<ul>
<li><a href="/faq.htm#8">Blank the screen</a> of your remote PC or Mac to prevent others from glancing at your activities during an ongoing remote session.</li>
<li><a href="/faq.htm#9">Transfer files and folders</a> between your computers, even from your mapped drives with less effort. Also, record and save remote sessions easily at your desired location on the local computer.</li>
<li><a href="/faq.htm#25">Print documents</a> and images that are stored on your distant computers from wherever you are. You can even chat with your partner sitting at the remote end by sending/receiving messages.</li>
<li><a href="/faq.htm#3">Collaborate</a> with your associates by drawing on the remote screen via Whiteboard during a session.</li>
<li>Switch between <a href="/faq.htm#24">multiple screens</a> if your distant computer has multiple monitors.</li>
</ul>
<p>There are different <a href="/pricing.htm">pricing plans</a> that can be selected by users depending upon their needs. Try our free plan as you can remotely access one computer for FREE. <a href="/rpcnew/signup">Sign up today with RemotePC</a>!</p>
