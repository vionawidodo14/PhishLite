        South Orange County Community College District (SOCCCD) was founded in 1967. It is a multi-campus district comprised of Saddleback College in Mission Viejo, Irvine Valley College in Irvine, and the Advanced Technology &amp; Education Park in Tustin. We serve more than 60,000 students per year and employ 3,000 faculty and staff. <a href="http://www.socccd.edu/about/about.html">District Profile</a></p>
