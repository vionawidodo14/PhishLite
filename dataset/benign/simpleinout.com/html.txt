<p>Our mission is to have the <strong>most accurate in/out board ever created</strong>. To accomplish this, Simple In/Out can automatically update your status by carrying your phone in and out of the office. We also support updates based on your computer activity.</p>
<h3>Staff Visibility</h3>
<p>No matter how you use Simple In/Out, you'll have an <strong>unprecedented level of visibility into your organization</strong>. View all employee statuses on your phone, computer, television, and more. We include email addresses and phone numbers for a great portable company directory. Using <a href="/en/features/notifications">Notifications</a>, Simple In/Out can keep you informed of your workforce's activity as it happens.</p>
<h3>Employee Time Keeping</h3>
<p>Replace your <strong>punch clock, time cards, and calculator</strong> with a modern approach that saves you time and includes all the benefits of Simple In/Out. Utilizing our app <a href="/en/timeclock">TimeClock</a> and a device in a common area, your users can check in and out with a simple scan of their employee badge.</p>

<h3>Simply Carry Your Phone</h3>
<p>
          Simple In/Out for your phone can be configured to keep your status up-to-date automatically by carrying the device in and out of your office. We have a range of solutions depending on your phone platform and your physical environment. <a href="/en/features/geofences">Geofences</a> use low-power location technology in a way that respects the user's privacy and works on all our phone platforms. <a href="/en/features/beacons">Beacons</a> rely on your phone detecting a Bluetooth signal from a physical device you can purchase pre-configured from our <a href="/en/store">Store</a>. <a href="/en/features/networks">Networks</a> use your existing WiFi Network to check you in/out based on your phone connecting for internet access (only supported on Android and Windows Phone).
        </p>
<h3>Simply Use Your Computer</h3>
<p>
          Our <a href="/en/desktop">Desktop</a> apps can be configured to automatically update your status based on your computer's activity. If you step away for a period of time, Simple In/Out can check you out, and when you return we'll check you back in. <a href="/en/features/desktop_activity">Learn More</a>
</p>
