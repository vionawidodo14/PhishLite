<h2 class="page-title">
Science Videos About Our Diverse World</h2>
<div class="content-entry">
<p><div class="embed-container"><iframe allow="accelerometer; autoplay; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture" allowfullscreen="" frameborder="0" src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/N3Q_wCuep_s?feature=oembed"></iframe></div></p>
<p>Welcome to our website!  We make science videos and we create extra resources for you to get the most out of the videos we make. Peruse our <a href="http://www.untamedscience.com/our-blog/">blogs</a> and the <a href="http://www.untamedscience.com/tree-of-life/">tree of life</a>, our <a href="http://www.untamedscience.com/world-biology/">biology guide</a>, and our <a href="http://www.untamedscience.com/biology/biomes/">biomes videos</a>. Have fun exploring…</p>
<p>Our mission at Untamed Science is to make science education fun. It doesn’t matter if you’re an armchair scientist, a student, a teacher or a scientist yourself. Our goal is to create these science videos and articles that are enjoyable to learn from! If you’re a teacher, we hope they inspire your students to become lifelong learners.</p>
<h2>Pick your Passion: The Untamed Science Portals</h2>
<p><img alt="" border="0" src="http://www.untamedscience.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/09/UNTAMED-SCIENCE-CATEGORIES.jpg" usemap="#Map"/></p>
<map id="Map" name="Map">
<area coords="4,9,211,287" href="http://www.untamedscience.com/tree-of-life/" shape="rect"/>
<area coords="252,8,462,287" href="http://www.untamedscience.com/world-biology/" shape="rect"/>
<area coords="509,10,717,287" href="http://www.untamedscience.com/how-to-filmmaking/" shape="rect"/> </map>
<p>This site is divided up into a few main sections, each of which is created as a unique resource. We have a <a href="http://www.untamedscience.com/tree-of-life/">tree of life</a> that has interesting science videos on biology and ecology on the great taxonomic tree of our world’s species. We have a <a href="http://www.untamedscience.com/world-biology/">biology</a> portal designed to cover everything students would cover in biology. We also have a site dedicated to learn <a href="http://www.untamedscience.com/how-to-filmmaking/">how to make your own science videos</a>.</p>
<p>We hope you enjoy what we’re creating. Start by watching our <a href="http://www.untamedscience.com-videos-list/">science videos</a> from the comfort of your own home. Just sit back and click away!</p>
