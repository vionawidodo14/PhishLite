<h2 class="block__title">Did you know?</h2>
<div class="block__content">
<div class="view view-quick-tips view-id-quick_tips view-display-id-block view-dom-id-21789d88521303e7e34cffb117fb9fe2">
<div class="view-content">
<div class="views-row views-row-1 views-row-odd views-row-first">
<div class="views-field views-field-field-video"> <div class="field-content"><a href="/videos/how-to-enroll-in-e-statements" target="_blank"><img alt="How to enroll in E-statements" src="https://www.lgfcu.org/sites/default/files/styles/video_library/public/video_embed_field_thumbnails/vimeo/132466867.jpg?itok=I6CumfWy" typeof="foaf:Image"/><div class="video-embed-description">How to enroll in E-statements</div>
<img alt="video play button" class="view-play-button" src="/sites/all/themes/lgfcu/images/video-play-sm.png"/></a></div> </div> </div>
<div class="views-row views-row-2 views-row-even">
<div class="views-field views-field-body"> <div class="field-content"><h3>LGFCU is a cooperative</h3>
<p>That just means we’re a group of people united to meet our common economic needs through a jointly-owned and democratically-controlled enterprise. Learn more about the <a href="https://www.lgfcu.org/about-lgfcu/membership/how-credit-unions-work#seven-principles">values</a> LGFCU puts to work for you.</p>
</div> </div> </div>
<div class="views-row views-row-3 views-row-odd">
<div class="views-field views-field-body"> <div class="field-content"><h3>There’s a no-fee coin sorter near you</h3>
<p>Your car console is a wealth-building opportunity. Use a <a href="https://www.lgfcu.org/benefits-and-services/branch-services/checks-and-coins">Fat Cat Coin Sorter</a> at any branch location to see how your spare change can add up! Retail stores often charge a fee to use theirs — we don’t! Don’t forget to check under your sofa cushions for more before you visit.</p>
</div> </div> </div>
<div class="views-row views-row-4 views-row-even">
<div class="views-field views-field-field-video"> <div class="field-content"><a href="/videos/saving-for-college" target="_blank"><img alt="Ways you can save for college" src="https://www.lgfcu.org/sites/default/files/styles/video_library/public/video_embed_field_thumbnails/vimeo/229709803.jpg?itok=fuIG_NrC" typeof="foaf:Image"/><div class="video-embed-description">Ways you can save for college</div>
<img alt="video play button" class="view-play-button" src="/sites/all/themes/lgfcu/images/video-play-sm.png"/></a></div> </div> </div>
<div class="views-row views-row-5 views-row-odd">
<div class="views-field views-field-field-video"> <div class="field-content"><a href="/videos/enroll" target="_blank"><img alt="Enroll in Member Connect" src="https://www.lgfcu.org/sites/default/files/styles/video_library/public/video_embed_field_thumbnails/vimeo/128912148.jpg?itok=xZpC81wJ" typeof="foaf:Image"/><div class="video-embed-description">Enroll in Member Connect</div>
<img alt="video play button" class="view-play-button" src="/sites/all/themes/lgfcu/images/video-play-sm.png"/></a></div> </div> </div>
<div class="views-row views-row-6 views-row-even">
<div class="views-field views-field-body"> <div class="field-content"><h3>You get a second chance here</h3>
<p>Members have the right to appeal the decision when a branch office denies a loan or credit card application. Discover how the volunteer <a href="https://www.lgfcu.org/about-lgfcu/lgfcu-volunteers/committees">Loan Review Committee</a> could help you.</p>
</div> </div> </div>
<div class="views-row views-row-7 views-row-odd">
<div class="views-field views-field-body"> <div class="field-content"><h3>Make saving automatic.</h3>
<p>Pay yourself first! Use <a href="/benefits-and-services/online-services/funds-transfer">automatic deductions</a> from your payroll or checking accounts to make deposits in IRAs, mutual funds, or other savings and investment accounts.</p>
</div> </div> </div>
<div class="views-row views-row-8 views-row-even">
<div class="views-field views-field-body"> <div class="field-content"><h3>Thanks to YOU, we're the best NC Credit Union!</h3>
<p>In 2018 <a href="/news/forbes-survey-ranks-lgfcu-best-credit-union-in-nc" style="background-color: white;">Forbes magazine ranked LGFCU No. 1 Credit Union in North Carolina</a> in its Best-In-State Banks and Credit Unions. With an impressive score of 94.08, factors highlighted included customer service, accessibility and <a href="https://www.lgfcu.org/personal-finance" style="background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255); color: rgba(0, 102, 153, 0.8); outline: 0px; border-bottom: 1px dotted rgb(0, 102, 153); font-size: 16px;">financial advice</a>.</p>
</div> </div> </div>
<div class="views-row views-row-9 views-row-odd">
<div class="views-field views-field-body"> <div class="field-content"><h3>Volunteers are the LGFCU secret sauce</h3>
<p><a href="/about-lgfcu/lgfcu-volunteers/advisory-council">Our volunteers</a><a href="https://www.lgfcu.org/about-lgfcu/lgfcu-volunteers/committees"> </a>are the best example of the credit union grassroots philosophy "people helping people!" Contributing time without pay, volunteers are crucial to LGFCU’s success.</p>
</div> </div> </div>
<div class="views-row views-row-10 views-row-even">
<div class="views-field views-field-body"> <div class="field-content"><h3>Blow your budget? Don’t give up.</h3>
<p>Okay, so you overspent and now you’re over budget. Don't get frustrated. Start fresh the next month and commit to stay in budget. If you need help with your finances <a href="/personal-finance">get information here</a> or make an appointment for <a href="/financial-advice/financial-counseling-program">financial counseling</a> at a branch near you.</p>
</div> </div> </div>
<div class="views-row views-row-11 views-row-odd">
<div class="views-field views-field-body"> <div class="field-content"><h3>Make your wishes known.</h3>
<p>Leave clear instructions about medical decisions that need to be made on your behalf if you can't make them. A<a href="/personal-finance/documents-to-include-in-your-estate-plan" target="_self"> </a>living will and a Healthcare Power of Attorney help make it easier for your loved ones to make tough decisions. Find out more about these important documents at <a href="/benefits-and-services/benefits-services/estate-planning-essentials-program">Estate Planning Essentials</a>. </p>
</div> </div> </div>
<div class="views-row views-row-12 views-row-even">
<div class="views-field views-field-field-video"> <div class="field-content"><a href="/videos/tap-pay-go-with-apple-pay" target="_blank"><img alt="Tap, pay and go with Apple Pay" src="https://www.lgfcu.org/sites/default/files/styles/video_library/public/video_embed_field_thumbnails/vimeo/217900681.jpg?itok=91_Nwbwd" typeof="foaf:Image"/><div class="video-embed-description">Tap, pay and go with Apple Pay</div>
<img alt="video play button" class="view-play-button" src="/sites/all/themes/lgfcu/images/video-play-sm.png"/></a></div> </div> </div>
<div class="views-row views-row-13 views-row-odd">
<div class="views-field views-field-body"> <div class="field-content"><h3>Use scholarships first to pay for college</h3>
<p>Look for scholarships before turning to financial aid to pay for college. Learn about the <a href="/benefits-and-services/scholarships/lgfcu-scholarship">LGFCU Scholarship</a> and find other resources at <a href="http://www.cfnc.org/" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">www.cfnc.org</a>.</p>
</div> </div> </div>
<div class="views-row views-row-14 views-row-even">
<div class="views-field views-field-body"> <div class="field-content"><h3>Life change? Update your will!</h3>
<p>Remember to update your Last Will and Testament each time you have significant life changes such as marriage, divorce or starting a family. Find out how LGFCU can help you get it done with the <a href="https://www.lgfcu.org/benefits-and-services/benefits-services/estate-planning-essentials-program" style="background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255); font-size: 16px;">Estate Planning Essentials Program</a>.</p>
</div> </div> </div>
<div class="views-row views-row-15 views-row-odd">
<div class="views-field views-field-body"> <div class="field-content"><h3>Sometimes a DIY will isn’t enough</h3>
<p>While do-it-yourself wills are cheaper, you might overlook something important that ends up costing your family to lose money or assets. An estate planning attorney in our <a href="/benefits-and-services/benefits-services/estate-planning-essentials-program">Estate Planning Essentials</a> program will ask you the right questions to make sure nothing is missed. </p>
</div> </div> </div>
<div class="views-row views-row-16 views-row-even">
<div class="views-field views-field-body"> <div class="field-content"><h3>Review your will regularly.</h3>
<p>If you created a Last Will and Testament several years ago, it might need updating. Try to review your will at least every three years. LGFCU can help you get it done with the <a href="https://www.lgfcu.org/benefits-and-services/benefits-services/estate-planning-essentials-program">Estate Planning Essentials Program</a>.</p>
</div> </div> </div>
<div class="views-row views-row-17 views-row-odd">
<div class="views-field views-field-body"> <div class="field-content"><h3>LGFCU is a part of an international movement that puts people first</h3>
<p>While our focus is North Carolina, we’re also a part of the worldwide credit union movement! That’s why in October we celebrate International Credit Union Day. This day is dedicated to member appreciation and the <a href="https://www.lgfcu.org/about-lgfcu/membership/how-credit-unions-work">seven basic principles</a> that make credit unions unique. See how <a href="https://www.lgfcu.org/about-lgfcu/lgfcu-story">LGFCU lives those values</a> everyday.</p>
</div> </div> </div>

<div class="views-field views-field-body"> <div class="field-content"><h3>Tech support scams</h3>
<p>If you get a call from someone saying your computer needs an emergency update, hang up. Don’t fall for this <a href="https://www.lgfcu.org/personal-finance/protect-yourself-against-online-scams">tech support scam</a> designed to steal your money or worse, install a virus or malware on your computer. Only allow people you know and trust access to your computer. </p>

<div class="views-row views-row-20 views-row-even">
<div class="views-field views-field-body"> <div class="field-content"><h3>Lock down your home network.</h3>
<p>Got guests? Create another wireless network name and password on your home router specifically for visiting devices. You’ll <a href="https://www.lgfcu.org/personal-finance/improve-your-wireless-network-security-now">improve the security of your wireless network</a> since it’s separate from the family network.</p>
</div> </div> </div>
<div class="views-row views-row-21 views-row-odd">
<div class="views-field views-field-body"> <div class="field-content"><h3>Put tax refunds to work.</h3>
<p>Getting a tax refund? Consider using the funds to <a href="https://www.lgfcu.org/pf-category/debt-management">pay off debt</a> or put into <a href="https://www.lgfcu.org/products/checking-and-savings/money-market">savings</a> to improve your financial situation.</p>
</div> </div> </div>
<div class="views-row views-row-22 views-row-even">
<div class="views-field views-field-body"> <div class="field-content"><h3>Should you have a special needs trust?</h3>
<p>If you have a disabled adult child receiving public benefits, a <a href="https://www.lgfcu.org/products/checking-and-savings/deposit-trust-accounts">Special Needs Trust</a> might be good to consider. This will help you gift money to your child upon your death while still protecting those public benefits. Talk to an attorney specializing in Special Needs Trusts to determine if this is right for you.</p>
</div> </div> </div>
<div class="views-row views-row-23 views-row-odd">
<div class="views-field views-field-body"> <div class="field-content"><h3>You don't have to pay for your credit score</h3>
<p>If you have a loan with LGFCU and have access to Member Connect, you can see your FICO credit score for free. Your score is updated quarterly.</p>
</div> </div> </div>
<div class="views-row views-row-24 views-row-even">
<div class="views-field views-field-body"> <div class="field-content"><h3>You’re a member AND an owner</h3>
<p><a href="/about-lgfcu/credit-union-business#annual-meeting">One member, one vote</a> is how LGFCU members equally participate in electing the leadership, setting policies and making decisions about the Credit Union. It’s what makes credit unions different from banks. We’re a financial cooperative owned and controlled by members.</p>
</div> </div> </div>
<div class="views-row views-row-25 views-row-odd">
<div class="views-field views-field-body"> <div class="field-content"><h3>Don’t take phishing scam bait</h3>
<p>If you’ve ever gotten an email saying you won a prize from a contest you didn’t enter or that a foreign prince wants you to cash a check, then it’s probably a phishing scam. Thieves hope to trick you into sharing personal and financial details. Spot the <a href="https://www.lgfcu.org/personal-finance/spot-the-red-flags-of-phishing-attacks">signs of a phishing attack</a>. If the message seems weird, don’t click; delete it.</p>
</div> </div> </div>
<div class="views-row views-row-26 views-row-even">
<div class="views-field views-field-body"> <div class="field-content"><h3>Shopping for a car?</h3>
<p><a href="/benefits-and-services/auto-services/car-values">Do a little research</a> to get the best deal possible. Visit multiple dealerships  and don’t be afraid to walk away if you don’t like what the dealership is offering you. Or use a <a href="/benefits-and-services/auto-services/new-car-buying-service">car buying service</a> and leave the negotiations to a trusted source.</p>
</div> </div> </div>

<div class="views-field views-field-body"> <div class="field-content"><h3>If you’re an LGFCU member, your family members can join, too!</h3>
<p>Invite your spouse, children, grandchildren, parents, siblings, grandparents, and others, to join the Credit Union focused on <em>you</em>! See a <a href="https://www.lgfcu.org/about-lgfcu/membership/join">complete list</a> of who’s eligible to be a member.</p>
</div> </div> </div>

<div class="views-row views-row-31 views-row-odd">
<div class="views-field views-field-body"> <div class="field-content"><h3>Help for a financial emergency</h3>
<p>In case of an extreme financial emergency, avoid increasing your debt by borrowing against high-interest rate credit cards. And never visit a payday lender! A small, short-term, low-interest<a href="/products/loans/salary-advance-loan" target="_self"> loan against future salary</a> from a trusted lender like LGFCU may be your best bet. </p>
</div> </div> </div>
<div class="views-row views-row-32 views-row-even">
<div class="views-field views-field-body"> <div class="field-content"><h3>Make your estate plan today!</h3>
<p>Don’t wait until your hair begins to gray to <a href="/benefits-and-services/benefits-services/estate-planning-essentials-program">plan for your estate</a>. Secure your wishes and intents in the present by completing your will, your health care directive, and a durable power of attorney.</p>
</div> </div> </div>

<div class="views-field views-field-body"> <div class="field-content"><h3>Big life changes? Review your  insurance.</h3>
<p>Have you recently gotten married or had a child? If so, you should review your insurance needs. Consider purchasing a <a href="/products/insurance/term-life-and-whole-life-insurance">Life Insurance</a> policy or adding to what you may already have.</p>
</div> </div> </div>
<div class="views-row views-row-35 views-row-odd">
<div class="views-field views-field-body"> <div class="field-content"><h3>Changing jobs? </h3>
<p>Resist the temptation to withdraw funds from your retirement account. Roll your funds into your new employer’s plan or into an IRA to save you from taxes and penalties.</p>
</div> </div> </div>
<div class="views-row views-row-36 views-row-even">
<div class="views-field views-field-body"> <div class="field-content"><h3>Save for education. Get tax breaks!</h3>
<p><a href="/products/checking-and-savings/coverdell-esa" target="_self">Coverdell Education Savings Accounts</a> and 529 Plans allow tax-deferred growth of investments and experience no taxes when withdrawn for qualified educational purposes.</p>
</div> </div> </div>
<div class="views-row views-row-37 views-row-odd">
<div class="views-field views-field-body"> <div class="field-content"><h3>Regularly check your credit report</h3>
<p>Check your credit report at least three times a year to make sure there are no errors on it. Visit <a href="http://www.annualcreditreport.com" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">www.annualcreditreport.com</a> to view your official, free credit reports from Equifax, Experian and Transunion. Find incorrect information? Contact the credit reporting agency as soon as possible to dispute it. </p>
</div> </div> </div>

<div class="views-field views-field-body"> <div class="field-content"><h3>Strong passwords protect your devices</h3>
<p>Whether you wear or carry your device, adding a strong password or passphrase can help <a href="/personal-finance/keep-devices-secure-anytime-anywhere">keep devices secure anytime, anywhere</a>. A strong password can’t easily be guessed, is longer, and uses a mix of upper and lowercase letters, numbers and symbols.</p>
</div> </div> </div>
<div class="views-row views-row-40 views-row-even views-row-last">
<div class="views-field views-field-body"> <div class="field-content"><h3>Denied a loan? Appeal it.</h3>
<p>Did you know we have a <a href="https://www.lgfcu.org/about-lgfcu/lgfcu-volunteers/committees">Loan Review Committee</a> composed of volunteers from the membership? Members have the right to ask for a loan decision to be reviewed by this committee. This is one of the unique benefits some credit unions offer that banks don't.</p>
</div> </div> </div>
