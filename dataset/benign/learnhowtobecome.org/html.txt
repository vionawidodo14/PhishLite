<h2 name="first_step">Take Your First Step...</h2>
<p>Many people use "job" and "career" interchangeably. While it's true that each involves working and a wage, a career goes well beyond a paycheck. It’s the teacher who lesson plans at 7 a.m. for 25 rowdy second graders. It’s the nurse who spends time with the disabled veteran on her ward 30 minutes after she’s clocked out. It’s the social worker who makes sure the kids in her care have a warm meal and clean clothes at Christmastime. A career is a commitment; a career is hard work; a career is your passion. Are you ready to find yours?</p>

<h2>Other Resources</h2>
<p>In addition to our guide, the following include some of the top career guide resources from across the Web:</p>
<div class="all-col">
<div class="col2">
<ul>
<li>
<a href="http://careers.state.gov/" target="_blank"><b>The U.S. Department of State’s Careers Representing America</b></a> <span>page includes a career navigator quiz.</span> </li>
<li>
<a href="http://www.bls.gov/ooh/" target="_blank"><b>The Bureau of Labor Statistics’ Occupational Outlook Handbook </b></a> <span>has information on a variety of careers, including average pay and the anticipated job market in coming years.</span> </li>
<li>
<a href="http://www.onetonline.org/" target="_blank"><b>O*net Online </b></a> <span>has descriptions for hundreds of occupations and can help match you with a career by your skills, abilities, interests, and more.</span> </li>
<li>
<a href="http://www.nbcc.org/" target="_blank"><b>The National Board for Certified Counselors</b></a> <span>can help match you with a qualified career counselor in your area.</span> </li>
<li>
<a href="http://www.careerinfonet.org/" target="_blank"><b>The Career InfoNet</b></a> <span>site has information on employment growth, occupational salaries, links to employers, and more.</span> </li>
<li> <a href="http://www.affordablecollegesonline.org/degrees/" target="_blank"><b>Online Degrees by ACO</b></a> <span>provides in-depth degree and career information on over 40 majors, including detailed online college rankings.</span> </li>
</ul>
</div>
<div class="col2 right">
<ul>
<li>
<a href="https://www.careeronestop.org/Site/american-job-center.aspx" target="_blank"><b>American Job Center</b></a> <span>has career guides as well as job search tools, information for veterans, and more.</span> </li>
<li>
<a href="https://career.berkeley.edu/" target="_blank"><b>UC Berkeley’s Career Center</b></a> <span>offers a free, downloadable PDF guide to preparing for the job search, finding an internship, developing a resume, making a career transition, and more.</span> </li>
<li>
<a href="http://www.ocs.fas.harvard.edu/" target="_blank"><b>Harvard’s Office of Career Services </b></a> <span>has an interactive guide to different types of careers, which also includes tips for "getting started" with internships as well as job opportunities abroad. Though some of their information is specific for Harvard students, much of it is helpful for anyone looking to get started on one of the career paths included on their site.</span> </li>
<li>
<a href="https://www.usajobs.gov/" target="_blank"><b>USAJOBS</b></a> <span>helps those interested in government jobs find openings.</span> </li>
<li>
<span><a href="http://www.accreditedschoolsonline.org/online-colleges/" target="_blank"><b>Accredited Schools: Online Colleges Guide</b></a> helps prospective students find and compare thousands of schools by degree type, location, tuition and other factors.</span> </li>
</ul>
