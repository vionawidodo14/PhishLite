<h1 class="title">AME CET 2020</h1>
<div class="content">
<p class="justify">Aircraft Maintenance Engineering Common Entrance Test (AME CET) is the national-level common entrance exam to offer admission for Aircraft Maintenance Engineering, Aerospace Engineering, and Aeronautical Engineering courses in approved Institutes.</p>
<p class="justify">An aircraft is made of numerous parts, engines, electrical, and electronic systems etc. Designing of aircraft is a complex task which is performed by the experts. Aeronautical Engineering is the engineering branch that deals with the designing and manufacturing of aircraft within the Earth’s atmosphere. Aerospace Engineering is the branch combines the designing and manufacturing of aircraft within the Earth atmosphere as well as outside the Earth atmosphere. Safety is an important aspect when it comes to the aviation sector. With time and use, parts tend to wear and tear down. Thus regular inspection and maintenance of aircraft have become very necessary. Aircraft Maintenance Engineering professionals are trained to inspect an aircraft, diagnose problems, report the problems found, and finally solve them.</p>
<p class="justify">AME CET Entrance exam has only one paper. The exam is of objective pattern in both Hindi &amp; English languages. Based on Entrance Test Rank of AME CET, students will participate in counselling and will confirm their admission in one of the best institutions according to their merit.</p>
<p class="justify">Candidates can apply for AME CET exam only three times. A candidate must have passed 10+2 with the PCM stream or equivalent AICTE approved 3 years engineering diploma or any higher qualification in science with Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics. NRIs, PIOs, OCIs &amp; Foreign national candidates can also apply.</p>
</div>

<h2 class="title">AME CET 2020 Scholarships </h2>
<div class="row">
<div class="col-12 col-sm-12 col-md-8 col-lg-8 col-xl-8 mt-50 mt-30-xs">
<p class="justify">AME CET scholarship programme upto 100% offered to the deserving/meritorious students to pursue Aircraft Engineering Courses which are Aircraft Maintenance Engineering, Aerospace Engineering, and Aeronautical Engineering. The educated youth will lead India to develop the nation, so AME CET is awarding a scholarship to the talented students. This scholarship provides an excellent opportunity to the students to build their golden career as Aircraft Maintenance Engineers, Aerospace Engineers, and Aeronautical Engineers. The scholarship will be purely based on the performance of the students.</p>
<p><strong>The scholarship be avail by performing the following procedure:-</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Student can check the availability to pursue Aircraft Engineering course.</li>
<li>From the Apply Now button, the student can fill up the AME CET  registration form online, and there is offline mode also available.</li>
<li>The AME CET written exam will be conducted at National Level in which student can participate to get admission in Aircraft Maintenance Engineering, Aerospace Engineering, and Aeronautical Engineering Courses.</li>
<li>Based on its All India Rank (AIR), students can avail upto 100 % scholarship on tuition fees for the first semester only.</li>
<li>If the student has topped their State Board / University and participated in AME CET examination, then also they can avail 100% scholarship on tuition fees for the first semester only.</li>
</ul>
