<div class="views-field views-field-title"> <span class="field-content"><a href="https://www.newberry.org/what-is-the-midwest-exhibition">What Is the Midwest?</a></span> </div>
<div class="views-field views-field-field-slideshow-subtitle"> <div class="field-content">Exhibition</div> </div>
<div class="views-field views-field-field-slideshow-date"> <div class="field-content">September 20-December 31</div> </div>
<div class="views-field views-field-body"> <div class="field-content"><p>Often called “the Heartland” or “flyover country,” the Midwest tends to be characterized as a homogeneous, barren space between the American coasts. This exhibition challenges long-standing narratives about the Midwest, exploring the confluence of peoples and environmental conditions that has defined the region and made it unique.</p>
</div> </div>

<div class="views-field views-field-title"> <span class="field-content"><a href="https://www.newberry.org/newberry-receive-collection-eastland-disaster-historical-society">The Newberry to Receive Eastland Disaster Collection</a></span> </div>
<div class="views-field views-field-field-slideshow-subtitle"> <div class="field-content"></div> </div>
<div class="views-field views-field-field-slideshow-date"> <div class="field-content"></div> </div>
<div class="views-field views-field-body"> <div class="field-content"><p>An agreement between the Newberry and the Eastland Disaster Historical Society will bring a comprehensive collection of photographs, family records, and historical documents to the Newberry. Together, these materials tell a vivid story about the Eastland Disaster of 1915, the 844 people who tragically died in the event, and the impact it had on their families and the city of Chicago at large.</p>
