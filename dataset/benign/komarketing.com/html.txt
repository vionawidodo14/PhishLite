<h3>John Deere Machine Finder</h3>
<p>When KoMarketing started working with John Deere in 2003 (they continue to be a client), the company’s website, machinefinder.com, had virtually no search engine optimization, and extremely dated site technology. Challenges included:</p>
<ul>
<li>Poor optimization overall, including lack of optimized foreign language content</li>
<li>Non-indexing of pages including foreign language URLs</li>
<li>Lack of backlinks pointing back to the website</li>
</ul>
<p>Results: The initial optimization quadrupled the search engine traffic to the site, with dealers seeing a pronounced improvement in direct inquiries for equipment.</p>
