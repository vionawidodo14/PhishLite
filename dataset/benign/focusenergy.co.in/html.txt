We have signed Production Sharing Contract (PSC) with Government of India to explore and develop Onshore and offshore oil and gas blocks in India named as "RJ-ON/6" and "CB-OSN-2004/1"– encompassing a total area of 4792 sq. km. We are one of the few companies which own in-house expertise along with full-fledged machineries for Drilling, Seismic, Mud Logging, Mud Engineering, Cementing, Open Hole Drill Stem Testing, Wireline and Hydraulic Fracturing services.
