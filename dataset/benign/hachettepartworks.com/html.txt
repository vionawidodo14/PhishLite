<h4>A PUBLISHING COMPANY WITH A STRONG CREATIVE HERITAGE</h4>
<p>Hachette Partworks publishes collectable series such as Marvel Graphic Novel Collections, Judge Dredd, Art therapy, the Art of Crochet and more!</p>
<h4>THE LEADER IN PARTWORKS PUBLISHING</h4>
<p>Hachette Partworks publish in most major English speaking markets including the UK, Republic of Ireland, Australia, New Zealand, South Africa and Malta.</p>
<p>We have an outstanding catalogue of both mass market and niche partwork collections, including our hugely successful, innovative craft collections such as Art therapy and The Art of Crochet and our exceptionally detailed build-up models such as Your Model Railway Village as well as our high-quality book collections including Marvel, The Ultimate Graphic Novel collection and Doctor Who: The Complete Collection.</p>
<p>With fascinating and inspiring editorial content across a wealth of subjects, and big name licenses such as Marvel and Disney, Hachette Partworks create high-quality products across every genre.</p>
