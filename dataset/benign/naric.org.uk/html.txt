<h1>Welcome to UK NARIC</h1>
<p><b>UK NARIC is the designated United Kingdom national agency for the recognition and comparison of international qualifications and skills. It performs this official
        function on behalf of the UK Government</b>.</p>
<p>
<a href="Individuals/Default.aspx">For individuals</a>
        – do you want to study, work, or settle in the UK?
        <a href="Individuals/Default.aspx">We can help you</a>.</p>
<p>
<a href="Organisations/Default.aspx">For organisations</a>, UK NARIC provides vital support to universities,
        colleges and employers with international recruitment and the processing of international applications for work or study.
        <a href="Organisations/product%20catalogue">A range of subscription packages</a>
       offers 24/7 access to our online data banks of international education systems and qualifications, plus online support. For occasional users,
        <a href="Organisations/product%20catalogue/Assessments%20Bundle.aspx">short term packages</a>
        and
        <a href="Organisations/product%20catalogue/One%20Off%20Enquiries.aspx">one-off services</a>
        are also available.</p>
<p>UK NARIC 
        <a href="Organisations/Product%20Catalogue/default.aspx">member organisations</a> are supported by a range of
        <a href="Organisations/training/default.aspx">training and events</a>, including 
        <a href="Organisations/Training/Default.aspx#eTraining">eTraining</a>, and an
        <a href="Organisations/conference/default.aspx">annual conference</a>.</p>
<p>UK NARIC also offers worldwide
        <a href="Organisations/Bespoke%20Research/Default.aspx">advanced research and consultancy</a>
        for employers, skills councils, awarding bodies, education authorities and governments.</p>
<p></p>
