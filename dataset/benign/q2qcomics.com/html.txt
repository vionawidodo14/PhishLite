<div class="entry-content">
<p>We had an excellent time at USITT in Louisville.</p>
<p>I signed all of the copies of Volume 2 and drew tons of doodles (propcat was especially popular this year). And you nerds brought it tons of weird crap for me to sign.  The list of unusual items I’ve signed now includes: a water bottle, a rescue inhaler, a rubber duckie, TWO resumes (please someone hire both of them), a kid’s fan art of Wuggles, a light plot, a Nerds plushie, and a knee brace.</p>
<p>And, as is now tradition, our own little gremlin La Ropasucia got up to no good on the Stage Expo floor.</p>
