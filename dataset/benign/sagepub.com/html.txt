<div class="field field-name-body field-type-text-with-summary field-label-hidden"><div class="field-items"><div class="field-item even"><p class="heading-medium">Welcome to SAGE</p>
<p>Sara Miller McCune founded SAGE Publishing in 1965 to support the dissemination of usable knowledge and educate a global community. SAGE is a leading international provider of innovative, high-quality content publishing more than 1000 <a href="https://us.sagepub.com/en-us/nam/Journals">journals</a> and over 800 new <a href="https://us.sagepub.com/en-us/nam/collegepublishing">books</a> each year, spanning a wide range of subject areas. Our growing selection of <a href="https://us.sagepub.com/en-us/nam/digital-products">library products</a> includes archives, data, case studies and <a href="https://us.sagepub.com/en-us/nam/sage-video">video</a>. SAGE remains majority owned by our founder and after her lifetime will become owned by a charitable trust that secures the company’s continued independence. Principal offices are located in Los Angeles, London, New Delhi, Singapore, Washington DC and Melbourne.</p>
