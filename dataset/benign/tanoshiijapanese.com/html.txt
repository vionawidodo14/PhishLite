<img class="main" height="140" src="../graphics/geisha.png" style="float: left; margin: 0px 12px 4px 0px;"/>
<p><span style="font-size: 14px; color: black;">Welcome!</span> <span class="minidictionary alt-color2" definition="楽しい">楽しい Japanese</span>
	is a site dedicated to making learning the Japanese language a
	<a href="../dictionary/index.cfm?e=fun%20enjoyable">fun and enjoyable</a>
	experience for you.  You will find here a <a href="../dictionary/">Japanese to English  dictionary</a>,
	various <a href="../practice/">fun games</a>,
	<a href="../lessons/">interactive lessons</a>,
	<a href="../blogs/">cultural resources</a> and a
	<a href="../forum/">friendly community</a>
	which will help you to read, write and speak Japanese, and allow you to
	share your passion about the Japanese culture.
	So, whether you are just beginning your journey learning about this beautiful culture
	and its language, are wanting to understand the latest anime and jdrama without the
	need for subtitles, or are looking to improve your fluency in preparation for working
	or living in Japan, there are a number of helpful tools and resources here that you may use.</p>
