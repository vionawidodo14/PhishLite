<div class="lo-container">
<div class="title-block">
<h2>How Exercism works</h2>
<p>Learning through Exercism is quite different to other programming websites, with a focus on individual practice and mentor-based learning. Here's how it works.</p>
</div>
<div class="step step-small pure-g">
<div class="pure-u-1">
<img alt="" src="https://assets.exercism.io/assets/how-step-3-cdf396c1da68deb92f387de5d5e22001820630a09edabb819ad918d8768d912a.png"/>
</div>
</div>
<div class="step pure-g lalign">
<div class="pure-u-1 pure-u-sm-1-3 step-image">
<img alt="" src="https://assets.exercism.io/assets/how-step-1-2d46db72deab8b19f60d1e0a94bd9aa6784f484f8d0d687d06ec8e38271450d4.png"/>
</div>
<div class="pure-u-1 pure-u-sm-1-12"></div>
<div class="pure-u-1 pure-u-sm-7-12">
<h3>1. Choose a language to master</h3>
<p>Object oriented, functional, popular, emerging, or just plain obscure. With 51 different languages to explore, Exercism has something for you!</p>
</div>
</div>
<div class="divider"></div>
<div class="step pure-g">
<div class="pure-u-1 pure-u-sm-1-3 step-image">
<img alt="" src="https://assets.exercism.io/assets/how-step-2-aa9d08a7873f6137fb875bf1e72e4292eb549f76670498e569383103ae9799ef.png"/>
</div>
<div class="pure-u-1 pure-u-sm-1-12"></div>
<div class="pure-u-1 pure-u-sm-7-12">
<h3>2. Complete a coding challenge on your computer</h3>
<p>All the coding happens on your machine in an environment you're familiar with. Download the template, solve the problem, and then upload your solution.</p>
</div>
</div>
<div class="divider"></div>
<div class="step pure-g">
<div class="pure-u-1 pure-u-sm-1-3 step-image">
<img alt="" src="https://assets.exercism.io/assets/how-step-3-cdf396c1da68deb92f387de5d5e22001820630a09edabb819ad918d8768d912a.png"/>
</div>
<div class="pure-u-1 pure-u-sm-1-12"></div>
<div class="pure-u-1 pure-u-sm-7-12">
<h3>3. Upload your solution and review it with a mentor</h3>
<p>Discussing code is one of the best ways to learn. Our friendly mentors will comment on your solutions online, introducing you to new ideas and techniques.</p>
</div>
</div>
<div class="divider"></div>
<div class="step step-4 pure-g">
<div class="pure-u-1 pure-u-sm-1-3 step-image">
<div class="waterfall" style="background-position: 0px 104.567px;"></div>
<img alt="" src="https://assets.exercism.io/assets/how-step-4-1d6d05fa8f4b23c3ebc67b88214b62f26b059bcd47d409128bb410a9976a85ab.png"/>
</div>
<div class="pure-u-1 pure-u-sm-1-12"></div>
<div class="pure-u-1 pure-u-sm-7-12">
<h3>4. Unlock different concepts and exercises</h3>
<p>Our tracks have between 40 and 100 exercise each - some are fun, some are tricky, some are weird, but all will teach you something new.</p>
</div>
</div>
<div class="divider"></div>
<div class="step pure-g">
<div class="pure-u-1 pure-u-sm-1-3 step-image">
<img alt="" src="https://assets.exercism.io/assets/how-step-5-615a85bcf66919966c5bf088c09d6c482f4e421122958f755bab87fea49e9c8b.png"/>
</div>
<div class="pure-u-1 pure-u-sm-1-12"></div>
<div class="pure-u-1 pure-u-sm-7-12">
<h3>5. Complete the language track and achieve fluency</h3>
<p>At the end of your language track you will have mastered the core concepts and idioms of your language and be ready to use it in the real world.</p>
</div>
</div>
</div>
