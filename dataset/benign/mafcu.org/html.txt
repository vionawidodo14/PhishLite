<h2 id="aboutHeader">About Us</h2>
<p>There's simply no better place to help manage your finances in Montgomery County, Maryland than at Mid-Atlantic Federal Credit Union (MAFCU) - and virtually anyone can. Consumers and businesses that are part of this community save more - and earn more - with MAFCU loans, savings and checking  accounts, which offer better rates and fewer fees than those at other financial institutions.</p>
<p>MAFCU is a not-for-profit financial cooperative managed for the sole benefit of our members. Our volunteer Board of Directors and professional management team work hard to make sure MAFCU helps our members achieve their financial goals.</p>
