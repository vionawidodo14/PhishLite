<h2 class="is-size-2-mobile">
<span class="label">Our College</span><br/>
                        A Catholic School for boys in the tradition of Edmund Rice
        </h2>
<p>St Kevin's has developed a strong academic tradition where each boy is encouraged to take responsibility for his own learning in an environment that values knowledge and scholarship.</p>
</div>
</section> <section class="text-width-bg block-left border-white" style="background-image: url('https://digistorm-websites.s3-ap-southeast-2.amazonaws.com/skc-au-vic-239-website/content/AA-Header-and-Hero-Images/_banner/Home-Page-Sub-Header.jpg?mtime=20180316133722');">
<div class="overlay wow fadeInUp" style="visibility: hidden; animation-name: none;">
<article>
<h2 class="is-size-2-mobile">
<span class="label style2">Our Approach</span><br/>
                                Edmund Rice <br/>
Tradition And History
            </h2>
<p><strong>Inspired by the contemplative spirit of St Kevin and Glendalough, and Christ's transforming vision of humanity.</strong></p>
<p>In this spirit, we aim to be a welcoming and hospitable community that will provide strong witness to Gospel values.</p>
</article>

<h2 class="is-size-2-mobile hack1 wow fadeInUp" style="visibility: hidden; animation-name: none;">
<span class="label color-white">Our History</span><br/>
                                        Saints and Scholars
                </h2>
<div class="wow fadeInUp" style="visibility: hidden; animation-name: none;">
<p>In founding the College in 1918 the Christian Brothers set out to establish a strong tradition of high academic standards.</p>
<p>St Kevin's was the Matriculation centre for all the Brothers' schools in Melbourne. With its early academic success, the enrolment soon outgrew its buildings in East Melbourne and so the College moved to Toorak in 1932. Here it grew to a full primary and secondary school.<br/></p>
</div>
</article>

                                        Excellence In Learning
                </h2>
<div class="wow fadeInUp" style="visibility: hidden; animation-name: none;">
<p>Central to the mission of St Kevin's College is the promotion of excellence in learning as an essential means of facilitating human growth and liberation, the recognition of family life as providing an affirming context for the nurturing of the whole person and a desire for all to achieve fullness of life</p>
