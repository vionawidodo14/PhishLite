<h2><a href="/backorder-expired-domains/">Expired Domain Names</a></h2>
<span class="pull-right"><strong>478</strong> TLDs supported and rising!</span>
</div>
<div class="box-content">
<p>
			Thousands of domain names expire every day. The reasons are different. Some owners forgot to renew the
			domains, some just don't want them anymore or they moved on to other projects.
			For most people these so called <strong>Expired Domains</strong> don't have any value.
			They just see a bunch of Domain Names someone else deleted and move on, but for the people
			who know about SEO or the value of good Backlinks, <strong>Expired Domain Names</strong> are money just
			waiting to get picked up from the street. The only problem is to separate the good ones from the bad ones.
			That is where ExpiredDomains.net comes into play.
		</p>
<p>
<strong>ExpiredDomains.net</strong> gathers all the information you need to find good
			<a href="/backorder-expired-domains/">Expired Domains</a> that are Pending Delete and you can
			<a href="/article/howto-backorder-expired-domains-15102.html">Backorder</a>.
			Depending on the domain extension you can search through thousands of domains every day before
			they get released to the public and pick what you like.
			ExpiredDomains.net currently supports
			<a href="/tlds/">478 TLDs</a>.
			From the classic gTLDs like .com, .net, .org to Droplists for ccTLDs you can only find here
			and now we even support some of the best new gTLDs like .xyz and .club.
		</p>
</div>
</div>
<div class="box">
<div class="box-header">
<h3><a href="/deleted-domains/">Dropped Domains</a></h3>
</div>
<div class="box-content">
<p>
			We do not only have Expired Domains, but you can also find lists of
			<a href="/deleted-domains/">Deleted Domains</a> for a lot of TLDs.
			Deleted Domains or <strong>Dropped Domains</strong> are available for registration and can be picked up
			for just the normal regfee at your preferred domain registrar!
			All Domains have the typical SEO relevant data, like Number of Backlinks, Archive.org Birth Date
			and a lot more.<br/>
			Just look through the thousands of Expired Domain Names dropping every day and pick what you like. :)
			You can use our numerous Filters and sort the lists as you like or use our Watchlist to keep track
			of Expired Domains after they dropped.
		</p>
<p>
			You can go through the lists you are interested in and look for Domains you like or you can use
			our super fast <a href="/domain-name-search/">Domain Name Search</a> to search all
			Domain Lists at once. Whatever you choose to do, you can use numerous filters to drill down the number of domains,
			so you find exactly what you are looking for!
		</p>
</div>
</div>
