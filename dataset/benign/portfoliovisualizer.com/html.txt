<h2>Portfolio Optimization</h2>
<p>Chart the efficient frontier to explore risk vs. return trade-offs based on historical or forecasted returns. Optimize portfolios based on mean-variance, conditional value-at-risk (CVaR), risk-return ratios, or drawdowns. Apply the Black-Litterman model to find the optimal portfolio based on market views.</p>
