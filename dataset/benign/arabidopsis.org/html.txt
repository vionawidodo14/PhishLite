<p>
</p><h3>About TAIR</h3><br/>
        The Arabidopsis Information Resource (TAIR) maintains a <a href="/search/ERwin/Tair.htm">database</a> of genetic and <a href="/about/datasources.jsp">molecular biology data</a> for the model higher plant <i><a href="/portals/education/aboutarabidopsis.jsp">Arabidopsis thaliana</a> </i>. Data available from TAIR includes the complete genome sequence
        along with gene structure, gene product information, gene expression, DNA and seed stocks, genome maps, genetic and physical markers,
        publications, and information about the Arabidopsis research community. Gene product function data is updated every week from the latest
        published research literature and community data submissions. TAIR also provides extensive linkouts from our data pages to other Arabidopsis
        resources.
    <p></p>
<p id="fund">
<a href="http://www.phoenixbioinformatics.org/"> <img alt="Phoenix Bioinformatics logo" height="40" id="phoenix" src="/i/phoenix_logo.png" width="69"/>
</a> TAIR is located at <a href="http://www.phoenixbioinformatics.org/">Phoenix Bioinformatics </a> and funded by <a href="http://www.arabidopsis.org/doc/about/tair_subscriptions/413">subscriptions</a>.
    </p>
<p>
        Full access to TAIR requires a subscription. Please see
        our <a href="/doc/about/tair_subscriptions/413">subscription page</a> for further details.
    </p>
