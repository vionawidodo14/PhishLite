<p class="opening-content">Ohio State faculty and staff contribute in significant ways to transform themselves and the world.

As one of the world's most comprehensive universities, opportunities are everywhere. Whether you teach students, provide professional services, care for patients, or perform research, Ohio State offers something for nearly everyone and your work makes a meaningful difference.

Faculty and staff aid Ohio State student's world-class education to help them make an impact and excel as scholars, athletes, artists and leaders.

Explore exciting employment opportunities.

<div class="alert alert-warning text-box-two eq-height" style="height: 219px;">
<h3 class="text-box-title">Discovery Themes</h3>
<div class="text-box-body"><p>The Discovery Themes provide Ohio State with an unprecedented opportunity to find durable solutions to today's and tomorrow's most compelling issues. With expertise in these areas, the Discovery Themes are long-term targets of university-wide teaching, research, and engagement. They include:
</p><ul>
<li>Energy and Environment</li>
<li>Food Production and Security</li>
<li>Health and Wellness</li>
<li>Humanities and the Arts</li>
</ul>

<div class="text-box-body">The Ohio State University provides reasonable accommodations to applicants and employees with disabilities.
