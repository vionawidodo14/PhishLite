					HDR Light Studio: Lighting Software for 3D Artists				</h1>
<p style="font-size:20px;">
					HDR Light Studio lets you easily create and edit HDR lighting (HDRI map &amp; HDR Area Lights) with your existing 3D software.				</p>
</div>

<img alt="HDR Light Studio interface" src="https://www.lightmap.co.uk/dynamic_resources/hdrls_introduction_content/4/image.png?t=5f45d9becb05f8e9ec471818a7467085" style="width:100%" title="HDR Light Studio interface"/>
<p>
</p><p>The HDRI map updates in real-time as lights are added and adjusted. The interactive render updates with these changes.</p> <p></p>
