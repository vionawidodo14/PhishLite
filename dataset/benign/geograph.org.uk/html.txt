<div class="interestBox" style="padding:2px;text-align:center;border-radius:6px;">
The <b>Geograph<sup style="font-size:0.4em">®</sup> Britain and Ireland</b> project aims to collect geographically
representative photographs and information for every square kilometre of <a href="/explore/places/1/">Great Britain</a> and
<a href="/explore/places/2/">Ireland</a>, and you can be part of it.</div>
<div style="text-align:center; padding:10px">Since 2005, <b class="nowrap">13,044 contributors</b> have submitted <b class="nowrap">6,274,426 images</b> <span class="nowrap">covering <b class="nowrap">280,187 grid squares</b>, or <b class="nowrap">84.3%</b> of the total squares</span></div>
