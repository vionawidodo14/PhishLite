<h3><a href="/documents-publications/show/818">Regional Indicative Strategic Development Plan</a></h3>
<p>The Regional Indicative Strategic Development Plan of SADC is a comprehensive 15–year strategic roadmap, which provides the strategic direction for achieving SADC’s long term social and economic goals. It also provides the SADC Secretariat and other SADC institutions with clear guidelines on SADC’s approved social and economic priorities and policies and therefore, enhancing their effectiveness in discharging their facilitating and coordinating role. It was approved by SADC Summit in 2003 and its effective implementation began in 2005.</p> <p><a class="btn-origin" href="http://twitter.com/home?status=Regional+Indicative+Strategic+Development+Plan+%23SADC+https://sadc.int/documents-publications/show/818" target="_new">Tweet!</a></p>

<h3><a href="/documents-publications/show/2033">SADC SIPO II (English)</a></h3>
<p>The Revised Edition of the Strategic Indicative Plan for the Organ (SIPO II) provides a review of the first edition of the Plan.
