style="text-decoration:underline;">» more</a></p></div></div><h1>DB-Engines</h1><p>DB-Engines is an initiative to collect and present information on database management systems (DBMS).
In addition to established relational DBMS, systems and concepts of the growing NoSQL area are emphasized.
</p><p>The <a href="https://db-engines.com/en/ranking">DB-Engines Ranking</a> is a list of DBMS ranked by their current popularity. The list is updated monthly.
</p><p>The most important properties of numerous systems are shown in the overview of <a href="https://db-engines.com/en/systems">database management systems</a>.
You can examine the properties for each system, and you can compare them side by side.
</p><p>In the <a href="https://db-engines.com/en/articles">database encyclopedia</a> terms and concepts on this topic are explained.
</p><p>DB-Engines has been created and is maintained by <a href="https://db-engines.com/en/about">solid IT</a>.
