<h2 id="section-Main-AuthorizationIsRequiredToEditPages"> <a class="wikipage" href="/wiki/Authorization">Authorization</a> is Required to <a class="wikipage" href="/wiki/Update">Edit</a> Pages<a class="hashlink" href="#section-Main-AuthorizationIsRequiredToEditPages">#</a></h2>
Anyone can <a class="wikipage" href="/wiki/ReadOnly">read</a> any of the WIKI pages (most anyhow). However to edit WIKI Pages requires an account.
<p>You will be prompted and need to provide:
</p>
<ul><li>A email address to which you have access
</li><li>First name
</li><li>Last Name
</li><li>Preferred UserID
</li><li>password
</li></ul><p>Should you encounter any problems, please <a class="external" href="mailto:info@willeke?subject=Comment from ldapwiki on registration">Contact us</a><img alt="" class="outlink" src="/images/out.png"/> and provide your name a real email address and we will <a class="wikipage" href="/wiki/Email%20Address">email Address</a> your desired initial <a class="wikipage" href="/wiki/Credential">credentials</a>.
</p>
<p>More information on <a class="wikipage" href="/wiki/ThisWilki">customization for this Wiki</a>.
</p>
