<h2 class="scroll-reveal text-sm-xxl">More About Us</h2>
</div>
<div class="col-lg-8">
<p class="no-indent-dc">Poetry in Translation is a <a href="https://www.poetryintranslation.com/Admin/Bio.php" title="About Us">niche, digital publisher</a> providing modern, high-quality open access translations of classic texts by famous poets, plus original poetry and critical works. If you value our work, please consider <a href="https://www.poetryintranslation.com/Admin/Donationspage.php" title="Support Us">supporting us</a>.</p>
<p>Our policy is that of providing open access, downloadable texts, free for <strong>non-commercial</strong> reuse. If you wish to enquire about commercial applications, <a href="https://www.poetryintranslation.com/Admin/Contact.php" title="Contact Us">contact us</a>. For further information, please see our <a href="https://www.poetryintranslation.com/Admin/Copyright.php">copyright statement</a>.</p>
<p>Many of our works are now available in  print or audio  editions. For further details please visit <a href="https://www.poetryintranslation.com/Admin/Hardcopy.php">our shop</a>. If you have any suggestions for further poetry texts that you would like to see translated, please don’t hesitate to <a href="https://www.poetryintranslation.com/Admin/Contact.php" title="Contact Us">contact us</a>.</p>
<p>You can search our archive via our <a href="https://www.poetryintranslation.com/Admin/Search.php" title="Search">search engine</a> and follow our <a href="https://www.poetryintranslation.com/Admin/Newsupdate.php" title="News">news feed</a> for regular updates. You may also be interested in our selection of <a href="https://www.poetryintranslation.com/Admin/Sitelinks.php">quality external links</a>.</p>
