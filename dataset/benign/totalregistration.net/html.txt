<h2><span>New Tools</span> to Help AP Coordinators with Exam Ordering Changes</h2>
</div>
<p>Total Registration has supported over 50+ schools that have piloted the College Board's Fall AP Exam Ordering.  From these schools we have learned about new challenges facing AP Coordinators and have added new tools to assist with these challenges.
      </p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Financial Reconciliation</strong> - Reconcile students' payments/waivers to CB enrollment.</li>
<li><strong>Join Code Distribution</strong> - TR will distribute to students  all the join codes they need for their unique courses.</li>
<li><strong>Late Fee Management</strong> - Assess late fees to late registrations for students in 1st semester or all your courses while exempting 2nd semester courses from the CB Late Fee.</li>
</ul>
<p>These new tools are on top of the existing TR tools/features:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Financial Tracking</strong> - Keep track of fees, waivers, scholarships, payments, refunds, etc.</li>
<li><strong>Payment Processing</strong> - TR can handle all the families' payments, refunds, etc.</li>
<li><strong>Communications</strong> - Communicate with Students, Parents, Teachers and Proctors</li>
<li>Exam Organization - Keep Track of Accommodations, Room Assignments, Generate Seating Charts (random at the click of a button) ,Organizational Labels</li>
<li> . . . and much, much more</li>
</ul>
