<h2 class="entry-title"><a href="https://order-order.com/2019/10/23/camerons-pps-refused-buy-book/" rel="bookmark">Cameron’s Own PPS Refused to Buy his Book</a></h2>
</div>
<div class="entry-content">
<!-- FEN: 20 -->
<p><img alt="" class="aligncenter size-large wp-image-361802" data-recalc-dims="1" height="283" sizes="(max-width: 540px) 100vw, 540px" src="https://i1.wp.com/order-order.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/book-backstabbing.png?resize=540%2C283&amp;ssl=1" srcset="https://i1.wp.com/order-order.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/book-backstabbing.png?resize=540%2C283&amp;ssl=1 540w, https://i1.wp.com/order-order.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/book-backstabbing.png?resize=172%2C90&amp;ssl=1 172w, https://i1.wp.com/order-order.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/book-backstabbing.png?resize=270%2C141&amp;ssl=1 270w, https://i1.wp.com/order-order.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/book-backstabbing.png?w=600&amp;ssl=1 600w" width="540"/></p>
<p>It was thought that Cameron having to <a href="https://order-order.com/2019/09/30/camerons-memoirs-reduced-20-tory-conference/">reduce the price of his book at Tory conference</a> would be the low point of the former PM’s literary career. However, Guido now hears that the aversion to forking out for his book extends well beyond Tory Party members – <span style="color: #ff0000;"><em>to the education secretary, Gavin Williamson: his former PPS…</em></span></p>
<p>In a recent meeting with the PM, Cameron asked Williamson if he’d purchased his book, to which Gavin had to sheepishly admit he hadn’t yet (and as a Yorkshireman was holding out for a larger discount). Cameron promptly responded by throwing a copy of the book at him. <em><span style="color: #ff0000;">Guido imagines Theresa will feel a twinge of jealousy over Cameron’s ability to lob something heavy at the former Defense secretary…</span></em></p>

</div>
<div class="entry-content">
<!-- FEN: 20 -->
<p><img alt="" class="aligncenter size-large wp-image-361808" data-recalc-dims="1" height="283" sizes="(max-width: 540px) 100vw, 540px" src="https://i0.wp.com/order-order.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/sadiq-climate.jpg?resize=540%2C283&amp;ssl=1" srcset="https://i0.wp.com/order-order.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/sadiq-climate.jpg?w=540&amp;ssl=1 540w, https://i0.wp.com/order-order.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/sadiq-climate.jpg?resize=172%2C90&amp;ssl=1 172w, https://i0.wp.com/order-order.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/sadiq-climate.jpg?resize=270%2C142&amp;ssl=1 270w" width="540"/></p>
<p>As any Londoner with a television knows, Sadiq Khan loves putting the environment at the forefront of his work in office and is today convening political and business leaders at the <em>International Clean Air Summit</em> to call for more powers to tackle air pollution. <em><span style="color: #ff0000;">Perhaps he’d be better off organising a hot air summit…</span></em></p>
<p>As ever with Labour, the words fail to match the actions; as since taking charge of City Hall Sadiq has done surprisingly little to clean up the 1,034 vehicles in his GLA fleet – <a href="https://www.london.gov.uk/questions/2019/19811">just<strong><em> </em></strong>7%</a> of which are zero-emission. <span style="color: #ff0000;"><em>93% of Sadiq’s fleet fails to meet the lofty standards he aims to force on Londoners…</em></span></p>
<p>This could have been expected, however, given the mayor is not on track to meet any of his lofty green promises. In 2016 he promised Londoners he<span style="font-family: -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, 'Segoe UI', Roboto, Oxygen-Sans, Ubuntu, Cantarell, 'Helvetica Neue', sans-serif;"> would plant over two million trees during his first term in office. Based on current projections, the Mayor would need to plant over 200 trees an hour from now until election day to make good on his promise. <span style="color: #ff0000;"><em>Unluckily for Londoners’ lungs, Guido has more dirt on their mayor than their mayor has under his fingernails…</em></span></span></p>

<h2 class="entry-title"><a href="https://order-order.com/2019/10/23/salma-yaqoob-shared-rothschild-conspiracy-theory-website/" rel="bookmark">Salma Yaqoob Shared Rothschild Conspiracy Theory Website</a></h2>
</div>
<div class="entry-content">
<!-- FEN: 20 -->
<p><img alt="" class="aligncenter size-large wp-image-361796" data-recalc-dims="1" height="283" sizes="(max-width: 540px) 100vw, 540px" src="https://i1.wp.com/order-order.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/yaqoob.jpg?resize=540%2C283&amp;ssl=1" srcset="https://i1.wp.com/order-order.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/yaqoob.jpg?w=540&amp;ssl=1 540w, https://i1.wp.com/order-order.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/yaqoob.jpg?resize=172%2C90&amp;ssl=1 172w, https://i1.wp.com/order-order.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/yaqoob.jpg?resize=270%2C142&amp;ssl=1 270w" width="540"/></p>
<p><a href="https://order-order.com/2019/09/19/another-labour-politician-rips-off-us-democrat-speech/">Salma Yaqoob</a> is the Corbynites’ favourite to become Labour’s candidate for West Midlands. The former Respect Party candidate – accused of intimidation and <a href="https://www.independent.co.uk/news/uk/politics/labour-lgbt-west-midlands-metro-mayor-homophobic-salma-yaqoob-a9164741.html">homophobia</a> – has been endorsed by <a href="https://twitter.com/owenjones84/status/1174354927709315072">Owen Jones</a>, <a href="https://twitter.com/jonlansman/status/1173931485629493248">Jon Lansman</a>, and <a href="https://twitter.com/PeoplesMomentum/status/1182261157714939904">Momentum</a>. <span style="color: #ff0000;"><em>Yaqoob has previously <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20120603140856/http://www.salmayaqoob.com/2010/10/viva-palestina-convoy-breaks-siege-and.html" style="color: #ff0000;">supported</a> the <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20121001000000*/http://www.terrorism-info.org.il/malam_multimedia/English/eng_n/html/ipc_e138.htm" style="color: #ff0000;">pro-Hamas</a> ‘Viva Palestina’ convoy, made up <a href="https://www.scribd.com/document/426569972/Salma-Yaqoob-Abu-Hamza" style="color: #ff0000;">part of the campaign team</a> which supported the family of Abu Hamza, and <a href="http://www.hurryupharry.org/wp-content/uploads/2008/11/salama_y.pdf" style="color: #ff0000;">wrote an article</a> in which she imagined Britain becoming an Islamic Republic…</em></span></p>
<p>Now Guido can reveal that Yaqoob has also egged on the Rothschild conspiracy theory – a trope that <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wFGjIEcvP7Q">even Momentum</a> admit is anti-semitic.</p>
<p><img alt="" class="aligncenter size-large wp-image-361764" data-recalc-dims="1" height="231" sizes="(max-width: 540px) 100vw, 540px" src="https://i2.wp.com/order-order.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/Screenshot-2019-10-23-10.38.09.png?resize=540%2C231&amp;ssl=1" srcset="https://i2.wp.com/order-order.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/Screenshot-2019-10-23-10.38.09.png?resize=540%2C231&amp;ssl=1 540w, https://i2.wp.com/order-order.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/Screenshot-2019-10-23-10.38.09.png?resize=172%2C74&amp;ssl=1 172w, https://i2.wp.com/order-order.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/Screenshot-2019-10-23-10.38.09.png?resize=270%2C116&amp;ssl=1 270w, https://i2.wp.com/order-order.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/Screenshot-2019-10-23-10.38.09.png?w=1070&amp;ssl=1 1070w" width="540"/></p>
<p>In 2013 the wannabe candidate <a href="https://twitter.com/SalmaYaqoob/status/313427252396314624">linked</a> on <em>Twitter</em> to <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20121015022228/https://current.com/community/93925713_iceland-dismantles-the-corrupt-arrests-10-rothschild-bankers-then-issues-interpol-arrest-warrant-for-rothschild-banker-sigurdur-einarsson.htm">conspiratorial website</a> about <em>“Rothschild bankers”</em> in Iceland and London, which itself pointed readers to a longer <a href="https://politicalvelcraft.org/2012/10/09/iceland-dismantles-the-corrupt-arrests-10-rothschild-bankers-then-issues-interpol-arrest-warrant-for-rothschild-banker-sigurdur-einarsson/">article</a> hitting out at Rothschild <em>“banking puppets”,</em> asks readers to <em>“End [the] New World Order”, </em>and praises Viktor Orban’s Hungarian Government for <em>“Kill[ing] The Rothschild Banks”. <span style="color: #ff0000;">She is favourite to win the selection…</span></em></p>

<h2 class="entry-title"><a href="https://order-order.com/2019/10/23/boris-promised-focus-tax-cuts/" rel="bookmark">Boris Promised Tax Cut Focus</a></h2>
</div>
<div class="entry-content">
<!-- FEN: 20 -->
<p><img alt="" class="aligncenter size-large wp-image-361768" data-recalc-dims="1" height="283" sizes="(max-width: 540px) 100vw, 540px" src="https://i1.wp.com/order-order.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/TAX-1.png?resize=540%2C283&amp;ssl=1" srcset="https://i1.wp.com/order-order.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/TAX-1.png?resize=540%2C283&amp;ssl=1 540w, https://i1.wp.com/order-order.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/TAX-1.png?resize=172%2C90&amp;ssl=1 172w, https://i1.wp.com/order-order.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/TAX-1.png?resize=270%2C141&amp;ssl=1 270w, https://i1.wp.com/order-order.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/TAX-1.png?w=600&amp;ssl=1 600w" width="540"/></p>
<p>In news that would attract more attention if not for Brexit, the government is <a href="https://www.ft.com/content/c3c5acc0-f4e7-11e9-a79c-bc9acae3b654">briefing</a> that there will be no the major tax cuts despite the promises Boris made during his leadership campaign, such as raising the income threshold for lower earners and increasing the 40% tax rate threshold from £50,000. <em><span style="color: #ff0000;">Going into an election without having delivered Brexit or tax cuts means Boris definitely won’t be running on his record as PM.<br/>
</span></em></p>
<p><iframe allowfullscreen="allowfullscreen" frameborder="0" height="315" src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/ydMSuhLIFZQ" width="560"></iframe></p>
<p>Guido has been told in explicit terms “<em>the cuts ain’t happening</em>“, with the Treasury instead prioritising spending on infrastructure, such as roads and hospitals. The Treasury is already weary of Boris’s spend-happy approach to government, with the policies announced at Tory conference alone totaling almost £60 billion. Government borrowing was £9.4 billion in September according to <a href="https://twitter.com/Fraser_ONS_PSF/status/1186560475875741697/photo/1">data released yesterday</a>. The first time in 5 years that the level of borrowing has increased year-on-year in September. Total borrowing for the financial year to date stands at £40.3 billion, £7.2 billion more than in the same period last year. <em><span style="color: #ff0000;">In comparison the revenue lost to HMRC keeping the promise Boris made to raise the higher tax threshold for the middle classes looks like mere pocket change.</span></em></p>

<p>Labour’s previously stated position is that they’re positively ecstatic about the thought of an election, provided Article 50 is extended to January next year. In a move as unsurprising as the sun rising in the morning, however, some Labour figures are showing signs of cold feet. <em><span style="color: #ff0000;">Any wonder given Boris’s 15 point lead in the polls?…</span></em></p>
<p>Most obviously giving the game away is Lord Wood, who tweeted he wouldn’t actually back an election until around 2020…</p>

Then Guido received an email from Momentum, crying out that Boris’s election call is, in fact, a ‘threat’.</p>

<h2 class="entry-title"><a href="https://order-order.com/2019/10/23/downing-street-sources-temporarily-silenced/" rel="bookmark">Downing Street Sources Temporarily Silenced</a></h2>
</div>
<div class="entry-content">
<!-- FEN: 20 -->
<div class="video-container"><span class="embed-youtube" style="text-align:center; display: block;"><iframe allowfullscreen="true" class="youtube-player" height="507" src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/4fCkCWs3yzU?version=3&amp;rel=1&amp;fs=1&amp;autohide=2&amp;showsearch=0&amp;showinfo=1&amp;iv_load_policy=1&amp;wmode=transparent" style="border:0;" type="text/html" width="900"></iframe></span></div>
<p>Yesterday, Peter Oborne published a characteristic <span class="cit type-translation"><span class="quote"><a href="https://www.opendemocracy.net/en/opendemocracyuk/british-journalists-have-become-part-of-johnsons-fake-news-machine/">scream of pain</a> at the over-use of<em> “Downing Street sources”</em> by Lobby hacks. Oborne <a href="https://www.opendemocracy.net/en/opendemocracyuk/british-journalists-have-become-part-of-johnsons-fake-news-machine/">argued</a> that the quoting of unnamed sources facilitates lying and makes hacks the hands free delivery mechanism for fake news. It is noticeable that, of late, tweeting Lobby hacks repeat anonymous threats from Downing Street sources which habitually do not eventually materialise. He pointed the finger at almost everyone from Laura Kuenssberg to Robert Peston. <span style="color: #ff0000;"><em>For hours after his piece was published, the customary tweeted insider nuggets of information were no longer dipped in the Downing Street source…</em></span></span></span></p>
<p>Nobody seemingly dared report anything<em> “according to a Downing Street source”.</em> ITV’s Paul Brand was the first to break the self-imposed embargo at teatime. His <a href="https://twitter.com/PaulBrandITV/status/1186674278726545410">attribution</a> was greeted by a barrage of abuse on Twitter. <span style="color: #ff0000;"><em>Twittering Lobby hacks went silent again on what Downing Street was saying…</em></span></p>
<p>At 10:00 pm Peston cracked, <a href="https://twitter.com/Peston/status/1186749070121025536">tweeting</a></p>
<blockquote><p><em>“So here is No.10 confirming that if EU gives the requested three-month Brexit delay, Boris Johnson will ask for an immediate general election. Loads of you have poured buckets of manure on me for telling you what Downing St is thinking and saying. So you are totally at liberty to ignore this statement or disbelieve it. But it would be wholly wrong of me to withhold this from you, on the patronising assumption you cannot analyse and contextualise it.”</em></p></blockquote>
<p>His tweet thread included a Whatsapp text from his undisclosed Downing Street source. <span style="color: #ff0000;"><em>He was combative (given he has made his career from repeating things he has been told by sources that is hardly surprising).</em></span></p>
<p>Perhaps emboldened by Pesto, at 10:05 pm <span class="cit type-translation"><span class="quote">Laura Kuenssberg</span></span> risked <a href="https://twitter.com/bbclaurak/status/1186750392371208192">tweeting</a><em> “No 10 confirms tonight after the vote they’ll try to push for election if EU offers delay.*”</em> <span style="color: #ff0000;"><em>She was greeted with tweets saying she was a Downing Street mouthpiece…</em></span></p>
<p>Oborne is right to say that hacks whose main journalistic asset appears to be phone numbers for spokesman really need to be less reliant on, and more careful about, re-broadcasting a line that the source would be too embarrassed to say in public. The reality is that it isn’t in the interests of hacks to open up the Lobby system or insist more often that quotes are on the record. Transparency will devalue their role because information scarcity makes their possession of a spokesman’s phone number more valuable. <span style="color: #ff0000;"><em>A start to improving and opening up the system would be to put Lobby briefings in the open and televised…</em></span></p>
<p>*Boris had in any case <a href="https://order-order.com/2019/10/22/boris-confirms-will-pull-wab-government-loses-vote-bill-timetable-tonight/">already said</a> this publicly earlier in the day in the Commons.</p>
