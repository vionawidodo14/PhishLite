<b><a href="/Mona" title="Mona">Mona</a></b> is a major <a href="/Human" title="Human">human</a> character in the <i><a href="/WarioWare_(series)" title="WarioWare (series)">WarioWare</a></i> series. One of <a href="/Wario" title="Wario">Wario</a>'s original employees and microgame programmer, she is also shown working part-time in many establishments of <a href="/Diamond_City" title="Diamond City">Diamond City</a>, most owned by <a href="/Joe" title="Joe">Joe</a>, while competing with <a href="/The_Dinosaurs" title="The Dinosaurs">The Dinosaurs</a>, a rival group. Since her introduction in <i><a href="/WarioWare,_Inc.:_Mega_Microgame$!" title="WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!">WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!</a></i>, Mona has appeared in all installments of the <i>WarioWare</i> series except <i><a href="/WarioWare:_D.I.Y._Showcase" title="WarioWare: D.I.Y. Showcase">WarioWare: D.I.Y. Showcase</a></i>.</div>

<a class="image" href="/File:AlphaDream_Logo.png"><img alt="AlphaDream Logo.png" height="105" src="/images/thumb/1/13/AlphaDream_Logo.png/140px-AlphaDream_Logo.png" srcset="/images/1/13/AlphaDream_Logo.png 1.5x" width="140"/></a></div>
<ul><li>An official <a href="/Nintendo" title="Nintendo">Nintendo</a> store called <b><a class="external text" href="https://www.nintendo.co.jp/officialstore/index.html" rel="nofollow">Nintendo Tokyo</a></b> was <a class="external text" href="https://twitter.com/NintendoNYC/status/1186831322779701248" rel="nofollow">announced</a> to open in Shibuya Parco in Japan on November 22, 2019.</li>
<li><b><a href="/AlphaDream" title="AlphaDream">AlphaDream</a></b> <i>(pictured)</i>, creators of the <i><b><a href="/Mario_%26_Luigi_(series)" title="Mario &amp; Luigi (series)">Mario &amp; Luigi</a></b></i> series, filed for bankruptcy proceedings on October 1, 2019, citing sluggish sales and increasing development costs.</li>
<li><i><b><a href="/Mario_Kart_Tour" title="Mario Kart Tour">Mario Kart Tour</a></b></i> was released worldwide on September 25, 2019.</li>
<li>During the Nintendo Direct on September 4, 2019, <b><a href="/Terry_Bogard" title="Terry Bogard">Terry Bogard</a></b> was announced as a DLC fighter in <i><a href="/Super_Smash_Bros._Ultimate" title="Super Smash Bros. Ultimate">Super Smash Bros. Ultimate</a></i>, and more information on <i><b><a href="/Luigi%27s_Mansion_3" title="Luigi's Mansion 3">Luigi's Mansion 3</a></b></i> and <i><b><a href="/Mario_%26_Sonic_at_the_Olympic_Games_Tokyo_2020" title="Mario &amp; Sonic at the Olympic Games Tokyo 2020">Mario &amp; Sonic at the Olympic Games Tokyo 2020</a></b></i> was revealed. <b><a href="/Super_Nintendo_Entertainment_System_-_Nintendo_Switch_Online" title="Super Nintendo Entertainment System - Nintendo Switch Online">Super Nintendo Entertainment System - Nintendo Switch Online</a></b> was also announced for release on September 5 in the Americas and September 6 in Japan, Europe, and Australia, with <i><a href="/Super_Mario_World" title="Super Mario World">Super Mario World</a></i>, <i><a href="/Super_Mario_Kart" title="Super Mario Kart">Super Mario Kart</a></i>, and <i><a href="/Super_Mario_World_2:_Yoshi%27s_Island" title="Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island">Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island</a></i> being among the 20 launch titles.</li></ul></div>

<h2><span id="Did_You_Know?"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Did_You_Know.3F">Did You Know?</span></h2>
</th></tr>
<tr>
<td>
<div style="padding:0 8px 1px 0; margin-top:-2px">
<div id="mf-dyk">
<div style="float:right; margin:0 0 0.2em 0.7em; border:1px solid #3B587E; background:#FFFFFF;">
<a class="image" href="/File:Statue_SMB_style.png"><img alt="Statue SMB style.png" height="140" src="/images/a/a1/Statue_SMB_style.png" width="70"/></a></div>
<ul><li>On the cover of <i><b><a href="/New_Super_Mario_Bros._Wii" title="New Super Mario Bros. Wii">New Super Mario Bros. Wii</a></b></i>'s game disc, <a href="/Mario" title="Mario">Mario</a>, <a href="/Luigi" title="Luigi">Luigi</a>, <a href="/Blue_Toad_(character)" title="Blue Toad (character)">Blue Toad</a>, and <a href="/Yellow_Toad_(New_Super_Mario_Bros._series)" title="Yellow Toad (New Super Mario Bros. series)">Yellow Toad</a> are positioned in the same pattern as the buttons on the European, Japanese, and Australian <a href="/Super_Nintendo_Entertainment_System" title="Super Nintendo Entertainment System">Super Nintendo Entertainment System</a> controller.</li>
<li>In <b><a href="/Bowser_in_the_Sky" title="Bowser in the Sky">Bowser in the Sky</a></b>, the pillars near the <a href="/Warp_Pipe" title="Warp Pipe">Warp Pipe</a> to the <a href="/Bowser" title="Bowser">Bowser</a> battle have engraved markings of an 8-bit Mario fighting an 8-bit Bowser <i>(pictured)</i>, likely a callback to the original <i><a href="/Super_Mario_Bros." title="Super Mario Bros.">Super Mario Bros.</a></i></li>
<li>In the mission <b><a href="/Blasting_through_the_Sand" title="Blasting through the Sand">Blasting through the Sand</a></b> in the <a href="/Dusty_Dune_Galaxy" title="Dusty Dune Galaxy">Dusty Dune Galaxy</a>, the <a href="/Eye_Beamer" title="Eye Beamer">Eye Beamers</a> on the third <a href="/Dusty_Dune_Galaxy#Speedy_Sand_Planets" title="Dusty Dune Galaxy">Speedy Sand Planet</a> initially spawn inside the large planet at the center of the galaxy, only moving to the Speedy Sand Planet after the level is loaded.</li>
<li><b><a href="/Water_Park" title="Water Park">Water Park</a></b>'s Aqua Cups ride plays the calliope music from the carousel in <a href="/Big_Boo%27s_Haunt" title="Big Boo's Haunt">Big Boo's Haunt</a>.</li></ul></div>
