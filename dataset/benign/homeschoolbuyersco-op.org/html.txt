<div class="containerSpecials">
<p><b>Why we're here:</b><br/>
School districts get huge discounts on their curriculum. <i>Why not homeschoolers?</i> The reason is simple: school districts have purchasing power and homeschoolers don't because we purchase as individuals. The <b>Homeschool Buyers Co-op</b> changes that for homeschoolers everywhere.
</p>
<p><b>What we provide:</b><br/>
We save you 20%-90% on your homeschool <b>curriculum</b>. By pooling the purchasing power of our entire membership, we are able to call major educational publishers and homeschooling curriculum providers and negotiate much better prices for our members than they can get on their own. And it works! Virtually every vendor will give discounts for their <b>homeschool curriculum</b> if they are assured adequate volumes.
</p>
<p><b>How we do it:</b><br/>
How do we get the volumes needed for affordable homeschooling programs? We do it through a unique process called a "<a href="/home-school-curriculum/">GroupBuy</a>". Essentially, we negotiate volume discounts with the <b>homeschool curriculum</b> publishers, then put an offer out to our members that says, for example, "Hey, Co-opers, you can get 20% off for this homeschool curriculum if you purchase by the end of the month, but if we get 20 members to sign up, everybody will get 30% off, and if 50 members sign up, everybody will get 40% off and so on." Once the GroupBuy is complete, we are often able to lock in those GroupBuy savings and offer them year-round.
</p>
<p><b>Why you should join:</b><br/>
Great deals are not the only reason for joining the Co-op. Read the <a href="/homeschool-curriculum/">top 5 reasons for joining</a>.
</p><p>
Then <a href="#" onclick="openSignUp();"><b>JOIN TODAY!</b></a> It's absolutely FREE and you can trust us to never use your contact info for anything other than to bring you the greatest homeschool curriculum values on earth. And please share and tell your homeschool friends and groups about the Co-op because the more of us who join, the greater our purchasing power, the more affordable the homeschooling programs, and the deeper the discounts we can get for our homeschool curriculum! Also, be sure to check for fun happenings on our <a href="https://www.facebook.com/homeschoolbuyers" target="_blank">Facebook</a> page as well.
</p> </div>
