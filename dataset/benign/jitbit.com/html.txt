				Deliver awesome support to your customers. Jitbit <a href="/helpdesk/">Help Desk ticketing system</a> is dead simple, amazingly powerful and takes seconds to set up.

<h3 class="leftHeader">Help Desk Automation</h3>
<p>Automate the repetitive parts of customer support. And save a heap of time in the process. </p>
<p>With our powerful automation triggers you can ask Jitbit to send automatic replies, assign technicians to tickets and set ticket due dates. You can even get the help desk to send HTTP requests to third-party servers.</p>
<p>A power user feature, Jitbit automation takes care of the routine. So you get to spend more time helping out your customers.</p>

<h3 class="leftHeader">Team mailbox</h3>
<p>Imagine a shared inbox for your helpdesk team. Then throw in some advanced reporting.</p>
<p>How about bulk actions and ticket-merging capabilities? And let’s add transparency for your customers into the mix.</p>
<p>That’s what Jitbit is all about.</p>
<p>The main ticket list lets you and your team see everything that's happening at a glance. It helps you prioritize your support ticket queue. And recognize which tickets need your attention right now.</p>
<p>With rich filtering and sorting features too, you’ll never forget to reply to another ticket again.</p>

<h3>Canned responses and Knowledge Base</h3>
<div class="span10 offset1">

<p>Insert canned responses or links to Knowledge Base articles with a couple of clicks. Jitbit even suggests relevant Knowledge Base articles to customers while they’re typing support requests.</p>

<p>Let your customers suggest and vote for new features. And discuss them in a public forum. This free add-on makes product management both easy and data-driven.</p>
<p>You can prioritize your roadmap publicly. And discover which features and improvements your users really want to see.</p>

<h3>Integration with 500+ applications</h3>
<div class="span10 offset1">
<img alt="Integrations" class="lazyimg slideinup" data-original="https://i.imgur.com/3MVbqGs.png" style="width: 580px; display: block;margin:10px auto;"/>
<noscript><img alt="Integrations" src="https://i.imgur.com/3MVbqGs.png"/></noscript>
<p>Jitbit Helpdesk offers built-in integration with Slack, Jira, Github, Dropbox, Google Drive, Asana, Harvest, Visual Studio Online and many more. It’s also connected to Zapier, which facilitates integration with 500+ different apps.</p>
<p>Built-in API and webhooks mean you can connect to your in-house apps in seconds.</p>
<p>We also have several built-in single sign-on options. So you can authenticate users via SAML, Active Directory (including Azure), Google Account or your own custom app or website.</p>
