<h4 class="u-txt--hp-block-header u-margin-bottom--large">Edhesive Courses</h4>
<div class="o-section">
<section class="o-section__column col-md-3 col-sm-6 col-xs-12">
<h5 class="u-txt--hp-block-title">Explorations in <span class="u-no-txt-breaks">Coding I</span></h5>
<p class="u-txt--hp-block-text">Project-based introduction to computer science with a focus on coding.</p>
<p class="u-txt--hp-block-duration u-margin-bottom--zero">Duration: 1 Semester</p>
</section>
<section class="o-section__column col-md-3 col-sm-6 col-xs-12 u-margin-top-on-xs">

<p class="u-txt--hp-block-text">A continuation of Explorations in <span class="u-no-txt-breaks">Coding I</span> with capstone projects.</p>
<p class="u-txt--hp-block-duration u-margin-bottom--zero">Duration: 1 Semester</p>
</section>
<section class="o-section__column col-md-3 col-sm-6 col-xs-12 u-break-on-sm u-margin-top-on-sm">
<h5 class="u-txt--hp-block-title"><span class="u-no-txt-breaks">Intro to CS I</span></h5>
<p class="u-txt--hp-block-text">Learn the fundamentals of computer programming and the Python language.</p>
<p class="u-txt--hp-block-duration u-margin-bottom--zero">Duration: 1 Semester</p>
</section>
<section class="o-section__column col-md-3 col-sm-6 col-xs-12 u-margin-top-on-sm">
<h5 class="u-txt--hp-block-title"><span class="u-no-txt-breaks">Intro to CS II</span></h5>
<p class="u-txt--hp-block-text">A continuation of Intro to Computer <span class="u-no-txt-breaks">Science I</span>, along with an introduction to web development.</p>
<p class="u-txt--hp-block-duration u-margin-bottom--zero">Duration: 1 Semester</p>
</section>
<div class="o-section__column col-xs-12">
<hr class="u-margin-vertical--element"/>
</div>
<section class="o-section__column col-md-6 col-sm-12 col-xs-12">
<h5 class="u-txt--hp-block-title">AP Computer Science Principles</h5>
<p class="u-txt--hp-block-text">Equivalent to a college-level Introduction to Computer Science for non-STEM majors, students learn foundational concepts of computer science and explore the impact computing and technology have on our society.</p>
<p class="u-txt--hp-block-duration u-margin-bottom--zero">Duration: 1 Year</p>
</section>
<section class="o-section__column col-md-6 col-sm-12 col-xs-12 u-margin-top-on-sm">
<h5 class="u-txt--hp-block-title">AP Computer <span class="u-no-txt-breaks">Science A</span></h5>
<p class="u-txt--hp-block-text">Equivalent to a college-level Introduction to Computer Science for STEM majors, students deep-dive into programming and learn the basics of the Java language.</p>
<p class="u-txt--hp-block-duration u-margin-bottom--zero">Duration: 1 Year</p>
</section>
<div class="o-section__column col-xs-12">
<hr class="u-margin-vertical--element"/>
</div>

<h5 class="u-txt--hp-block-title">AP Statistics</h5>
<p class="u-txt--hp-block-text">This course covers the basics of statistical research and analysis, teaching methods for data collection, interpretation and analysis.</p>
<p class="u-txt--hp-block-duration u-margin-bottom--zero">Duration: 1 Year</p>
