It is an exam intended for non-native English speakers. Its purpose is to find out what your level of English is (your result is a <a href="http://www.ielts.org/test_takers_information/getting_my_results.aspx" target="_blank" title="IELTS results">band score</a> from 0 to 9). Usually you need to take the IELTS test if you are applying for a job or to study  in a college or university in an English-speaking country or if you are migrating to such country. There are 2 modules of the IELTS test – Academic and General. The Academic Module is for future students and the General Module is for immigrants.</p><p><strong>The IELTS Routine</strong></p><p>The IELTS test consists of four parts in the following order (*)</p><p> <strong><a href="https://www.ielts-blog.com/1/listening-at-a-glance/" target="_blank" title="More about IELTS Listening test">Listening test</a></strong></p><p> <strong><a href="https://www.ielts-blog.com/1/reading-at-a-glance/" target="_blank" title="More about IELTS Reading test">Reading test</a></strong></p><p> <strong><a href="https://www.ielts-blog.com/1/writing-at-a-glance/" target="_blank" title="More about IELTS Writing test">Writing test</a></strong></p><p> <strong><a href="https://www.ielts-blog.com/1/speaking-at-a-glance/" target="_blank" title="More about IELTS Speaking test">Speaking test</a></strong></p><p><strong>The Listening test</strong> takes about 40 minutes – 30 minutes to listen to a tape and to answer questions on what you hear, and 10 minutes to transfer your answers to the Answer Sheet.</p><p><strong>The Reading test</strong> takes 1 hour and your task is to read passages of text and answer questions according to what you have read. There are also other types of assignments which I will cover later on in other posts on this website.</p><p><strong>The Writing test</strong> also takes 1 hour and is divided into 2 sub-parts: you are given 20 minutes to write a letter/report and 40 minutes to write an essay.</p><p><strong>The Speaking test</strong> takes up to 15 minutes and consists of 3 parts: a Personal Interview, a Cue Card talk and a Discussion.</p><p>All the parts continue one after another, and only before the Speaking test you get a break. Sometimes the Speaking test is even held on a different day.<br/> (*) The order of IELTS sub-tests has been changed in Australia – <a href="https://www.ielts-blog.com/1/ielts-test-in-australia-a-change-in-the-order-of-component/" target="_blank" title="Changes in the order of IELTS sub-tests">learn more here</a>.</p><p><strong>The IELTS test formalities</strong></p><p>When it becomes clear to you that you need to take the IELTS test, you’ll have to go through some formalities – which are:</p><p>1. Note: the dates could be different for the Academic and General IELTS exams.</p><p>2.
