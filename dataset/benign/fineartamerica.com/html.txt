<h2>About Fine Art America</h2>
<p class="headerSubtext" style="padding-top: 0px;">Fine Art America is the world's largest art marketplace and print-on-demand technology company.   We've been helping artists sell wall art, home decor, apparel, and other products since 2006 and are home to hundreds of thousands of artists, photographers, graphic designers, illustrators, and iconic brands.</p>
<p class="headerSubtext" style="padding-top: 20px;">With just a few clicks, artists and photographers can upload their images to FineArtAmerica.com, set their prices for hundreds of different print-on-demand products, and then instantly <a href="/sell-art-online.html" style="font-size: inherit; font-family: inherit;">sell</a> those products to a global audience of online, mobile, and real-world buyers.   Fine Art America fulfills each order on behalf of the artists - taking care of the printing, framing, matting, packaging, shipping, collecting payments from the buyers, and sending profits to the artists.   Each product is manufactured at one of our 14 global production facilities and delivered "ready-to-hang" with a 30-day money-back guarantee.</p>
<p class="headerSubtext" style="padding-top: 20px;">In addition to providing an online marketplace and fulfillment service, Fine Art America provides artists and photographers with sales and marketing tools to help simplify and accelerate their careers.   These tools allow artists to set up branded web stores, sell prints on Facebook, create e-newsletters, and much more.   More than 500,000 living artists and photographers currently offer ten million images for sale on FineArtAmerica.com - with thousands of new artists and images being added each week.</p>
<p class="headerSubtext" style="padding-top: 20px;">Hundreds of thousands of artists and iconic brands... millions of images... billions of products... 14 global production facilities in 5 different countries running 24 hours per day... and the world's hardest working staff running the show from Santa Monica, California.</p>
<p class="headerSubtext" style="padding-top: 20px;">Welcome to Fine Art America.</p>
