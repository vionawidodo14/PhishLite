<h2 class="text-truncate">Choosing a Homestay Program</h2>
</div><!-- .text-center -->
        There are three main accommodation options during your time in the US as an international student: an apartment, university residence halls, or a homestay. While all three options offer their own benefits, a homestay will offer an experience unlike the others. Homestays provide international students with additional support during their studies; along with an inside look at life in the United States.
