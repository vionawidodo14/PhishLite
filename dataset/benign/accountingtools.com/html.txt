It contains continuing professional education (CPE) courses, business books, the Accounting Best Practices podcast, and articles on thousands of topics. Our emphasis is on clear explanations of the accounting standards and the practical management aspects of accounting, finance, and operations. The core areas of the site are noted below.</p><h2 style="white-space:pre-wrap;"><a href="/cpe">CPE Courses</a></h2><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">We offer more than 150 courses for those needing continuing professional education training.
