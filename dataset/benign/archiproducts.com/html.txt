<p class="pre-wrap hidden-text"></p><p>With over 200,000 products, 3,500 brands and 2.7 million registered users, Archiproducts is the largest network for Architecture and Design. Every day thousands of Architects, Designers, retailers and brands from all over the world visit the website in search of the finest solutions to <b>design</b>, inspire, <b>build</b> and <b>furnish online</b>. A continuously updated product catalogue with detailed product data sheets, including technical specs, information request forms, <b>pdf catalogues</b> and ready-for-download <b>BIM and 3D files</b>.</p>
<p>Browse among 12 categories – from <a href="https://www.archiproducts.com/en/products/furniture" title="furnishing">Furnishing</a> to <a href="https://www.archiproducts.com/en/products/lighting" title="Lighting">Lighting</a>, from <a href="https://www.archiproducts.com/en/products/bathroom" title="Bathroom">Bathroom</a> to Outdoor, from <a href="https://www.archiproducts.com/en/products/office" title="Office">Office</a>, to <a href="https://www.archiproducts.com/en/products/kitchen" title="Kitchen">Kitchen</a>, <a href="https://www.archiproducts.com/en/products/decor" title="Decor">Decor</a>, <a href="https://www.archiproducts.com/en/products/wellness" title="Wellness">Wellness</a> and <a href="https://www.archiproducts.com/en/products/contract" title="Contract">Contract</a> – choose and shop any type of product on our <b>e-commerce platform</b>. From the domestic to the professional space, Archiproducts Shop is the official retailer of 600 brands including <a href="https://www.archiproducts.com/en/vitra" title="Vitra">Vitra</a>, <a href="https://www.archiproducts.com/en/knoll">Knoll</a>, <a href="https://www.archiproducts.com/en/flos" title="Flos">Flos</a>, <a href="https://www.archiproducts.com/en/artemide" title="Artemide">Artemide</a>, <a href="https://www.archiproducts.com/en/zanotta" title="Zanotta">Zanotta</a> and many more. A multilingual customer care offers consulting services, customized shipping and assistance throughout the whole shopping experience.</p>
<p><b>Sofas and armchairs</b> are the stars of the <b>living area furniture</b>. There are thousands of products available, from the <a href="https://www.archiproducts.com/en/poltrona-frau" title="Poltrona Frau">Poltrona Frau</a> leather sofas to the accessories signed by Billiani, Tacchini, Living Divani, Flexform, Casamania&amp;Horm, Gufram, <a href="https://www.archiproducts.com/en/magis" title="Magis">Magis</a>, Desalto, Dedon, Riva 1920, Paola Zani, Saba Italia and many more.  <b>Storage units and bookcases</b> offer the chance of maximizing a space and experimenting with a mix &amp; match of materials, colors and shapes.</p>
<p><b>Tables and chairs</b> are necessary to create a dining area and furnish the kitchen. On Archiproducts you can choose between folding chairs and extendable tables, kitchen furniture and accessories, browsing the catalogues of Abimis, Alpes Inox, Cucine Lube, L'Ottocento, <a href="https://www.archiproducts.com/en/alessi" title="Alessi">Alessi</a>, Strato Cucine, Valcucine, Falmec and many more.</p>
<p>Looking for solutions to <b>decorate the garden</b>? Make the most out of your <b>outdoor space with outdoor sofas and armchairs</b>, coffee tables, poufs, tables and chairs. Design an <b>outdoor living area</b> with the gazebos, umbrellas and accessories designed by Paola Lenti, Ethimo, Gan, Gervasoni, B&amp;B Italia Outdoor, Vondom, Extremis, Kettal and many more.</p>
<p>From wall tiles, sinks and <b>wall-mounted sanitary fixtures</b> to toilets and taps, <b>Jacuzzis</b>, showers and equipment for Spas, Archiproducts has a wide selection of <b>bathroom furniture</b>. Among all brands for sale, there are Salvatori, Agape, Boffi, Duravit, Marazzi, Ceadesign, Geberit, Aboutwater, <a href="https://www.archiproducts.com/en/grohe" title="Grohe">Grohe</a>, Ceramica Cielo, Ex.t, Ever Life Design and many more.</p>
<p>Design <b>interior and outdoor lighting</b> with <b>suspension, table and floor lamps</b>, and create an accent light with <b>spotlights</b>, steplights and lighting posts. On Archiproducts you can leaf through the Vibia, Martinelli Luce, Davide Groppi, Bover, FontanaArte, Simes, Oluce, Louis Poulsen, Fabbian, Karman, Andlight, Cini&amp;Nils and Bel-Lighting catalogs – and many more.</p>
<p>The <b>office furniture</b> section offers scalable and modular solutions, which respond to the trend of an increasingly flexible workplace. Desks, <b>office chairs</b> and accessories adapt to a constantly evolving space: discover the <b>office accessories</b> of Arper, Alias, Dieffebi, BuzziSpace, Diemmebi, Actiu, Bene, Archiutti, Fantoni, Ibebi, Ioc, Pedrali and many others. </p>
