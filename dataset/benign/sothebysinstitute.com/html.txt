<h2>Explore <span>London /</span> <span>New York /</span></h2>
<p>Founded in 1969, Sotheby’s Institute of Art is the Graduate School of Art and its Markets with campuses in London and New York, and online courses accessible anytime, from anywhere in the world.</p>
