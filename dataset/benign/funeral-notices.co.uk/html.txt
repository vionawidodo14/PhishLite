<div class="editorial_content_container">Setting up a custom alert is quick and easy. Just follow these simple steps to start receiving alerts directly to your email.
<br/><br/>
<b><span style="color:#C45791;">Firstly</span></b> you will need to be registered/logged in. If you haven't registered yet, don’t worry. Follow this link to register for free today.<a href="https://funeral-notices.co.uk/register" target="_blank"> Register Here</a>
<br/><br/>
<b><span style="color:#96B422;">Step 1</span></b><br/>
Select the drop-down arrow next to <b><span style="color:#C45791;">Logged in as</span></b>.
<br/><br/>
<b><span style="color:#96B422;">Step 2</span></b><br/>
Select <b><span style="color:#C45791;">'Set &amp; View Alerts'</span></b>.
<br/><br/>
<b><span style="color:#96B422;">Step 3</span></b><br/>
You can now enter any search criteria you wish to use (Surname / Region / County / Area)
Select <b><span style="color:#C45791;">'Confirm'</span></b>.
<br/><br/>
<b><span style="color:#96B422;">Step 4</span></b><br/>
Your new alert will now appear within <b><span style="color:#C45791;">'Your Alerts'</span></b>  where you can delete at a later date if you require by just selecting the bin icon.
<br/><br/>
<b><span style="color:#96B422;">Future Emails</span></b><br/>
Once an alert is set up, you will receive a custom alert email with a link to any matching notices placed if the criteria you have selected matches any new notices that are placed online.
<br/><br/>
<b><span style="color:#96B422;">Finding your alerts</span></b><br/>
There will be a notification number alongside your alerts displaying the number of notices that match. Just select the individual <b><span style="color:#C45791;">'Alert'</span></b> and a list will drop-down with all the matching notices for you to view. </div>

			var name = cookieName + "=";
