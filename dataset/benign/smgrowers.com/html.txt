<tr><td colspan="5" valign="top">
<font color="#4E8975" size="+1">We hope that you enjoy and find the information on the San Marcos Growers website useful. We are a wholesale plant nursery located in the eastern Goleta Valley in Santa Barbara, California. This year we celebrate forty year of growing plants appropriate to California's mediterranean climate, including many California native plants, as well as trees, vines, shrubs, perennials, ferns, succulents, ornamental grasses and grass-like plants from other areas around the world. The information on this website shares our experiences with many of these great plants.</font>
<p></p>
<p><font color="#4E8975" size="+1">As a wholesale nursery, <font color="#FF0000"><b>we do not sell directly to the public</b></font>, but we do sell to many retail nurseries and landscape professionals, as we note on our <a href="purchase/salespolicy.asp" onmouseout="window.status='San Marcos Growers'; return true" onmouseover="window.status='Sales Policy'; return true" title="Sales Policy">Sales Policy Page</a>.  If you would like to locate a retail nursery selling our plants, please visit our <a href="purchase/locator.asp" onmouseout="window.status='San Marcos Growers'; return true" onmouseover="window.status='Retail Nursery Locator'; return true" title="Retail Nursery Locator">Retail Nursery Locator</a></font>. <font color="#4E8975" size="+1">Please also consult with your local retail nursery first with any questions about the plants that we list on our website.</font></p>
<p><font color="#4E8975" size="+1">If you are in the nursery or landscape profession, please request a password to view price information by clicking on "New User" at the top of this, or any other page. Please note that we only ship using our own trucks and only <a href="purchase/deliveries.asp" onmouseout="window.status='San Marcos Growers'; return true" onmouseover="window.status='Delivery Page'; return true" title="Delivery Page">within California</a>.  Any shipping arrangements for outside of our delivery areas will need to be made by the receiver.</font></p><font color="#4E8975" size="+1">
