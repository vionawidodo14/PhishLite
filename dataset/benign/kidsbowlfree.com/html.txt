<h1 class="text-center">Registered Kids Receive <em>2 FREE GAMES</em> Of Bowling Each Day Of The KBF Program All Summer Long, Valued At Over $500 Per Child!</h1>
<p>
            Select bowling centers and schools around the country are
            participating in the first ever <b>Kids Bowl Free</b> program. This
            program is designed by bowling centers to give back to the
            community and provide a safe, secure, and fun way for kids to
            spend time this summer.
        </p>
<p>
            Children whose age does not exceed a limit by a participating
            bowling center are eligible to register for 2 free games each day of the KBF program,
            all summer long, courtesy of the participating bowling centers
            along with the schools and organizations.
        </p>
