<h3 class="title-large" style="font-size: 22px;">FAQ</h3>
                                 
                                <div style="border-bottom: 5px solid #eee;">
<a name="what-does-id-mean-in-legal-documents"></a>
<p>
</p><h4 class="title-large" style="font-size: 18px; font-weight: 600;padding-bottom:10px;">What does ID mean in legal documents?</h4>
<p class="text" style="font-weight: 400;">The term ID refers to the document that identifies a person. In most cases, it is a card that contains the person’s photo, name, citizenship, age, etc.</p>
<p></p>
</div>
<div style="border-bottom: 5px solid #eee;">
<a name="what-does-ss-mean-on-legal-documents"></a>
<p></p>
<h4 class="title-large" style="font-size: 18px; font-weight: 600;padding-bottom:10px;">What does SS mean on legal documents?</h4>
<p class="text" style="font-weight: 400;">This term is used in business legal documents and for notaries in particular. It is inherited from the Latin word which means “in particular” or “namely”.</p>
<p></p>
</div>
<div style="border-bottom: 5px solid #eee;">
<a name="what-legal-forms-do-i-need-to-start-a-business"></a>
<p></p>
<h4 class="title-large" style="font-size: 18px; font-weight: 600;padding-bottom:10px;">What legal forms do I need to start a business?</h4>
<p class="text" style="font-weight: 400;">Every business owner should have an Employee Identification Number and select the correct variant of business (Independent contractor or self-employed, LLC, corporation, etc.). Then, just select the corresponding category and you'll see the full list of legal documents for starting a business.</p>
<p></p>
</div>
<div style="border-bottom: 5px solid #eee;">
<a name="how-long-should-i-keep-legal-papers"></a>
<p></p>
<h4 class="title-large" style="font-size: 18px; font-weight: 600;padding-bottom:10px;">How long should I keep legal papers?</h4>
<p class="text" style="font-weight: 400;">Every paper has its own term. For example, tax documents filed by companies have a term of at least three to four years.</p>
