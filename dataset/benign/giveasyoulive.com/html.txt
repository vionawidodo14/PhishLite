<h2 class="heading-md text-center mb-3">Got questions? Here's what we're asked most</h2>
<div class="row">
<div class="col-12">
<div class="faqs faqs--horizontal" id="faqs">
<div class="faqs__answer faqs__placeholder">
<p>Select a question on the left to view its answer.</p>
</div>
<div class="card faqs__group">
<div>
<a aria-controls="answer1" aria-disabled="true" aria-expanded="true" class="faqs__question" data-toggle="collapse" href="#answer1" id="question1" role="button">
<b><span class="sr-only">1. </span>What is Give as you Live Online and how does it work?</b>
</a>
</div>
<div aria-labelledby="question1" class="faqs__answer collapse show" data-parent="#faqs" id="answer1">
<p>Give as you Live Online is a free and easy way to raise money for your favourite charity, just by shopping online.</p>
<p>Once you've signed up, you can browse our website for the store you want to shop with. Click 'shop &amp; raise' to go to their website, then continue to shop as normal.</p>
<p>We'll then email you within 1-7 days to let you know how much you've raised.</p>
</div>
</div>
<div class="card faqs__group">
<div>
<a aria-controls="answer2" aria-disabled="false" aria-expanded="false" class="faqs__question collapsed" data-toggle="collapse" href="#answer2" id="question2" role="button">
<b><span class="sr-only">2. </span>Who can I raise money for?</b>
</a>
</div>
<div aria-labelledby="question2" class="faqs__answer collapse" data-parent="#faqs" id="answer2">
<p>You can support any of the 200,000 UK-registered charities.</p>
<p>Our charities range from big national charities to local schools, churches and social groups.</p>
</div>
</div>
<div class="card faqs__group">
<div>
<a aria-controls="answer3" aria-disabled="false" aria-expanded="false" class="faqs__question collapsed" data-toggle="collapse" href="#answer3" id="question3" role="button">
<b><span class="sr-only">3. </span>How much can I raise for my charity?</b>
</a>
</div>
<div aria-labelledby="question3" class="faqs__answer collapse" data-parent="#faqs" id="answer3">
<p>The donation amount varies from store to store. Most of the time you raise a percentage of what you spend - between 1% and 10% - whilst sometimes it's a fixed amount.</p>
<p>With some stores there are different rates or restrictions depending on what you buy - you can see more information on each store's page on our website.</p>
</div>
</div>
<div class="card faqs__group">
<div>
<a aria-controls="answer4" aria-disabled="false" aria-expanded="false" class="faqs__question collapsed" data-toggle="collapse" href="#answer4" id="question4" role="button">
<b><span class="sr-only">4. </span>And it's free?</b>
</a>
</div>
<div aria-labelledby="question4" class="faqs__answer collapse" data-parent="#faqs" id="answer4">
<p>Yes! You don't pay anything extra for your shopping, the donation is made at no extra cost to you.</p>
<p>It's also completely free for your charity. There are no joining or payment fees, so 100% of the donation will reach the charity.</p>
</div>
</div>
<div class="card faqs__group">
<div>
<a aria-controls="answer5" aria-disabled="false" aria-expanded="false" class="faqs__question collapsed" data-toggle="collapse" href="#answer5" id="question5" role="button">
<b><span class="sr-only">5. </span>When will my charity receive the donation?</b>
</a>
</div>
<div aria-labelledby="question5" class="faqs__answer collapse" data-parent="#faqs" id="answer5">
<p>We pay charities once a month by BACS via our payment partner, The Online Foundation.</p>
<p>You can view the status of every donation within your account. A donation will typically move from 'pending' to 'approved' to 'paid' over the course of a few months.</p>
</div>
</div>
<div class="card faqs__group">
<div>
<a aria-controls="answer6" aria-disabled="false" aria-expanded="false" class="faqs__question collapsed" data-toggle="collapse" href="#answer6" id="question6" role="button">
<b><span class="sr-only">6. </span>Where does the donation come from?</b>
</a>
</div>
<div aria-labelledby="question6" class="faqs__answer collapse" data-parent="#faqs" id="answer6">
<p>As a "thank you" for sending them sales and customers, our stores pay us a commission.</p>
<p>We use this commission to make a donation to your chosen charity.</p>
</div>
</div>
<div class="card faqs__group">
<div>
<a aria-controls="answer7" aria-disabled="false" aria-expanded="false" class="faqs__question collapsed" data-toggle="collapse" href="#answer7" id="question7" role="button">
<b><span class="sr-only">7. </span>How does Give as you Live Online make money?</b>
</a>
</div>
<div aria-labelledby="question7" class="faqs__answer collapse" data-parent="#faqs" id="answer7">
<p>We donate 50% of the commission we receive, with the other 50% used to fund the on-going development of Give as you Live Online, which is free to all charities and users.</p>
<p>All donation amounts we display represent the amount the charity will receive - a 5% donation means a 5% donation!</p>
</div>
</div>
</div>
