<h2 class="hdg hdg_h1 hdg_mainColor"><a href="https://www.hoopsrumors.com/2019/10/warriors-notes-green-durant-thompson-iguodala.html">Warriors Notes: Green, Durant, Thompson, Iguodala</a></h2>
</div>
<div class="post-author">
<span>October 23rd, 2019 at 10:50pm CST</span> by Arthur Hill  
		</div>
</div>
<div class="post-bd">
<div class="userContent">
<p><strong><a data-wpel-link="external" href="https://www.basketball-reference.com/players/g/greendr01.html?utm_campaign=Linker&amp;utm_source=direct&amp;utm_medium=linker-" rel="nofollow noopener" target="_blank">Draymond Green</a></strong> addressed last year’s <a data-wpel-link="internal" href="https://www.hoopsrumors.com/2018/11/latest-on-draymond-green-kevin-durant-warriors.html" target="_blank">altercation</a> with <strong><a data-wpel-link="external" href="https://www.basketball-reference.com/players/d/duranke01.html?utm_campaign=Linker&amp;utm_source=direct&amp;utm_medium=linker-" rel="nofollow noopener" target="_blank">Kevin Durant</a></strong> on a <a data-wpel-link="external" href="http://www.espn.com/espnradio/play?id=27908368" target="_blank">podcast</a> by ESPN’s Adrian Wojnarowski that also includes <strong>Warriors</strong> GM <strong>Bob Myers</strong>. Green, who was suspended for one game after the November incident, said he began to question his future with the franchise and his standing with Myers.</p>
<p><em>“I started to tell myself in my mind, ‘Wow, [Myers is] flipping on me,'” </em>Green said.<em> “And it just felt like, ‘Wow, OK, is this not the guy I’ve known for all these years? Is he turning on me?’ And I started to tell myself all of these things, and then everybody’s like, ‘Oh my God, the Warriors sided with Kevin Durant.’ That was the hardest thing for me, because a lot of people don’t understand me. Bob does.”</em></p>
<p>Green added that he had to get over his “stubbornness” and accept that he mishandled the situation. He refused to apologize to Durant after being told to do so by Myers and coach <strong>Steve Kerr</strong>, and didn’t offer an apology “until I came to grips with myself.”</p>
<p><em>“What people don’t know — which is so hard to know, which requires time and energy — is Kevin and Draymond probably will be the closest guys,” </em>Myers said.<em> “They’re going to be friends for their whole life like this. … In the aftermath when they spoke, they laid it out for each other and they’re like, OK, I got ya. Like, now I know where you’re coming from, from both sides. But that could have happened earlier and that would’ve prevented it.”</em></p>
<p>There’s more Warriors news to pass along:</p>
<ul>
<li>Green is preparing to play the entire season without <strong><a data-wpel-link="external" href="https://www.basketball-reference.com/players/t/thompkl01.html?utm_campaign=Linker&amp;utm_source=direct&amp;utm_medium=linker-" rel="nofollow noopener" target="_blank">Klay Thompson</a></strong>, <a data-wpel-link="external" href="https://twitter.com/NickFriedell/status/1187123330446290944" target="_blank">tweets</a> Nick Friedell of ESPN. Kerr said yesterday that Thompson seems <a data-wpel-link="internal" href="https://www.hoopsrumors.com/2019/10/kerr-klay-thompson-unlikely-to-play-this-season.html" target="_blank">“unlikely” to return</a> from an ACL injury, although the team hasn’t made any official announcement. Green said that it will be an “added bonus” if the All-Star guard can get back on the court by the end of the season.</li>
<li><a data-wpel-link="external" href="https://messaging-custom-newsletters.nytimes.com/template/oakv2?uri=nyt://newsletter/67974a97-4264-4072-be82-9d7523bc9ef1&amp;te=1&amp;nl=marc-stein&amp;emc=edit_msb_20191022?campaign_id=48&amp;instance_id=13299&amp;segment_id=18144&amp;user_id=06dd95981ee204ce999cbd1064c86b56&amp;regi_id=84522290" target="_blank">Marc Stein of The New York Times</a> believes <strong><a data-wpel-link="external" href="https://www.basketball-reference.com/players/i/iguodan01.html?utm_campaign=Linker&amp;utm_source=direct&amp;utm_medium=linker-" rel="nofollow noopener" target="_blank">Andre Iguodala</a></strong> will sign with the Warriors next summer and finish his career with Golden State. Iguodala remains in limbo with the <strong>Grizzlies</strong>, where the Warriors traded him to open cap space. Memphis is hoping to get something in return for the former Finals MVP and has been reluctant to discuss a buyout.</li>
<li>Rookie <strong><a data-wpel-link="external" href="https://www.basketball-reference.com/players/p/pascher01.html?utm_campaign=Linker&amp;utm_source=direct&amp;utm_medium=linker-" rel="nofollow noopener" target="_blank">Eric Paschall</a></strong> has a chance to get consistent minutes, writes <a data-wpel-link="external" href="https://www.nbcsports.com/bayarea/warriors/steve-kerr-explains-why-warriors-rookie-eric-paschall-will-play-lot" target="_blank">Monte Poole of NBC Bay Area</a>. Paschall was used at center and power forward during the preseason, but his best opportunity may come at small forward.<em> “Most guys when they come in, they’re just not ready for this level of strength and force that exists in the game,”</em> Kerr said.<em> “Eric’s ready for all that. He’s gonna play a lot this year. We’re really excited about him.”</em></li>
</ul>

<h2 class="hdg hdg_h1 hdg_mainColor"><a href="https://www.hoopsrumors.com/2019/10/hornets-nicolas-batum-breaks-finger.html">Hornets’ Nicolas Batum Breaks Finger</a></h2>
</div>
<div class="post-author">
<span>October 23rd, 2019 at 9:29pm CST</span> by Arthur Hill  
		</div>
</div>
<div class="post-bd">
<div class="userContent">
<p><strong>Hornets</strong> forward <strong><a data-wpel-link="external" href="https://www.basketball-reference.com/players/b/batumni01.html?utm_campaign=Linker&amp;utm_source=direct&amp;utm_medium=linker-" rel="nofollow noopener" target="_blank">Nicolas Batum</a></strong> suffered a broken finger on his left hand during tonight’s season opener, <a data-wpel-link="external" href="https://twitter.com/ShamsCharania/status/1187176252299366400" target="_blank">tweets</a> Shams Charania of The Athletic. The fracture is located at the base of the middle finger, according to Rick Bonnell of The Charlotte Observer (<a data-wpel-link="external" href="https://twitter.com/rick_bonnell/status/1187190444637196289" target="_blank">Twitter link</a>). The team hasn’t set an estimate for how much time he might miss.</p>
<p>Batum came off the bench in tonight’s game, playing 11 minutes before the injury. He grabbed four rebounds and handed out two assists, but didn’t attempt a shot.</p>
<p>Charlotte’s highest-paid player at more than $25.5MM, Batum has accepted a reserve role as the Hornets are launching a youth movement after losing star guard <strong><a data-wpel-link="external" href="https://www.basketball-reference.com/players/w/walkeke02.html?utm_campaign=Linker&amp;utm_source=direct&amp;utm_medium=linker-" rel="nofollow noopener" target="_blank">Kemba Walker</a></strong> in free agency. Bonnell notes that five of the team’s top seven salaries are not part of the starting lineup (<a data-wpel-link="external" href="https://twitter.com/rick_bonnell/status/1187142616871247873" target="_blank">Twitter link</a>). Batum talked to coach <strong>James Borrego</strong> this week about his role on the team and said he doesn’t mind coming off the bench, <a data-wpel-link="external" href="https://twitter.com/rodboone/status/1186334409210773504" target="_blank">tweets</a> Rod Boone of The Athletic.</p>
<p>Batum is in the fourth season of a five-year, $120MM deal he signed with the Hornets in 2016. He has a $27.13MM player option for next season that he is virtually certain to pick up, considering his market value. Batum averaged 9.3 PPG in 75 games last season, his worst scoring numbers since his rookie year.</p>

<h2 class="hdg hdg_h1 hdg_mainColor"><a href="https://www.hoopsrumors.com/2019/10/knick-notes-point-guards-rabb-morris-robinson.html">Knicks Notes: Point Guards, Rabb, Morris, Robinson</a></h2>
</div>
<div class="post-author">
<span>October 23rd, 2019 at 8:58pm CST</span> by Arthur Hill  
		</div>
</div>
<div class="post-bd">
<div class="userContent">
<p>Nobody in the <a data-wpel-link="internal" href="https://www.hoopsrumors.com/2019/10/knicks-notes-point-guards-ntilikina-fizdale-free-agency.html" target="_blank">three-way battle</a> for the <strong>Knicks</strong>‘ starting point guard spot played well enough to earn a start in tonight’s season opener, writes <a data-wpel-link="external" href="https://nypost.com/2019/10/23/knicks-starting-lineup-rj-barrett-allonzo-trier-in-the-backcourt/?utm_source=twitter_sitebuttons&amp;utm_medium=site%20buttons&amp;utm_campaign=site%20buttons" target="_blank">Marc Berman of The New York Post</a>. <strong><a data-wpel-link="external" href="https://www.basketball-reference.com/players/p/paytoel01.html?utm_campaign=Linker&amp;utm_source=direct&amp;utm_medium=linker-" rel="nofollow noopener" target="_blank">Elfrid Payton</a></strong>, <strong><a data-wpel-link="external" href="https://www.basketball-reference.com/players/s/smithde03.html?utm_campaign=Linker&amp;utm_source=direct&amp;utm_medium=linker-" rel="nofollow noopener" target="_blank">Dennis Smith Jr.</a></strong> and <strong><a data-wpel-link="external" href="https://www.basketball-reference.com/players/n/ntilila01.html?utm_campaign=Linker&amp;utm_source=direct&amp;utm_medium=linker-" rel="nofollow noopener" target="_blank">Frank Ntilikina</a></strong> were all on the bench for the beginning of the game in San Antonio as coach <strong>David Fizdale</strong> opted to use rookie <strong><a data-wpel-link="external" href="https://www.basketball-reference.com/players/b/barrerj01.html?utm_campaign=Linker&amp;utm_source=direct&amp;utm_medium=linker-" rel="nofollow noopener" target="_blank">RJ Barrett</a></strong> at the point alongside <strong><a data-wpel-link="external" href="https://www.basketball-reference.com/players/t/trieral01.html?utm_campaign=Linker&amp;utm_source=direct&amp;utm_medium=linker-" rel="nofollow noopener" target="_blank">Allonzo Trier</a></strong>.</p>
<p><em>“I just like that combination of RJ and Allonzo Trier to be out there,’’ </em>Fizdale told reporters before the game.<em> “The competition is still on and I’m still searching for combinations that are going to fit. Coming into this, I want to see how that looks. I’m going to keep putting it on them to really have to earn it and really force me to play you. None of this is in stone, but at the same time the competition is on.”</em></p>
<p>Payton, Smith and Ntilikina all struggled in the preseason, especially with shooting, while Trier stood out with an improved jumper. Berman suggests the decision could be seen as a slight against the front office, which signed Payton this summer and <a data-wpel-link="internal" href="https://www.hoopsrumors.com/2019/10/knicks-exercising-options-on-ntilikina-knox-dsj.html" target="_blank">picked up Ntilikina’s fourth-year option</a> earlier this week.</p>
<p>There’s more from New York:</p>
<ul>
<li>Fizdale has previous experience with <strong><a data-wpel-link="external" href="https://www.basketball-reference.com/players/r/rabbiv01.html?utm_campaign=Linker&amp;utm_source=direct&amp;utm_medium=linker-" rel="nofollow noopener" target="_blank">Ivan Rabb</a></strong>, who was signed today to a <a data-wpel-link="internal" href="https://www.hoopsrumors.com/2019/10/knicks-to-sign-ivan-rabb-to-two-way-deal.html" target="_blank">two-way contract</a>, Berman notes in a <a data-wpel-link="external" href="https://nypost.com/2019/10/23/knicks-sign-ivan-rabb-as-they-give-up-on-kris-wilkes/?utm_source=twitter_sitebuttons&amp;utm_medium=site%20buttons&amp;utm_campaign=site%20buttons" target="_blank">separate story</a>. Fizdale was Rabb’s first coach as a rookie in Memphis, although he was fired after 19 games.<em> “Good kid, hard worker,” </em>he said of Rabb.<em> “Really skilled big. Rebounds the ball well. A high-IQ player. It will be good to get back with him and develop him.”</em></li>
<li><strong>Spurs</strong> coach <strong>Gregg Popovich</strong> remains bitter about <strong><a data-wpel-link="external" href="https://www.basketball-reference.com/players/m/morrima03.html?utm_campaign=Linker&amp;utm_source=direct&amp;utm_medium=linker-" rel="nofollow noopener" target="_blank">Marcus Morris</a></strong>‘ decision to back out of a commitment to San Antonio this summer and sign with the Knicks instead (<a data-wpel-link="external" href="https://twitter.com/KSATRJ/status/1187141973804802048" target="_blank">video link</a> from RJ Marquez of KSAT in San Antonio). Morris verbally agreed to a two-year, $19MM deal with the Spurs before changing his mind when New York was able to offer $15MM for one season. As expected, the San Antonio crowd booed him loudly before tonight’s game.</li>
<li><a data-wpel-link="external" href="https://theathletic.com/1315607/2019/10/23/this-is-where-youll-find-him-at-the-story-of-mitchell-robinson-and-the-gym-that-shaped-the-knicks-next-star/" target="_blank">Mike Vorkunov of The Athletic</a> takes a look at how <strong><a data-wpel-link="external" href="https://www.basketball-reference.com/players/r/robinmi01.html?utm_campaign=Linker&amp;utm_source=direct&amp;utm_medium=linker-" rel="nofollow noopener" target="_blank">Mitchell Robinson</a></strong> was able to overcome a negative college experience and provide some hope for the future in New York.</li>
</ul>

<article class="post-list post-142783 post type-post status-publish format-standard has-post-thumbnail category-los-angeles-clippers category-los-angeles-lakers category-patrick-beverley category-patrick-patterson category-paul-george" id="post-142783">

<div class="post-heading">
<h2 class="hdg hdg_h1 hdg_mainColor"><a href="https://www.hoopsrumors.com/2019/10/clippers-notes-opener-patterson-george-beverley.html">Clippers Notes: Opener, Patterson, George, Beverley</a></h2>
</div>
<div class="post-author">
<span>October 23rd, 2019 at 7:57pm CST</span> by Arthur Hill  
		</div>
</div>
<div class="post-bd">
<div class="userContent">
<p>The <strong>Clippers</strong> showed off championship potential in Tuesday’s win over the <strong>Lakers</strong>, writes <a data-wpel-link="external" href="https://theathletic.com/1316451/2019/10/23/statement-delivered-the-clippers-showed-why-theyre-the-nba-title-favorites-in-beating-the-lakers/?=twittered" target="_blank">Jovan Buha of The Athletic</a>. Although it was opening night, the game featured playoff intensity as <strong><a data-wpel-link="external" href="https://www.basketball-reference.com/players/h/harremo01.html?utm_campaign=Linker&amp;utm_source=direct&amp;utm_medium=linker-" rel="nofollow noopener" target="_blank">Montrezl Harrell</a></strong>, <strong><a data-wpel-link="external" href="https://www.basketball-reference.com/players/w/willilo02.html?utm_campaign=Linker&amp;utm_source=direct&amp;utm_medium=linker-" rel="nofollow noopener" target="_blank">Lou Williams</a></strong>, <strong><a data-wpel-link="external" href="https://www.basketball-reference.com/players/j/jamesle01.html?utm_campaign=Linker&amp;utm_source=direct&amp;utm_medium=linker-" rel="nofollow noopener" target="_blank">LeBron James</a></strong> and <strong><a data-wpel-link="external" href="https://www.basketball-reference.com/players/d/davisan02.html?utm_campaign=Linker&amp;utm_source=direct&amp;utm_medium=linker-" rel="nofollow noopener" target="_blank">Anthony Davis</a></strong> were each on the court for at least 36 minutes.</p>
<p>With all the talent on hand, <strong><a data-wpel-link="external" href="https://www.basketball-reference.com/players/l/leonaka01.html?utm_campaign=Linker&amp;utm_source=direct&amp;utm_medium=linker-" rel="nofollow noopener" target="_blank">Kawhi Leonard</a></strong> stood out as the best player on the court, Buha states. The Clippers’ bench held a 60-19 scoring advantage and their defense took over the game after a slow start.</p>
<p><em>“We got players on the team that want to play defense,” </em>Leonard said of the Clippers, who were missing defensive stalwarts <strong><a data-wpel-link="external" href="https://www.basketball-reference.com/players/g/georgpa01.html?utm_campaign=Linker&amp;utm_source=direct&amp;utm_medium=linker-" rel="nofollow noopener" target="_blank">Paul George</a></strong> and <strong><a data-wpel-link="external" href="https://www.basketball-reference.com/players/m/mcgruro01.html?utm_campaign=Linker&amp;utm_source=direct&amp;utm_medium=linker-" rel="nofollow noopener" target="_blank">Rodney McGruder</a></strong>.<em> “We got some big bodies, we’re long. As long as we come out and be aggressive and play with our active hands, be in spots, it’s gonna be tough for people to score on us when it’s in a half-court. So, I guess, that’s just taking pride in our defense and wanting to win the ball game. Just got them a little flustered.”</em></p>
<p>There’s more Clippers news to pass along:</p>
<ul>
<li>Coach <strong>Doc Rivers</strong> made a surprise decision to start veteran forward <strong><a data-wpel-link="external" href="https://www.basketball-reference.com/players/p/pattepa01.html?utm_campaign=Linker&amp;utm_source=direct&amp;utm_medium=linker-" rel="nofollow noopener" target="_blank">Patrick Patterson</a></strong>, Buha adds in the same story. Patterson had been with the third unit in nearly every practice and scrimmage that was open to the media, but he delivered when Rivers called on him Tuesday. Buha notes that the Lakers adjusted their strategy in an attempt to take advantage of Patterson guarding Davis in the post, which slowed down the offense. Rivers has promised a flexible starting lineup, so Patterson’s role may change from game to game.</li>
<li>Sources tell <a data-wpel-link="external" href="https://sports.yahoo.com/clippers-announce-they-are-no-longer-the-little-brothers-of-la-anymore-141238527.html" target="_blank">Chris Hayes of Yahoo Sports</a> that George, who is recovering from offseason surgery on both shoulders, is expected to make his season debut in about three weeks. That matches <a data-wpel-link="internal" href="https://www.hoopsrumors.com/2019/10/paul-george-may-miss-the-clippers-first-10-games.html" target="_blank">an earlier report</a> in which Rivers estimated that George will miss the season’s first 10 games.</li>
<li><strong><a data-wpel-link="external" href="https://www.basketball-reference.com/players/b/beverpa01.html?utm_campaign=Linker&amp;utm_source=direct&amp;utm_medium=linker-" rel="nofollow noopener" target="_blank">Patrick Beverley</a></strong> has been fined $25K for throwing a ball into the crowd late in Tuesday’s game, <a data-wpel-link="external" href="https://twitter.com/ShamsCharania/status/1187144269175242753" target="_blank">tweets</a> Shams Charania of The Athletic.</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="hdg hdg_h1 hdg_mainColor"><a href="https://www.hoopsrumors.com/2019/10/celtics-notes-horford-brown-walker.html">Celtics Notes: Horford, Brown, Walker</a></h2>
</div>
<div class="post-author">
<span>October 23rd, 2019 at 6:54pm CST</span> by Arthur Hill  
		</div>
</div>
<div class="post-bd">
<div class="userContent">
<p><strong><a data-wpel-link="external" href="https://www.basketball-reference.com/players/h/horfoal01.html?utm_campaign=Linker&amp;utm_source=direct&amp;utm_medium=linker-" rel="nofollow noopener" target="_blank">Al Horford</a></strong> is wearing an opponent’s uniform as the <strong>Celtics</strong> open their season tonight, but his influence can still be felt in Boston’s locker room, writes <a data-wpel-link="external" href="https://www.bostonherald.com/2019/10/23/a-little-bit-of-al-horford-remains-with-celtics/" target="_blank">Steve Bulpett of The Boston Herald</a>. Horford was an inspirational leader during his three seasons with the Celtics, and he remains close to many of the players who are still there. <strong><a data-wpel-link="external" href="https://www.basketball-reference.com/players/s/smartma01.html?utm_campaign=Linker&amp;utm_source=direct&amp;utm_medium=linker-" rel="nofollow noopener" target="_blank">Marcus Smart</a></strong> said Horford kept texting him even after signing with the <strong>Sixers</strong>.</p>
<p><em>“He made my job way easier. His experience helped me out,” </em>Smart said.<em> “He showed me things and taught me things that I had to learn probably on my own like other guys, but I had help from him. Having Al over those years definitely helped me to be a professional and to be more professional.”</em></p>
<p>The players Bulpett talked to all seem to understand Horford’s decision to leave, even though he landed with a bitter division rival. Philadelphia gave him a four-year, $109MM contract with $97MM guaranteed, which is more than the Celtics were willing to offer.</p>
<p><em>“That was a smart decision,” </em><strong><a data-wpel-link="external" href="https://www.basketball-reference.com/players/t/tatumja01.html?utm_campaign=Linker&amp;utm_source=direct&amp;utm_medium=linker-" rel="nofollow noopener" target="_blank">Jayson Tatum</a></strong> said.<em> “He got a lot of money, which he deserved. Everybody should do what makes them happy, so I’m happy for Al.”</em></p>
<p>There’s more Celtics news on opening night:</p>
<ul>
<li>Although he might have gotten a bigger contract by waiting for restricted free agency next summer, <strong><a data-wpel-link="external" href="https://www.basketball-reference.com/players/b/brownja02.html?utm_campaign=Linker&amp;utm_source=direct&amp;utm_medium=linker-" rel="nofollow noopener" target="_blank">Jaylen Brown</a></strong> likes the security of signing with the Celtics now, <a data-wpel-link="external" href="https://twitter.com/Murf56/status/1186689407279095809" target="_blank">tweets</a> Mark Murphy of The Boston Herald. Brown’s negotiations went down to the wire before he agreed to a <a data-wpel-link="internal" href="https://www.hoopsrumors.com/2019/10/celtics-jaylen-brown-agree-to-four-year-extension.html" target="_blank">four-year, $115MM rookie scale extension</a> on Monday. <em>“Boston is a top-five organization in this league,” </em>Brown said.<em> “Once I got to look at the offer in front of me, it was easy.”</em></li>
<li>Executives around the league had a mixed response to Brown’s extension, according to <a data-wpel-link="external" href="https://www.nbcsports.com/boston/celtics/nba-execs-have-differing-opinions-jaylen-browns-new-celtics-contract" target="_blank">Justin Leger of NBC Sports Boston</a>. One thought it was too much money to commit, while another understands because it includes $12MM in incentives. An agent questioned why the team felt it was necessary to reach a deal now rather than waiting for the summer.</li>
<li><strong><a data-wpel-link="external" href="https://www.basketball-reference.com/players/w/walkeke02.html?utm_campaign=Linker&amp;utm_source=direct&amp;utm_medium=linker-" rel="nofollow noopener" target="_blank">Kemba Walker</a></strong> tells <a data-wpel-link="external" href="https://www.nbcsports.com/boston/celtics/bigger-market-expectations-highlight-changes-celtics-kemba-walker" target="_blank">A. Sherrod Blakely of NBC Sports Boston</a> that he’s ready for life in a bigger market with greater expectations. <em>“This is what I wanted, the opportunity I wanted,” </em>Walker said.<em> “I’m happy to be here.”</em></li>
</ul>

<li><strong><a data-wpel-link="external" href="https://www.basketball-reference.com/players/o/oquinky01.html?utm_campaign=Linker&amp;utm_source=direct&amp;utm_medium=linker-" rel="nofollow noopener" target="_blank">Kyle O’Quinn</a></strong></li>
<li><strong><a data-wpel-link="external" href="https://www.basketball-reference.com/players/r/reedda01.html?utm_campaign=Linker&amp;utm_source=direct&amp;utm_medium=linker-" rel="nofollow noopener" target="_blank">Davon Reed</a></strong> (two-way)</li>
<li><strong><a data-wpel-link="external" href="https://www.basketball-reference.com/players/y/youngth01.html?utm_campaign=Linker&amp;utm_source=direct&amp;utm_medium=linker-" rel="nofollow noopener" target="_blank">Thaddeus Young</a></strong></li>

<ul>
<li>Reached <a data-wpel-link="internal" href="https://www.hoopsrumors.com/2019/04/pacers-reach-25-year-deal-to-remain-in-indianapolis.html" target="_blank">25-year deal</a> to remain in Indianapolis.</li>
<li><strong><a data-wpel-link="external" href="https://www.basketball-reference.com/players/o/oladivi01.html?utm_campaign=Linker&amp;utm_source=direct&amp;utm_medium=linker-" rel="nofollow noopener" target="_blank">Victor Oladipo</a></strong> continues <a data-wpel-link="internal" href="https://www.hoopsrumors.com/2019/10/central-notes-oladipo-bledsoe-brogdon-bulls.html" target="_blank">to rehab</a> torn quad tendon; <a data-wpel-link="internal" href="https://www.hoopsrumors.com/2019/09/latest-on-victor-oladipo.html" target="_blank">out to start season</a>.</li>

<h3>Story of the summer:</h3>
<p>The Pacers entered the offseason at an interesting crossroads. While franchise cornerstones like <strong><a data-wpel-link="external" href="https://www.basketball-reference.com/players/o/oladivi01.html?utm_campaign=Linker&amp;utm_source=direct&amp;utm_medium=linker-" rel="nofollow noopener" target="_blank">Victor Oladipo</a></strong>, <strong><a data-wpel-link="external" href="https://www.basketball-reference.com/players/t/turnemy01.html?utm_campaign=Linker&amp;utm_source=direct&amp;utm_medium=linker-" rel="nofollow noopener" target="_blank">Myles Turner</a></strong>, and <strong><a data-wpel-link="external" href="https://www.basketball-reference.com/players/s/sabondo01.html?utm_campaign=Linker&amp;utm_source=direct&amp;utm_medium=linker-" rel="nofollow noopener" target="_blank">Domantas Sabonis</a></strong> remained under contract, most of the rest of the team’s rotation headed to free agency this summer. Of the seven players who played the most minutes for Indiana in 2018/19, five were unrestricted free agents.</p>
<p>Re-signing most of those players would’ve been the safe move. After all, the Pacers were comfortably a top-five team in the East last season, and their ceiling could get even higher once Oladipo gets healthy, as Turner and Sabonis continue to improve.</p>
<p>Instead though, not one of those five key free agents returned to the Pacers on a new deal. In some cases, there was good reason for that — <strong><a data-wpel-link="external" href="https://www.basketball-reference.com/players/c/collida01.html?utm_campaign=Linker&amp;utm_source=direct&amp;utm_medium=linker-" rel="nofollow noopener" target="_blank">Darren Collison</a></strong> announced his retirement, while <strong><a data-wpel-link="external" href="https://www.basketball-reference.com/players/e/evansty01.html?utm_campaign=Linker&amp;utm_source=direct&amp;utm_medium=linker-" rel="nofollow noopener" target="_blank">Tyreke Evans</a></strong> was dismissed and disqualified from the NBA for at least two years as a result of a drug violation. But it was still a little surprising to see so much turnover in Indiana.</p>
<p>The Pacers did well to fill out their roster around their top players through a series of free agent signings and trades, but there’s still a degree of uncertainty surrounding the franchise heading into the 2019/20 season.</p>
<p>Can <strong><a data-wpel-link="external" href="https://www.basketball-reference.com/players/b/brogdma01.html?utm_campaign=Linker&amp;utm_source=direct&amp;utm_medium=linker-" rel="nofollow noopener" target="_blank">Malcolm Brogdon</a></strong> carry the ball-handling load with Oladipo out? Will a starting frontcourt duo of Turner and Sabonis have its ups and downs? And just when will Oladipo be ready to return?</p>
<p>This is a well-coached team that still has plenty of talent on its roster, but with so many reliable veterans no longer in the mix, there will be a lot riding on the players the Pacers brought in to replace them.</p>
<p><a class="more-link" data-wpel-link="internal" href="https://www.hoopsrumors.com/2019/10/2019-offseason-in-review-indiana-pacers.html" id="fv-more-142691" onclick=" fv_more_onclick( '142691' ); return false;" target="_blank">Read more</a></p>
<div id="fv-more-text-142691" style="display:none;">
<h3>Key offseason losses:</h3>
<p>While neither player was a star, Collison and <strong><a data-wpel-link="external" href="https://www.basketball-reference.com/players/j/josepco01.html?utm_campaign=Linker&amp;utm_source=direct&amp;utm_medium=linker-" rel="nofollow noopener" target="_blank">Cory Joseph</a></strong> had performed admirably handling point guard duties for the Pacers for the past two seasons. Collison, the starter, knocked down 43.9% of his three-point attempts in his two years as a Pacer, and distributed the ball well, averaging a career-high 6.0 assists per game in 2018/19. Those numbers made it all the more surprising that he decided to call it a career in June at the age of 31.</p>
<p>Joseph, meanwhile, was one of the only NBA players to appear in all 82 games in each of the last two seasons, and has been one of the league’s most reliable backup point guards during that stretch, particularly on the defensive end. His play earned him a three-year, $37MM+ deal from the Kings which Indiana was presumably unwilling to match.</p>
<p>The Pacers’ frontcourt losses were perhaps even more impactful, as <strong><a data-wpel-link="external" href="https://www.basketball-reference.com/players/b/bogdabo02.html?utm_campaign=Linker&amp;utm_source=direct&amp;utm_medium=linker-" rel="nofollow noopener" target="_blank">Bojan Bogdanovic</a></strong> and <strong><a data-wpel-link="external" href="https://www.basketball-reference.com/players/y/youngth01.html?utm_campaign=Linker&amp;utm_source=direct&amp;utm_medium=linker-" rel="nofollow noopener" target="_blank">Thaddeus Young</a></strong> both departed in free agency, securing nice paydays in Utah and Chicago respectively. Bogdanovic was perhaps overextended down the stretch and in the postseason as Indiana’s go-to play-maker with Oladipo out, but he still enjoyed a career year, establishing new career highs in PPG (18.0), FG% (.497), and 3PT% (.425), among other categories.</p>
<p>As for Young, he had been a nice fit as the Pacers’ starting power forward over the last three years. His offensive numbers were modest (11.8 PPG on .513/.346/.597 shooting), but his versatility and toughness were a major asset on defense — he had the athleticism to guard scorers on the perimeter and the length to challenge them inside as well.</p>
<p>Evans was inconsistent during his lone season as a Pacer, though he was one of the club’s few players capable of creating his own shot on offense. Indiana may miss <strong><a data-wpel-link="external" href="https://www.basketball-reference.com/players/m/matthwe02.html?utm_campaign=Linker&amp;utm_source=direct&amp;utm_medium=linker-" rel="nofollow noopener" target="_blank">Wesley Matthews</a></strong> more — he provided the team with some much-needed floor spacing after Oladipo went down.</p>
<h3>Key offseason additions:</h3>
<p>The Pacers made a splash early in free agency this year, agreeing to a sign-and-trade deal with the division-rival Bucks for Brogdon. Besides costing Indiana a lottery-protected first-round pick and a pair of future second-rounders, Brogdon also represents the biggest free agent expenditure in team history, on a four-year, $85MM deal.</p>
<p>At times last season, Brogdon looked like the second-best player on a 60-win team, so it’s no surprise that he did well on the open market. The two big questions facing him this season will be whether he can stay healthy (he has a history of foot problems) and whether he can maintain anything close to his .505/.426/.928 shooting line as his usage rate increases.</p>
<p><img alt="" class="alignright size-medium wp-image-142760" height="300" sizes="(max-width: 200px) 100vw, 200px" src="https://cdn.hoopsrumors.com/files/2019/10/T.J.-Warren-200x300.jpg" srcset="https://cdn.hoopsrumors.com/files/2019/10/T.J.-Warren-200x300.jpg 200w, https://cdn.hoopsrumors.com/files/2019/10/T.J.-Warren-768x1152.jpg 768w, https://cdn.hoopsrumors.com/files/2019/10/T.J.-Warren-900x1350.jpg 900w" width="200"/>The Pacers’ trade for <strong><a data-wpel-link="external" href="https://www.basketball-reference.com/players/w/warretj01.html?utm_campaign=Linker&amp;utm_source=direct&amp;utm_medium=linker-" rel="nofollow noopener" target="_blank">T.J. Warren</a></strong> was one of the most widely praised deals of the summer. Considering the team also secured three second-round picks in addition to Warren in exchange for just $1.1MM in cash, that move has to be considered a big win even if the veteran forward is a disappointment in Indiana. His offensive numbers for Phoenix were certainly promising, but scoring empty points for a lottery team is different than playing a key role on a playoff contender. Indiana will want to see improvements from him on the defensive end of the court.</p>
<p><strong><a data-wpel-link="external" href="https://www.basketball-reference.com/players/l/lambje01.html?utm_campaign=Linker&amp;utm_source=direct&amp;utm_medium=linker-" rel="nofollow noopener" target="_blank">Jeremy Lamb</a></strong> and <strong><a data-wpel-link="external" href="https://www.basketball-reference.com/players/h/holidju01.html?utm_campaign=Linker&amp;utm_source=direct&amp;utm_medium=linker-" rel="nofollow noopener" target="_blank">Justin Holiday</a></strong> will also join the Pacers on the wing, having signed as free agents. Lamb, who was Charlotte’s second-leading scorer last season with a 15.3 PPG, was a nice pickup, though Holiday’s one-year, $4.77MM deal makes him the more low-risk addition of the two. Indiana will want to see Holiday improve upon the .378 FG% he posted over the last two years in Chicago and Memphis.</p>
<p>Finally, one of the Pacers’ most interesting additions of the offseason came on draft night, when the club used its first-round pick to nab big man <strong><a data-wpel-link="external" href="https://www.basketball-reference.com/players/b/bitadgo01.html?utm_campaign=Linker&amp;utm_source=direct&amp;utm_medium=linker-" rel="nofollow noopener" target="_blank">Goga Bitadze</a></strong>. His skill set seemingly overlaps with Sabonis’ and Turner’s, which immediately led to trade speculation, but the Pacers <a data-wpel-link="internal" href="https://www.hoopsrumors.com/2019/10/pacers-notes-culture-bitadze-sabonis-lemon.html" target="_blank">have said</a> they simply targeted the best player available. Plus, if Sabonis and Turner are both starting, Bitadze makes sense as a depth piece. If he develops quickly and proves he deserves regular minutes, it will be interesting to see how the Pacers handle their potential frontcourt logjam.</p>
<h3>Outlook for 2019/20:</h3>
<p>While they flew under the radar to some extent during a wild 2019 offseason, the Pacers underwent some significant roster changes and have an interesting season ahead of them. It’s not out of the question that Indiana finishes as high as No. 3 in the Eastern Conference. It also wouldn’t be a total shock if the club misses the postseason altogether.</p>
<p>Oladipo’s recovery timetable – and how he looks upon returning – will go a long way toward determining where the Pacers finish in 2019/20, but even without the former All-NBA guard in the lineup, this team should have enough talent to hang onto a playoff seed. The safe bet is that Indiana ultimately lands somewhere in the 5-7 range in the East.</p>

<h2 class="hdg hdg_h1 hdg_mainColor"><a href="https://www.hoopsrumors.com/2019/10/rockets-signed-waived-ronshad-shabazz.html">Rockets Signed, Waived Ronshad Shabazz</a></h2>
</div>
<div class="post-author">
<span>October 23rd, 2019 at 4:24pm CST</span> by Luke Adams  
		</div>
</div>
<div class="post-bd">
<div class="userContent">
<p><strong>4:24pm:</strong> Agent <strong>Cam Brennick</strong> of RBA Sports confirms to JD Shaw of Hoops Rumors (<a data-wpel-link="external" href="https://twitter.com/JShawNBA/status/1187116840897728512" target="_blank">Twitter link</a>) that Shabazz will enter the G League draft, since the Rockets won’t hold his affiliate rights.</p>
<p>While Houston can’t designate Shabazz as an affiliate player, it’s probably safe to assume the Rio Grande Valley Vipers will target him in the NBAGL draft.</p>
<p><strong>3:30pm:</strong> The <strong>Rockets</strong> were one of many NBA teams that signed and immediately waived a player before the regular season, doing so with undrafted rookie guard <strong><a data-wpel-link="external" href="https://www.sports-reference.com/cbb/players/ronshad-shabazz-1.html?utm_campaign=Linker&amp;utm_source=direct&amp;utm_medium=linker-" rel="nofollow noopener" target="_blank">Ronshad Shabazz</a></strong>, according to <a data-wpel-link="external" href="https://basketball.realgm.com/nba/teams/Houston-Rockets/10/Transaction_History/2020" target="_blank">RealGM’s transactions log</a>.</p>
<p>In his senior season in 2018/19, Shabazz averaged 18.3 PPG, 3.3 RPG, and 2.3 APG with a shooting line of .496/.376/.815 in 32 games (32.2 MPG).</p>
<p>These sign-and-waive transactions are always G League-related. They’re designed to either make a player eligible to be designated as a G League “<a data-wpel-link="internal" href="https://www.hoopsrumors.com/2019/10/hoops-rumors-glossary-affiliate-players-2.html" target="_blank">affiliate player</a>” or to ensure that a returning-rights G League player receives an <a data-wpel-link="internal" href="https://www.hoopsrumors.com/2019/07/hoops-rumors-glossary-exhibit-10-contract-2.html" target="_blank">Exhibit 10</a> bonus.</p>
<p>However, the Rockets’ deal with Shabazz was a little different than every other sign-and-waive maneuver completed this fall. Rather than signing the former Appalachian State standout to an Exhibit 10 contract, Houston inked him a two-way contract before releasing him. The moves occurred on Monday, before the club officially converted <strong><a data-wpel-link="external" href="https://www.basketball-reference.com/players/c/clemoch01.html?utm_campaign=Linker&amp;utm_source=direct&amp;utm_medium=linker-" rel="nofollow noopener" target="_blank">Chris Clemons</a></strong>‘ contract to a two-way deal.</p>
<p>On the surface, it looks as if the Rockets were trying to secure Shabazz’s G League rights without adding any dead money to their cap. If Shabazz had signed an Exhibit 10 contract on Monday and been cut right away, he wouldn’t have cleared waivers until the second day of the regular season, meaning the tax-conscious Rockets would have been on the hook for about $10K in dead money ($18K for tax purposes) — two days’ worth of his salary.</p>
<p>According to <a data-wpel-link="external" href="https://2ways10days.com/do-player-rights-transfer-for-waived-two-way-players-2c0f362c1dd1" target="_blank">Adam Johnson of 2 Ways &amp; Days</a>, however, NBA teams don’t retain affiliate rights for waived two-way players. That would seem to make Shabazz ineligible to be designated as an affiliate player.</p>
<p>General manager <strong>Daryl Morey</strong> and the Rockets are pretty savvy with their roster moves, so it’s possible they’ve found a loophole here, or perhaps there’s another motivation for the signing. The official list of 2019 affiliate players should be released next week when G League training camps open, so we’ll see at that point whether or not Shabazz shows up on the Rio Grande Valley Vipers’ roster.</p>
</div>
</div>

<h2 class="hdg hdg_h1 hdg_mainColor"><a href="https://www.hoopsrumors.com/2019/10/hawks-claim-tyrone-wallace-off-waivers.html">Hawks Claim Tyrone Wallace Off Waivers</a></h2>
</div>
<div class="post-author">
<span>October 23rd, 2019 at 4:08pm CST</span> by Arthur Hill  
		</div>
</div>
<div class="post-bd">
<div class="userContent">
<p>The <strong>Hawks</strong> submitted a successful waiver claim for former <strong>Timberwolves</strong> point guard <strong><a data-wpel-link="external" href="https://www.basketball-reference.com/players/w/wallaty01.html?utm_campaign=Linker&amp;utm_source=direct&amp;utm_medium=linker-" rel="nofollow noopener" target="_blank">Tyrone Wallace</a></strong>, <a data-wpel-link="external" href="https://twitter.com/wojespn/status/1187111950846697472" target="_blank">tweets</a> ESPN’s Adrian Wojnarowski. Wallace was Minnesota’s <a data-wpel-link="internal" href="https://www.hoopsrumors.com/2019/10/timberwolves-waive-tyrone-wallace.html" target="_blank">final cut</a> on Monday as the Wolves trimmed their roster to the league limit.</p>
<p>Atlanta will inherit his $1.6MM non-guaranteed contract, which comes with a daily cap hit of $8,973, according to Bobby Marks of ESPN (<a data-wpel-link="external" href="https://twitter.com/BobbyMarks42/status/1187112882846212097" target="_blank">Twitter link</a>). The Hawks are now up to 15 players and will have $3.7MM in remaining cap room as long as Wallace stays on their roster. His contract will become fully guaranteed on January 7, which is the league-wide guarantee date.</p>
<p>Wallace, 25, spent the past two seasons with the <strong>Clippers</strong>, averaging a 5.5 PPG in 92 combined games. He had a promising rookie season, but fell out of the rotation last year and saw his playing time cut to 10.1 minutes per night. The Wolves claimed him in July after he was waived by L.A.</p>

<h2 class="hdg hdg_h1 hdg_mainColor"><a href="https://www.hoopsrumors.com/2019/10/nets-sign-timothe-luwawu-cabarrot-to-two-way-deal.html">Nets Sign Timothe Luwawu-Cabarrot To Two-Way Deal</a></h2>
</div>
<div class="post-author">
<span>October 23rd, 2019 at 4:08pm CST</span> by Luke Adams  
		</div>
</div>
<div class="post-bd">
<div class="userContent">
<p>The <strong>Nets</strong> have officially signed former first-round pick <strong><a data-wpel-link="external" href="https://www.basketball-reference.com/players/l/luwawti01.html?utm_campaign=Linker&amp;utm_source=direct&amp;utm_medium=linker-" rel="nofollow noopener" target="_blank">Timothe Luwawu-Cabarrot</a></strong> to a two-way contract, the team announced today in a <a data-wpel-link="external" href="https://www.nba.com/nets/news/2019/10/23/brooklyn-nets-sign-timothe-luwawu-cabarrot-two-way-contract" target="_blank">press release</a>. Brooklyn had an open two-way slot alongside <strong><a data-wpel-link="external" href="https://www.basketball-reference.com/players/e/ellenhe01.html?utm_campaign=Linker&amp;utm_source=direct&amp;utm_medium=linker-" rel="nofollow noopener" target="_blank">Henry Ellenson</a></strong>, so a corresponding move wasn’t necessary.</p>
<p>The 24th overall pick in the 2016 draft, Luwawu-Cabarrot spent his first two seasons in Philadelphia before being traded to the Thunder in the <strong><a data-wpel-link="external" href="https://www.basketball-reference.com/players/s/schrode01.html?utm_campaign=Linker&amp;utm_source=direct&amp;utm_medium=linker-" rel="nofollow noopener" target="_blank">Dennis Schroder</a></strong> three-team trade in July 2018. After suiting up for Oklahoma City for the first half of 2018/19, the 24-year-old forward was traded again to Chicago in February. He became a free agent this summer and signed with the Cavs, but was waived in advance of the regular season over the weekend.</p>
<p>In 171 total games for three NBA teams, TLC has averaged 5.7 PPG and 1.9 RPG with a .387/.320/.820 shooting line. He’ll now get a chance to continue developing for the Long Island Nets, Brooklyn’s G League affiliate. His two-way deal will also make him eligible to spend up to 45 days with the NBA squad.</p>
<p>After Toronto, New York, and Brooklyn signed players to two-way deals today, the Suns are now the only team in the NBA with an open two-way slot on their roster.</p>
