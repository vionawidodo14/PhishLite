<div class="clearfix text-formatted field field--name-body field--type-text-with-summary field--label-hidden field__item" property="schema:text"><h2>Welcome…</h2>
<p><strong>to skillsworkshop, the home of free adult literacy, numeracy and Functional Skills resources since 2001.</strong></p>
<p>The site was redeveloped in 2011 and had a major upgrade on 28 September 2018. New items are added regularly and there are now more than <strong>2000 free Functional Skills</strong> and <strong>Skills for Life</strong> resources.</p>
<p>The <a href="/about">about</a> pages describe the <a href="about/history">history</a> of the site, the benefits of <a href="about/registering">registration</a>, and explain how <em>you</em> can <a href="about/contributing">contribute</a>.</p>
<h2>Using this site</h2>
<p>The site has a powerful faceted search function: try typing something into the “Search” box at the top right of every page and then filter by author and type (resource or external link).</p>
<p>To look for <strong>specific curriculum topics</strong> in a given subject area use the tabs at the top of every page, such as the blue <a href="/maths">Maths</a> tab. Then use the Maths search box  (left hand side) and/or filter the resource list by level, maths topic and/or resource type.</p>
<p>You can see our 500+ <a href="/contributors">resource contributors</a> and follow the links to their information pages to see lists of all the resources they’ve contributed.</p>
<p><a href="http://d8.skillsworkshop.org/latest-resources">Latest Resources &amp; Links</a> is a chronological list (2018-2001) of all our items.  The <a href="/news">News</a> page has all the latest site information and <a href="/recent">Recent Changes</a> has details of updated site content. </p>
