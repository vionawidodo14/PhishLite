<h2>One platform. Endless possibilities.</h2>
<p class="lead">
          Whether you are mapping people, systems, or concepts in general,
          if you are focused on relationships we've got you covered.
        </p>
<ul class="markets-list">
<li>
<h4>Stakeholder mapping</h4>
<p>
              Explore the complex web of loyalties, interests,
              influence, and alignment of key players around important issues.
            </p>
</li>
<li>
<h4>Systems mapping</h4>
<p>
              Understand and engage complex systems more effectively using
              systems maps and causal loop diagrams.
            </p>
</li>
<li>
<h4>Social network mapping</h4>
<p>
              Capture the structure of personal networks and reveal key players.
              Visualize the informal networks within your organization and
              see how work really gets done.
            </p>
</li>
<li>
<h4>Community asset mapping</h4>
<p>
              Keep track of the evolving relationships among community members
              and resources.
            </p>
</li>
<li>
<h4>Concept mapping</h4>
<p>
              Brainstorm complex ideas and relate individual concepts to the bigger picture.
              Unfold convoluted series of events using Lombardi diagrams.
            </p>
</li>
</ul>
