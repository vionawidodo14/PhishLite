<h1 style="border-bottom:0; text-align:center;"><strong class="orange">⭣</strong> Take a Quick Tour of Testmoz <strong class="orange">⭣</strong></h1>
<h2>Building a quiz is easy.</h2>
<div class="feature-row feature-row-vertical flex-fix">
<div class="screenshot">
<img alt="A screenshot of the Testmoz quiz builder" src="/static/1797b3a/help/builder.png" style="max-width:100%;"/>
</div>
<div class="features-vertical">
<div class="feature-item">
<p>You can quickly create questions, move them around, copy them, import questions from your other tests, change question types, and move questions into pools.</p>
<p>The test is <strong>autosaved</strong> while you're working on it, so you don't have to worry about losing your changes.</p>
<p>You can also <strong>undo and redo</strong> your changes, just in case you make a mistake.</p>
<p>You can mark questions as <strong>ungraded</strong>, you can <strong>shuffle</strong> the questions for each test taker, you can shuffle the answer choices, and you can show an <strong>explanation</strong> for any question.</p>
<p>Testmoz is powerful, and more features are being added all the time.</p>
</div>
</div>
</div>
<h2 style="margin-top:50px">Distributing your test is easy too.</h2>
<div class="feature-row feature-row-vertical flex-fix">
<div class="features-vertical">
<div class="feature-item">
<p>Testmoz provides you with a simple URL that you can email to your students so they can start the test. You can choose from a variety of authentication schemes to ensure <em>only</em> your students take the test.</p>
<p>Your students will have no technical excuse for not completing your tests because...</p>
<ul>
<li>Testmoz works on virtually all web browsers on <strong>desktops, tablets and phones</strong></li>
<li>Testmoz works well on <strong>low-bandwidth</strong> and spotty internet connections</li>
<li>Testmoz will automatically <strong>restore the student's test</strong> if they accidentally close their browser</li>
</ul>
<p>You can <strong>customize the look and feel</strong> to match your school, or corporate color scheme.</p>
<p>Testmoz lets you choose whether to display <strong>all the questions at once, or one-per-page</strong>. You can set a <strong>time limit</strong>, and specify the <strong>max number of times</strong> a student can take your test.</p>
<p>When the student finishes, you can choose to display their score, their responses, the explanations, and/or the correct answers.</p>
</div>
</div>
<div class="screenshot">
<img alt="A screenshot of the Testmoz quiz maker" src="/static/1797b3a/help/screenshot-long.png" style="max-width:100%;"/>
</div>
</div>
<h2 style="margin-top:50px">And when your test takers finish...</h2>
<p><img alt="A screenshot of the Testmoz results page" src="/static/1797b3a/help/results-wide.png" style="max-width:100%;"/></p>
<p>You can easily see who took your test, how they performed, which questions were difficult, how long it took people to finish and more. All the information is easily accessible from one page.</p>
<p>You can dive into a particular student's result to see all their responses in detail.</p>
