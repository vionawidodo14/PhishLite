<div class="cfct-mod-content copy"><h2>Request a UW Colleges Transcript</h2>
<p>The University of Wisconsin Extended Campus serves as the records custodian of UW Colleges education records <em><strong>prior to September 1, 2019</strong></em>. The UW Colleges transcript remains the official academic record of former students.</p>
<p><a href="https://www.wisconsin.edu/uwc/requesting-a-transcript/">Click here for more information on ordering a transcript.</a></p>
</div>
</div>
<div class="cfct-module uwsa-rich-text">
<div class="cfct-mod-content copy"><h2>UW Colleges Archived Catalogs</h2>
<p>The course catalog archives provides important information about academic policies, course descriptions and degree requirements. Please visit the link below to view UW Colleges catalogs from 1969-2018 or to request a course syllabus.</p>
<p><a href="https://www.wisconsin.edu/uwc/uw-colleges-course-catalog-archive/">UW College Catalog Archives</a></p>
</div>
</div>
<div class="cfct-module uwsa-rich-text">
<div class="cfct-mod-content copy"><h2>Replacement Diplomas</h2>
<p><a href="https://www.wisconsin.edu/uwc/replacement-diplomas/">Request Replacement Diplomas</a></p>
</div>
</div>
<div class="cfct-module uwsa-rich-text">
<div class="cfct-mod-content copy"><h2>Degree and Enrollment Verification</h2>
<p>Outside agencies and other third parties can verify a student’s degree using DegreeVerify through the National Student Clearinghouse.</p>
<p><a href="https://www.wisconsin.edu/uwc/verification/">National Student Clearinghouse</a></p>
</div>
</div>
<div class="cfct-module uwsa-callout">
<div class="cfct-mod-content callout standard grey-bg">
<h3 class="cfct-mod-title">UW College Courses Online</h3><p>For more information about course offerings and the latest news, please visit the link below.</p>
<p><a class="btn btn-primary btn-sm" href="https://online.uwc.edu/" role="button">UW College Courses Online</a></p></div>
</div>
<div class="cfct-module uwsa-callout">
<div class="cfct-mod-content callout standard grey-bg">
<h3 class="cfct-mod-title">Collection of Outstanding Debt</h3><p>UW Extended Campus will monitor any outstanding student account balances of former UW Colleges students that existed prior to September 1, 2018. This includes, answering student questions about account details, and clearing student accounts’ of transcript holds associated with past due balances.</p>
<p><a class="btn btn-primary btn-sm" href="https://www.wisconsin.edu/uwc/collection-of-outstanding-debt/" role="button">Collection of Outstanding Debt</a></p></div>
</div></div><div class="col-md-5 cfct-block">
<div class="cfct-module uwsa-rich-text">
<div class="cfct-mod-content copy"><p class="lead">On July 1, 2018, UW Colleges officially joined seven four-year UW institutions as branch campuses.</p>
<p>Learn more about the <a href="https://www.wisconsin.edu/uw-restructure/">UW Colleges Restructuring Project</a>.</p>
