<h2 onclick="switchdom(this);">
			Social/Roleplaying - RP News
			<span class="newsdate">08.01.2018</span>
<span class="openup"></span>
</h2>
<div class="nc" id="news6936">
<h5>By Khuri</h5>
			A recent stunning revelation from an unexpected appearance from the long-missing clan The Pilgrims has shown that the original Omega, long thought gone from this universe, have returned, and Omni-Tek has been making unknown deals. Omni-Tek CEO Eva Pourais denied these allegations, citing the lack of tangible proof, as the Pilgrims could only show a recording of the supposed encounter. The Omega, the original nano-based immortals, can be remembered from the book Prophet Without Honor, available for free at the old official site ( <a class="postlink" href="http://archive.anarchy-online.com/wsp/anarchy/frontend.cgi?func=publish.show&amp;template=content&amp;func_id=2319&amp;table=CONTENT">http://archive.anarchy-online.com/wsp/a ... le=CONTENT</a> )<br/>
<br/>
CoT Speaker elections have been extended until the meeting on February 3rd. The Council site at <a class="postlink" href="http://counciloftruth.org/">http://counciloftruth.org/</a> has more information, including statements from the candidates and information on how to file a vote.<br/>
<br/>
The RP community and ARK hosts are hard at work developing a new RP channel/bot to make finding events and roleplayers simple. Be alerted to one time events, surprise appearances, or find out about planned storyline elements, in the near future.<br/>
<br/>
There is a special call out to revive an Omni-Tek organization to balance out the Council of Truth, the Clan player representation. If you're interested, please check out the forums (link below)!<br/>
<br/>
All of the storylines, large and small, and information about roleplaying organizations and meetings, can be found at the Fourth Wall forum on the official AO site, at <a class="postlink" href="https://forums.anarchy-online.com/forumdisplay.php?532-The-Fourth-Wall">https://forums.anarchy-online.com/forum ... ourth-Wall</a><br/>
