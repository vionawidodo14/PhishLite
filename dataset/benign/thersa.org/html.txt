<h2>What is the RSA?</h2>
<p>At the RSA (Royal Society for the encouragement of Arts, Manufactures and Commerce) we believe in a world where everyone is able to participate in creating a better future, by uniting people and ideas to resolve the challenges of our time.</p>
<p>We do this by bringing together a global community of proactive problem solvers, supported by our Fellows and partners.</p>
