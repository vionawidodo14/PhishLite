<div class="article-content">
<div class="text-content">
<p>
                                    As a proud member of the <a class="link-article" href="https://www.whiteflash.com/about-diamonds/news/whiteflash-store-approved-for-membership-in-american-gem-society-1418.htm">American Gem Society</a> Whiteflash is fully dedicated to consumer protection and education. 
                                    Our team is composed of dozens of GIA trained diamond and jewelry professionals including several Graduate Gemologists, Diamond Graduates, and AGS Certified Gemologists. We offer extensive <a class="link-article" href="https://www.whiteflash.com/about-diamonds/diamond-education/">Diamond Education</a>
                                    in the form of articles on a wide range of diamond and jewelry topics including a co-authored series on 
                                    <a class="link-article" href="https://www.whiteflash.com/about-diamonds/diamond-education/laboratory-graded-diamonds-what-are-the-benefits-for-the-consumer-1173.htm">diamond grading at the American Gem Society Laboratories</a> (AGSL). And while others debate 
                                    <a class="link-article" href="https://www.whiteflash.com/ags-vs-gia/">GIA vs AGS</a>, we fully embrace both of these sister organizations as they each hold a place as the premier diamond labs in the world. Please visit our diamond education page for links to the most up to date information on the 4Cs –diamond color, diamond clarity, diamond carat, and the most important C- diamond cut.
                                </p>
</div>
</div>

<div class="item">
<div class="title-text padding-right-left-8">IDEAL PRINCESS CUT DIAMONDS</div>
<div class="article-content">
<div class="text-content">
<p>
                                    The dazzling sister to the A CUT ABOVE®  Hearts and Arrows round diamond is the <a class="link-article" href="https://www.whiteflash.com/about-diamonds/diamond-education/whiteflash-aca-princess-cut-diamonds.htm">A CUT ABOVE<sup>®</sup> Princess</a> Ideal Cut Diamond. To achieve the status of A CUT ABOVE<sup>®</sup> the diamond must first be certified as an AGS Ideal Princess. It then must pass additional evaluations including the most stringent light performance image testing in the industry, demonstrating unparalleled fire and brilliance. AGS diamonds are some of the finest cut diamonds in the world - the AGS Princess with a cut grade of Ideal is in itself an extremely rare find. An Ideal Princess Cut that carries the A CUT ABOVE<sup>®</sup> brand is a world-class super ideal diamond of unsurpassed performance. So, if you are in the market for a world-class princess diamond of unrivalled performance, you have just found it! A CUT ABOVE<sup>®</sup> Super Ideal Princess.
                                </p>
