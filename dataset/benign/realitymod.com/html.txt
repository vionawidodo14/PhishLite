<h2 class="news-title"><a class="link2" href="https://www.realitymod.com/forum/showthread.php?t=148786" title="Read Full Article">PR:BF2 v1.6/WW2 Status Update March 2019</a></h2>
<div class="toggleInfo">
<div class="news-body"><div align="center"><img alt="" border="0" onload="NcodeImageResizer.createOn(this);" src="https://media.realitymod.com/headers/prbf2_update.png"/></div><br/>
First of all, a late welcome to 2019! It will hopefully be another good year for PR:BF2. <br/>
Last year saw the release of v1.5 with the Polish forces and also this year we plan a big release! We are still hard at work at v1.6 and it's highly anticipated inclusion of the WW2 factions and maps. We know it's been a long development cycle and while it will take a bit longer still, we wanted to give you all a peak at what's in store for the next release. Not only with work done on the WW2 factions and maps, but also other content additions to PR in general. <br/>
<br/>
Without further delay, here's an updated look of what we've recently finished or are still working on for the release of PR:BF2 v1.6 with WW2 implemented. However, this is by no means all that we have been working on, and only a small sample of what's in store for v1.6! Do note that all of this is still a work in progress and might change before release.<br/>

<h2 class="news-title"><a class="link2" href="https://www.realitymod.com/forum/showthread.php?t=148763" title="Read Full Article">Website Update 2019</a></h2>
<div class="toggleInfo">
<div class="news-body">As many of you noticed, for the last few days the website has been offline. This was done as a set of very necessary updates had to be applied. <a href="https://www.realitymod.com/forum/showthread.php?t=147424" target="_blank">While these were planned a long time ago</a>, we decided to do them now with a higher urgency as right before we took the website offline, we noticed that our forum database had been tampered with. However, at this time we do not believe any data was compromised. No passwords or personal data were acquired. <br/>
<br/>
However, as a precaution and to start the upgraded forum with a fresh start in terms of security for all users, we have reset the passwords for everyone. Meaning you will have to use the <a href="https://www.realitymod.com/forum/login.php?do=lostpw" target="_blank">password reset system</a> to properly regain access to your account. As always, we recommend to never use the same password on two services and suggest to use a <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Password_manager" target="_blank">password manager</a>. If you have any trouble getting access back to your account, feel free to contact us on any platform you can find us or use the <a href="https://www.realitymod.com/contact" target="_blank">contact form</a>.<br/>
<br/>
Now what work has been done while the website was offline? You can read a more detailed description of it <a href="https://www.realitymod.com/forum/blog.php?b=468" target="_blank">here</a>. In short the following was done:<ul><li>Updated our webserver</li>
<li>Upgraded forum software and it's dependencies</li>
<li>Improved security and performance of various webpages and systems</li>
<li>Improved our outgoing email system</li>
</ul><br/>
This comes with the benefit of not only increased security but also a noticeable performance boost throughout the various pages and the forum.<br/>

Hi guys, first of all, thank you so much for your patience over the years. It's been a long time since the beta and we know you're anxiously waiting for updates or better yet, a release date. </div><br/>
Originally created by the <b>Battlegroup42 mod</b> (BF1942 mod team) and brought to the BF2 engine by <b>Anthony</b> and his team. We plan to implement it into PR alongside other vehicles we could potentially use.<br/>
<br/>

Sporting the potent 3 inch M7 anti-aircraft cannon refitted as a tank cannon and the same drive train as the M4 Sherman, it was about as powerful as the aforementioned in terms of offensive capabilities. Its open top turret design however meant the crew was exposed to artillery, shrapnel and rifle fire, whereas its thin armour meant it was quite fragile compared to proper main battle tanks and was supposed to not even engage them over open ground.
