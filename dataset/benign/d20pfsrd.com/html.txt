<li><strong>Fantasy Classes</strong>: Using a 300 point system, fantasy classes are broken down into their features, then presented with new archetypes to choose from that give them a more anime-based flare.</li>
<li><strong>Character Options and Attributes</strong>: Learn how to build a point-based character, or pick up a race and class using the point-based system as a guideline for how to hand out experience and when to level up. Includes over 100 attributes, defects, and restrictions.</li>
