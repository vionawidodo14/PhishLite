<h2>About Delfont Mackintosh Theatres</h2>
<hr class="title-hr"/>
<p>Delfont Mackintosh Theatres is owned by Sir Cameron Mackintosh and operates eight of the most prestigious theatres in London's West End. <a href="theatres/prince-edward-theatre/">Prince Edward</a> and <a href="theatres/prince-of-wales-theatre/">Prince of Wales</a>, <a href="theatres/novello-theatre/">Novello</a>, <a href="theatres/gielgud-theatre/">Gielgud</a>, <a href="theatres/sondheim-theatre/">Sondheim (formerly Queen's)</a>, <a href="theatres/noel-coward-theatre/">Noël Coward</a> and <a href="theatres/wyndhams-theatre/">Wyndham's</a> have all had major refurbishments in the last few years, combining beautiful restoration with twenty first century comfort. We have also recently welcomed <a href="theatres/victoria-palace-theatre/">Victoria Palace</a> to the Delfont Mackintosh Theatres group.</p>

<h2>Access at Delfont Mackintosh Theatres</h2>
<hr class="title-hr"/>
<p>Delfont Mackintosh Theatres are keen to ensure any customers who have specific requirements due to health conditions or a disability have an enjoyable visit to all of our venues. We have access prices available for most productions and dedicated staff on hand at all theatres to make your visit as comfortable as possible.</p>
