<h1 class="featured-box-header">Government Affairs</h1>
<p class="featured-paragraph">NBCC supports the counseling profession through many initiatives at the local, state, and federal levels.</p>
<br/>
<p class="featured-paragraph">NBCC’s current government affairs objectives include:</p>
<div class="featured-bullets">
<ul style="list-style:square;">
<li>Improved counselor employment by the Department of Veterans Affairs.</li>
<li>Inclusion of counselors in the Medicare program.</li>
<li>Increased counselor inclusion and recognition by the Military Health Systems.</li>
<li>The creation of a Federal Occupational Series for Counselors.</li>
<li>Advocacy for the Minority Fellowship Program and other federal workforce programs.</li>
<li>Counselor recognition by the Indian Health Service.</li>
</ul>
</div>
<br/>
<h3 class="featured-box-header-two">How You Can Help</h3>
<p class="featured-paragraph">If you want to advance  the counseling profession and receive additional news about government affairs activities, join the NBCC grassroots network by emailing your contact information and National Certified Counselor (NCC) number to <a href="mailto:govtaffairs@nbcc.org">govtaffairs@nbcc.org</a>. NBCC’s grassroots network consists of board certified counselors who care about important issues and are motivated to get involved by contacting their legislators to help pass measures that support the profession.</p>
