<h1 class="sc-content-block-header-text">Welcome to Publishing house SciPress Ltd.</h1>
</div>
<p>
</p><h2 class="sc-paragraph-header">Become our Author</h2>
                We invite researchers to publish their findings in our periodicals covering different fields of science: physics, mathematics, engineering, pharmacology, natural sciences, social and humanistic sciences.
            <p></p>
<p>
</p><h2 class="sc-paragraph-header">Contribute to the <a href="/home/open-access" target="_blank">Open Access</a> Publishing</h2>
                Choosing SciPress all participants contribute to the Open Access scientific publishing movement, which proclaims the ideas of unrestricted access to academic research and sustainability of academic publishing. Entrusting us with their manuscripts the authors benefit from the Open Access model of publishing which increases the visibility, use, and impact of their research output.
            <p></p>
<p>
</p><h2 class="sc-paragraph-header">Gain from the Online Publishing System</h2>
                SciPress provides authors, editors, and reviewers with a user-friendly interface to manage the publishing process in a convenient and clear way. The system allows creation the personal accounts through which the manuscripts are submitted and tracked while they pass all the stages of the editorial process. The system ensures a peer review for each manuscript and enables authors to receive the feedback from reviewers and editors.
            <p></p>
<p>
</p><h2 class="sc-paragraph-header">Progress together with SciPress</h2>
                Staying in-step with the times and serving the needs of scientists from all over the world, SciPress Team is always looking for new solutions to give the best possible support and service to our authors.
            <p></p>
</div>

<div class="sc-partner-text sc-inline-block">
<h3 class="sc-paragraph-header">SciPress cooperates with <a href="https://www.crossref.org/" rel="nofollow" target="_blank">Crossref</a></h3>
                    Each paper published with SciPress is assigned a <a href="https://www.doi.org/" rel="nofollow" target="_blank">Digital Object Identifier (DOI)</a>, which guarantees an unambiguous identification of the paper and gives a tenacious link to its location on the web. The content of all our journals is added to the anti-plagiarism database allowing SciPress and other publishers to perform the Similarity Check using the <a href="https://www.ithenticate.com/" rel="nofollow" target="_blank">iThenticate</a> software.
                </div>
</div>
<div class="sc-partner-block">
<div class="sc-partner-icon sc-inline-block">

<img alt="SHERPA/RoMEO" src="/Content/app/images/Partners/Sherpa.png"/>
</a>
</div>
<div class="sc-partner-text sc-inline-block">
<h3 class="sc-paragraph-header">SciPress is a <a href="http://www.sherpa.ac.uk/romeo/search.php?id=2785&amp;fIDnum%3d%7c%26mode=simple&amp;la=en" rel="nofollow" target="_blank">SHERPA/RoMEO</a> «green» publisher</h3>
                    Being an Open Access Publisher, we endorse the universal practice of self-archiving. To make our policy clear and available we got registered with SHERPA/RoMEO. Our authors can be confident in our support of their intent to distribute and archive their papers published with SciPress.
                </div>

SciPress employs E-Journal Preservation Service provided by PORTICO to secure the published content in any circumstances.
