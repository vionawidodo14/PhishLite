<h1 class="firstHeading" id="firstHeading">SNPedia</h1>
<!-- tagline; usually goes something like "From WikiName" primary purpose of this seems to be for printing to identify the source of the content -->

<div class="mw-content-ltr" dir="ltr" id="mw-content-text" lang="en"><div class="mw-parser-output"><p>SNPedia is a wiki investigating human genetics. We share information about the effects of variations in DNA, citing peer-reviewed scientific publications. It is used by <a href="/index.php/Promethease" title="Promethease">Promethease</a> to create a personal report linking your DNA variations to the information published about them. Please see the <a href="/index.php/SNPedia:FAQ" title="SNPedia:FAQ">SNPedia:FAQ</a> for answers to common questions.
</p><p><br/>
</p>
