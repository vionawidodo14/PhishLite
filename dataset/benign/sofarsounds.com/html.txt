<section class="text-center" id="what-is-a-sofar-show">
<div class="container">
<div class="row">
<div class="col-md-10 col-md-offset-1 what-is-a-sofar-show pt3">
<div class="row">
<div class="col-md-12">
<div class="what-is-a-sofar-show__title mb2">
<h2>
What is a Sofar show?
</h2>
<p>
A Sofar gig is an intimate show held in an unique venue, like someone's living room or an antique shop. It typically features three artists, playing four songs each. Every lineup is specially curated to give our audiences a diverse show.
</p>
</div>
</div>
</div>
<div class="row mt3">
<div class="col-md-4">
<div class="what-is-a-sofar-show__info">
<img class="what-is-a-sofar-show__icon pt1 pb1" src="//dax2lgcd0wbaz.cloudfront.net/assets/icons/apply-new-v11-2fdf963a2f753708f1fddf825ae41b441bdb3e86f737068f3a703182948739aa.png"/>
<h3>
1. Apply for tickets
</h3>
<p>
It’s free! Apply up until the day before show time or tickets sell out, whichever comes first.
</p>
</div>
</div>
<div class="col-md-4">
<div class="what-is-a-sofar-show__info">
<img class="what-is-a-sofar-show__icon pt1 pb1" src="//dax2lgcd0wbaz.cloudfront.net/assets/icons/selected-new-v11-8cc638e162e68ee62800a32d67bfc0e1ce908d8a11a41318a96406e762fd8568.png"/>
<h3>
2. Get selected
</h3>
<p>
If selected, you'll receive an email to purchase your tickets.
</p>
</div>
</div>
<div class="col-md-4">
<div class="what-is-a-sofar-show__info">
<img class="what-is-a-sofar-show__icon pt1 pb1" src="//dax2lgcd0wbaz.cloudfront.net/assets/icons/icons-tickets-v11-43961e8b8b18fd249c967b057b06c1438a8aa93b06fe8a4d6caba3fc1964710b.png"/>
<h3>
3. Buy tickets
</h3>
<p>
Purchase tickets for yourself and friends. Then we’ll email you with the venue location at least a day before the show.
</p>
</div>
</div>
</div>
</div>
</div>
</div>
