<section id="how_it_works">
<div class="container">
<header class="large_header">
<h2>How does it work?</h2>
</header>
<div class="row">
<div class="col-md-12">
<div class="thumbnail without_border">
<img alt="The dropshipping workflow with Duoplane" src="https://duostatic.s3.amazonaws.com/resources/What_is_dropshipping_numbered.png" title="How Duoplane works"/>
</div>
</div>
</div>
<div class="row">
<div class="col-md-8 col-md-offset-2">
<ol class="styled rounded_list">
<li><div class="list_item">Your customer places an order on your site.</div></li>
<li><div class="list_item">Duoplane automatically imports the order.</div></li>
<li><div class="list_item">Based on rules that you've set, Duoplane separates the order's items into one or more vendor purchase orders.</div></li>
<li><div class="list_item">Duoplane sends the purchase orders to the correct vendors or warehouses. POs can be sent by email or FTP in any of a number of different formats (e.g. PDF, CSV, XML files) and on a variety of custom schedules.</div></li>
<li><div class="list_item">Once a vendor receives a purchase order, they can use your Vendor Portal to confirm the PO, send you a message about the order, or download a branded packing slip that has been customized with your logo and policies.</div></li>
<li><div class="list_item">After the purchase order ships, your vendor or you upload or enter tracking information. Duoplane can also automatically retrieve tracking information if the order was shipped on your UPS or FedEx account.</div></li>
<li><div class="list_item">Duoplane transmits the tracking number to your cart software to indicate that that part of the order has shipped.</div></li>
<li><div class="list_item">With our optional accounting sync, Duoplane can also export vendor invoices to your accounting system.</div></li>
</ol>
</div>
</div>
</div>
</section>
