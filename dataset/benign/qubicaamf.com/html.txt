<h1 class="documentFirstHeading">Making Bowling Amazing is at the heart of everything we do</h1>
</header>
<section id="content-core">
<div class="document-body body compact"><p>Qubica began as a small startup in 1993, founded by three young product engineers.</p>
<p>Today, QubicaAMF is the largest and most innovative bowling equipment provider in the world with 600 employees! In 2018, we are celebrating our 25 year anniversary.</p>
<p>Because of our amazing products, we build and modernize more bowling entertainment centers than any other company in the world, every year.</p>
<p>Our ongoing mission is <strong><em>Making Bowling Amazing</em></strong>. We are convinced that bowling has so much more potential to attract and entertain even more people, more often. With our products, we will help our customers fully unleash that potential.</p>
<p>To make bowling amazing, we are creating an ever-growing ecosystem of products that work together and build on one another to deliver an AMAZING, never before seen, experience to bowling consumers.</p>
<p>We will continuously innovate to ensure your long-term success. That is our promise to you.</p>
