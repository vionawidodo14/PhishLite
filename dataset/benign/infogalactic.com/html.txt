<h2 id="mp-tfa-h2" style="margin:0.5em; background:#cef2e0; font-family:inherit; font-size:120%; font-weight:bold; border:1px solid #a3bfb1; color:#000; padding:0.2em 0.4em;"><span class="mw-headline" id="From_today.27s_featured_article">From today's featured article</span></h2>
<div id="mp-tfa" style="padding:0.1em 0.6em;">
<div class="thumb tleft">
<div class="thumbinner" style="width:302px;"><a class="image" href="/info/File:Battle_of_Lepanto_1571.jpg"><img alt="" class="thumbimage" height="161" src="/w/images/thumb/e/e0/Battle_of_Lepanto_1571.jpg/300px-Battle_of_Lepanto_1571.jpg" width="300"/></a>
<div class="thumbcaption">
<div class="magnify"><a class="internal" href="/info/File:Battle_of_Lepanto_1571.jpg" title="Enlarge"></a></div>
The Battle of Lepanto, unknown artist, late 16th century</div>
</div>
</div>
<p>The <b>Battle of Lepanto</b> was a naval engagement taking place on 7 October 1571 in which a fleet of the <a href="/info/Holy_League_(1571)" title="Holy League (1571)">Holy League</a>, a coalition of European <a href="/info/Catholic_Church" title="Catholic Church">Catholic</a> <a href="/info/Maritime_nation" title="Maritime nation">maritime states</a> arranged by <a href="/info/Pope_Pius_V" title="Pope Pius V">Pope Pius V</a> and led by <a href="/info/Spanish_Empire" title="Spanish Empire">Spanish</a> admiral <a href="/info/John_of_Austria" title="John of Austria">Don Juan of Austria</a>, decisively defeated the fleet of the <a href="/info/Ottoman_Empire" title="Ottoman Empire">Ottoman Empire</a> on the northern edge of the <a href="/info/Gulf_of_Corinth" title="Gulf of Corinth">Gulf of Corinth</a>, off western <a href="/info/Greece" title="Greece">Greece</a>. The Ottoman forces sailing westwards from their naval station in <a class="mw-redirect" href="/info/Naupactus" title="Naupactus">Lepanto</a> (<a href="/info/Turkish_language" title="Turkish language">Turkish</a>: <span lang="tr" xml:lang="tr"><i>İnebahtı</i></span>; <a href="/info/Greek_language" title="Greek language">Greek</a>: <span lang="el" xml:lang="el">Ναύπακτος or Έπαχτος <i>Naupaktos</i> or <i>Épahtos</i></span>) met the Holy League forces, which came from <a class="mw-redirect" href="/info/Messina,_Italy" title="Messina, Italy">Messina</a>, <a href="/info/Sicily" title="Sicily">Sicily</a>, where they had previously gathered.</p>
The victory of the Holy League prevented the Ottoman Empire from expanding further along the European side of the Mediterranean. Lepanto was the last major naval battle in the Mediterranean fought entirely between <a href="/info/Galley" title="Galley">galleys</a> and has been assigned great symbolic and historical importance by several historians. (<a href="/info/Battle_of_Lepanto" title="Battle of Lepanto"><b>Full article...</b></a>)</div>
