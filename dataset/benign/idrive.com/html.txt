<h1><a class="flt-none" href="/unlimited-device-online-backup">Protect Data From Multiple Devices With A Single IDrive Cloud Backup Account!</a></h1>
<span>October 25, 2019</span>
<p>In this fast-evolving world of technology, it has become impossible for one to imagine their lives without smartphones, laptops or computers. With so many different devices comes huge amounts of data. And the first rule of thumb with data, be it personal or work related, is to have a backup.</p>
<p>There are numerous backup solutions to choose from based on your needs. For backing up a few hundred music and video files or a handful of documents, an external hard drive might do just fine.  But, if you have a fair amount of data – 50 Blu-ray movies, 20,000 MP3s, documents, emails, photos, videos, and a few virtual machines- then an online backup solution like IDrive is probably a better fit for your data.</p>
<p>It doesn’t matter which device you use - PCs, Macs, iPhones, iPads or Androids – IDrive online backup can transfer data from all of them into a single account. You can install IDrive cloud backup application on any device and transfer critical data effortlessly. When you perform online backup from a device, a unique folder with the name of the device will be created in your IDrive cloud backup account for easy accessibility and prevents data overlap. Also there is no limit to the number of devices you can connect to your IDrive cloud backup account.</p>
