<h1>Our Quality Guarantee</h1>
<p class="p1">Many companies can provide your laboratory solutions, but not many can provide them with the level of quality that LabChem can provide.  Why would you accept anything less?</p>
<p class="p2">LabChem utilizes the highest quality raw materials appropriate to your product.  Most raw materials meet or exceed the specifications established by the American Chemical Society.  Where appropriate, many finished products are traceable to NIST Standard Reference Materials.</p>
<p class="p2">Manufacturing, quality control testing, and packaging are all performed in LabChem’s own facilities.  Record keeping and sample retention of all produced lots ensure product consistency and complete traceability.</p>
<p class="p2">LabChem has been ISO 9001 certified since 1994.  In fact, we were one of the first companies of our size to achieve ISO certification.  Through our ISO 9001 program we have a commitment to continuous improvement – and, in turn, a commitment to you.</p>
