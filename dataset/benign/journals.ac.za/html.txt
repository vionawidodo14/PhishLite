<h1><span class="mw-headline" id="About_the_ESAT_project"><b>About the ESAT project</b></span></h1>
<p>The <i><a href="/index.php/ESAT" title="ESAT">ESAT</a></i> encyclopaedia-project was conceived as an interactive resource for researchers interested in the evolution, history and forms of performance and media in South Africa, and it is based on research originally undertaken by the <i><a href="/index.php/Centre_for_South_African_Theatre_Research" title="Centre for South African Theatre Research">Centre for South African Theatre Research</a></i> under the auspices of, and with the support of, the <a href="/index.php/Human_Sciences_Research_Council" title="Human Sciences Research Council">Human Sciences Research Council</a> (<a href="/index.php/HSRC" title="HSRC">HSRC</a>) between 1979 and 1987. In 1988 the research was transferred to, and continued at, the <a href="/index.php/University_of_Stellenbosch" title="University of Stellenbosch">University of Stellenbosch</a> by the <i><a href="/index.php/Centre_for_Theatre_and_Performance_Studies" title="Centre for Theatre and Performance Studies">Centre for Theatre and Performance Studies</a></i>, with the support of the University's Division for Research Development, the former <a class="mw-redirect" href="/index.php/Centre_for_Science_Development" title="Centre for Science Development">Centre for Science Development</a> (<a class="mw-redirect" href="/index.php/CSD" title="CSD">CSD</a>)  and the <a href="/index.php/National_Research_Foundation" title="National Research Foundation">National Research Foundation</a> (<a class="mw-redirect" href="/index.php/NRF" title="NRF">NRF</a>). The website was opened for public use in 2011. 
</p><p><i><b>Please note that any opinion, finding and conclusion or recommendation expressed in this material is that of the authors and that none of the sponsors of the project accept any liability in this regard.</b></i>
</p><p>The project is currently headed by <a href="/index.php/Temple_Hauptfleisch" title="Temple Hauptfleisch">Temple Hauptfleisch</a> (founder, editor and project director of the undertaking), with <a href="/index.php/Freddy_Ogterop" title="Freddy Ogterop">Freddy Ogterop</a>, <a href="/index.php/Ivor_Markman" title="Ivor Markman">Ivor Markman</a> and <a href="/index.php/Miriam_Terblanche" title="Miriam Terblanche">Miriam Terblanche</a> as assistant editors, while numerous contributors across the globe have helped in collating and writing the information since its first inception. 
</p><p>The encyclopaedia is published on the web with the assistance of  the <a class="mw-redirect" href="/index.php/University_of_Stellenbosch,_Department_of_Drama" title="University of Stellenbosch, Department of Drama">Drama Department</a> (<a class="external free" href="http://www.sun.ac.za/drama" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">http://www.sun.ac.za/drama</a>) and its former <a href="/index.php/Centre_for_Theatre_and_Performance_Studies" title="Centre for Theatre and Performance Studies">Centre for Theatre and Performance Studies</a>, the <a href="/index.php/J.S._Gericke_Library" title="J.S. Gericke Library">J.S. Gericke Library</a> and  the division for Information Technology at the <a href="/index.php/University_of_Stellenbosch" title="University of Stellenbosch">University of Stellenbosch</a> (<a class="external free" href="http://www.sun.ac.za/index.asp" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">http://www.sun.ac.za/index.asp</a>). The format of the work thus derives from the format used for the <a href="/index.php/University_of_Stellenbosch" title="University of Stellenbosch">University of Stellenbosch</a>'s <a href="/index.php/Libopedia" title="Libopedia">Libopedia</a><a class="external autonumber" href="http://wiki.lib.sun.ac.za/index.php/Libopedia:About" rel="nofollow noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">[1]</a>
project, and uses the same <a href="/index.php/Wiki" title="Wiki">Wiki</a> format and programmes.
</p><p><i>For more detailed information on the nature of the project and the history and aims of the encyclopaedia, click on:<b><a href="/index.php/About_ESAT" title="About ESAT">About ESAT</a></b></i>
</p><p><b>You can also contact <a href="/index.php/ESAT" title="ESAT">ESAT</a> at the following addresses:</b>
</p><p><i>Postal address</i>: The Editor: ESAT, Department of Drama, University of Stellenbosch, Private Bag X1, MATIELAND 7602, South Africa.
</p><p><i>E-mail address</i>: satj@sun.ac.za 
</p><p><i>Fax number</i>: (+ 27) 21 882 9141
</p>
<h1><span class="mw-headline" id="How_to_use_ESAT"><b>How to use <i>ESAT</i></b></span></h1>
<p>While <a href="/index.php/ESAT" title="ESAT">ESAT</a> uses the <a href="/index.php/Wiki" title="Wiki">Wiki</a> programme, which makes provision for a very useful <i><b>search</b></i> function, the database has avoided contemporary multi-screen web-design, and has been constructed rather more traditionally, like a printed encyclopaedia, with an <b><a href="/index.php/The_ESAT_Entries" title="The ESAT Entries">Index</a></b> to the various entries (also called <b><a href="/index.php/The_ESAT_Entries" title="The ESAT Entries">The ESAT Entries</a></b>). The entries in the <a href="/index.php/The_ESAT_Entries" title="The ESAT Entries">Index</a> have been grouped in <b>sixteen</b> distinct categories, each one managed and edited by one of the research editors. 
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Accessing_the_database_of_entries"><b>Accessing the database of entries</b></span></h2>
<p>To access <b>database material</b> on the relevant category, the reader has two options:
</p>
<h2><span id='Using_the_"Search"_function'></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Using_the_.22Search.22_function">Using the <b>"Search"</b> function</span></h2>
<p>Go to the <i><b>"Search"</b></i> block at the top right of this page (or type: Alt+F),  type in the search word/phrase and click on the <b>search</b> icon there (the magnifying glass) or press the <i><b>Enter</b></i> button on your keyboard.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Using_the_Index_to_ESAT">Using the <b>Index</b> to <i><b>ESAT</b></i></span></h2>
<p>Simply click on <b><a href="/index.php/The_ESAT_Entries" title="The ESAT Entries">The ESAT Entries</a></b> at the start of this main page (or <b><a href="/index.php/The_ESAT_Entries" title="The ESAT Entries">HERE</a></b>) , to go to the list of 16 categories into which the material has been divided. On the list provided, click on the appropriate one, then follow instructions on the landing page.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="NB:_Copyright_and_referencing_of_material_used"><b>NB: Copyright and referencing of material used</b></span></h2>
<p><a href="/index.php/ESAT" title="ESAT">ESAT</a> is an <a href="/index.php/Open_access" title="Open access">open access</a> publication, which means that even though the material contained in the encyclopaedia is <a class="mw-redirect" href="/index.php/Copyright" title="Copyright">copyrighted</a> to <a href="/index.php/ESAT" title="ESAT">ESAT</a> and the <a href="/index.php/University_of_Stellenbosch" title="University of Stellenbosch">University of Stellenbosch</a>, it may be freely used for academic and artistic purposes as long as such use is clearly referenced and the material used is attributed to The <b><a href="/index.php/Encyclopaedia_of_South_African_Theatre,_Film,_Media_and_Performance" title="Encyclopaedia of South African Theatre, Film, Media and Performance">Encyclopaedia of South African Theatre, Film, Media and Performance</a></b> (<b><a href="/index.php/ESAT" title="ESAT">ESAT</a></b>), the reference also including the <b>URL for the particular entry</b> in <a href="/index.php/ESAT" title="ESAT">ESAT</a>.
</p>
<h1><span class="mw-headline" id="Contributing_to_ESAT"><b>Contributing to <i>ESAT</i></b></span></h1>
<p>All researchers, artists and other interested parties are invited to help us to expand, improve, update and correct <a href="/index.php/ESAT" title="ESAT">ESAT</a> since, by its very nature, the database will always be incomplete and prone to error on occasion.
</p><p>To find how you can contribute, click here: <b><a href="/index.php/Contributing_to_ESAT" title="Contributing to ESAT">Contributing to ESAT</a></b>.
</p>
