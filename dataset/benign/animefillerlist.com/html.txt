<h2>What is Filler?</h2>
<div class="Bubbles clearfix">
<div class="Bubble">
<img alt="Anime character reading." height="200" src="/sites/all/themes/afl/i/bubble1.jpg" width="200"/>
<p>Anime is usually based on Manga, a type of Japanese comic popular with people of all ages.</p>
</div>
<div class="Sep"></div>
<div class="Bubble">
<img alt="Anime characters racing." height="200" src="/sites/all/themes/afl/i/bubble2.jpg" width="200"/>
<p>"Filler" refers to story in an Anime that was not in the Manga. These are created because Anime production usually outpaces the Manga.</p>
</div>
<div class="Sep"></div>
<div class="Bubble">
<img alt="Ninja ostrach." height="200" src="/sites/all/themes/afl/i/bubble3.jpg" width="200"/>
<p>Fillers do not further the story and usually are of a lower quality both visually and narratively.</p>
</div>
