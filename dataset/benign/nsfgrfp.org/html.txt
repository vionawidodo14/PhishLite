<h2 style="color:#005699">About the Program</h2>
<p>The NSF Graduate Research Fellowship Program recognizes and supports outstanding graduate students in NSF-supported science, technology, engineering, and mathematics disciplines who are pursuing research-based Master's and doctoral degrees at accredited United States institutions.  <strong><a href="http://www.nsfgrfp.org/general_resources/about">Read More »</a></strong></p>
<h2 style="color:#005699"> </h2>
<h2 style="color:#005699">Eligibility Guidelines </h2>
<p>See the current <a href="https://www.nsf.gov/publications/pub_summ.jsp?ods_key=nsf19590">Program Solicitation</a><a href="https://www.nsf.gov/pubs/2019/nsf19590/nsf19590.htm"> </a>for eligibility guidelines. Those already enrolled in graduate school may apply one time only.</p>
<p> </p>

<h2 style="color:#005699">In the News</h2>
<p><span style="color:#FF0000;"><b><u>Clarification to the Eligibilty Requirements for Individuals Already Holding a Joint Bachelor's-Master's Degree</u></b></span></p>
<p>Please note that the GRF Program Solicitation has been updated with the following new language:</p>
<p><strong>Individuals holding joint bachelor's-master's degrees currently enrolled as first-year doctoral students, who have not previously applied as graduate students and enrolled in the doctoral program the semester following award of the joint degree, may apply as first-year doctoral students only.</strong></p>
