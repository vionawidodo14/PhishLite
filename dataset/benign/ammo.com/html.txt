<h3>We Arm Customers Physically &amp; Philosophically</h3>
<p><img alt="People of the Gun" class="image-right" height="135" src="https://d1w4q6ldc8l0qo.cloudfront.net/skin/frontend/ammonet/rwd/images/potg-badge-large.jpg" width="200"/> What makes the right to keep and bear arms so important? Simply put, the individual right to defend against aggression is fundamental to civil society. <a href="https://ammo.com/articles/gun-ownership-in-america" target="_self" title="Gun Ownership in America">Self defense</a> is a natural safeguard against violations by individuals or oppression by institutions, such as an increasingly powerful and unaccountable government. We believe the best ammo deals will help Americans stay armed.</p>
<p>Criminals want soft targets, individuals who can't or won't fight back. Unchecked power corrupts even the best, and people who have known too much power over others for too long become increasingly comfortable with harsh and extreme measures. This is part of what happens when police officers brutalize the citizens they have sworn to protect.  The corrupting effect of too much power is what permits a government to slaughter their own unarmed citizens, as we have seen<a href="https://ammo.com/articles/democide-hitler-stalin-mao-state-violence-guide" target="_self" title="Democide"> too many times</a> in the last century.</p>
<p>The <a href="https://www.law.cornell.edu/wex/second_amendment" target="_blank" title="Second Amendment">Second Amendment</a> was established to prevent such an imbalance of power.     Overwhelmed With Ammo Options? Start Here    <svg class="icon icon-sort"><use xlink:href="#icon-sort"></use></svg>
</h2>
<div class="section-content collapse in" id="section_right_2ndamendmentsupport_contents">
<p>Welcome to the best place to buy ammo online. Whether you're hunting, target shooting or looking for the best self-defense rounds – ammo shopping can be overwhelming. The good news? You've arrived at the best online ammo store that will help you find what you need and get it into your hands as quickly as possible.</p>
<p>If you don't know where to start, here is a quick overview of the types of ammunition, the brands that carry them (both imported and American ammo), and their many varieties.</p>
<h3>Handgun Ammo</h3>
<p>True to its name, a handgun is small and lightweight – making it popular for concealed carry. And while you may lose accuracy compared to shooting a rifle or shotgun,  some of the most popular calibers in the world are designed for handguns. The <a href="https://ammo.com/handgun/45-long-colt-ammo" target="_self" title=".45 Long Colt Ammo">.45 Colt</a>. The <a href="https://ammo.com/handgun/9mm-ammo" target="_self" title="9mm ammo">9mm Luger</a>. The <a href="https://ammo.com/handgun/357-magnum-ammo" target="_self" title=".357 mag ammo">.357 Magnum</a>. The <a href="https://ammo.com/handgun/40-cal-ammo" target="_self" title=".40 S&amp;W ammo">.40 Smith &amp; Wesson</a>. Not only are each of these cartridges an iconic piece of ammo history, but they also influenced the development of modern handgun ammunition.</p>
<p>These legendary calibers are just the beginning. From military and law enforcement use to home protection and concealed carry, you will find <a href="https://ammo.com/handgun" target="_self" title="handgun ammo">handgun ammo</a> for a variety of uses.</p>
<h3>Rifle Ammo</h3>
<p>The rifle's unmatched versatility is perhaps why it has been around for centuries. Its power and accuracy makes it popular for hunting, self defense and target shooting – but the wide variety of rifles and ammo can be mind-boggling.</p>
<p>Hunting with a rifle allows you to choose ammo for specific game. Small or large, varmint or predators, short range or long range – the <a href="https://ammo.com/rifle" target="_self" title="rifle ammo">rifle ammunition</a> available is so particular, it can mean the difference between hunting a black bear or a brown bear. And while a rifle's length can make it difficult to maneuver in tight spaces, its accuracy makes it a popular choice for home defense, with the AR-15 among the most trusted in the U.S.</p>
<h3>Rimfire Ammo</h3>
<p>With less power than its centerfire counterpart (due to primer being at the base as opposed to the center), <a href="https://ammo.com/rimfire" target="_self" title="rimfire ammo">rimfire ammunition</a> is most commonly used for plinking, target shooting and varmint hunting. Among rimfire ammo is one of the most widely used cartridges in the world – the <a href="https://ammo.com/rimfire/22-lr-ammo" target="_self" title=".22 LR ammo">.22 Long Rifle</a>. Many novice shooters use this iconic ammo as a learning tool and go on to use it for plinking and small-game hunting.</p>
<p>For those looking for a training ammunition that doesn't need the power of a centerfire cartridge, rimfire ammo is fun to shoot and is generally among the cheapest ammunition online.</p>
<h3>Shotgun Ammo</h3>
<p>Arguably the most specialized type of ammo on the market today, <a href="https://ammo.com/shotgun" target="_self" title="shotgun ammo">shotgun ammunition</a> can be loaded for uses as specific as deer, squirrel and a variety of game birds. Shot sizes ranging from 000 (triple aught) to 12 shot and with varieties in lead, steel and buckshot – the choices are seemingly endless, and they provide several excuses to own a shotgun. From sport shooting and hunting to home and personal defense, this firearm is as versatile as the amount of shotgun shells on the market.</p>
<p>Less lethal ammo is also available for shotguns, including rubber bullets, which are often used by law enforcement for crowd control.</p>
<h3>Ammo Brands</h3>
<p>When most people think of the history of firearms, they think of the weapons themselves. But a gun is useless without its ammunition, which has a long and storied history in the United States – dating back to the Founding Fathers and the use of armories. Commercial ammo as we know it today began with a familiar name, Eliaphalet Remington, who founded what is now the oldest manufacturer of firearms in the U.S. in 1816. <a href="https://ammo.com/brands/remington-ammo" target="_self" title="Remington ammo">Remington</a> paved the way for <a href="https://ammo.com/brands" target="_self" title="ammo brands">ammo brands</a> across the country like <a href="https://ammo.com/brands/hornady-ammo" target="_self" title="Hornady ammo">Hornady</a> and <a href="https://ammo.com/brands/winchester-ammo" target="_self" title="Winchester ammo">Winchester</a>, as well as international brands like Italy's <a href="https://ammo.com/brands/fiocchi-ammo" target="_self" title="Fiocchi ammo">Fiocchi</a>, Russia's <a href="https://ammo.com/brands/wolf-ammo" target="_self" title="Wolf ammo">Wolf</a>, and South Korea's <a href="https://ammo.com/brands/pmc-ammo" target="_self" title="PMC ammo">PMC</a>.</p>
<p>We offer these brands and many more. Browse our inventory and learn the fascinating history behind these iconic ammunition manufacturers.</p>
<h3>Casings, Primers, Bullets &amp; Shotgun Projectiles</h3>
<p>We go beyond offering you the <a href="https://ammo.com/ammo-on-sale" target="_self" title="Ammo on sale now">best deals on ammunition</a> – we want our customers to understand the anatomy of ammo. To do this, we must begin with casings. Available in <a href="https://ammo.com/casing-type/aluminum" target="_self" title="what is aluminum-cased ammo">aluminum</a>, <a href="https://ammo.com/casing-type/brass" target="_self" title="what is brass-cased ammo">brass</a>, <a href="https://ammo.com/casing-type/nickel-plated-brass" target="_self" title="what is nickel-plated ammo">nickel</a>, <a href="https://ammo.com/casing-type/steel" target="_self" title="what is steel-cased ammo">steel</a> and mixed varieties, casings are the ammunition "packaging" and can determine cost, weight, shelf life, and reloading capabilities.</p>
<p>Inside these casings are primers, which is what gets struck by the firing pin, creates a flash and ignites the powder. They are available in <a href="https://ammo.com/primer-type/boxer" target="_self" title="what is boxer-primed ammo">Boxer</a>, <a href="https://ammo.com/primer-type/berdan" target="_self" title="what is berdan-primed ammo">Berdan</a> and <a href="https://ammo.com/primer-type/rimfire" target="_self" title="what is rimfire-primed ammo">rimfire</a> – each factors for performance, size and calibers.</p>
<p>Propelled by this ignition is the bullet, which is simply the projectile and can come in dozens of varieties – including <a href="https://ammo.com/bullet-type/hollow-point-hp" target="_self" title="what is hollow point ammo">hollow point</a>, <a href="https://ammo.com/bullet-type/full-metal-jacket-fmj" target="_self" title="what is FMJ ammo">full metal jacket</a>, <a href="https://ammo.com/bullet-type/lead-round-nose-lrn" target="_self" title="what is lead round nose ammo">lead round nose</a>, soft point and more – that determine power, penetration, speed, expansion, safety and many other factors. Shotgun projectiles have similar qualities, but with pellets instead of a single bullet.</p></div> </main>

                        (hit.image_url ? '<div class="thumb"><img src="' + hit.image_url + '" /></div>' : '') +
                        '<div class="info">' +
                        hit._highlightResult.name.value +
                                                '</div>' +
                        '<div class="clearfix"></div>' +
                        '</a>';
                }
            }
            $('search_autocomplete_categories').update(html);

            // Display products
            html = '';
            if (displayProducts && products.length > 0) {
                showNoResults = false;
                html = '<div class="algoliasearch-autocomplete-title">Product Results:</div>';
                for (i = 0; i < products.length; ++i) {
                    var hit = products[i];
                    var productName = hit._highlightResult.name.value;
                    var parts = productName.split('|');
                    if (parts.length > 1) {
                        if (searchQuery.length <= 5 || parts[0].indexOf(searchQuery) !== -1 || parts[0].indexOf('<em>') !== -1) {
                            productName = parts[0] + '<br/><small class="hint">Grouped product. Your query matches one of its variants.</small>';
                        } else {
                            productName = parts[0];
                            var children = [];
                            parts.each(function(name, index){
                                if (index > 0) { // skip the configurable/grouped product name
                                    if (parts[index].indexOf(searchQuery) !== -1 || parts[index].indexOf('<em>') !== -1) {
                                        children.push(parts[index]);
                                        if (children.length >= 3) {
                                            throw $break;
                                        }
                                    }
                                }
                            });
                            if (children.length) {
                                productName += '<br/><small class="hint">options: ' + children.join(' | ') + '</small>';
                            }
                        }
                    }
                    html += '<a class="algoliasearch-autocomplete-hit" href="' + hit.url + '">' +
                        (hit.thumbnail_url ? '<div class="thumb"><img src="' + hit.thumbnail_url + '" /></div>' : '') +
                        '<div class="info">' +
                        productName +
                        (hit.categories && hit.categories.length > 0 ? '<div class="algoliasearch-autocomplete-category">in ' + hit.categories.join(', ') + '</div>' : '') +
                        '</div>' +
                        '<div class="clearfix"></div>' +
                        '</a>';
                }
                if (products.length > 0 && (allProducts = nbHits - categories.length) > 0) {
                    html += '<div class="algoliasearch-autocomplete-all"><a><span>'+'View All %d Product Results'.replace('%d', allProducts.toString())+'</span></a></div>';
                }
            }
            $('search_autocomplete_products').update(html);

            if($$('.section-header ~ .algoliasearch-backdrop').length == 0) {
                $$('.section-header').first().insert({after:'<div class="algoliasearch-backdrop" />'});
            }

            if(showNoResults) {
                html = '<div class="algoliasearch-autocomplete-title">Search Results:</div>';
                html += '<div class="algoliasearch-autocomplete-empty">No results.</div>';
                $('search_autocomplete_products').update(html);
            }

            if (products.length > 0) {
                $('search_autocomplete_products').select('.algoliasearch-autocomplete-all a').first().observe('click', function(e){
                    e.stop();
                    $('search_mini_form').submit();
                });
            }

            var savedQuery = Mage.Cookies.get('lastAutocompleteSearchQuery');
            if(savedQuery != searchQuery) {
                Mage.Cookies.set('lastAutocompleteSearchQuery', searchQuery);
                Event.fire(document, 'searchAutocomplete:change', {action: 'SearchQuery', label: searchQuery, value: null});
            }

            if(searchQuery){
                $('search_autocomplete').select('a').invoke('observe', 'click', function(e){
                    Mage.Cookies.set('lastSearchQuery', searchQuery);
                });
            }
        },
        clearResults: function() {
            setTimeout(function(){
                $('search_autocomplete').hide();
                $('search_autocomplete_categories').update('');
                $('search_autocomplete_products').update('');
                $('search_autocomplete_pages').update('');
                $$('.section-header ~ .algoliasearch-backdrop').invoke('remove');
            }, 500);
        }
    });
    $('search').observe('keydown', function(e) {
        if (e.keyCode == 13) { // Enter
            var label = $('search').getValue();
            Event.fire(document, 'search:action', {action: 'Submit simple search', label: label, value: null});

            var element = Event.element(e);
            if (element.value != '' && element.tagName == 'INPUT' && $$('.algoliasearch-autocomplete-hit.marked').length == 0) {
                e.stop();
                $('search_mini_form').submit();
            }
        }
