<div class="titlefont font-2 fontshadow white bold">Never Get Lost in The Woods Again!</div>

<h1 class="white larger normal margin-bottom-25">
            Trailforks is a trail database, map &amp; management system for users, builders and associations.
        </h1>
<p class="white larger" style="max-width: 650px">
            A platform for trail associations to keep track of trail conditions, builders to log work &amp; users to discover, plan and share their activities.
        </p>
<br/>
<span class="button1 yellow large clickable" onclick="scroll_to_anchor('chooseactivity')">Choose Your Activity <span class="openarrow"></span></span>
</div>
</div>
<div class="section wrapper center">
<div class="titlefont font-2 green bold margin-bottom-25">
        440,043 km of 206,169 trails in 109 countries    </div>
<p class="large grey">
        Trailforks is dedicated to giving you the best interactive trail maps, custom designed for your selected activity.
    </p>
<p class="large grey">
        Topographic layer, points of interest, trail popularity, heatmaps, routes, trail conditions, Strava segments, photos, videos and so much more. Our Map empowers you with the info you need to navigate trail networks, so you won't be scared of getting lost.
    </p>
<p class="large grey">
        Trailforks is world-wide with trails mapped in over 100 countries. Track your rides to contribute trail usage data back to the biking community.
    </p>
