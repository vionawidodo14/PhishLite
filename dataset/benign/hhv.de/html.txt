<h2>Our roots: Hip Hop and vinyl</h2>
<p>The story of <span class="caps">HHV</span> starts at the end of the 1990s – at a time where the rare vinyl LP was not just a mouse click away. Hip hop music from the <span class="caps">USA</span>, especially that outside the mainstream, was difficult to get hold of in this country. At the time you could hardly buy it on vinyl – let alone for a fair price. The idea of changing this, in 2002, was the foundation for what is today <span class="caps">HHV</span>.</p>
<h2>Record store turns into online vinyl shop</h2>
<p>What started as a small record store in Berlin-Friedrichshain, soon became an online shop. Musically it did not just stop at <a href="/shop/en/hip-hop/p:n5G3mH">hip hop</a> and rap. <h2>Vinyl records from our own label</h2>
<p>Since then, the selection criteria have no longer been based on a particular style, but increasingly on quality. The latter is also the basic motive of the releases from our home label <span class="caps">HHV</span>, which was formed in the course of our gradual further development. It enables us, alongside our label <a href="/shop/en/project-mooncircle-hhv-vinyl-cd-tape/p:7d2Amh">Project: Mooncircle</a>, to work closely with musicians and label operators and create releases according to our own taste.</p>
<h2>Growth beyond the vinyl shop</h2>
<p>But it is not only the music section which has experienced organic growth these past years. The whole range at <span class="caps">HHV</span> has gradually grown.
