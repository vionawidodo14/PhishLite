<h1>Welcome to Horaro</h1>
<p>This tool allows you to create schedules (specifically tailored towards live stream marathons).
			It's free (as in free beer) and available both as a hosted service and as an
			<a href="https://github.com/sgt-kabukiman/horaro" target="_blank">open-source application</a>.</p>
<p class="h-small">
<a href="/-/oauth/start?provider=twitch">
<img alt="Connect with Twitch" src="/assets/images/twitch-connect.949eaaeb.png"/>
</a>
 to create your own schedules. Your
					e-mail address and password are never revealed to Horaro.
				</p>
<p>
<small>…or instead <a href="/-/register">create a classic account</a> without
					Twitch, if you like.</small>
</p>

<h3>Some Features</h3>
</div>
</div>
<div class="row">
<div class="col-lg-3 col-md-3 col-sm-4 col-xs-6 text-center">
<h4>Responsive</h4>
<p><img alt="Screenshot showing a schedule on a small screen" class="img-thumbnail" src="/assets/images/feature-responsive.085c2a03.png"/></p>
<p><small>Schedules work well even on tablets and phones.</small></p>
</div>
<div class="col-lg-3 col-md-3 col-sm-4 col-xs-6 text-center">
<h4>Always Up-to-Date</h4>
<p><img alt="Screenshot showing the current/next item in a schedule" class="img-thumbnail" src="/assets/images/feature-up-to-date.d0c5b00b.png"/></p>
<p><small>See immediately what's on right now and what's next to come.</small></p>
</div>
<div class="col-lg-3 col-md-3 col-sm-4 col-xs-6 text-center">
<h4>Open Data</h4>
<p><img alt="Screenshot showing the JSON export of a schedule" class="img-thumbnail" src="/assets/images/feature-exports.8406c3da.png"/></p>
<p><small>Access all schedules as JSON(P), XML or CSV.</small></p>
</div>
<div class="col-lg-3 col-md-3 col-sm-4 col-xs-6 text-center">
<h4>Flexible</h4>
<p><img alt="Screenshot showing the JSON export of a schedule" class="img-thumbnail" src="/assets/images/feature-flexible.6efadb5d.png"/></p>
<p><small>You can control the schedule columns as you see fit.</small></p>
</div>
<div class="col-lg-3 col-md-3 col-sm-4 col-xs-6 text-center">
<h4>Keep Your Privacy</h4>
<p><img alt="" class="img-thumbnail" src="/assets/images/feature-privacy.221a5fe5.png"/></p>
<p><small>No Facebook, no Twitter, no Google+, no Adsense, no nothing.</small></p>
</div>
<div class="col-lg-3 col-md-3 col-sm-4 col-xs-6 text-center">
<h4>Themes</h4>
<p><img alt="Screenshot of a schedule using the Amelia theme" class="img-thumbnail" src="/assets/images/feature-themes.531dab3f.png"/></p>
<p><small>Select from a number of different themes for your schedules.</small></p>
</div>
<div class="col-lg-3 col-md-3 col-sm-4 col-xs-6 text-center">
<h4>Private Schedules</h4>
<p><img alt="" class="img-thumbnail" src="/assets/images/feature-private.71f3f475.png"/></p>
<p><small>Mark schedules as private and only share them with people you want to.</small></p>
</div>
<div class="col-lg-3 col-md-3 col-sm-4 col-xs-6 text-center">
<h4>iCal Feeds</h4>
<p><img alt="Screenshot showing the default Android calendar showing imported schedule items" class="img-thumbnail" src="/assets/images/feature-ical.79f01c64.png"/></p>
<p><small>Subscribe to a schedule to get all dates constantly updated in your personal calendar.</small></p>
</div>
