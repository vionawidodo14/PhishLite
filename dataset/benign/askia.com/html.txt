<h2 class="section-heading">Collect data with askiafield, our control centre</h2>
<p class="lead" id="intro-field">A reliable and scalable platform for collecting data across a wide range of modes and a control centre which allows you to monitor all survey projects in a single environment.</p>
</div>
</div>
<div class="row">
<div class="col-md-1"></div>
<div class="col-md-10">
<div class="col-md-4 subFeatureBlock">
<div id="integrated-management"></div>
<p class="feature-title">Integrated management</p>
<p>Monitor your telephone, web, face to face &amp; mobile surveys from a single environment. Askiafield provides complete visibility and control over sample &amp; quotas.
                  <br/>Easily review progress and carry out performance checks on agents. You can also assign sample files, link to external databases and run a range of reports.</p>
</div>
<div class="col-md-4 subFeatureBlock">
<div id="boost-performance"></div>
<p class="feature-title">Boost performance</p>
<p>Askiafield is fully integrated with <a href="https://myforce.be/mrx/cati/" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank" title="CTArchitect dialler - The perfect addition for your Askia CATI environment">CTArchitect</a>, our dialler solution.
                  It provides Preview, Progressive and Predictive dialling.
                  <br/>Furthermore, a special agent-based predictive dialling algorithm - called Smart Research Dialling - has been developed specifically for market research.</p>
</div>
<div class="col-md-4">
<div id="scalable"></div>
<p class="feature-title">Scalable</p>
<p>Askiavoice enables you to easily deploy in-house or remote agents and supervisors for your telephone surveys or just monitor survey progress in real-time, from anywhere.
                  <br/>Askiaweb rests on a Load-Balanced architecture that allows you to deliver thousands of simultaneous connections.</p>
</div>
</div>

                  share fieldwork progress.
                  <br/>Also our latest askiafield portal allows you to fully manage your fieldwork online. You can monitor (see-in and listen-in) your agents via your browser as well as reporting on the progress of surveys in field.</p>

<div class="col-md-4 subFeatureBlock">
<div id="reliable"></div>
<p class="feature-title">Reliable</p>
<p>We’ve created a reliable &amp; scalable platform which monitors all survey projects in a single environment. Agents won’t be left in the lurch if you upload a new version of the survey, change quotas or add more sample. Even if the network
