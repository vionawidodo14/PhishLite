<h1>Student Universal Support Ireland (SUSI)</h1>
<p>Welcome to the SUSI website where you can find information on making a student grant application. Before applying for your grant, we would encourage you to review the eligibility criteria by selecting your student type based on whether you are <a href="/?page_id=36">PLC</a>, <a href="/?page_id=38">Undergraduate</a>, <a href="/?page_id=40">Postgraduate</a> or <a href="/?page_id=42">Studying Outside the State</a>.</p>
<p>You can also use our <a href="/eligibility-reckoner-app-irish/index.html" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Eligibility Reckoner </a> where you can quickly and easily self-assess whether you may be eligible for a grant.</p>
<p>Links to the student grant legislation and a step-by-step guide to help with filling in an application can be found in our <a href="/?page_id=19">Resources</a> page.</p>
