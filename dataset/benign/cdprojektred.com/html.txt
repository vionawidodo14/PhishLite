<header class="entry-header">
<h2 class="entry-title"><a href="https://en.cdprojektred.com/our-history/" rel="bookmark">Our History</a></h2>
<div class="entry-summary">
<p>Established in 2002, located in Warsaw (HQ), Kraków and Wrocław, Poland, CD PROJEKT RED was born out of raw passion for video games.</p>
</div>
</header>
<div class="entry-content">
<p dir="ltr" style="color: #222222;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">The studio’s founders: Michał Kiciński and Marcin Iwiński, both pioneers in video game distribution in Poland in the 90s, decided to employ their gaming industry experience in video game development. In 2007, The Witcher was born.</span></p>
<p dir="ltr" style="color: #222222;">
</p></div>

<a class="more-link" href="https://en.cdprojektred.com/our-history/">Read More</a>
</div>

<header class="entry-header">
<h2 class="entry-title"><a href="https://en.cdprojektred.com/our-mission/" rel="bookmark">Our Mission</a></h2>
<div class="entry-summary">
<p>There is an inner fire, fueled in every member of the studio. It comes from what we surround ourselves with, from the games we play, from where we live and who we are accompanied by.</p>
</div>
</header>
<div class="entry-content">
<p dir="ltr"><span style="font-weight: 400;">We want to hone that fire and make it grow. It’s what makes the games we create so unique. As a studio, we have one mission: making the best video games on the planet.</span></p>
<p dir="ltr"><span style="font-weight: 400;">We want to tell breathtaking, mature stories to global audiences, without losing the attention to detail that has made us who we are today.</span></p>
<p dir="ltr" style="color: #222222;">
</p></div>
<div class="entry-controls">
<a class="more-link" href="https://en.cdprojektred.com/our-mission/">Read More</a>
</div>

<header class="entry-header">
<h2 class="entry-title"><a href="https://en.cdprojektred.com/meet-the-rebels/" rel="bookmark">Meet the rebels</a></h2>
<div class="entry-summary">
<p>The studio is home to well over 550 game-making professionals from all over the globe. We speak many different languages but share one love — games.</p>
</div>
</header>
<div class="entry-content">
<p dir="ltr" style="color: #222222; text-align: justify;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Whether it’s through the unique atmosphere of the work environment we create or the fresh approach to the projects we’re involved in, CD PROJEKT RED is a place that escapes the confines of corporate-style game development. Thanks to this approach, we offer you what others cannot — an opportunity to make games you’d like to play and the freedom to express yourself by creating what you love.</span></p>
<p dir="ltr" style="color: #222222; text-align: justify;">
