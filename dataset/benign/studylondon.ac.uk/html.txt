<h2>London: The Best Place to Study</h2>
<p>Nearly 50 <a href="/universities/index">universities in London</a>, including some of the best universities in the world, offer over <a href="/courses/index">10,000 courses</a> for students from around the world. </p>
<p>Study in London for a <a href="/application-advice/education-in-the-uk">UK degree</a> or enrol on a <a href="/application-advice/faqs/london-summer-school">Summer School course</a> or <a href="/application-advice/faqs/study-abroad-programs-london">Study Abroad Programme</a>. </p>
<p>Join more than 100,000 international students who already study abroad at one of the fully accredited universities in London. Enjoy life in a <a href="/why-study-in-london/welcoming">welcoming</a> city to boost your <a href="/why-study-in-london/work-opportunities">employment</a> prospects with an internationally recognised London university course.</p>
<p>Find advice on university entry requirements, <a href="/application-advice/uk-student-visa-information">student visas</a>, London university <a href="/application-advice/tuition-fees-at-london-universities">tuition fees</a>, <a href="/application-advice/london-university-scholarships">scholarships</a> and <a href="/application-advice/cost-of-studying-in-london">living costs</a> and <a class="register-link" href="/register">create a student profile</a> that you can send to the universities.</p>
<p><a class="register-link" href="/register">Register now</a> to start your journey to studying in London.</p>
