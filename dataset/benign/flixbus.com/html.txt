<h2></h2>
<h2>Benefit from comfortable, low-cost European bus travel</h2>
<p>Jump on board one of our green buses and travel throughout Europe. Choose your route from our extensive network; with over <a href="https://www.flixbus.com/bus-routes">350,000 daily connections</a> to over 2,000 destinations in 28 European countries, you really can explore Europe!</p>
<p>Planning a backpacking trip in Europe? Want to go on a last minute city break? Or a holiday with your family or friends? No problem at all! We'll take you from A to B at an unbeatable price. With our bus journeys you can choose from <a href="/bus/france">France</a>, <a href="/bus/netherlands">the Netherlands</a>, <a href="/bus/switzerland">Switzerland</a>, <a href="/bus/germany">Germany</a>, <a href="/bus/italy">Italy</a>, <a href="/bus/czechia">Czech Republic</a>, <a href="/bus/hungary">Hungary</a>, <a href="/bus/croatia">Croatia </a>and many more countries! Not to mention <a href="/bus/sweden">Sweden</a>, <a href="/bus/denmark">Denmark</a>, <a href="/bus/luxembourg">Luxembourg</a>, <a href="/bus/slovenia">Slovenia </a>and <a href="/bus/austria">Austria </a>– FlixBus provides the travel you want, when you want it!</p>
<p>Getting you from A to B stress free: thanks to real-time bus stop information, up-to-date and current bus schedules, helpful staff and friendly on-site bus drivers, you don't need to worry about a thing. You can plan your bus trip and jump on board feeling completely relaxed.</p>
<h2>Your FlixBus Service</h2>
<p>Our aim is to offer you convenient, low cost and straightforward bus service. If you have any questions during the planning of your journey, take a look at our <a href="https://global.flixbus.com/service/faq" target="_self">FAQs</a>. Should you still need further assistance, our friendly customer service team will gladly help with any problems or uncertainties you may have!</p>
<p>Buy your low cost <a href="https://shop.global.flixbus.com/" target="_self">bus tickets online</a>, via our <a href="https://global.flixbus.com/service/bus-app" target="_self">bus app</a>, in one of our FlixBus stores or directly from the bus driver. Your ticket is your guarantee to a comfortable seat on board. Snacks &amp; drinks are also available from our friendly bus drivers along with free Wi-Fi and plug sockets - on each one of our buses! So you can surf the web, watch movies and stay connected for the duration of your journey.</p>
<p>Save with FlixBus! Save money with our unbeatably cheap bus tickets, save time with our detailed bus schedules and punctual buses and save the environment, as bus travel is the most environmentally friendly means of transport. So whether you're traveling with FlixBus to <a href="https://global.flixbus.com/bus/berlin" target="_self">Berlin</a>, <a href="https://global.flixbus.com/bus/prague" target="_self">Prague</a>, <a href="https://global.flixbus.com/bus/milan" target="_self">Milan</a>, <a href="https://global.flixbus.com/bus/paris" target="_self">Paris</a> or <a href="https://global.flixbus.com/bus/frankfurt" target="_self">Frankfurt</a>, you'll always travel green!</p>
<p>Get exclusive offers, exciting competition updates and the latest announcements by signing up to our <a href="https://global.flixbus.com/service/newsletter" target="_self">newsletter</a> and liking our <a href="https://www.facebook.com/FlixBus">Facebook</a> page. You'll be the first to know about any FlixBus news.</p>
