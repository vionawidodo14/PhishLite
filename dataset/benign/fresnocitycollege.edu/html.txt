<h3>Community Partnerships</h3>
<div class="flex mobilespotlight">
<p>We would like to thank our community partners for all the work done over the past few months. The dedication and commitment has resulted in the Transformative Climate Community (TCC) proposal for the Fresno City College West Fresno Satellite becoming one step closer to reality.</p>

</div>

<div>
<h3>Central Valley Promise</h3>
<div class="flex mobilespotlight">
<p>The Central Valley Promise is a community effort to guarantee a path to college for students in our region. The Promise will ensure that if students and their parents pledge to do what it takes in middle and high school to prepare, college will be there for them in the future, guaranteed.</p>
<img alt="graduate" src="uploaded-files/images/common/link-thumbnails/2019-spring-class-schedule-link-thumbnail.jpg"/>
</div>
</div>
</a>
</div><div aria-describedby="slick-slide02" aria-hidden="true" class="swiper-slide slick-slide" data-slick-index="2" role="option" style="width: 680px;" tabindex="-1">
<a href="campus-life/ram-pantry/index.html" tabindex="-1">
<div>
<h3>Ram Pantry</h3>
<div class="flex mobilespotlight">
<p>Ram Pantry has extended their hours. The Ram Pantry is a free food assistance program for college students currently enrolled at Fresno City College with an Associated Student Body (ASB) card. The Ram Pantry believes that food insecurity and hunger should not be a barrier to success for students. The goal is to increase the graduation and course completion rate of our students. </p>
<img alt="free food" src="_files/images/thumbs/ram-pantry-homepage-slider-thumb1.jpg"/>
</div>
</div>
</a>
</div><div aria-describedby="slick-slide03" aria-hidden="true" class="swiper-slide slick-slide" data-slick-index="3" role="option" style="width: 680px;" tabindex="-1">
<a href="academics/career-and-technical-education/fire-technology/index.html" tabindex="-1">
<div>
<h3>Fire Technology</h3>
<div class="flex mobilespotlight">
<p>The Basic Fire Academy program prepares students with the knowledge and skills necessary to become a State of California Certified Firefighter 1.  The curriculum is designed to provide the extensive hands-on training required by many fire agencies for entry-level employment as a firefighter.</p>
<img alt="Fire Fighters" src="uploaded-files/images/academics/fire-spotlight.jpg"/>
</div>
</div>
</a>
</div><div aria-describedby="slick-slide04" aria-hidden="true" class="swiper-slide slick-slide" data-slick-index="4" role="option" style="width: 680px;" tabindex="-1">

<div>
<h3>Theatre - Music - Dance</h3>
<div class="flex mobilespotlight">
<p>The Performing Arts at Fresno City College are vibrant and alive.  Check out the latest events!  Something is always happening.</p>
<img alt="sing" src="_files/images/news-singers.png"/>
</div>
</div>
</a>

<div>
<h3>Culinary Arts</h3>
<div class="flex mobilespotlight">
<p>Want to be a chef?  Start your career in under 18 months.  Three levels of certification.  Learn everything you need to know to work in the food industry.</p>
<img alt="Vote Spot" src="_files/images/spotlights/culinary---spotlight-topic.jpg"/>
</div>
</div>
</a>
