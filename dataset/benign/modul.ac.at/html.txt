<p class="bodytext" style="font-size: 16px;"><span style="font-weight: bold;">Modul University Vienna</span> is widely considered as <span style="font-weight: bold;">Austria's leading international private university</span> and is owned by the Vienna Chamber of Commerce and Industry, the largest provider of private education in Austria.
