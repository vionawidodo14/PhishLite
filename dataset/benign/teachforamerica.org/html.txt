<div class="m-featured-content-top__text">

Lasting change takes bold, grounded leaders working together, inside and outside of schools, fighting for the aspirations of children and their families. </p>

<h2 class="m-featured-content-bottom__title">Our Approach</h2>
<div class="m-featured-content-bottom__text">
<p>Teach For America finds outstanding leaders who commit to expanding educational opportunity, beginning with at least two years teaching in an under-resourced public school. Working shoulder-to-shoulder with students, educators, and community members, corps members go beyond traditional expectations to support the academic and personal growth of their students. The impact corps members have in the classroom fuels a lifelong commitment to their students and shapes the trajectory of their lives and careers.</p>
