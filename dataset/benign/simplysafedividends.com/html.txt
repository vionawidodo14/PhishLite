<h2 class="centered"><strong>Our service is different by design.</strong></h2>
<p class="differences__intro centered push-double--ends">It's the Wild West these days in the online finance industry — anyone can launch a service and promise the world. Here's why Simply Safe Dividends stands out:</p>
<ol class="differences__list">
<li class="differences__list-item"><strong>All of our research is published by <a class="decorated" href="/about">Brian Bollinger</a>, a CPA and former analyst at an investment fund.</strong> We don't outsource research, customer support, or anything else. Everything is done in-house so you can trust the work.</li>
<li class="differences__list-item"><strong>We don't sell ads and have no incentive to write sensational headlines to attract eyeballs.</strong> 100% of our revenue is from subscriptions, which aligns our incentives with those of our customers.</li>
<li class="differences__list-item"><strong>We don't promote frequent trading to justify the value of our service.</strong> Everything we do revolves around helping investors stick to a plan when faced with uncertainty. Often, that means making people feel comfortable doing nothing.</li>
<li class="differences__list-item"><strong>We'll never publish a "10 hot stocks to buy right now" list or anything like that.</strong> We don't ask people to blindly follow any recommendations. Our M.O. is to give people the tools they need to make informed, confident decisions.</li>
<li class="differences__list-item"><strong>We set the standard for transparency in this industry.</strong> We publish a <a class="decorated" href="/dividend-safety-scores">realtime track record</a> of our Dividend Safety Scores and performance metrics for our portfolios (<a class="decorated" href="https://1.simplysafedividends.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/07/Intelligent-Income-July-2019.pdf#page=7">see a sample newsletter</a>). No backtesting bias, just real results.</li>
<li class="differences__list-item"><strong>We have over 2,000 customers and have grown <em>every</em> year without <em>any</em> active marketing.</strong> No paid ads, no gimmicky promotions, no stuffing our content all over the web. We focus all of our energy on improving our service, and our business grows primarily through word-of-mouth.</li>
