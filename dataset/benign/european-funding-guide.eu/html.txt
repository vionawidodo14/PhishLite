<h2 class="block-title"><span>5 Myths</span> about scholarships and grants and in the EU</h2>
<ul><li><strong class="intro-number">1</strong><div class="item-text"><strong>All scholarships and grants are awarded on the basis of merit or need. </strong><p>This is not true! There is a wide variety of selection criteria. This can be anything from your parents’ jobs, to where you live. </p></div></li>
<li><strong class="intro-number">2</strong><div class="item-text"><strong>There are only a few institutions that offer financial aid.  </strong><p>Wrong! There are more than 12,000 scholarships, grants, loans and other funding options in the EU worth a total value of more than 27 billion Euros.</p></div></li>
<li><strong class="intro-number">3</strong><div class="item-text"><strong>You have to be in your first semester to get support. </strong><p>Also wrong! There are many programmes targeting established students. Financial aid is available twelve months of the year and if you continue to look, you’ll find more opportunities. </p></div></li>
<li><strong class="intro-number">4</strong><div class="item-text"><strong>Too many people apply for scholarships! </strong><p>This is not true either! In fact over two-thirds of students never apply for any type of scholarship. </p></div></li>
<li><strong class="intro-number">5</strong><div class="item-text"><strong>Finding the right programme and applying for it takes forever. </strong><p>Not anymore! European Funding Guide will help you find financial assistance that matches your profile with just a few clicks. Often all you only need is a covering letter outlining your motivation, and a CV to apply. </p></div></li>
</ul>
</section>
