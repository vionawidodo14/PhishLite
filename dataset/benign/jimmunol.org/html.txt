<h2 class="pane-title">About The Journal of Immunology</h2>
<div class="pane-content">
<div class="snippet jnl-jimmunol-about-journal-snippet" id="jnl-jimmunol-about-journal-snippet">
<div class="snippet-content">
<p><em>The Journal of Immunology (The JI)</em> publishes novel, peer-reviewed findings in all areas of experimental immunology, including innate and adaptive immunity, inflammation, host defense, clinical immunology, autoimmunity and more. <em>The JI</em> is published by <a href="http://www.aai.org/">The American Association of Immunologists (AAI)</a>.</p>
<p><em>The JI</em> thanks the <a href="/sites/default/files/additional-assets/PDFs/AdHocReviewers_2017.pdf">scientists</a> who reviewed manuscripts in the past year. </p>
