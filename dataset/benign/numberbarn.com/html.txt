<div class="title">Frequently Asked Questions</div>
<div><div class="half-col">
<h4>What does "port my number" mean?</h4>
<p class="faq-a">"Porting" simply means transferring your existing landline or cell phone number from one telephone service provider to another. </p>
<h4>Who owns my phone number?</h4>
<p class="faq-a">You own it! We simply take care of it for you. We "port in" your number from your current provider and maintain it. Under the <a href="http://www.fcc.gov/guides/portability-keeping-your-phone-number-when-changing-service-providers" target="_blank">FCC’s “local number portability” (LNP) rules</a>, you are legally able to keep your number when you move or change phone companies.</p>
<h4>Do I have to purchase a number in my local area code?</h4>
<p class="faq-a">You can purchase a new number from any of the <a href="/search">area codes we offer</a>.</p>
<h4>Can I store more than one number with NumberBarn?</h4>
<p class="faq-a">You can port and/or purchase as many phone numbers as you want and manage them all within a single NumberBarn account.</p>
<h4>What if I decide to cancel?</h4>
<p class="faq-a">If you no longer wish to use NumberBarn, you can cancel at any time.</p>
</div><div class="half-col">
<h4>What is "park" my number and why would I do it?</h4>
<p class="faq-a">Parking your number means storing it with an active service so you can maintain ownership of the number. You can activate it when you need it and not have to give out a new phone number.</p>
<h4>How does call forwarding work?</h4>
<p class="faq-a">We forward all incoming calls to your NumberBarn number to the active phone number you enter in your control panel.</p>
<h4>Do I need an internet connection for my NumberBarn number to work?</h4>
<p class="faq-a">No. We make it ring, you just answer it.</p>
<h4>What are the extra fees and taxes?</h4>
<p class="faq-a">Call forwarding plans are subject to the <a href="https://www.fcc.gov/general/universal-service" target="fcc">Federal Universal Service Fund (FUSF)</a> fee imposed by the FCC.</p>
<h4>What happens when someone calls my parked NumberBarn number?</h4>
<p class="faq-a">Any callers who dial your parked number will hear a short, pre-recorded message telling them the number is parked with NumberBarn. To hear this message, call 844-586-5686</p>
</div></div>
