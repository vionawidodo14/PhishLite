<h2 class="title"><a href="/koala_dumbest_animal">Why koalas are the world’s dumbest animals</a></h2>
<span class="rdf-meta element-hidden" content="Why koalas are the world’s dumbest animals" property="dc:title"></span><span class="rdf-meta element-hidden" content="0" datatype="xsd:integer" property="sioc:num_replies"></span>
