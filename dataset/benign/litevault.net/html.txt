<div class="col-sm-5 col-sm-offset-1 info_box">
<h3>What is LiteVault?</h3>
<p>
                        Litevault is an online wallet service which uses in-browser cryptography to reduce the risk of the coin
                        operator running with the funds. This same system is used by Blockchain.info - a well known online
                        Bitcoin wallet (UNAFFILIATED WITH LITEVAULT).
                    </p>
</div>
<div class="col-sm-5 info_box">
<h3>How does it work?</h3>
<p>
                        When you login to LiteVault, your browser sends your identifier to our server,
                        we return an encrypted version of your wallet with the AES algorithm,
                        your browser then decrypts this using your password (which is never sent to the server)
                        to load your private keys used to sign transactions.
                    </p>
</div>
</div>
<div class="row index_faq">
<div class="col-sm-5 col-sm-offset-1 info_box">
<h3>Who made LiteVault?</h3>
<p>
                        Litevault was made by <a href="http://someguy123.com">Someguy123</a>:<br/>
                        "I am a trusted website developer within the Litecoin community responsible for various services including the <a href="http://explorer.litecoin.net">Litecoin Block Explorer</a>, and administrating various official Litecoin resources such as the <a href="https://litecoin.info">Wiki</a> and IRC.
                        <br/><br/>I feel Litecoin hasn’t had enough backing or support, so this is my contribution."
                    </p>
</div>
<div class="col-sm-5 info_box">
<h3>What technology was used?</h3>
<p>
                        The backend was built in Node.JS, For the frontend Bootstrap was used (and completely re-styled) for the HTML and CSS to ensure a good, responsive layout was possible,
                        allowing the website to work well on mobiles, tablets and computers.
                    </p>
</div>
