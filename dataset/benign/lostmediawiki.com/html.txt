<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Victoriaville_vs._Hull_QMJHL_5OT_Game_.28lost_footage_of_Canadian_ice_hockey_game.3B_1999.29"></span><a href="/Victoriaville_vs._Hull_QMJHL_5OT_Game_(lost_footage_of_Canadian_ice_hockey_game;_1999)" title="Victoriaville vs. Hull QMJHL 5OT Game (lost footage of Canadian ice hockey game; 1999)"></a><a href="/Victoriaville_vs._Hull_QMJHL_5OT_Game_(lost_footage_of_Canadian_ice_hockey_game;_1999)" title="Victoriaville vs. Hull QMJHL 5OT Game (lost footage of Canadian ice hockey game; 1999)">Victoriaville vs. Hull QMJHL 5OT Game (lost footage of Canadian ice hockey game; 1999)</a></h3>
<div class="spotlight-description description">
<div class="title"><span style="color:#777; font-style:italic">« Brock Boucher player card.</span></div>
<p>On March 19th, 1999, the Victoriaville Tigres and the Hull Olympiques faced each other in a <b>QMJHL division quarterfinals game</b>, which was the opening round of a playoff tournament for the QMJHL at the time, a league of major junior teams in Quebec and the Maritimes., This game is known by hockey fans in Gatineau (which was formed when Hull amalgamated with surrounding communities in 2002) due to its length. The game lasted 146:31 (or 86:31 of overtime), which made it the longest game in not only QMJHL history, but in CHL history.
</p>

<div class="spotlight-description description">
<div class="title"><span style="color:#777; font-style:italic">« The cast of the show in a promotional photo.</span></div>
<p><i><b>Executive Suite</b></i> is a soap opera co-produced by Norman Felton and Stanley Rubin in 1976 that aired on CBS Monday nights during prime time., The show is loosely based on the 1954 film of the same name produced by MGM, which in turn was based on the same-name novel published by Cameron Hawley two years prior.

<div class="spotlight-description description">
<div class="title"><span style="color:#777; font-style:italic">« A screenshot from one of the incomplete ads.</span></div>
<p>H. H. Gregg (stylized as "hhgregg") is an American then-retail chain, now-online store for home appliances and consumer electronics. From 2009 to 2011, the company <b>used a mascot called HH (also referred to as "h.h.") for its TV commercials, radio spots and other media</b>. After H. H. Gregg's revival in 2017 from filing for bankruptcy previously the same year, HH was briefly used again on the company's social media and website, although no new commercials were made using him.

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Zoobe_.28inaccessible_video_messaging_app.2Fwebsite.3B_2011-2017.29"></span><a href="/Zoobe_(inaccessible_video_messaging_app/website;_2011-2017)" title="Zoobe (inaccessible video messaging app/website; 2011-2017)"></a><a href="/Zoobe_(inaccessible_video_messaging_app/website;_2011-2017)" title="Zoobe (inaccessible video messaging app/website; 2011-2017)">Zoobe (inaccessible video messaging app/website; 2011-2017)</a></h3>
<div class="spotlight-description description">
<div class="title"><span style="color:#777; font-style:italic">« The original icon for the app.</span></div>
<p><i><b>Zoobe</b></i> was a video messaging platform created by Zoobe Message Entertainment GmbH, founded by German filmmaker/animator Lenard Fritz Krawinkel. The platform had a web-based and iOS/Android version and was available from October 2011 to April 2017., <i>Zoobe</i> allowed the user to record &gt; 30-second long voice messages, which would then be dictated by a 3D-animated character.
</p>

<div class="spotlight-description description">
<div class="title"><span style="color:#777; font-style:italic">« Adam West's version of <i>Batman</i> and <i>Godzilla</i>.</span></div>
<p><i><b>Batman Meets Godzilla</b></i> is an unproduced comedy film that was a crossover of the 1966 Adam West version of <i>Batman</i> and <i>Godzilla</i>. It was written around the time <i>Batman</i> premiered on ABC, even possibly before that., Two treatments were written for the film before the project was abandoned, but only one is known to have been archived, and the status of <b>the other</b> is unknown.

<div class="spotlight-description description">
<div class="title"><span style="color:#777; font-style:italic">« The title screen of the prototype (April 28th, 1995 build).</span></div>
<p><i><b>Tom vs. Jerry: The Chase is On!</b></i> is the title of an unreleased game for the Super Nintendo Entertainment System, based on the classic MGM cartoon duo of the same name. The game was being developed by Software Creations, the Manchester-based studio known for producing such games as <i>Silver Surfer</i>, <i>Plok!</i>, <i>Solstice</i> and its sequel <i>Equinox</i>, and meant to be published by Hi-Tech Expressions, who owned the video game rights of Tom &amp; Jerry at the time.

<div class="spotlight-description description">
<div class="title"><span style="color:#777; font-style:italic">« The full cast of the show in a group picture.</span></div>
<p><i><b>Sirota's Court</b></i> is a courtroom sitcom that aired from December 6th, 1976 to April 13th, 1977 on NBC. The show was produced by Peter Engel Productions and Universal Television and is notable for including the first same-sex marriage on television in the episode <i>Court Fear</i>, as well as setting the stage for <i>Night Court</i>, another courtroom comedy that came seven years after the show's broadcasting period which shared a similar plot, and spanned 193 episodes through nine seasons in eight years.

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="All_That_Glitters_.28partially_found_television_sitcom.3B_1977.29"></span><a href="/All_That_Glitters_(partially_found_television_sitcom;_1977)" title="All That Glitters (partially found television sitcom; 1977)"></a><a href="/All_That_Glitters_(partially_found_television_sitcom;_1977)" title="All That Glitters (partially found television sitcom; 1977)">All That Glitters (partially found television sitcom; 1977)</a></h3>
<div class="spotlight-description description">
<div class="title"><span style="color:#777; font-style:italic">« The cast of the show in front of the fictional Globatron logo.</span></div>
<p><i><b>All That Glitters</b></i> is a television sitcom that parodies the soap opera format by reversing the traditional gender roles of the characters. The series is notable for having television's first transgender character, as well as spawning Neil Diamond's <i>You Don't Bring Me Flowers</i> which was rejected as the show's theme song in favor of Kenny Rankin's <i>Genesis Revisited</i> and hit the top spot of the Billboard Top 100 the following year.

<div class="spotlight-description description">
<div class="title"><span style="color:#777; font-style:italic">« Naomi Stevens and Joe Sirola as Rose and Tony "Papa" Montefusco, the heads of the family in the series.</span></div>
<p><i><b>The Montefuscos</b></i> is a television sitcom that aired on Thursday nights from September 4th to October 23rd as part of the 1975 NBC "superseason" lineup made to combat the success of rival network CBS., The show follows the New Canaan family of Rose (Naomi Stevens) and Tony "Papa" Montefusco (Joe Sirola), who have their three-generation family come for dinner every Sunday as part of a family tradition.

What's up guys, dycaite here with an exclusive, rare peek at the once-thought extinct <i>notitiam tabula</i>, seen here in its natural habitat for the first time in three months (seriously though, I know it's been a long time since the last one and I both apologise for my lack of updates and thank you for your patience 🙏🙏🙏).
Since this notice board is gunna be such a big one, I've decided to make it a hybrid forum post/notice board post, with the highlights being outlined here on the homepage and the full list of found media being given here via the forums (big thanks to LMW regular <a href="/User:Ryanskip" title="User:Ryanskip">Ryanskip</a> for keeping track for us). Now, onto the highlights of the past few months:
</p>
<ul><li> First up, a rare 2004 pilot trailer for <a href="/JoJo%27s_Bizarre_Adventure:_Phantom_Blood_(partially_found_anime_film_based_on_manga;_2007)" title="JoJo's Bizarre Adventure: Phantom Blood (partially found anime film based on manga; 2007)"><i>JoJo’s Bizarre Adventure: Phantom Blood</i></a> has been found! You can hear all about the newly unearthed clip above, courtesy of LSuperSonicQ’s brand new video on the topic and see the 40 second trailer for yourself (also above - or, for the purists among us, a raw copy <a class="external text" href="https://mega.nz/#F!XdRCHYIC!LcTnli0MlPDi-o0UbTS5Rw" rel="nofollow">can be obtained here</a>)!
