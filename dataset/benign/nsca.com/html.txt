<h5>Understanding and Managing Stress in Collegiate Athletics</h5>
<small>

<em>June 01, 2016</em>

<p>It is important for strength and conditioning coaches, sport coaches, athletic trainers, and administrators to recognize and address the evidence of stress within student-athletes in order to avoid chronic stress-related anxiety and injury.</p>
</div>
</a>
<a class="small-12 medium-6 large-4 cell card-link-color show-for-medium" href="https://www.nsca.com/education/articles/ptq/land-based-strength-and-conditioning-for-swimming/">

<h5>Land-Based Strength and Conditioning for Swimming</h5>
<small>
                                by Chat Williams, MS, CSCS*D, NSCA-CPT*D<br/>
<em>June 01, 2017</em>
</small>
<p>Incorporating land-based strength and conditioning into a training regimen can give a swimmer a competitive edge. As with every sport, there are sport-specific exercises the athlete should perform in order to mimic the specific sport movement they are trying to improve.</p>
