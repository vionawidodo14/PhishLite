<h1 class="mk-fancy-title simple-style color-single" id="fancy-title-17">
<span>
<span style="color: #53565a;">Canada’s leader in English language testing.</span> </span>
</h1>
<div class="clearboth"></div>
<div class="mk-divider divider_full_width center shadow_line" id="divider-18">
<div class="divider-inner"><span class="divider-shadow-left"></span><span class="divider-shadow-right"></span></div>
</div>
<div class="clearboth"></div>
<div class="mk-text-block" id="text-block-19">
<p class="p1"><span class="s1">Paragon Testing Enterprises, a subsidiary of <a href="http://www.ubc.ca/" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">The University of British Columbia (UBC)</a>, was created in 2009 to commercialize the university’s English language proficiency tests.</span></p>
<p class="p1">Paragon’s mission is to make its English language proficiency tests the tests of choice for those wishing to immigrate to Canada, become Canadian citizens, or study in Canada at all levels. To achieve this mission, Paragon conducts ongoing test research in collaboration with internationally recognized scholars at Canada’s leading universities. This test research program ensures the highest quality tests, and enables Paragon to compete with large international testing agencies.</p>
<p class="p1">Paragon currently administers three tests: the <a href="http://www.celpip.ca/" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Canadian English Language Proficiency Index Program (CELPIP) Test</a>, the <a href="http://www.cael.ca/" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Canadian Academic English Language (CAEL)Test</a>, and the <a href="http://www.lpitest.ca/" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Language Proficiency Index (LPI) Test</a>.</p>
