<div class="p-1 col-12 news_content text">This week's poll was suggested by JustaVagabond. Legal digital ways to read manga have increased in the last few years. Even some of the the Japanese publishers are endorsing it. And there's also the explosion of webcomics, especially those of the Chinese variety. Are you satisfied with all these changes? If not, what could be improved?<br/><br/>You can submit poll ideas here<br/><!--Auto_parse_begin--><a href="http://www.mangaupdates.com/showtopic.php?tid=3903" rel="nofollow" target="_blank"><u>http://www.mangaupdates.com/showtopic.php?tid=3903</u></a><!--Auto_parse_end--><br/><br/>Previous Poll Results:<br/>Question: <b>Which of the follow best reflects how you would like scanlators to treat off-bubble SFX?</b><br/>Choices:<br/>SFX are a distraction, even as notes. SFX notes clutter the margins and panels and are a drag on the typesetter and even more on the redrawer. Scanlators should ignore them altogether. - votes: 109 (4.7%)<br/>SFX are essential for scanlation to be true to the original. They should be typeset and redrawn all the time, regardless of how it might affect release pace. - votes: 103 (4.4%)<br/>SFX significantly increase enjoyment of reading.
