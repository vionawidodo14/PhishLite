                </li><li>To enable language learners to experience different countries and immerse themselves in their target

                </li><li>To give projects around the world opportunities to receive support from skilled helpers allowing

                </li><li>To enable travellers to find paid positions to help them to continue to fund their travels.</li><li>To enable like-minded travellers to connect and meet, wherever they are in the world.</li></ul><h2>How Workaway.info works:</h2><p>

                They are looking to welcome travellers and people from different

                paid work opportunities too resulting in a huge range of different fields to
                choose from; Language learning, pet sitting, house sitting, from building
                to babysitting and shopping to shearing, Workaway.info aims to
                introduce culture seekers, travellers and language learners to like
                minded hosts and friends without having to pay expensive agency fees.</p><p>
