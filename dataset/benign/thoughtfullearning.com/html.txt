<h3 class="headingCentered">Award-winning resources offered by Thoughtful Learning</h3>
<ul class="resourceCategoryContainer"><li class="resourceCategoryItem currentResourceItem">Books</li><li class="resourceCategoryItem">Units</li><li class="resourceCategoryItem">Minilessons</li><li class="resourceCategoryItem">Student Models</li><li class="resourceCategoryItem">Writing Topics</li><li class="resourceCategoryItem">Writing Assessment</li><li class="resourceCategoryItem">Infographics</li><li class="resourceCategoryItem">FAQ</li><li class="resourceCategoryItem">Blog</li><li class="resourceCategoryItem">State Standards</li><li class="resourceCategoryItem">Video</li></ul>
<div class="resourcePreviewContainer">
<div class="resourcePreviewDescr" style="display: block;">
<img class="resourceSiloImg_frontPage" src="/sites/k12/files/images/booksResources.png"/>
<h3>Books</h3>
<p>Do you need classroom curricula for teaching English language arts, 21st century skills, or social-emotional learning? Check out these high-quality books created by the team that brought you <em>Writers INC</em> and <em>Write Source</em>!</p>
<p class="frontSiloLink"><a href="/resources/books" title="Books">View a complete list of this resource.</a></p>
</div>
<div class="resourcePreviewDescr" style="display: none;">
<img class="resourceSiloImg_frontPage" src="/sites/k12/files/images/unitResources.png"/>
<h3>Units</h3>
<p>Are you looking for online, interactive curricula for writing and thinking? You’ve come to the right place. Each writing unit leads students step by step through the process of creating a specific form, with models, examples, activities, assignments, videos, and much more! All units are optimized for viewing on any device, from smartphone to whiteboard.</p>
<p class="frontSiloLink"><a href="/resources/units" title="Units">View a complete list of this resource.</a></p>
</div>
<div class="resourcePreviewDescr" style="display: none;">
<img class="resourceSiloImg_frontPage" src="/sites/k12/files/images/minilessonResources.png"/>
<h3>Minilessons</h3>
<p>Do you want quick lessons that teach concepts or skills? Each 10-15 minute minilesson presents a concept and engages students in an activity. Minilessons are optimized to display on your interactive whiteboard, smart phone, or anything in between.</p>
<p class="frontSiloLink"><a href="/resources/minilessons" title="Minilessons">View a complete list of this resource.</a></p>
</div>
<div class="resourcePreviewDescr" style="display: none;">
<img class="resourceSiloImg_frontPage" src="/sites/k12/files/images/studentModelResources.png"/>
<h3>Student Models</h3>
<p>When you need an example written by a student, check out our vast collection of free student models. Scroll through the list, or search for a mode of writing such as “explanatory” or “persuasive.” </p>
<p class="frontSiloLink"><a href="/resources/studentmodels" title="Student Models">View a complete list of this resource.</a></p>
</div>
<div class="resourcePreviewDescr" style="display: none;">
<img class="resourceSiloImg_frontPage" src="/sites/k12/files/images/writingTopicResources.png"/>
<h3>Writing Topics</h3>
<p>Do you want to inspire your students to write great narratives, essays, and reports? Check out these grade-specific writing topics organized by mode (explanatory, creative, and so on). Or search for writing topics that relate to a theme, such as “life” or “animals” or “family.”</p>
<p class="frontSiloLink"><a href="/resources/writingtopics" title="Writing Topics">View a complete list of this resource.</a></p>
</div>
<div class="resourcePreviewDescr" style="display: none;">
<img class="resourceSiloImg_frontPage" src="/sites/k12/files/images/assessmentModelResources.png"/>
<h3>Writing Assessment</h3>
<p>When you want students to understand how writing is graded, turn to our vast selection of assessment examples. You'll find elementary and middle school models in all of the major modes of writing, along with rubrics that assess each example as "Strong," "Good," "Okay," or "Poor."</p>
<p class="frontSiloLink"><a href="/resources/writingassessment" title="Writing Assessment">View a complete list of this resource.</a></p>
</div>
<div class="resourcePreviewDescr" style="display: none;">
<img class="resourceSiloImg_frontPage" src="/sites/k12/files/images/infographicResources.png"/>
<h3>Infographics</h3>
<p>Check out our growing storehouse of free infographics that teach English language arts, 21st century skills, and social-emotional intelligence. Search by content area, theme, or title. </p>
