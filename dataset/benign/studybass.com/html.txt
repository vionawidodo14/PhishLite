<h2>What is StudyBass?</h2>
<ul>
<li><b>FREE</b> bass lessons, tools and resources.</li>
<li>Lessons are based on my tens of thousands of hours teaching hundreds of students over 25 years.</li>
<li>Start from zero and learn step-by-step. Lessons are highly-organized so you don't get lost.</li>
</ul>
</div>
<div class="tab2">
<h2>The StudyBass Approach</h2>
<p>StudyBass is for <b>bass creators</b>—bassists who want to use the bass in a creative, original way.</p>
<p>Anyone can learn to play other peoples' basslines and bass solos. Does learning to recite a poem make you a poet? No! And, that's where StudyBass is different...</p>
<p><b>The goal of StudyBass</b> is to educate you on how music works and how to create your own basslines and solos, not just to parrot those of others.</p>
<h2>Bass—The Misunderstood Instrument</h2>
<p>Bass is a unique instrument. Unfortunately, it is often taught the same way as other instruments causing lots of needless confusion for the student.</p>
<p>Don't waste years of precious practice time learning the wrong things.</p>
<h2>Organized Learning</h2>
<p>Even good lessons are ineffective when presented to the student in the wrong order or at the wrong point in his or her development.</p>
<p>On StudyBass I've organized and paced my bass lessons based on seeing hundreds of students learn to play the bass.</p>
<h2>What Does StudyBass Cover?</h2>
<p>Technique, Harmony, Rhythm, Music Reading, All Styles, Fretboard Note Names, and there's lots more on the way.</p>
</div>

<p>In 2003, I started developing StudyBass—my dream of the best bass lessons on the net.</p>
<p>Everything you see on StudyBass was created by me. I think you'll see my passion and commitment to help you become a great musician and bassist.</p>
<p>I love music. I love the bass. And, I love teaching it to you.</p>

<div class="tab4">
<h2>Getting Started</h2>
<p>First, read over the <a href="/using-the-site/">using the site</a> page. That will help you understand how the site's features work and how I expect you to use it.</p>
<p>Next, go to the bass lesson study guide and get started with the lessons. Even if you feel like you know the contents of a lesson already, go through it anyway. Most of the lessons build on prior lessons. If you skip ahead, you may end up lost.</p>
</div>

<h4>StudyBass' My Practice 2.0!</h4>
<p>I'm excited to announce that the My Practice practice organization tool has been updated. This is an extra feature for <a href="https://www.studybass.com/shop/studybass/">supporters of the site</a>. <p>Currently I am working on the the next lesson block which will cover some deeper concepts involving diatonic chords. You'll need to know these ideas as you begin your journey of analyzing and understanding common chord progressions.</p>
