<p>Occasionally, customers tell us that someone has asked to enter their home, often to "test the water." If this happens to you, <strong>be alert: this is not a Water District employee</strong>.</p>
<p><a href="customer-service/water-service/identify-employees.html">Our employees</a> drive official vehicles, wear photo ID badges and will never ask to enter your home to collect a water sample.</p>
