<h2>Free Classified Ads in Pakistan</h2>
<p>Clasf Pakistan is a website to <strong>publish classified ads completely free of charge</strong> in  Pakistan  , without any limit. You can buy and sell new or second-hand products and get extra money.</p>
</div>
<div class="col-md-6">
<p>Every day hundreds of free ads are published, in which you can add the photos you want and a video to make the ads more attractive.</p>
<p>You decide how long you want your ad to be published for free, as you can have it as long as you want on our bulletin board.</p>
<p>We are one of the most important second-hand advertisement pages in Pakistan, and for this reason we have more than 3 million advertisements.</p>
<p>It is aimed at both individuals and local companies or businesses <strong>that want to advertise for free.</strong></p>
</div>

<ul>
<li>Buy-sell announcements related to the engine (cars, motorcycles, etc).</li>
<li>Sale and purchase announcements related to real estate (rent of houses, sale of flats, garages, etc).</li>
<li>Computer-related sales and purchase announcements (laptops, desktops, etc.).</li>
<li>Sale and purchase advertisements related to fashion (clothing, accessories, etc.).</li>
<li>Buy-sell announcements related to telephony (mobile phones, etc).</li>
<li>Advertisements for the adoption of animals (dogs, cats, etc.).</li>
<li>Advertisements for training and employment (job offers, courses, books, online seminars, etc).</li>
