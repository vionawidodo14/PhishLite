<h1>Au Pair Definition -  Who is an Au Pair?</h1>
<div class="hr"></div>
<div class="homeContentLeft">
<img alt="Au Pair Definition" src="/pageImages/au-pair-wiki-au-pair-definition-2322.jpg"/>

<div class="homeContentRight">
<div>
	An Au Pair is a young person that wants to experience another culture and learn a new language by living in a foreign country with a Host Family. In exchange for a private room and some pocket money, the Au Pair takes care of tasks related to childcare and easy household chores. They are considered temporary "big brothers or sisters" for the Host Children and will hopefully become a new additional member to the Host Family. </div>

<dl class="faqBox" itemprop="mainEntity" itemscope="" itemtype="https://schema.org/Question">
<dt itemprop="name">Can Au Pairs work more than 45 hours?</dt>
<dd itemprop="acceptedAnswer" itemscope="" itemtype="https://schema.org/Answer">
<span itemprop="text">
					No. Every country has its own regulations for how many hours/week the Au Pair is allowed to work, and they correspond to a specific monthly pocket money. In some countries, like the USA, this includes extra babysitting hours. In other Host Countries it depends on the agreement between Au Pair and Host<a href="/en/p-faq.php" style="font-weight:bold;"> » » »</a>
</span>
</dd>
</dl>
<dl class="faqBox" itemprop="mainEntity" itemscope="" itemtype="https://schema.org/Question">
<dt itemprop="name">Do Au Pairs pay taxes?</dt>
<dd itemprop="acceptedAnswer" itemscope="" itemtype="https://schema.org/Answer">
<span itemprop="text">
					It depends on the Host Country. In most countries, Au Pairs do not have to pay taxes, as their income is not high enough to be taxable. But it is important to check with your local authorities, they will be able to give you more specific information on the matter.<a href="/en/p-faq.php" style="font-weight:bold;"> » » »</a>
</span>
</dd>
</dl>
<div class="clearfix"></div> <dl class="faqBox" itemprop="mainEntity" itemscope="" itemtype="https://schema.org/Question">
<dt itemprop="name">How many hours a week can an Au Pair work?</dt>
<dd itemprop="acceptedAnswer" itemscope="" itemtype="https://schema.org/Answer">
<span itemprop="text">
					Every Host Country has different regulations in terms of working hours for the Au Pair. They could go from a maximum of 20 hours/week in some countries to 45 in others. Check the specific Au Pair programs for every Host Country and write down the details in the Au Pair contract.<a href="/en/p-au-pair-salary-pocket-money.php" style="font-weight:bold;"> » » »</a>
</span>
</dd>
</dl>
<dl class="faqBox" itemprop="mainEntity" itemscope="" itemtype="https://schema.org/Question">
<dt itemprop="name">Can you hire an Au Pair without an agency?</dt>
<dd itemprop="acceptedAnswer" itemscope="" itemtype="https://schema.org/Answer">
<span itemprop="text">
					Yes! By using an online matching platform like AuPair.com, you will be able to get in touch with Au Pairs from all over the world and organise the Au Pair stay directly with them. The only exception is the USA and NL, where it is necessary to get in touch with a certified agency in order to get the J1 visa.<a href="/en/p-faq.php" style="font-weight:bold;"> » » »</a>
