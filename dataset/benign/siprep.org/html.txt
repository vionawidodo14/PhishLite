<h4 style="text-align: center;"><strong>Welcome to St. Ignatius College Preparatory</strong></h4>
<p style="text-align: center;">St. Ignatius College Preparatory is more than merely a high school. It is a community of lifelong learners that supports students as they develop into thriving adults. With a commitment to intellectual excellence, leadership, service, and justice, our students strive to respond courageously to the opportunities and challenges of our time. Through a rigorous and integrated program of academic, spiritual, and co-curricular activities, St. Ignatius educates all aspects of our students according to the Jesuit philosophy of <em>Cura Personalis</em>, care for the whole person.</p>
