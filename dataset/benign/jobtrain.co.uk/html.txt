<h3><b>Our Credentials</b></h3>
<p>Today we are proud to be working with such high profile, forward-thinking <a href="https://jobtrain.co.uk/success-stories/">organisations</a> as <a href="https://jobtrain.co.uk/success-stories/isle-of-man-government/">The Isle of Man Government</a>, Yorkshire Building Society, Paul Smith, <a href="https://jobtrain.co.uk/success-stories/odeon-cinemas/">Odeon Cinemas</a>, <a href="https://jobtrain.co.uk/success-stories/university-of-london/">The University of London</a>, Merseyside Police, Warburtons and WWF. Our clients range across the private, public and not-for-profit sectors and their workforces stretch from 50 people to 162,000, so the scalability and versatility of our <a href="https://jobtrain.co.uk/applicant-tracking-system/">applicant tracking systems</a> has been proven time and time again. We work internationally too, with partnerships across Europe as well as in Abu Dhabi, USA, China, South Korea, South Africa and Malaysia.</p>
<h3><strong>The Jobtrain Applicant Tracking System works your way</strong></h3>
<p>By automating every aspect of the online recruitment process, we can enable your organisation to manage the sourcing and hiring of the right people with minimum effort and maximum efficiency. From <a href="https://jobtrain.co.uk/what-does-it-do/planning-and-approvals/vacancy-authorisation/">authorising the vacancy</a> and <a href="https://jobtrain.co.uk/what-does-it-do/job-advertising-and-posting/">advertising the job</a> to <a href="https://jobtrain.co.uk/what-does-it-do/review-score-and-screen/">scoring and screening applications</a>. Then onwards to arranging interviews, <a href="https://jobtrain.co.uk/what-does-it-do/hire/">managing offers and onboarding</a> the chosen candidates.</p>
<p>Everything is controlled and facilitated by one seamless system. We look after all the technology, and our innovative candidate management system is fully hosted and delivered by the cloud.</p>
<p>Each candidate is welcomed into a specially created portal featuring your brand. We empower your team to create talent pools, manage them simply and keep the information flowing with up-to-the-minute management reports.</p>
<p>Jobtrain recruitment software is above all secure. Our vast experience has shown that candidates, recruiters and hiring managers all find it intuitive and easy to work with. Support is on hand whenever required from our UK based Head Office from a dedicated team of HR &amp; recruitment specialises.</p>
<h3><strong>Recruitment that puts people first</strong></h3>
<p>By tailoring the right applicant tracking and candidate management system to each organisation, we free recruiters to focus on what really matters: people, skills and opportunities.</p>
<p>Our innovative and intuitive technology is created by a team with wide experience in HR and corporate recruitment. Each client has a single point of contact at Jobtrain: a dedicated <a href="https://jobtrain.co.uk/our-people/">Client Success Manager</a>, experienced in supporting <a href="https://jobtrain.co.uk/applicant-tracking-system/">candidate management systems</a> and the people who work with them. Add a technical team unrivalled in the industry and everything is in place to keep your online recruitment processes running smoothly, working securely, growing with you and delivering optimum results and the right candidates every time.</p>
