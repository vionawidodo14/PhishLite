<a class="title" href="/wildflower-seeds/planting-wildflowers">How to Plant Wildflowers in 6 Simple Steps</a>
<p style="font-size:14px;padding-left:36px;text-indent:-14px"><span class="yellow-text">1.</span> Choose a spot on your property that receives at least 6 hours of sun per day.<br/></p>
<p style="font-size:14px;padding-left:36px;text-indent:-14px"><span class="yellow-text">2.</span> Prepare your soil by clearing the area of all existing growth.<br/></p>
<p style="font-size:14px;padding-left:36px;text-indent:-14px"><span class="yellow-text">3.</span> Mix the Wildflower seeds with sand for better visibility.<br/></p>
<p style="font-size:14px;padding-left:36px;text-indent:-14px"><span class="yellow-text">4.</span> After spreading the seed, we recommend compressing the seed into the soil.<br/></p>
<p style="font-size:14px;padding-left:36px;text-indent:-14px"><span class="yellow-text">5.</span> After planting, give the area a good water.<br/></p>
<p style="font-size:14px;padding-left:36px;text-indent:-14px"><span class="yellow-text">6.</span> Enjoy!<br/></p>
