<h4>User Accounts - Another Update</h4>
<p>Hi - sorry - I have decided not to remove the user accounts part of the site. Apologies for the confusion - <a href="https://jimpix.co.uk/updates.asp?id=209">the update from 27th October 2018</a> no longer applies.</p>
<small class="gray">16-Dec-2018</small>
</div>
<div class="bs-callout bs-callout-info">
<h4>Future Send Dates</h4>
<p>I have decided to retain the facility to allow people to send ecards with a future send-date. This facility will still be available even after the user-accounts side of the site is removed at the start of 2019.
<br/>
<br/>You will still be able to send ecards with a future send-date, and use the <a href="https://jimpix.co.uk/ecards/history-email.asp">ecard history by email</a> page to get a list of ecards you have sent and from there, either preview or delete them.</p>
<small class="gray">30-Oct-2018</small>
</div>
<div class="bs-callout bs-callout-info">
<h4>User Account Update</h4>
<p></p><h3>User Account Update - October 2018</h3>
<br/> <ul>
<br/> <li>Unfortunately the ecards part of this site is no longer very busy and the upkeep of the user account side of the site is no longer cost-effective.</li>
<br/> <li>Therefore all jimpix user accounts will be deleted on 1st January 2019.</li>
<br/> <li>All associated data such as reminders, email addresses in your address book and ecards older than 1 month old will be deleted.</li>
<br/> <li>Please make copies of any data you don't want to lose.</li>
<br/> <li>Until that time, you will no longer be able to send ecards with a future send-date, edit or copy cards, or do anything else that was previously associated with the user-account side of the site.</li>
<br/> <li><b>Ecards which have already been queued to be sent with a future send-date will be honoured and sent at the appropriate time</b></li>
<br/> <li>You will still be able to send ecards after the user-account side of the site has gone, but just as a "regular" user...</li>
<br/> </ul><p></p>
<small class="gray">27-Oct-2018</small>
</div>
