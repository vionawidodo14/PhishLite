<h1 class="o-heading__level1-home">How it works</h1>
<div class="c-why-share">
<div class="c-why-share__info">
<div aria-hidden="true" class="c-why-share__icon"><i aria-hidden="true" class="fa fa-sign-in fa-2x"></i></div>
<div class="c-why-share__text-group">
<h2 class="o-heading__level2-why-share">Login</h2>
<div>
          Use your ORCiD. If your institution is a <a href="/stash/our_community#institutional">Dryad member</a>, connect to your existing credentials.
        </div>
</div>
</div>
<div class="c-why-share__info">
<div aria-hidden="true" class="c-why-share__icon"><i aria-hidden="true" class="fa fa-cloud-upload fa-2x"></i></div>
<div class="c-why-share__text-group">
<h2 class="o-heading__level2-why-share">Submit</h2>
<div>
          Whether or not your data are related to an article, <a href="/stash/submission_process#upload-methods">upload</a> your data files and receive a citable DOI.
        </div>
</div>
</div>
<div class="c-why-share__info">
<div aria-hidden="true" class="c-why-share__icon"><i aria-hidden="true" class="fa fa-users fa-2x"></i></div>
<div class="c-why-share__text-group">
<h2 class="o-heading__level2-why-share">Review</h2>
<div>
          Our <a href="/stash/submission_process#curation">curators</a> will check through your submission to ensure the data are usable. They may contact you with advice or questions.
        </div>
</div>
</div>
<div class="c-why-share__info">
<div aria-hidden="true" class="c-why-share__icon"><i aria-hidden="true" class="fa fa-quote-left fa-2x"></i> <i aria-hidden="true" class="fa fa-quote-right fa-2x"></i></div>
<div class="c-why-share__text-group">
<h2 class="o-heading__level2-why-share">Cite</h2>
<div>
<a href="/stash/submission_process#citation">Cite</a> and promote your data publication!
        </div>
</div>
</div>
</div>

<div class="c-why-use__group">
<h1 class="o-heading__level1-home">Why use Dryad?</h1>
<div class="c-why-use__items">
<div class="c-why-use__blurb">
<div class="c-why-use__icon">
<i aria-hidden="true" class="fa fa-folder-open-o fa-3x fa-fw"></i>
</div>
<div class="c-why-use__text">
<strong class="c-why-use__strong">Any field. Any format.</strong> Submit data in any file format from any field of research. Share all of the data from a project in one place.
      </div>
</div>
<div class="c-why-use__blocker"></div>
<div class="c-why-use__blurb">
<div class="c-why-use__icon">
<i aria-hidden="true" class="fa fa-users fa-3x fa-fw"></i>
</div>
<div class="c-why-use__text">
<strong class="c-why-use__strong">Quality control and assistance.</strong> Our curators will check your files before they are released, and help you follow best practices.
      </div>
</div>
<div class="c-why-use__blocker"></div>
<div class="c-why-use__blurb">
<div class="c-why-use__icon">
<i aria-hidden="true" class="fa fa-check-square fa-3x fa-fw"></i>
</div>
<div class="c-why-use__text">
<strong class="c-why-use__strong">Straightforward compliance.</strong> Submit your data to satisfy publisher and funder requirements for preservation and availability with a minimum of effort. We work directly with many publishers -- including Wiley, The Royal Society, and PLOS -- to streamline the process.
      </div>
</div>
<div class="c-why-use__blocker"></div>
<div class="c-why-use__blurb">
<div class="c-why-use__icon">
<i aria-hidden="true" class="fa fa-globe fa-3x fa-fw"></i>
</div>
<div class="c-why-use__text">
<strong class="c-why-use__strong">Community-led.</strong> Dryad is a nonprofit membership organization that is committed to making data available for research and educational reuse now and into the future. Modest Data Publishing Charges help ensure our sustainability.
      </div>
</div>
