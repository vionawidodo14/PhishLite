<h2 style="text-align: center;">DiiA: The global industry alliance for DALI lighting control</h2>
<table align="center" border="1" cellpadding="1" cellspacing="1" style="width: 640px;">

<br/>
<p><a href="https://www.digitalilluminationinterface.org/events/chinasummit2019.html" title="chinasummit2019"><img align="right" alt="" class="right" height="228" src="/data/images/3/3/1/china-2019_promo-image.jpg" width="400"/></a>The Digital Illumination Interface Alliance (DiiA) is an <strong>open, global consortium</strong> of lighting companies that aims to grow the market for lighting-control solutions based on <strong>Digital Addressable Lighting Interface (DALI)</strong> technology.</p>
<p>Building on the long-established benefits of DALI, the <strong>DALI-2 certification program</strong> brings the promise of significantly improved interoperability and additional functionality compared with current DALI systems in the market.</p>
