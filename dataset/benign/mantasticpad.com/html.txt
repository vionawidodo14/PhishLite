<h2>Introduction</h2>
<p data-adtags-visited="true">The Halloween REM returns to North America in time for this spooky day and also means we are receiving this 2-3 months ahead of “schedule.” This is because we are having this event at essentially the same time as the JP server which is exciting to say the least. Furthermore, there have been numerous buffs across all rarities along with new cards and evolutions.</p>
<p data-adtags-visited="true">Not only can we partake in the hype of new changes, but we are in theory receiving stronger cards ahead of schedule. This can be important for players who are pushing through challenging content as they are able to potentially gain irreplaceable tools.</p>
<p data-adtags-visited="true">With that being said, NA faces a unique problem in that we do not know what Power Rangers Collab will hold as it will be a debut event in the near future. This has the potential to range from amazing to lackluster and everything in between.</p>
<p data-adtags-visited="true">Regardless, this article will cover the strengths and weaknesses of each card to give players a better understanding of what to expect in this year’s Halloween REM.</p>

<h5 style="text-align:center;"><span style="color:#ff0000;">The Reset will not provide a new selection after the next maintenance on 10/22 (Tues) 6pm (UTC-8). Failure to use before will cause it to be lost<br/>
</span></h5>
<h2>Introduction</h2>
<p data-adtags-visited="true">On Tuesday October 22 at 6pm (UTC-8), PAD will go under maintenance and this will also reset the Best Friend Token, Present, and One-Time Bargain set.</p>
<p data-adtags-visited="true">The Best Friend reset will grant an additional Token which will allow you to choose a new Best Friend. This additional selection will not override your existing ones but failing to use a current selection will not carry over after the reset.</p>
<p data-adtags-visited="true">Best Friends are a vital part of your PAD experience as it will enable you to expand the leaders available as well as gaining additional Pal Points.</p>
<p data-adtags-visited="true">This article will explain the Friend and Best Friend System along with how you can improve your chances of being selected by strangers as well as how to form BFF Triangles.</p>
<p data-adtags-visited="true">From the previous reset, I was the first player on any PAD server to achieve 300/300 Best Friends on my Mantastic account and wondering if I can achieve that same status on Fantastic as I am around 175. With this in mind, if you wish to Best Friend Mantastic or Fantastic, please leave your ID number, your in game name, and which account you want to Best Friend. Mantastic can only accept a handful of players as I can only delete a few inactive BFF.</p>

<p data-adtags-visited="true">The first step to Pal Point domination is to find friends to populate your list. As you rank up, your friends list will max out at 50 players. <h2>Introduction</h2>
<p data-adtags-visited="true">The PAD Harvest Ranking Tournament has gone live in North America and gives players a chance to play for lucrative rewards along with a 7% Crown.</p>
<p data-adtags-visited="true">As a twist, this Fixed Team will only have to face a one floor but is nearly impossible to kill in a single turn. In fact, killing in two turns is difficult and if players are able to do so, they are almost always going to obtain a Crown.</p>
<p data-adtags-visited="true">This article will explain my approach to this Ranking Tournament for clearing it in two turns.</p>
<h2>Score breakdown</h2>
<p data-adtags-visited="true">In Ranking Dungeons, players are scored based on the following:</p>
<ul>
<li>Each Combo is worth 5,000 points</li>
<li>Each second remaining is worth 500 points</li>
<li>Hitting 40 Million damage is 10,000 points</li>
<li>Each active used to kill a floor (no combo made) is -5,000 points</li>
</ul>

<div class="entry-meta">
<span class="entry-date"><a href="https://mantasticpad.com/2019/10/01/question-time-with-mantastic-october-2019/" rel="bookmark"><time class="entry-date" datetime="2019-10-01T00:35:24-07:00">October 1, 2019</time></a></span> <span class="byline"><span class="author vcard"><a class="url fn n" href="https://mantasticpad.com/author/mantasticpad/" rel="author">Mantastic</a></span></span> <span class="comments-link"><a href="https://mantasticpad.com/2019/10/01/question-time-with-mantastic-october-2019/#comments">52 Comments</a></span>

<h2>Introduction</h2>
<p data-adtags-visited="true">The general gist of Question Time posts are for players to ask various PAD-related questions that do not have a simple/Google-able answer or related article on my website. I do play other games and have often found myself having specific/unique questions about it but have no where to go and can imagine this may hold true for some of my readers.</p>
<p data-adtags-visited="true">With that being said, there will be some restrictions on the types of questions I will be answering.</p>
<p data-adtags-visited="true">If planning on commenting on a regular basis, it is advantageous to create a WordPress account to both receive notifications as well as giving me a point of reference as to who I am responding to.</p>
<h2>Do’s and Don’t</h2>
<p data-adtags-visited="true"><img alt="download" class="alignnone wp-image-6992" data-attachment-id="6992" data-comments-opened="1" data-image-description="" data-image-meta='{"aperture":"0","credit":"","camera":"","caption":"","created_timestamp":"0","copyright":"","focal_length":"0","iso":"0","shutter_speed":"0","title":"","orientation":"0"}' data-image-title="download" data-large-file="https://mantasticpadblog.files.wordpress.com/2016/04/download.jpg?w=237" data-medium-file="https://mantasticpadblog.files.wordpress.com/2016/04/download.jpg?w=237" data-orig-file="https://mantasticpadblog.files.wordpress.com/2016/04/download.jpg" data-orig-size="237,213" data-permalink="https://mantasticpad.com/?attachment_id=6992" height="22" sizes="(max-width: 25px) 100vw, 25px" src="https://mantasticpadblog.files.wordpress.com/2016/04/download.jpg?w=25&amp;h=22" srcset="https://mantasticpadblog.files.wordpress.com/2016/04/download.jpg?w=25&amp;h=22 25w, https://mantasticpadblog.files.wordpress.com/2016/04/download.jpg?w=50&amp;h=44 50w" width="25"/> For the most part, almost any question will be answered, even some non-invasive Off-Topic ones; however, it is best to ask questions that require explanation/why/my personal opinion. The following examples are Fantastic types of questions to ask:</p>
<ul>
<li>Of these options, what is the best Yusuke team I can make for Arena 3?</li>
<li>What direction do you think GungHo will take with the 4x HP leads?</li>
<li>Can Heart Cross teams be revived and if so, how?</li>
</ul>
<p data-adtags-visited="true"><img alt="x" class="alignnone wp-image-6991" data-attachment-id="6991" data-comments-opened="1" data-image-description="" data-image-meta='{"aperture":"0","credit":"","camera":"","caption":"","created_timestamp":"0","copyright":"","focal_length":"0","iso":"0","shutter_speed":"0","title":"","orientation":"0"}' data-image-title="x" data-large-file="https://mantasticpadblog.files.wordpress.com/2016/04/x.png?w=222" data-medium-file="https://mantasticpadblog.files.wordpress.com/2016/04/x.png?w=222" data-orig-file="https://mantasticpadblog.files.wordpress.com/2016/04/x.png" data-orig-size="222,227" data-permalink="https://mantasticpad.com/?attachment_id=6991" height="26" sizes="(max-width: 25px) 100vw, 25px" src="https://mantasticpadblog.files.wordpress.com/2016/04/x.png?w=25&amp;h=26" srcset="https://mantasticpadblog.files.wordpress.com/2016/04/x.png?w=25&amp;h=26 25w, https://mantasticpadblog.files.wordpress.com/2016/04/x.png?w=50&amp;h=52 50w" width="25"/> The following types of questions will not be answered by me either because it has a simple Google-able answer or shows a lack of effort on the reader’s part:</p>
<ul>
<li>Anything that Google/Padx can answer</li>
<li>A link to your entire Monster Box and saying “make me a team”</li>
</ul>
<h2>Conclusion</h2>
<p data-adtags-visited="true">I hope my readers use this opportunity to ask questions that will hopefully improve their overall game play and progression.</p>
