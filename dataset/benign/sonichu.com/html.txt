<p><i><b>Chris-Chan: A Comprehensive History</b></i> is a <a class="mw-redirect" href="/cwcki/Internet" title="Internet">webseries</a> created by producer and musician Geno Samuel, which began in <a href="/cwcki/February_2018" title="February 2018">February 2018</a>. It is essentially a documentary about <a href="/cwcki/Christian_Weston_Chandler" title="Christian Weston Chandler">Chris</a>'s life, similar to <a href="/cwcki/Chris_Chan_Documentary_(Sachumo)" title="Chris Chan Documentary (Sachumo)">Sachumo</a>'s. However, while Sachumo's film is an abridged summary of Chris's life for newcomers, Geno's videos go into greater detail, each video being around 30 to 40 minutes long. The series is in chronological order, taking several videos just to cover Chris's life before his internet fame, and Geno has said that he plans to step through the <a href="/cwcki/Saga" title="Saga">densely packed events</a> of 2009–10 in increments of a few months. The series is still ongoing, and Geno predicts that it will be around 50 episodes long by the time he reaches the present day.  Geno plans to continue the series incrementally until ultimately it reaches <a href="/cwcki/Chris_and_death#Chris_himself_.281982.E2.80.9320.3F.3F.29" title="Chris and death">an end</a>. Chris is aware of the series, and has commented on it several times over <a href="/cwcki/Twitter" title="Twitter">Twitter</a> and <a href="/cwcki/YouTube" title="YouTube">YouTube</a>.
</p><p>(<b><a href="/cwcki/Chris-Chan:_A_Comprehensive_History" title="Chris-Chan: A Comprehensive History">More...</a></b>)
