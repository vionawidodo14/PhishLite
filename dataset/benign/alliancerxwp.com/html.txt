<h3>Prescription preparedness during severe weather</h3>

<p>&nbsp;</p>

<p>During hurricane season, your local area could be affected by severe weather. Whether you stay through the storm or need to evacuate&mdash;safety is the main concern. To ensure your family has access to their medications during severe weather such as hurricanes, here are some prescription tips to help you plan for these emergencies:</p>

<p>&nbsp;</p>

<h4>General</h4>

<ul class="unordered-list">
	<li>Place all medications in a waterproof container such as a Ziploc&reg; bag</li>
	<li>If you lose power and your medication needs refrigeration, keep in mind most prescriptions can be left at room temperature for a certain period of time</li>
	<li>If you lose phone service, authorize a family member or friend to call the pharmacy on your behalf</li>
	<li>Call your care coordinator if you have any questions about your delivery</li>
</ul>

<p>&nbsp;</p>

<h4>Evacuating</h4>

<ul class="unordered-list">
	<li>Take all your prescriptions with you, even if you won&rsquo;t be gone long</li>
	<li>Bring all insurance cards and prescription information</li>
	<li>Add your doctor&rsquo;s contact information to your phone</li>
</ul>

<p>&nbsp;</p>

<p>If you&rsquo;re traveling out of town to escape severe weather, we can send your medication to an alternate address or you can pick it up at a nearby Walgreens. Additionally, our Care Team will call you to schedule an early delivery if you&rsquo;re in the path of a storm.</p>
