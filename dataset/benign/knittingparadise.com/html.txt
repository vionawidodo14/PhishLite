<div style="text-align:center; font-size:1.25em;">Toddler / Child Sweater</div>
<div style="line-height:1.5em; margin-top:1%;">I found this pattern a few days ago and thought it would be worth sharing. I've always held back from trying to knit a fairisle sweater, they always look so complicated. This little jumper is worked flat on two needles, the four completed pieces are then joined at the shoulders so the stitches can be picked up on a straight needle and the yoke worked flat. If like me you are not to clever with circular knitting then this is the answer.   A lovely little design that uses UK double knit yarn or the equivalent.  I have tried to add the link to the web site I purchased this pattern but haven't been able to do so.  Google cabbage patch treasures and you will get to the site.</div>
