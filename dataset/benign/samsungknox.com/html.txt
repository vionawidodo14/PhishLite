<h1 class="page-title">Meet Samsung Knox</h1>
<p>Knox is Samsung’s guarantee of security, and a secure device gives you the freedom to work and play how, where, and when you want. Samsung Knox consists of a highly secure <a class="alt" href="#benefits" id="ga-home-intro-link-platform">platform</a> built into Samsung <a class="alt" href="/en/knox-platform/supported-devices" id="ga-home-intro-link-devices">devices</a> and a set of <a class="alt" href="#tabbed-benefit" id="ga-home-intro-link-solutions">solutions</a> that leverage this platform.</p>
<p>Whether you’d like to keep your personal photos private, or remotely manage a batch of business smartphones, Knox has you covered.</p>

<span class="benefits-subtitle">Knox Platform</span>
<h1>A secure device is a reliable device</h1>
<div class="explanation">
<p>The Samsung Knox platform is built into Samsung smartphones, tablets, and wearables at the manufacturing stage, so you have it the moment you unpack your device.
