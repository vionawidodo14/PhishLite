<p>Companies come to us for solutions to problems in <a href="//www.johndcook.com/blog/applied-math/">applied math</a> and <a href="//www.johndcook.com/blog/data-privacy/">data privacy</a>. The need for these skills cuts across industries, and we have especially helped clients working in <strong>software</strong>, <strong>biotech</strong>, and <strong>law</strong>.</p>
<p>Clients have included large companies such as Amazon, Google, Microsoft, and Amgen, as well as a number of <a href="https://www.johndcook.com/blog/expert-testimony/">law firms</a>, start-ups, and smaller businesses.</p>
