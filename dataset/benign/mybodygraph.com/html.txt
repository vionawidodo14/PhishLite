<p><strong>The Human Design System</strong> is a logical system that provides a practical <strong>tool for self-discovery</strong> and <strong>correct decision-making.</strong></p>
<p>How your life unfolds depends on the decisions you make. Each decision is critical, taking you along a particular trajectory, ultimately making up the sum total of your life. A right decision keeps you aligned to your path in life.</p>
<p><strong>But how do you make a decision?</strong> Through reasoning, working out the pros and cons, and ultimately arriving at a well-reasoned guess? Mental reasoning is an ineffective and unreliable decision-making process that more often than not leads to resistance and regrettable choices. This knowledge reveals the most natural way for you, helping you build a foundation for a life without resistance in which decisions can be made with certainty.</p>
<p>Your BodyGraph (Chart) reveals simple techniques for correct decision-making and is also a concrete map that offers a logical way of seeing your unique and individual nature—an extremely accurate tool for self-knowledge.</p>
<p class="p1" style="text-align: left;"><strong>MyBodyGraph is your user manual.</strong> Create your account and explore the free material tailored specifically to your design.</p>
