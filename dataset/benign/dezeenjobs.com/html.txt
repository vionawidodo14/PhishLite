What does FF&amp;E design stand for?
Furniture, fixtures and equipment.


What does FF&amp;E design mean?
The scope of work for an FF&amp;E designer involves designing anything in an interior space that isn’t attached/connected to the architecture/structure of the building. FF&amp;E design means leaving all the walls, windows, doors - anything part of the original structure - and integrating new furniture, appliances, lighting, hardware, and decoration. What is the job role of an FF&amp;E designer?
The job role of an FF&amp;E designer is to work on design concepts and presentations; source images; hand sketch; 2D CAD furniture drawings; Photoshop editing; setting up/printing presentation boards; communicate with suppliers and manage the purchase and manufacturing of highly detailed, bespoke furniture and lighting pieces.
