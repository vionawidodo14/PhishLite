<div class="large">Welcome to <strong><a href="/wiki/Help:The_Free_Family_Tree" title="WikiTree is 100% free to use and our shared tree will never be locked up behind a pay wall.">The Free Family Tree</a></strong>, growing stronger since 2008.</div>
<div class="eight columns">
<ol class="star large no-indent">
<li>Together we're growing an accurate <a href="/wiki/Help:Collaborative_Family_Tree" title="How does a single family tree work?">single family tree</a> using <a href="/wiki/Help:DNA" title="Why DNA + WikiTree = Awesome">DNA</a> and traditional genealogical <a href="/wiki/Help:Sources" title="How and why we always record the source of genealogical information.">sources</a>.</li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Help:Privacy" title="Click here for details on our extensive privacy controls.">Privacy controls</a> enable us to integrate modern family history.</li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Help:Protecting_Our_Shared_Tree" title="Click here for details on the extraordinary steps we take to protect our shared genealogy so that it will never disappear.">Extraordinary protections</a> ensure that our shared tree will never be lost.</li>
<li>Everything is <a href="/wiki/Help:The_Free_Family_Tree" title="WikiTree is 100% free to use and our shared tree will never be locked up behind a pay wall.">100% free</a> including many <a href="/wiki/Help:Community_Membership" title="Click here for a summary of the benefits of using WikiTree.">benefits for genealogists</a> who sign our <a href="/wiki/Special:Honor_Code" title="The Honor Code makes our collaboration productive and enjoyable. Click here to read its nine simple points.">Honor Code</a>.</li>
</ol>
<div class="large">Our tree includes 21,713,200 profiles (5,964,474 with <a href="/wiki/Help:DNA_Test_Connections" title="What are DNA test connections?">DNA test connections</a>) edited by 651,497 genealogists from around the world.</div>
