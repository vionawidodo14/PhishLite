<h1 class="widget-header header-emphasis">New Agreement with EISZ</h1>
<div class="widget-body body body-emphasis"><p>Akadémiai Kiadó (AK) and the Hungarian Academic Consortium (EISZ) announced a two-year agreement that combines reading access and Open Access publishing in one single framework. Effective from 2019 , researchers from the participating consortium institutions will gain access to 60+ AK journals and will have the right to publish their research via the Open Access model without any direct costs at journals of AK.</p>
<p>Press release of EISZ (in Hungarian): <a href="http://www.eisz.mtak.hu/index.php/hu/open-access/295-open-access-megallapodas-az-akademiai-kiadoval.html" target="_blank">http://www.eisz.mtak.hu/index.php/hu/open-access/295-open-access-megallapodas-az-akademiai-kiadoval.html</a><br/>Agreement with the list of participating institutions and journals (in Hungarian): <a class="recent_links" href="http://www.eisz.mtak.hu/images/szerzodesek/Akademiai_Kiado_Folyoiratcsomag_2019.pdf" target="_blank">http://www.eisz.mtak.hu/images/szerzodesek/Akademiai_Kiado_Folyoiratcsomag_2019.pdf</a></p></div>

<h1 class="widget-header header-emphasis">New Impact Factor Released</h1>
<div class="widget-body body body-emphasis"><p align="justify">

June 22, 2017<br/>
<br/>
On June 14, 2017, Clarivate Analytics – formerly Thomson Reuters – released the 2017 Journal Citation Reports (JCR). The majority of our journals (67%) witnessed an increase in their impact factors. <br/>
<br/>
The highest increase was observed for the Journal of Behavioral Addictions (JBA), from 2.492 up to 4.134. <br/>
<br/>
Another success is the growing IF of our oldest journal, the Orvosi Hetilap (Hungarian Medical Weekly, founded in 1857), which also saw a rise in its impact factor from 0.291 to 0.349.
<br/>
<br/>
We are extremely grateful to our editors-in-chief, editors, authors, reviewers, and all those who contributed their expertise and hard work to this great achievement. <br/>
 You can see the new scores directly at the journals' infopages. 
</p></div>
