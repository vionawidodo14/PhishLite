<p><strong>Smart charting.</strong> Minimize data re-entry with shortcuts and a library of customizable templates built by providers like you.</p>

<p><strong>Order from anywhere.</strong> Cloud-based e-prescribing lets you manage medication orders, refill requests, and pharmacy communication from any location.</p>
<p><strong>Send to anywhere.</strong> Connect to most e-prescribing enabled pharmacies in the country to quickly fill medication orders electronically.</p>
<p><strong>Save time.</strong> Submit common orders more efficiently with patient-specific templates and 2-click prescription refills.</p>

<p><strong>Go paperless.</strong> Never lose a fax again with the convenience of HIPAA compliant electronic faxing for your referrals and practice documents.</p>
