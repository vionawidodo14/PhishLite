<div class="page-row section print-break">
<h2>Students demonstrate understanding</h2>
</div>
<div class="page-row">
<div class="row-item">
<img alt="" class="centered" src="https://static.wixie.com/images-static/student-tools.jpg"/>
<h3>Amplify student voice</h3>
<p>Blending text and voice recording with original art and images makes Wixie the perfect canvas for today’s digital learners.</p>
<p>Wixie gives students powerful tools to create original projects that demonstrate understanding, encourage critical thinking, and build creativity and communication skills.</p>
</div>
<div class="row-item">
<img alt="" class="centered" src="https://static.wixie.com/images-static/student-choices-2.jpg"/>
<h3>Give students choice</h3>
<p>Wixie allows each student to build an original representation of their learning. While the learning goals remain the same, each student’s Wixie projects are unique. </p>
<p>With Wixie, students may choose to create a book trailer, illustrate an essay, design a comic strip, develop a timeline, write and record a literary adaptation, and much more.</p>
</div>
<div class="row-item">
<img alt="" class="centered" src="https://static.wixie.com/images-static/student-collaboration.jpg"/>
<h3>Empower collaboration</h3>
<p>Wixie supports essential team-building and organization skills through real-time collaborative projects.</p>
<p>Wixie's Team feature allows students to work together on projects in the classroom or at home. Collaborating on projects allows peers to draw on individual expertise and communication skills.</p>
</div>
</div>
<div class="page-row noPrint">
<p class="cta noPrint">
<a class="buttonLink" href="https://static.wixie.com/ideas">One tool, unlimited uses</a>
</p>
</div>
<div class="pageDividerFull noPrint"></div>
<div class="page-row align-center section">
<div class="row-item-two">
<h2>Instructional tools for creative teaching</h2>
<p>Wixie helps teachers plan, implement, and evaluate student work. Teachers can manage assignments, track student progress toward curriculum goals, and assess student work using customizable rubrics.</p>
</div>
<div class="row-item">
<img alt="" class="centered" src="https://static.wixie.com/images-static/student-choices.jpg"/>
</div>
</div>
<div class="page-row">
<div class="row-item">
<h3>Easily access student work!</h3>
<p>Teachers have access to view their students' Wixie projects. Students don't have to manage files or upload documents to submit assignments!</p>
<p>Teachers can assess student progress throughout a project, provide feedback, and assess the final product. </p>
</div>
<div class="row-item">
<h3>Meet your curriculum goals</h3>
<p>Choose from hundreds of templates correlated to academic standards, and assign standards to original student work. </p>
<p>Create your own Wixie templates and assign them to students. Schools and districts can build their own libraries of templates specific to their curriculum.</p>
</div>
<div class="row-item">
<h3>Showcase exemplary work</h3>
<p>Create a page of high quality student work to share at open house or collect all the work for a project in one place for presentation day.</p>
<p>Teachers have a unique URL to use to show off the work their students are doing in Wixie.</p>
</div>
