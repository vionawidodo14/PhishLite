<section class="block block-block-content block-block-contentf562213b-47ef-4a31-b2b7-46bbba5bc9a2 clearfix" id="block-whyshopindie">
<div class="field field--name-body field--type-text-with-summary field--label-hidden field--item"><hr/><h3><strong>Why shop Indie?</strong></h3>
<p>When you shop at an independently owned business, your entire community benefits:</p>
<p><strong>The Economy</strong></p>
<ul><li>Spend $100 at a local-owned business and $52 of that stays in your community. </li>
<li>Spend $50 at a national chain and keep $6.50 in the local community.</li>
<li>Spend $50 online with a remote vendor with no sales tax collected and keep not one penny in your local community.</li>
<li>Local businesses create higher-paying jobs for our neighbors.</li>
<li>More of your taxes are reinvested in your community--where they belong.</li>
</ul><p><strong>The Environment</strong></p>
<ul><li>Buying local means less packaging, less transportation, and a smaller carbon footprint.</li>
<li>Shopping in a local business district means less infrastructure, less maintenance, and more money to beautify your community.</li>
</ul><p><strong>The Community</strong></p>
<ul><li>Local retailers are your friends and neighbors—support them and they’ll support you.</li>
<li>Local businesses donate to charities at more than twice the rate of national chains.</li>
<li>More independents means more choice, more diversity, and a truly unique community.</li>
</ul><p>Now is the time to stand up and join your fellow individuals in the IndieBound mission supporting local businesses and celebrating independents.</p>
