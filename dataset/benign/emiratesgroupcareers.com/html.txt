<h5>Salaheddin Allan</h5>
<strong>Decal, Stencil and <br/>Livery Draftsman</strong><br/><br/><strong>When did you join the company?</strong><br/>I joined Emirates in 2011 to help set up the Graphic Shop, where we produce decals, markings and cabin placards for our aircraft. <br/><br/><strong>What inspired you to take on this career? </strong><br/>As a child, art was my favourite hobby. I enjoyed sitting at home and sketching different things. As time passed, I realised I wanted my hobby to be my career. <br/>To hone my skills further, I took up AutoCAD and other graphic design software courses. I’ve been in this line of work for more than 20 years.</div>

</div><div aria-describedby="slick-slide-control41" aria-hidden="true" class="slide slick-slide" data-slick-index="1" data-slide="" id="slick-slide41" role="tabpanel" style="width: 660px;" tabindex="-1">
<h2>A day in the life of...</h2>
<div class="description description-smaller">
<div><strong>What are you most proud of?</strong><br/>Before 2011, the Gulf region didn’t have silkscreen machines to print decals and placards for aircraft. When I joined Emirates, I requested for high-end silkscreen machines with specifications that I provided. <br/>Since then, these machines have made printing decals cheaper, more efficient, and more readily available to our mechanics and engineers. <br/>I also take pride in researching methods adopted by other companies, which helps me keep up to date with industry best practices. I often find that we haven’t adopted some of their simple ideas, such as creating templates. So I made a positioning template for the different markings on our A380 passenger doors, which cut down the installation time from two hours to ten minutes.</div>
</div>
</div><div aria-describedby="slick-slide-control42" aria-hidden="true" class="slide slick-slide" data-slick-index="2" data-slide="" id="slick-slide42" role="tabpanel" style="width: 660px;" tabindex="-1">
<h2>A day in the life of...</h2>
<div class="description description-smaller">
<div><strong>What are some of the biggest challenges you’ve faced at work?</strong><br/>In June 2016, we were given 17 days to complete and apply the LA Dodgers decal to our A380. I decided to take a risk and not use digital printers, as that would’ve taken 44 days. Instead, we used silkscreen printers and a complicated process involving the meshing of different colours. The entire team worked hard to get the job done in eight days - we saved around Dhs33,000 solely on ink alone. <br/><br/><strong>What do you do outside of work?</strong><br/>Every Friday, I go to the pool or beach to swim about 5km as it helps me relax. I love spending time with my two children and watching movies with them. I also take care of my two pet cockatiels, Lucas and Rosie.</div>
