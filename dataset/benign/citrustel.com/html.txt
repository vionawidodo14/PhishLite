<h3><span>Do you offer any subscription based or monthly VOIP calling plans?</span></h3>
<p>Unfortunately, we do not offer any monthly or subscription based VOIP phone calling plans. Our pricing model is pay as you go based. You get charged every time you make a call based on the destination and the call duration.</p>
<h3><span>Can I download CitrusTel VOIP calling app from the App Store?</span></h3>
<p>CitrusTel does NOT offer any Android or iOS based native apps. CitrusTel is a browser based VOIP application and does not require any app downloads. Welcome to world's best <a href="free-online-phone">free online phone</a>.</p>
<h3><span>You can use CitrusTel to make free calls online without registration.</span></h3>
<p>Registration is not needed to make free online calls using Citrustel. We never ask you to sign up before you can start calling.</p>
<h3><span>What are VOIP calls?</span></h3>
<p>VOIP, in simple terms, allows phone calls to be made using internet to a regular mobile or landline based phone. Once you dial the phone number and click on the CALL button, CitrusTel connects your call from your web browser, using internet protocol, to a mobile or landline based end point.</p>
<h3><span>How do you accept payments? Do you accept PayPal?</span></h3>
<p>We accept payments via PayPal, Credit Card, Debit Card and Bitcoins. We also accept payment in more than 20 different currencies.</p>
<h3><span>Are my calls private? How secure is my communication?</span></h3>
<p>Your trust is the most important thing to us. We encrypt all communication and do NOT record any phone calls.</p>
<h3><span>How is CitrusTel different from Skype or WhatsApp that also offer free internet calls?</span></h3>
<p>Skype or other communication platforms only offer free calls between application users. As you know, you cannot make free calls to an actual phone using Skype. Citrustel is different in that the person you are calling does not have to be on CitrusTel or need CitrusTel. Since CitrusTel connects calls from a computer to a regular mobile or landline phone, the person you are calling does not even need internet connectivity to receive free online calls made through CitrusTel.</p>
