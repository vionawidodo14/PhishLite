href="/Learning-Center/SARE-Program-Materials/National-Program-Materials/What-is-Sustainable-Agriculture" target="_self" title="">What is Sustainable Agriculture?</a></h2>
<div class="object-left"><div class="content-view-embed" style="width:170px;">
<div class="class-image">
<img alt="SARE-Folder_03_lowres.jpg" height="113" src="/var/ezflow_site/storage/images/media/images/outreach-images/promo-pubs/sare-folder/sare-folder_03_lowres/1032773-1-eng-US/SARE-Folder_03_lowres_articleimage.jpg" style="" title="SARE-Folder_03_lowres.jpg" width="170"/>
</div>
</div>
</div> <p>Every day, farmers and ranchers around the world develop new, innovative strategies to produce and distribute food, fuel and fiber sustainably. While these strategies vary greatly they all embrace three broad goals, or what SARE calls the 3 Pillars of Sustainability:</p> <p></p><div>• <b>Profit</b> over the long term</div><div>• <b>Stewardship</b> of our nation's land, air and water</div>• <b>Quality of life</b> for farmers, ranchers and their communities<p></p> <p>There are almost as many ways to reach these goals as there are farms and ranches in America.</p> <p>To learn more, check out the award-winning <i><a href="/Learning-Center/SARE-Program-Materials/National-Program-Materials/What-is-Sustainable-Agriculture" target="_self" title="">What is Sustainable Agriculture?</a>.</i></p> </div>
