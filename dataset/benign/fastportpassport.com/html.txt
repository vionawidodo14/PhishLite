Child passports cannot be renewed.</p> <a class="btn btn-primary with-arrow" href="/checkout/step-1;type=passport-renewal"><span>Order Now</span></a></div></div></div></li>

Second passports are valid for 4 years and are ideal for frequent travelers who often apply for travel visas and visit countries with restrictive entry requirements.</p> <a class="btn btn-primary with-arrow" href="/checkout/step-1;type=second-passport"><span>Order Now</span></a></div></div></div></li>
<li> <div class="open-close"> <a class="opener" href="#">Child Passport <span class="icon-arrow-down"></span></a> <div class="slide js-slide-hidden"> <div class="slide-holder"> <p>To apply for a child’s passport the applicant must be 15 years old or younger. If the applicant is 16 or older they must apply for a new passport. All travelers require a passport to travel internationally, including infants.</p> <a class="btn btn-primary with-arrow" href="/checkout/step-1;type=child-passport"><span>Order Now</span></a></div></div></div></li>
<li> <div class="open-close"> <a class="opener" href="#">Name Change <span class="icon-arrow-down"></span></a> <div class="slide js-slide-hidden"> <div class="slide-holder"> <p>To apply for a name change passport you must be in possession of your current passport and you must provide legal documentation of your name change. If you do not have your current passport you must apply for a new passport along with proper name change documentation.</p> <a class="btn btn-primary with-arrow" href="/checkout/step-1;type=name-change"><span>Order Now</span></a></div></div></div></li>

<section class="content-page">
<div class="heading-section"> <h2>Common Questions</h2> <p>Getting your passport can be a stressful task if you don’t know the right questions to ask.</p> <p>We have compiled an easy <a href="/resources/common-questions/">FAQ guide</a> to help you navigate expediting your passport.</p></div><ul class="accordion">
<li class="acc-item"> <a class="opener" href="#"> What Is a Registered Passport Agent? <span class="icon-arrow-down"></span> </a> <div class="slide js-slide-hidden"> <div class="slide-content"> <p>A Registered Passport agent is approved with the U.S. Department of State Regional Passport Agencies to help U.S. citizens get their passports expedited. In other words, we can help you get your passport fast. Routine passport processing can take 6 weeks or longer. But using our service, we can get your passport processed as quickly as 24 hours! Applicants won’t need an appointment at a regional passport agency, so you can save time by avoiding the long lines of people all trying to get a passport on time. Applicants will still need to be seen by an acceptance agent in order to get their identity verified and documents adjudicated. Don’t worry though, there are over 8000 local passport acceptance agents across the country, so we will help you find one close to you.</p></div></div></li>
<li class="acc-item"> <a class="opener" href="#"> How fast can you process a passport? <span class="icon-arrow-down"></span> </a> <div class="slide js-slide-hidden"> <div class="slide-content"> <p>Passport expeditors hand carry your passport application inside of a regional passport agency to secure the passport in hand as quickly as 24-48 hours. Once the passport is complete, the courier can deliver the passport to the applicant in hand the same day or use an overnight shipping company to have the passport to the applicant the next morning. Expedited passport processing usually starts at 2 weeks and can be processed as fast as 24 hours.</p></div></div></li>
<li class="acc-item"> <a class="opener" href="#"> How do I send in my application? <span class="icon-arrow-down"></span> </a> <div class="slide js-slide-hidden"> <div class="slide-content"> <p>Our processing times begin once we get the documents from you. Follow our easy to use instruction guidelines and Fedex the documentation as instructed or come visit us in person at our Brooklyn, NY Office or in Florida at our Boca Raton location. When using the Fedex services that we provide make sure to print the labels as instructed. We provide expedited services for passport applicants in ALL 50 STATES. Always have your tracking number available when contacting our representatives.</p></div></div></li>
<li class="acc-item"> <a class="opener" href="#"> What about my passport photos? <span class="icon-arrow-down"></span> </a> <div class="slide js-slide-hidden"> <div class="slide-content"> <p>Passport photos MUST be included in the documents that you will send to us. Passport photos can be taken at most CVS or Walgreens locations. You can provide your own passport photos as long as they adhere to the strict U.S. Department of State passport photo requirements.</p></div></div></li>
<li class="acc-item"> <a class="opener" href="#"> How much does it cost? <span class="icon-arrow-down"></span> </a> <div class="slide js-slide-hidden"> <div class="slide-content"> <p>The fee to expedite your passport depends on when you are traveling. Our pricing options start at $119 for two-week service and go all the way up to $349 for 24-hour turnaround. The government fees are paid separately. For a new adult passport, the expedited passport application fee is $170 and the acceptance agent fee is $35.</p></div></div></li>
</ul>
