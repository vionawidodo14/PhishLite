<h1>About the International Schools Database</h1>
<p>We're here to help you make one of your most important expat parenting decisions; where to send your children to
      school.</p>
</div><!-- /.page-header -->
<div class="row">
<div class="col-sm-6">
<div class="post-detail">
<h3>The Problem</h3>
<p>
          As expat parents ourselves, we know how challenging it can be to choose an international or English school for
          your children - especially in a new or foreign city.
        </p>
<img alt="Photo of co-founder" class="rounded pull-left mt20 mb20 mr20" src="/assets/gerardo-robledillo-c1431338a2dc598765edc7042329e927b87b8f01a363ee5d201ec973b75841ef.jpg"/>
<img alt="Photo of co-founder" class="rounded pull-right mt20 mb20 ml20" src="/assets/andrea-robledillo-cf2de5a629afe0ac8a727cdc4423e07c2c6f72af1e2ef522eaa33304c4c487d0.jpg"/>
<p>
          First, finding all of the available schools in a foreign city is not easy. Second, the information that is
          readily available lacks any of the important details. And to make things even more difficult, contacting
          these schools to get answers to your questions is slow and frustrating.
        </p>
<p>
          Then when you finally settle on some potential schools, you end up spending yet more time chasing down the finer details that are relevant to you
          and your children.
        </p>
<p>
          We know it. We experienced it ourselves.
        </p>
<p>
          And now we want to make it easier for other expat parents out there.
        </p>
</div>
</div><!-- /.col-* -->
<div class="col-sm-6">
<div class="post-detail">
<h3>The Solution</h3>
<p>
          We have collected all of the important information about every international school in dozens of cities
          worldwide. We personally contact every school in our database to get answers to those questions that can make
          or break your child's experience in their new school. From fees to languages to extra-curricular activities,
          we've done all the research for you.
        </p>
<img alt="Girls talking in classroom" class="post-content-image pull-right mt0" src="/assets/girls-in-classroom.380x253-f5c3876b4dbdf32f15a74a7cb8ac2299b44137e082ddcc61f3a925c7d7cded52.jpg"/>
<p>
          We know all too well that every family and every child is different. That's why you can personalise all of our
          research to your unique situation; search based on how many children you have, their ages, your preferences,
          and much more.
        </p>
<p>
          For example, you can choose the number and ages of your children, and automatically you will only see schools
          available for those ages (with prices adjusted accordingly). If you're only interested in a specific language
          or curriculum, you can search using those parameters too. Whatever you're looking for, we'll help you find it.
        </p>
</div>
