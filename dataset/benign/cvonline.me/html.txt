<h2 class="subtitle">Online CV Benefits</h2>
<p>Did you ever wondered why you weren't interviewed for a position you were perfect for? </p>
<p>Our experience tells us that in the first stage of a recruiting process, the content of your cv is as important as the way you write it and how it's formating. That's the reason why good candidates go often "under the radar" of the recruiters and get lost, never getting an interview.</p>
<p>The average job hunter in the UK has to apply to 27 positions in order to get a single interview. Cvonline.me is here to help you navigate this situation by helping you create both online and offline cvs that will capture the attention of the recruiters to actually read your cv.</p>
<p> With CV online:</p>
<p><span class="fa fa-check"></span> You will obtain a unique and customizable link for every CV you create.</p>
<p><span class="fa fa-check"></span> Publish as many CV's as you need, with a different link for each application.</p>
<p><span class="fa fa-check"></span> Choose the design of your online cvs and cutomize it with your own background images to make it completely unique.</p>
<p><span class="fa fa-check"></span> Create as many cvs as you need, both online cvs and offline cvs and target its content to each application.</p>
