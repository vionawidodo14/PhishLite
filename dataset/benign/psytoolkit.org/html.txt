<h2 id="_about_psytoolkit">About PsyToolkit</h2>
<div class="sectionbody">
<div class="paragraph">
<p><span class="image" style="float: left"><img alt="x1" src="https://www.psytoolkit.org/x1.png"/></span> PsyToolkit is a
free-to-use toolkit for demonstrating, programming, and running
cognitive-psychological experiments and surveys, including personality
tests. <strong>PsyToolkit is frequently used for academic studies, for student
projects, and for teaching cognitive and personality psychology.</strong>
<br/>
<br/></p>
</div>
<div class="sidebarblock">
<div class="content">
<div class="ulist">
<ul>
<li>
<p>Design and run experiments or questionnaire surveys online (browser-based) or offline</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Online data collection, storage, analysis, and download</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>No limits on numbers of questions or participants</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Multi-lingual survey interface (including Spanish, French, German, 中文, and many more)</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Extensive <a href="https://www.psytoolkit.org/doc2.6.0/">online documentation</a> and <a href="https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCVlurdelHTebCZoa_MhaoYw/videos">YouTube channel with tutorial videos</a></p>
</li>
<li>
<p>It is suitable for teaching psychology, starting from secondary education</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Ideal for student and professional research projects</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Ideal for support in teaching cognitive or personality psychology</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Rapid development possible by using questionnaires and experiments from the large library</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Non-commercial and free to use</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Made for and by academics</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Reliably hosted by a professional data center</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>It uses free-software resources (click for <a href="acknowledgements.html">acknowledgements</a>)</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Lab based version for Linux with support for special keyboards (Cedrus, BlackBox, etc)</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>There is a <a href="https://www.psytoolkit.org/faq.html">Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) section</a></p>
</li>
<li>

<h2 id="_why_use_psytoolkit">Why use PsyToolkit?</h2>
<div class="sectionbody">
<div class="ulist">
<ul>
<li>
<p>PsyToolkit is the only free website offering running programmable online psychological experiments and surveys.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Because many thousands of other students and academics around the world are using it.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Because it supports many different languages in the online surveys</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>PsyToolkit is well documented with many examples you can copy for your own project.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>You can program your experiment, collect data, and analyze all with just this one website.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>You can run complex psychological surveys, and use from more than 100 surveys in the <a href="/survey-library">survey library</a>.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>If you also use cognitive psychological experiments, you can simply copy them from the <a href="/experiment-library">experiment library</a>.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>PsyToolkit is fast and reliable (server runs from professional data center).</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>PsyToolkit is reliably maintained by a <a href="contact.html">UK professor in Psychology</a>.</p>
</li>
</ul>
</div>
