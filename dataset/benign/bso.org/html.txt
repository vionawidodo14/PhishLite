</a>
<p class="additional-event-description">
                            Andris Nelsons leads the BSO in the orchestra’s first-ever Symphony Hall performances of Shostakovich’s rarely heard Symphony No. 2, To October, written to commemorate the tenth anniversary of the Bolshevik Revolution. Featuring violinist Daniel Lozakovich.
