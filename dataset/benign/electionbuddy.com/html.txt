<section id="home-benefits">
<div class="container width--wide">
<h2 class="centered orange-underline">Take Elections to the Next Level</h2>
<div class="flexbox-row">
<div class="col-1-3">
<h3>Improve voter turnout</h3>
<p>Notify voters through email, mail, text message or with your own systems. Receive undeliverable notice alerts and schedule reminders. Voters vote by phone, computer, mail or in person.
</p>
</div><!-- .col-1-3 -->
<div class="col-1-3">
<h3>Voting integrity</h3>
<p>Voter can only vote once and voting choices remain anonymous. Each ballot has one, secure voting key and the vote is auditable, verifiable and can be independently observed.
</p>
</div><!-- .col-1-3 -->
<div class="col-1-3">
<h3>Every type of vote</h3>
<p>Ballots are configurable to manage <a href="/process/ballot-types/candidate-member-approval-elections">candidate elections</a>, <a href="/process/ballot-types/board-of-directors-elections">board positions</a>, <a href="/process/ballot-types/contract-ratifications">contract ratifications</a>, <a href="/process/ballot-types/bylaw-amendments">bylaw amendments</a>, <a href="/process/ballot-types/budget-approvals">budget approvals</a>, <a href="/process/ballot-types/candidate-acclamations">acclamations</a>, <a href="/process/ballot-types/motion-votes">motions</a> and <a href="/process/ballot-types/membership-polls">member polls</a>.
</p>
</div><!-- .col-1-3 -->
</div><!-- .flexbox-row -->
<div class="flexbox-row">
<div class="col-1-3">
<h3>Simply automated</h3>
<p>Complete <a href="/process">setup in 3 steps</a> with extensive online help and email support - spend your time monitoring, not counting. Or engage our experts for dedicated assistance or independent election oversight.
</p>
</div><!-- .col-1-3 -->
<div class="col-1-3">
<h3>Fast results</h3>
<p>Winners are immediately calculated using <a href="/features/voting-systems/plurality-voting">first past the post</a>, <a href="/features/voting-systems/cumulative-voting">cumulative voting</a>, <a href="/features/voting-systems/preferential-voting">preferential ballot</a>, <a href="/features/voting-systems/stv-voting">STV Single transferable vote</a> or <a href="/features/voting-systems/approval-voting">approval voting</a>. You can tabulate results yourself too!
</p>
</div><!-- .col-1-3 -->
<div class="col-1-3">
<h3>Election security</h3>
<p>256-bit encryption used to safeguard your vote — the same security as major banks. And we don’t share or use voter data — your elections stay private for both you and your voters.
</p>
</div><!-- .col-1-3 -->
</div><!-- .flexbox-row -->
</div><!-- .container -->
