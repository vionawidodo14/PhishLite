<div class="container-fluid noImage anchor" id="features" style="padding-right: 0px; padding-left: 0px;  background-color: #FFF; ">
<div class="row">
<div class="col-sm-12">
<div class="container col-sm-12" style="font-size: 18px;">
<div class="container col-sm-12" style="background-image: url(https://www.duet3d.com/image/catalog/banners/TMC_background.jpg); background-position:left;    background-repeat: no-repeat;background-color:#fff;height:70px; width: 100%; overflow: hidden; width=:100%;    margin-left: -15px;">
<h1 style="text-align: center; color:#040073;"><strong>Features</strong></h1>
</div>
<div class="container col-sm-6 col-md-3 borderHighlight" style="color:#040073; min-height:880px; text-align: center; padding:0px 10px; overflow:hidden;">
<div style="min-height:80px">
<h2><strong>Fast, quiet, high quality prints</strong></h2>
</div>
<div style="padding-bottom: 20px;"><img src="https://www.duet3d.com/image/catalog/icons/CPU.png" style="max-height: 50px; padding-bottom: 10px;"/><br/>
<strong>Powerful 32 Bit Processor</strong><br/>
Atmel SAM4E8E: 120MHz ARM Cortex-M4 microcontroller with floating point unit, 512Kb flash memory, 128Kb RAM and many peripherals.</div>
<div style="padding-bottom: 20px;"><span class="fa-stack" style="font-size: 20px;"> <i class="fa fa-volume-off fa-stack-2x" style=" color: #000; "></i> <i class="fa fa-ban fa-stack-2x" style="color:rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.5); "></i></span><br/>
<strong>Super quiet TMC2660 stepper drivers</strong><br/>
SPI controlled stepper drivers capable of up to 256 microstepping. Current and microsteps can both be changed on the fly for optimum speed and power efficiency.</div>
<div style="padding-bottom: 20px;"><img src="https://www.duet3d.com/image/catalog/icons/Nozzlex2.png" style="max-height: 50px; padding-bottom: 10px;"/><br/>
<strong>Dual extruders</strong><br/>
3 heater/thermistor channels for a heated bed and 2 extruders. 3 PWM controllable and 2 always-on fans. These can be run from either the input voltage, from 5V, or from external power for added flexibility.</div>
<div style="padding-bottom: 20px;"><i aria-hidden="true" class="fa fa-bolt faIcon"></i><i aria-hidden="true" class="fa fa-bolt" style="font-size: 40px; color: #000;"></i><br/>
<strong>High Power rating</strong><br/>
Each stepper driver is capable of 2.4A motor current, with further increases possible after thermal testing. The bed heater channel is specifically designed for high currents of up to 18A.</div>
</div>
<div class="container col-sm-6 col-md-3 borderHighlight" style="color:#040073;min-height:880px; text-align: center; padding:0px 10px; overflow:hidden;">
<div style="min-height:80px">
<h2><strong>Network connectivity </strong></h2>
</div>
<div style="padding-bottom: 20px;"><i aria-hidden="true" class="fa fa-wifi faIcon"></i> <span style="font-size:48px; color:#000000;">/ </span><img src="https://www.duet3d.com/image/catalog/icons/ethernet_icon.png" style="max-height: 50px; padding-bottom: 20px;"/><br/>
<strong>Dedicated Wifi or Ethernet module</strong><br/>
The main processor is free to do precise stepper pulse timing and implement other advanced features while the low level networking is handled by the dedicated networking module.</div>
<div style="padding-bottom: 20px;"><i aria-hidden="true" class="fa fa-mobile faIcon"></i><i aria-hidden="true" class="fa fa-desktop faIcon" style="padding:0 0px 0px 5px;"></i><i aria-hidden="true" class="fa fa-tablet faIcon fa-rotate-90" style="padding: 0 0px 0px 10px;"></i><br/>
<strong>Flexible</strong><br/>
Connect via PC, tablet or smartphone on the same network: there is no need for an app install or internet connectivity. Also connect via USB or serial if desired.</div>
<div style="padding-bottom: 20px;"><i aria-hidden="true" class="fa fa-chain-broken faIcon"></i><br/>
<strong>Cable-free control</strong><br/>
Use your printer in any room covered by your WiFi or Ethernet network. No need to be tethered by USB cabling any more.</div>
<div style="padding-bottom: 20px;"><i aria-hidden="true" class="fa fa-lock faIcon"></i><br/>
<strong>Security</strong><br/>
Wifi has WPA-2 encryption for network security, with further security changes coming soon. The DuetWifi and Duet Ethernet do not need to be connected to the internet, we recommend you keep it on a local network for added security.</div>
</div>
<div class="container col-sm-6 col-md-3 borderHighlight" style="color:#040073;min-height:880px; text-align: center; padding:0px 10px; overflow:hidden;">
<div style="min-height:80px">
<h2><strong>A pleasure to use</strong></h2>
</div>
<div style="padding-bottom: 20px;"><i aria-hidden="true" class="fa fa-chrome faIcon"></i> <i aria-hidden="true" class="fa fa-firefox faIcon"></i> <i aria-hidden="true" class="fa fa-safari faIcon"></i><br/>
<strong>Easy and convenient</strong><br/>
Connect directly to your printer from any modern web browser. Control your printer, upload and start prints from the browser. Monitor prints from an external IP webcam (not provided).</div>
<div style="padding-bottom: 20px;"><img src="https://www.duet3d.com/image/catalog/icons/printer_types_4.png" style="max-height: 50px; padding-bottom: 10px;"/><br/>
<strong>Most Printers Supported</strong><br/>
All common 3D printer geometries are supported: Cartesian, Delta, CoreXY, IDEX, SCARA. Along with 3D printers a wide variety of CNC machines and lasercutters can be controlled.</div>
<div style="padding-bottom: 20px;"><i aria-hidden="true" class="fa fa-cogs faIcon"></i> <i aria-hidden="true" class="fa fa-wrench faIcon"></i><br/>
<strong>Easy to configure</strong><br/>
Setup your printer and update the firmware through the web interface. No need to compile firmware.</div>
<div style="padding-bottom: 20px;"><i aria-hidden="true" class="fa fa-file-text faIcon"></i><br/>
<strong>G-code everywhere</strong><br/>
All settings are done through G-code, giving maximum control flexibility. Write your own macros for homing, pause, resume and much more.</div>
</div>
<div class="container col-sm-6 col-md-3 borderHighlight" style="color:#040073;min-height:880px; text-align: center; padding:0px 10px; overflow:hidden;">
<div style="min-height:80px">
<h2><strong>Expandable</strong></h2>
</div>
<div style="padding-bottom: 20px;"><img src="https://www.duet3d.com/image/catalog/icons/Motorx7.png" style="max-height: 50px; padding-bottom: 10px; padding-right: 5px;"/><img src="https://www.duet3d.com/image/catalog/icons/Nozzlex5.png" style="max-height: 50px; padding-bottom: 10px; padding-left: 5px; "/><br/>
<strong>Up to 7 additional drives</strong><br/>
Support for a further 7 stepper drivers and 5 heaters on expansion headers. Firmware support for mixing nozzles, multiple Z motor bed levelling and remapping axes to use high power external drivers.</div>
<div style="padding-bottom: 20px;"><img src="https://www.duet3d.com/image/catalog/icons/touchscreen.png" style="max-height: 40px; padding-bottom: 10px;"/><br/>
<strong>Touch Screen Support</strong><br/>
The optional PanelDue controller provides a full colour graphic touch screen controller with virtual keyboard. Also talks G-code for maximum flexibility.</div>
<div style="padding-bottom: 20px;"><i aria-hidden="true" class="fa fa-magic faIcon"></i><br/>
<strong>Advanced Calibration Support </strong><br/>
Use an optional add-on of a smart delta effector or DC42’s contactless IR bed probe combined with advanced firmware features for more accurate printer calibration.</div>
<div style="padding-bottom: 10px;"><img src="https://www.duet3d.com/image/catalog/icons/temp.png" style="max-height: 50px; padding-bottom: 10px;"/><br/>
<strong>Accurate temperature control</strong><br/>
ADC gain calibration for thermistors allows for accurate and repeatable temperature setting. PT100 and thermocouples are supported through digital temperature daughterboards.</div>
</div>
<div class="container col-sm-12" style="color:#040073; min-height:200px; width: 100%; background-color:rgb(207, 243, 224); overflow:hidden; text-align: center; margin-top:10px;">

<div class="container col-sm-6 col-md-3" style="padding-bottom: 20px; min-height: 210px;"><a href="http://www.ohwr.org/licenses/cern-ohl/v1.2" target="_blank"><img alt="CERN OHL 1.2" src="https://www.duet3d.com/image/catalog/icons/oshw-logo-bw-px.png" style="max-height: 50px; padding-bottom: 10px;" title="CERN OHL 1.2"/></a><br/>

All the hardware designs are licensed under the CERN OHL 1.2. The design files are released on GitHub.</div>

<strong>Open Source Software</strong><br/>
<br/>
Both RepRapFirmware and DuetWebControl are licensed under the GPL.</div>
<div class="container col-sm-6 col-md-3" style="padding-bottom: 20px;min-height: 210px;"><a href="http://kicad-pcb.org/" target="_blank"><img alt="KiCAD" src="https://www.duet3d.com/image/catalog/icons/KiCAD.png" style="max-height: 50px; padding-bottom: 10px;" title="Open Tools"/></a><br/>

<strong>Built with Open Tools</strong><br/>
<br/>
Designed in KiCAD and Eclipse: using open tools means the barrier to getting involved is as low as possible.</div>
<div class="container col-sm-6 col-md-3" style="padding-bottom: 20px;min-height: 210px;"><a href="https://github.com/dc42/RepRapFirmware" target="_blank"><i aria-hidden="true" class="fa fa-code-fork faIcon"></i></a><br/>

Fork the project on github, design a new hardware feature, make a new template for your printer or document your build. We look forward to you getting involved.</div>

<h1 style="text-align: center;">Meet Duet3D</h1>
</div>
<p class="col-sm-12" style="height: 120px; overflow: hidden; text-align: center; width: 80%; margin-left: 10%; margin-right: 10%; margin-top: 60px;"><span style="font-family:lucida sans unicode,lucida grande,sans-serif;"><span style="font-size:20px;">We are a UK based company, formed in 2019, following on from a successful joint venture between Think3DPrint3D, one of the original developers of the Duet, </span></span><span style="font-family:lucida sans unicode,lucida grande,sans-serif;"><span style="font-size:20px;">and Esher3D, responsible primarily for firmware and addons. We design and produce the Duet3D range of machine control electronics, along with the advanced RepRapFirmware and DuetWebControl software. Initially we focused on 3D printing control but are now branching out to other forms of motion and machine control.</span></span></p>
<div class="col-sm-12 col-md-6 bgImage" style="background-image: url(https://www.duet3d.com/image/catalog/banners/square_left_banner2_lighter.png); background-position:center; min-height:700px; text-align: right;background-color:rgba(255, 165, 0, 0.42); padding:0px 12px; overflow:hidden;">
<p><span><img alt="" class="img-responsive" src="https://www.duet3d.com/image/catalog/TCTshow.jpg" style="text-align:right; max-height: 200px;display: inline-flex;"/></span></p>
<h1><span>The Think3dPrint3d team</span></h1>
<div style="text-align: left;width: 65%;position: relative;margin-left: 30%;">
<p><span style="font-size: 16px;font-weight: bolder; color: #10183C;">Think3DPrint3D are a UK based family business formed in 2011. We have 8 years experience supplying components and complete 3D printer kits to individuals and resellers around the world, focussing primarily on OpenSource electronic hardware. We were one of the original developers of the Duet and now concentrate on the design and supply of Duet3D electronics.</span></p>
</div>
