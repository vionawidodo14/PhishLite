<h3 style="text-align: center;">Grow Your Brand With Every Click</h3>
<h4 style="text-align: center;">Branded links can drive a 34% higher click-through versus non-branded links, meaning they help get more eyeballs on your brand and its content.</h4>
</div>
</div>
</div>
<div class="inner-content grid-x grid-margin-x grid-padding-x align-top">
<div class="cell large-4">
<div class="content-padding"><p> </p>
<p><img alt="branded links" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-5316" height="400" src="https://docrdsfx76ssb.cloudfront.net/static/1571857277/pages/wp-content/uploads/2019/08/Homepage-Branded-Links-Illustration-1.png" width="720"/></p>
<h5 class="header-s"><b>Inspire trust</b></h5>
<p>As your click numbers go up, your brand recognition increases. And the more that grows, the more confident people become in the integrity of your content and communications. (It’s a wonderful cycle.)</p>
</div>
</div>
<div class="cell large-4">
<div class="content-padding"><p> </p>
<p><img alt="campaign analytics" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-5315" height="400" src="https://docrdsfx76ssb.cloudfront.net/static/1571857277/pages/wp-content/uploads/2019/08/Homepage-Branded-Links-Illustration-2.png" width="720"/></p>
<h5 class="header-s"><b>Boost results </b></h5>
<p>Better deliverability and improved click-through are just the start. Rich link-level data allows you to understand who is clicking your links, as well as when and where, so you can make smarter decisions around the content and communications you share.</p>
</div>
</div>
<div class="cell large-4">
<div class="content-padding"><p> </p>
<h5><img alt="auto branding" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-5314" height="400" src="https://docrdsfx76ssb.cloudfront.net/static/1571857277/pages/wp-content/uploads/2019/08/Homepage-Branded-Links-Illustration-3.png" width="720"/></h5>
<h5 class="header-s"><b>Gain control</b></h5>
<p>On top of being able to fully customize your links, auto-branding boosts awareness of your brand by giving you credit for your content and more insight into how it’s being consumed.</p>
</div>
</div>
