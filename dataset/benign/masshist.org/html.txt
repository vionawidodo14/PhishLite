<h2>Who We Are</h2>
<div class="cnode_label_Who We Are">

<a href="/collections"><h2>Our Collections</h2></a>
<a href="/highlights">
<h3>Collection Highlights</h3>
<p>View a selection of our favorite items from the MHS collection.</p>
</a>
<a href="/collections/online">
<h3>Online Resources</h3>
<p>Browse a list of our digital collections or limit your search by topic, format, or time period.</p>
</a>
<a href="/adams">
<h3>Adams Family Resources</h3>
<p>The Adams Family Papers consist of the writings of two presidents—John Adams and John Quincy Adams—and their families. View documents and transcriptions, biographies, a timeline, and more.</p>
</a>
<a href="/thomasjeffersonpapers">
<h3>Thomas Jefferson Papers</h3>
<p>The Coolidge Collection of Thomas Jefferson Manuscripts, containing almost 9,000 pages of correspondence, personal manuscript volumes, and more than 400 architectural drawings, is the largest gathering of the personal papers of our third president.</p>
</a>
<a href="/collections/descriptions">
<h3>Description of Our Collections</h3>
<p>Comprised of manuscripts, photographs, books, pamphlets, broadsides, maps, graphics, newspapers, works of art, and artifacts, MHS collections are unparalleled in their importance for the study of American history.</p>
</a>
