<p class="p1" style="text-align: justify;"><span class="s1">Μόττο τους τα λόγια του Πλάτωνα: «Η μουσική δίνει ψυχή στο σύμπαν, φτερά στο μυαλό, πτήση στη φαντασία, και τη γοητεία και τη ευθυμία στη ζωή και σε όλα»</span></p>
<hr/>
<p style="text-align: justify;">It is a web radio inspired by MR G born in Krestena Ilia.The beginning is related to his experiments in order to coverthe play list of Vanilla café for which he was responsible. It’s all about a channel dedicated to dance music, named Deep Flavors Channel. Passing the time and due to increasing dynamics, the inspirers decide to separate music and a new channel named Smooth Flavors Channel .</p>
<p>Deep Flavors Channel,  presents dj sets οf already successful and promising disc jockeys from all over the world as well as large and successful releases of all kinds of electronic music like nu disco, deep, soulful, tech house.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">In Smooth Flavors Channel, music moves into downtempo rhythms, smooth jazz, bossa, swing and other species, aiming toquality and endurance as well as incitement to new sounds. Additionally, collaborations with foreign and Greek artists are presented on an ongoing basis.</p>
<p>Nowdays, Vanilla Radio team can provide to you two new paid services, the <a href="https://www.vanillaradio.com/vanilla-premium-plans/">Vanilla Radio Premium</a> (music for premises without interruptions for spots or advertisements) and <a href="https://www.vanillaradio.com/premium-contact/">Vanilla Radio Dedicated</a> (personalized music channels with customer’s music on demand) at really competitive prices.</p>
