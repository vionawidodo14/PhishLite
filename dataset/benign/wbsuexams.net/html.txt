<td align="left" colspan="2" valign="top" width="71%">Friends:<br/>
                          I have joined West Bengal State University, Barasat as its Vice-Chancellor today. The University is located at a beautiful place where air is clean and fields are green. The vast expanse of water all around reminds “Dighi Kalojal” as described by Rabindranath in one of his poems. I have liked the surroundings. <br/>
<br/>
                          I had a chance of speaking to the teachers and they are full of enthusiasm to contribute to teaching and research. The officers are well-behaved and willing to work hard. The students are disciplined. The employees are gentle and dutiful. All these are necessary conditions for WBSU to grow and become a good University. However, WBSU requires continuous nurturing and a very compassionate leadership and I will try my best to provide that.<br/>
<br/>
                          Universities are established by Acts passed in the Assembly or in the Parliament, but they achieve eminence through commitment of teachers and students to the teaching – learning process, dedication, hard work and discipline. No nation can prosper without discipline. I will expect this trait of character from all concerned. <br/>
<br/>
                          Work is more important to me than word and I will try my best to bring laurels to the WBSU during the stint I work here. I am sure my friends will be with me in this journey.<br/>
<br/>
                          Best wishes.</td>
