<ul id="featured1"><li><a class="title" href="/annualmeeting/theme/">AOM 2020 Theme - 20/20: Broadening our Sight</a><br/><p></p><h2 style="text-align: center;">80th Annual Meeting of the Academy of
Management</h2>
<p style="text-align: center;"><img align="null" height="256" src="/uploadedImages/Meetings/annualmeeting/2020/AoM-2020-logo.jpg" style="width: 200px; height: 256px;" width="200"/></p>
<p align="center"><span>August 7-11, 2020 | Vancouver, British
Columbia, Canada</span></p>
<p align="center"><span>Vice President and Program Chair: <strong>Herman Aguinis</strong>, The George Washington University</span></p>
<p align="center"><strong><span>Theme</span></strong><span>: 20/20: Broadening our Sight</span></p>
<p>The most
pressing challenges in the 21<sup>st</sup> century are directly or indirectly related
to management and organizations: conflict, discrimination, corruption,
wellbeing, economic opportunity and equality, and climate change. At the
same time, there are important challenges that we are facing in our own
profession such as changing models and innovations for teaching and learning,
increasing pressure to publish in “A” journals, tensions over the MBA market,
and the evolving nature of the university business model. We, members of the
Academy of Management are in a privileged position to produce and disseminate knowledge
to address these challenges.</p>
<p>However,
there are dichotomies that stand in the way of producing actionable knowledge
to address these monumental challenges. In many cases, these are <em>self-imposed</em> <strong><em>dichotomies</em></strong> that have
emerged over time and produced strong professional and personal identities. In
turn, these barriers often prevent us from creating and sharing knowledge that
would create value for individuals, organizations, society, and our own
profession. Examples of these dichotomies are embracing micro <em>or</em> macro theories and research, using qualitative
<em>or</em> quantitative methods, focusing on knowledge
creation <em>or</em> knowledge dissemination, emphasizing
research <em>or</em> teaching, highlighting rigor
<em>or</em> relevance, and so <a href="/annualmeeting/theme/"> [more...]</a></p></li></ul><ul class="hide" id="featured2" style="position: relative; top: 0px; left: 0px; display: none;"><li><a class="title" href="/Publications/Annals/ANNALS-News.aspx">ANNALS News</a><br/><p><strong>Academy of Management <em>Annals</em>: The highest impact, peer-reviewed management journal!</strong><br/></p><p>Academy of Management <em>Annals</em> is an essential piece of the totality of the AOM portfolio of publications, and we are excited to be able to highlight the unique content which makes Annals the top journal in its field. You will see a <a href="http://aom.org/Publications/Annals/Changes-in-Volume-11.aspx">number of changes</a> to <em>Annals</em>, effective immediately, with the new edition of <em>Annals</em> for 2017, Volume 11. First, we have streamlined the AOM member online content experience by officially adding <em>Annals</em> to our internal journal content platform. Second, beginning with Volume 11, the Academy of Management will add <em>Annals</em> to the portfolio of our self-published titles, thus bringing to a close our relationship with <em>Annals</em>' previous publisher, Taylor &amp; Francis. Third, we have also redesigned the <em>Annals</em> format—we have brought it into alignment with our other journals with a new cover and paperback binding. And finally, beginning with Volume 11 (2017), <em>Annals</em> will publish two issues per year, the first issue (Volume 11, Issue 1) in January 2017 followed by Volume 11, Issue 2 in August 2017. Stay tuned for more exciting news to come with <em>Annals</em> in the coming year!</p>
<p>Because of its origins as an invitation-only, once-per-year hardback volume, the question often arises regarding how to count an <em>Annals</em> publication on one's CV. We are often asked whether the articles are book chapters or journal articles, and whether they are peer-reviewed.</p>
<p>The answer is simple:  <em>Annals</em> articles are peer-reviewed journal articles.  But many schools and promotion and tenure committees are unaware of this. <u>So spread the word</u>!</p>
<p><u>Why the confusion?</u> Volumes 1-8 of Annals were not peer reviewed.  Beginning with Volume 9, <em>Annals</em> changed its submission and review process from invited <a href="/Publications/Annals/ANNALS-News.aspx"> [more...]</a></p></li></ul><ul class="hide" id="featured3" style="position: relative; top: 0px; left: 0px; display: none;"><li><a class="title" href="/Meetings/Specialized-Conferences-Initiative.aspx">Specialized Conferences Initiative</a><br/><p></p><p><img alt="Specialized Conferences" src="/uploadedImages/Meetings/Specialized_Conferences/IconicSCIimage610x132.jpg" title="Specialized Conferences"/></p>
<p> </p>
<blockquote><h2>AOM Initiative: Specialized Conferences</h2>
<p>The Academy of Management’s Specialized Conferences feature a portfolio of smaller, meaningful, and sustainable new meeting opportunities, designed to offer deep dialogue and interactions through intimate, highly-focused gatherings.</p>
<p><a alt="Responsible Leadership" href="http://slovenia.aom.org" title="Responsible Leadership">Responsible Leadership in Rising Economies</a> 23-25 October 2019, Bled, Slovenia
<br/>
This conference looks at what “responsible leadership” means in the context of a rapidly changing world, particularly in rising and transforming economies. How can the scholarly management community -- with businesses, policy makers, NGOs, media, and other stakeholders -- best support the development of responsible management and sustainable innovation?</p>
<p><a alt="Advancing Management Research in Latin America" href="http://mexico.aom.org" title="Advancing Management Research in Latin America">Advancing Management Research in Latin America</a> 15-17 April 2020, Mexico City, Mexico
<br/>
This event seeks to foster the development of research from Latin American scholars and institutions and to attract global authorship to focus on relevant issues in the Latin America context. Academic activities will take place in English, Spanish and Portuguese to provide an immersive experience that will foster the development of lasting research networks.</p>
<p>Learn More: <a href="https://www.eiseverywhere.com/ehome/aomsci/" title="AOM's Specialized Conferences Initiative">AOM's Specialized Conferences Initiative</a></p>
