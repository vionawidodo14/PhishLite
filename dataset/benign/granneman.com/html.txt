<p><strong>Scott Granneman</strong> is an <a href="/writing/" title="Writing">author</a>, <a href="/teaching">educator</a>, &amp; partner in a Web development company in St. Louis, Missouri. Scott has written six books, contributed to two, and penned monthly columns for SecurityFocus and <em>Linux Magazine</em>. He is an Adjunct Professor at Washington University in St. Louis &amp; Webster University, <a href="/teaching/washingtonuniversity">teaching courses</a> on technology, security, web development, &amp; the Internet. As a Principal of <a href="http://www.websanity.com/">WebSanity</a>, he plans, develops, and hosts websites for clients in <span style="text-decoration: dotted;" title="CT, GA, IL, MA, MO, MN, MS, NJ, NY, PA, SC, TX">12</span> states using an open source, UNIX-based <a href="http://www.concrete5.org">content management system</a>. He has given <a href="/presentations">presentations</a> all over the United States, and loves speaking to people.</p>
