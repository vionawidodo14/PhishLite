<h1 class="lines">About Thompson &amp; Morgan</h1>
<p>Based in Ipswich, Suffolk since 1855, Thompson &amp; Morgan is the UK's most successful horticultural mail order company. It is the only mail order seed and plant specialist to develop its own lines of <a href="https://www.thompson-morgan.com/flowers-plants-shrubs/plants">plants</a> in the UK. Due to the continued success of its breeding programme, the company has introduced more species and varieties to the British gardening public than any other mail order company in the industry. Its product range includes an award-winning choice of <a href="https://www.thompson-morgan.com/seeds">seeds</a>, <a href="https://www.thompson-morgan.com/plug-plants">plug plants</a>, bulbs, seed potatoes, onion and garlic sets, soft fruit and <a href="https://www.thompson-morgan.com/fruit/fruit-trees">fruit trees</a>, as well as an extensive range of gardening equipment and outdoor living products.</p>
