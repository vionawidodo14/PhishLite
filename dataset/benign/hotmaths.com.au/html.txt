<h2>About Cambridge HOTmaths</h2>
<p><i>Cambridge HOTmaths</i> is a comprehensive mathematics learning system – an
                            interactive online maths learning, teaching and assessment resource for students and
                            teachers, for individuals or whole classes, for school and at home.</p>
<p>A suite of flexible resources (printable and online) is provided for all types of classrooms
                            ranging from those with no technology to full laptop access, and includes projectable
                            material in all lessons for interactive and non-interactive whiteboards.</p>
<p>Developed by a dedicated group of educators, technicians and designers, <i>Cambridge
                            HOTmaths</i> is a high-quality maths resource for students and teachers.</p>
<p>
                            Since joining forces with Cambridge University Press in 2009, a range of products have been published with integrated digital resources powered by Cambridge HOTmaths, including <i>Essential Mathematics</i>, <i>Cambridge Senior Mathematics</i>, <i>ICE-EM Mathematics Third Edition</i> and <i>CambridgeMATHS Stage 6</i>. These products deliver a host of interactive features to enhance the teaching and learning experience, and when connected to a class teacher account offer a powerful Learning Management System.
                        </p>

<h1>About Cambridge HOTmaths</h1>
<br/>
<p><i>Cambridge HOTmaths</i> is a comprehensive mathematics learning system – an interactive online maths learning, teaching and assessment resource for students and teachers, for individuals or whole classes, for school and at home.</p>
<p>A suite of flexible resources (printable and online) are provided for all types of classrooms ranging from those with no technology to full laptop access, and includes projectable material in all lessons for interactive and non-interactive whiteboards.</p>
<p>Developed by a dedicated group of educators, technicians and designers, <i>Cambridge HOTmaths</i> is a high quality maths resource for students and teachers.</p>
<p>In September 2009 Cambridge and HOTmaths combined forces to offer cutting-edge technology and best practice to support mathematics teaching and learning in Australian and New Zealand schools.</p>
<p>Cambridge University Press has since published a range of products with integrated digital resources powered by Cambridge HOTmaths, including <i>Essential Mathematics</i>, <i>Cambridge Senior Mathematics</i>, <i>ICE-EM Mathematics Third Edition</i> and <i>CambridgeMATHS Stage 6</i>. These products deliver a host of interactive features to enhance the teaching and learning experience, and when connected to a class teacher account offer a powerful Learning Management System.</p>
<p>Cambridge University Press has been publishing high-quality curriculum-based materials for school students for over 150 years. It publishes across a range of subject areas including Mathematics, Business and Commerce, Information Technology, History and English.</p>
<p><i>Cambridge HOTmaths</i> is committed to the future of education in the region, and will continue to develop exceptional resources that reflect specific syllabus requirements and broader curriculum developments and incorporate educational change and technological innovation.</p>
<p>To learn more about Cambridge University Press visit <a href="http://www.cambridge.edu.au/education" target="_blank">www.cambridge.edu.au/education</a></p>
