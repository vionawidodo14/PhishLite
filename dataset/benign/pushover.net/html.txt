      <a href="/clients/desktop"><strong>Desktop Browser</strong></a> clients,
      you can receive unlimited push notifications on all of your devices from
      dozens of <a href="/apps">websites, services, and applications</a> that
      already integrate with Pushover.  Just supply your <a href="https://support.pushover.app/i7-what-is-pushover">Pushover User
      Key</a> or your <a href="https://support.pushover.app/i29-email">Pushover
      e-mail address</a> and you'll be getting push notifications in an instant.
      </p>
<h2 class="light" style="margin-top: 1em; text-align: center;">
        Pushover for Teams
      </h2>
<p style="margin-top: 1em;">
<a href="/teams"><strong>Pushover for Teams</strong></a> is a monthly
      service offering for organizations sending messages to multiple users and
      includes a number of extra features such as user management and failover
      SMS message delivery.  Pricing is per month, per user, and more
      information can be found on our <a href="/teams">Teams page</a>.
      </p>
<h2 class="light" style="margin-top: 1em; text-align: center;">
        Pushover for Everyone
      </h2>
<p style="margin-top: 1em;">
      Individuals and organizations not needing our Team features can use
      Pushover for Android, iOS, and Desktop for a simple <a href="https://support.pushover.app/i8-cost"><strong>one-time</strong>
      in-app purchase</a> on each platform where you need it, after a
      <strong>7-day free trial</strong>.  Invididuals
      can use Pushover with no subscription fees.
      </p>
<h2 class="light" style="margin-top: 1em; text-align:center">
        Simple Integration
      </h2>
<p style="margin-top: 1em; margin-bottom: 2em;">
      For developers, system administrators, and everyone with just some
      technical savvy, our <a href="/api"><strong>API</strong></a> makes it
      easy to integrate Pushover into your web app, network monitor, shell
      script, and anything else you can think of to send notifications to
      yourself or thousands of users.  Pushing messages is as easy as using the
      HTTP libraries available in <a href="https://support.pushover.app/i44-examples">nearly every programming
      language</a> with no custom modules required.
      </p>
