</div>

<div class="row" style="height: 450px;">
<div class="middle-content-box three columns">
<img alt="Screenshot of Event Management" src="images/srcnsht-1.png" style="width:100%"/>
<h4>Event Management</h4>
<p>Post all of your chapter's events all in one place with APOonline's event
								management system. Attach requirement credit such as service hours to events 
								which will be awarded to attendees once the event has been completed.
								</p>
</div>
<div class="middle-content-box three columns">
<img alt="Screenshot of Membership Manager" src="images/srcnsht-2.png" style="width:100%"/>
<h4>Membership Manager</h4>
<p>Each brother will create their own profile complete with picture and contact information to 
								be added to a single centralized membership roster. As brothers graduate the beginnings of an 
								alumni database is formed.
								</p>
</div>
<div class="middle-content-box three columns">
<img alt="Screenshot of Brother Profiles" src="images/srcnsht-3.png" style="width:100%"/>
<h4>Brother Profiles</h4>
<p>View any APO brother's profile within the APOonline community and send them a message even 
								outside of your chapter. Contact chapter presidents across the country with APOonline's brother profiles!
								</p>
</div>
<div class="middle-content-box three columns">
<img alt="Screenshot of Additional Features" src="images/srcnsht-4.png" style="width:100%"/>
<h4>and much more...</h4>
<p>Chapter budgeting, collecting dues online, chapter awards, blog, photo albums, 
								meeting attendance, minutes, CoE and PPoE progress tracking, membership and event reports just to 
								name a few.
								</p>
