<div class="wpcom-reblog-snapshot"> <div class="reblog-post"><p class="reblog-from"><span class="avatar-container"><img alt="" class="avatar avatar-32 grav-hashed grav-hijack" height="32" id="grav-031af6596136ba5d987556e179205439-0" src="https://0.gravatar.com/avatar/031af6596136ba5d987556e179205439?s=32&amp;d=identicon&amp;r=G" width="32"/></span><a href="https://geraldineubeda.wordpress.com/2019/04/13/self-correction-and-recording-speaking-activities-fce-speaking-part-2/">Geraldine teacher</a></p><div class="reblogged-content">
<p>Speaking by its very nature is ephemeral. As a teacher, it’s the skill that I find most difficult to correct. Do I interrupt and impact on fluency? Or should I wait until the end and board some of the most common errors? Either way, I seem to correct the same errors again and again. The students can often identify the mistakes they’ve made relatively easily but they continue to produce the errors. Those are just the grammar and vocabulary issues. What about the task itself? In a group of 12 students, when perhaps 6 people are completing the task simultaneously, I can’t tune in to them all at the same time. I often ask the partner to evaluate, but 99% of the time they lack the confidence to say anything other then it was ‘good’.</p>
<p>Ideally, I’d love to give detailed feedback for each individual every time we do this…</p>

<span class="posted-on">Posted on <a href="https://freeenglishlessonplans.com/2019/03/21/off-menu-podcast-listening-sindhu-vee/" rel="bookmark"><time class="entry-date published" datetime="2019-03-21T22:55:05+00:00">March 21, 2019</time><time class="updated" datetime="2019-03-21T22:55:09+00:00">March 21, 2019</time></a></span><span class="byline"> by <span class="author vcard"><a class="url fn n" href="https://freeenglishlessonplans.com/author/timwarre/">Tim Warre</a></span></span> </div><!-- .entry-meta -->

<p data-adtags-visited="true"><img alt="Image result for off menu podcast" class="" height="218" src="https://mediacdn.acast.com/assets/b19ac1f5-6adf-4c8b-aa1a-2af2160f99e4/-joo50o5o-off-menu-podcast-final-web.jpg" width="218"/></p>
<p data-adtags-visited="true">Image credit: <a href="https://play.acast.com/s/offmenu/" rel="nofollow">https://play.acast.com/s/offmenu/</a></p>
<p data-adtags-visited="true">This is a lesson plan for B2+ students based around a clip from the brilliant Off Menu Podcast presented by Ed Gamble and James Acaster. The idea is to use the listening exercise as a way to encourage students to listen to podcasts for pleasure outside class. Download the student handout and answer key below:</p>
<p data-adtags-visited="true"><a href="https://freeenglishlessonplans.files.wordpress.com/2019/03/off-menu-podcast.docx" title="Off Menu Podcast">Off Menu Podcast</a></p>
<p data-adtags-visited="true">The Off Menu Podcast is hosted by comedians Ed Gamble and James Acaster. Every week a different guest comes onto the show and talks about their ultimate dream meal. The meal must include a starter, main course, side dish, dessert and drink. There are no limits to what each dish could be, some guests have chosen things as varied as McDonald’s fries and Pizza Hut pizza or their mum’s Christmas dinner or a traditional Indian curry.</p>
<p data-adtags-visited="true">This week’s guest is the Indian comedian Sindhu Vee. Sindhu was born in India and has also lived in the Philippines; she now lives in London with her Danish husband.</p>
<p data-adtags-visited="true"><em>Predict with your partner: You’re going to listen to the part where Sindhu chooses her dream main course. What do you think she chooses?</em></p>
<p class="inline-ad-slot" data-adtags-visited="true" id="inline-ad-0" style="display:block;height:12px;margin-left:auto;margin-right:auto;overflow:hidden;width:60px;"><div style="height:2px;width:60px;"><iframe frameborder="0" height="2" scrolling="no" style="border:none;margin:0;overflow:hidden;width:60px;height:2px;" width="60"></iframe></div><div class="ata-controls" id="inline-ad-0__controls" style="height:10px;width:60px;"><span class="ata-controls__complain-btn" id="inline-ad-0__complain-btn" style="display: inline;">Report this ad</span></div></p><p data-adtags-visited="true"><a href="https://play.acast.com/s/offmenu/ep15-sindhuvee">Link to the episode.</a> Timing: 29:49-32:50</p>
<h2>Listen for Gist</h2>
<p data-adtags-visited="true">First listen: What was the dish?</p>
<h2>Listen for Detail</h2>
<p data-adtags-visited="true">2<sup>nd</sup> listen:</p>
<ol>
<li>Where did she try the dish?</li>
<li>Who made the dish?</li>
<li>How did the different members of her family react to the chef’s driving?</li>
<li>What surprised her about Sicilian food?</li>
<li>What was her husband’s reaction to the dish?</li>
<li>What really impressed her about the dish?</li>
</ol>
<h2>Language focus</h2>
<p data-adtags-visited="true"><em>Look at the expressions Sindhu uses, what do you think they mean?</em></p>
<p data-adtags-visited="true">“Maniacal driving”</p>
<p data-adtags-visited="true">“I had to keep face”</p>
<p data-adtags-visited="true">“Not cognisant of the world around him.”</p>
<p data-adtags-visited="true">“You have a fight and flight response.”</p>
<p data-adtags-visited="true">“You’re gonna fight through to the taste.”</p>
<h2>Follow up</h2>
<p data-adtags-visited="true">Listen to the rest of the episode for homework, what does Sindhu choose for the other parts of her dream menu?</p>
<h2>Answer Key</h2>
<h2>Listen for Gist</h2>
<p data-adtags-visited="true">First listen: What was the dish? <em>Pasta arrabbiata with angel hair pasta</em></p>
<h2>Listen for Detail</h2>
<p data-adtags-visited="true">2<sup>nd</sup> listen:</p>
<ol>
<li>Where did she try the dish? <em>Near Florence</em></li>
<li>Who made the dish? <em>Mr Mancini the boss of the hotel</em></li>
<li>How did the different members of her family react to the chef’s driving? <em>She was fine, her husband was scared, her kids loved it</em></li>
<li>What surprised her about Sicilian food? <em>How spicy it was</em></li>
<li>What was her husband’s reaction to the dish? <em>He went blind</em></li>
<li>What really impressed her about the dish? <em>How it maintained its flavour despite being so spicy</em></li>
</ol>
<h2>Language focus</h2>
<p data-adtags-visited="true"><em>Look at the expressions Sindhu uses, what do you think they mean?</em></p>
<p class="inline-ad-slot" data-adtags-visited="true" id="inline-ad-1" style="display:block;height:12px;margin-left:auto;margin-right:auto;overflow:hidden;width:60px;"><div style="height:2px;width:60px;"><iframe frameborder="0" height="2" scrolling="no" style="border:none;margin:0;overflow:hidden;width:60px;height:2px;" width="60"></iframe></div><div class="ata-controls" id="inline-ad-1__controls" style="height:10px;width:60px;"><span class="ata-controls__complain-btn" id="inline-ad-1__complain-btn" style="display: inline;">Report this ad</span></div></p><p data-adtags-visited="true">“Maniacal driving” – driving like a crazy person</p>

<p data-adtags-visited="true"><a href="https://freeenglishlessonplans.files.wordpress.com/2019/03/2ts-in-a-pod-food-listening.docx" title="2Ts in a Pod Food listening">2Ts in a Pod Food listening</a></p>

<p class="inline-ad-slot" data-adtags-visited="true" id="inline-ad-2" style="display:block;height:12px;margin-left:auto;margin-right:auto;overflow:hidden;width:60px;"><div style="height:2px;width:60px;"><iframe frameborder="0" height="2" scrolling="no" style="border:none;margin:0;overflow:hidden;width:60px;height:2px;" width="60"></iframe></div><div class="ata-controls" id="inline-ad-2__controls" style="height:10px;width:60px;"><span class="ata-controls__complain-btn" id="inline-ad-2__complain-btn" style="display: inline;">Report this ad</span></div></p><p data-adtags-visited="true"><strong>Student Handout</strong></p>
<p data-adtags-visited="true"><strong>Pre-listening</strong></p>
<p data-adtags-visited="true">1. Discuss these questions with your partner:<br/>
• What’s your favourite comfort food?<br/>
• What do you think a ‘supper club’ is?<br/>
• What do you know about food from the USA? What are some traditional American dishes?</p>
<p data-adtags-visited="true">2. First listen</p>
<p data-adtags-visited="true">We’re going to listen to Nicole talk about her supper club. What kind of food does she serve?</p>
<p data-adtags-visited="true">3. Second listen: listen again and answer the following questions.</p>
<p data-adtags-visited="true">1. How long has Nicole been living in Barcelona?<br/>
2. What did Nicole serve with the brisket?<br/>
3. Do the supper club guests usually know each other?<br/>
4. Where did gumbo come from?<br/>
5. Which cultures does the dish gumbo mix?<br/>
6. What’s the first thing you do when making gumbo?<br/>
7. What is the holy trinity?<br/>
8. What were the main ingredients of the gumbo that Nicole made?<br/>
9. How does Katy express that she likes the sound of Nicole’s gumbo?</p>
<p data-adtags-visited="true">4. Look at this quote from the audio and discuss its meaning with your partner.</p>
<p data-adtags-visited="true">“I love to gather people around the table that are from different walks of life”</p>
<p data-adtags-visited="true"><strong>Answer key:</strong></p>

3. Do the supper club guests usually know each other? <strong>no</strong><br/>
