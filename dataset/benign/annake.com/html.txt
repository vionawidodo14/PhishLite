<h2 class="rich-text__heading">ANNAKE X MOTHER NATURE</h2>
<div class="rich-text__text--medium">
<p>At <em>Annake Swimwear</em>, we understand the critical impact that the fashion industry has on our environment and we understand our responsibility as a brand to maintain transparency from design to manufacture. <em>Annake Swimwear</em> is proudly Australian made and owned on the Gold Coast, with a core focus on quality, versatility and style without compromising our environment. Our swimwear is made from a mix of recycled post-consumer fabrics and ethically sourced materials, printed locally and in collaboration with local artists. We choose to post our products in compostable packaging sourced from <a class="text-link" href="https://betterpackaging.co/">The Better Packaging Co.</a> with all packaging kept to a minimum and plastic free! We are proud to produce locally and ethically sourced, high quality and environmentally conscious swimwear. Our team at <em>Annake</em> are always looking for more ways to minimise our environmental footprint.</p>
