Ad Network mediation services send out ad requests on behalf of your website in order to find those ad networks that are the most likely to maximize fill rates and increase monetization potential.</p><p class="prg-6">Ad network performance varies, depending on the geographic location and industry type. Therefore, certain ad networks will work great for some publishers, and be less effective for others. Ad network mediation helps to identify the networks that will work best for that site’s specific characteristics.</p><div class="read-more"><a href="https://www.adspyglass.com/blog/what-is-ad-mediation-and-how-does-it-work/" target="_blank">Read more</a></div></div></div></nav></div><div class="faq---line"></div><div aria-labelled-by="w-dropdown-toggle-2" class="faq---dropdown w-dropdown" data-delay="0" role="menu"><div aria-controls="w-dropdown-list-2" aria-haspopup="menu" class="faq---dropdown-toggle w-dropdown-toggle" id="w-dropdown-toggle-2" style="outline: none;" tabindex="0"><div class="icon-2 w-icon-dropdown-toggle"></div><div class="question-title">How does it work?</div></div><nav class="faq---dropdown-list w-dropdown-list" id="w-dropdown-list-2" style="height: 0px;"><div class="div-block-4"><div class="text-wrapper"><p class="prg-6">Let’s take a look at how ad network networks normally operate. When you want a specific ad spot filled, a request is sent out to the ad network. The ad network searches its database to match the site to the advertiser with the most potential for profit. Once that advertiser is identified, the network keeps serving the advertiser’s ads to the site, until it displays all available ads. Once all ads have been displayed, the network will then move on to displaying ads to other sites based in order of revenue potential. This is known as the “waterfall” method.</p><p class="prg-6">While this method works well for advertisers, publishers are not always able to get the highest amount of revenue possible for each ad spot. This is because the network will display ads on the site determined to have the greatest revenue potential, despite how much the publisher is willing to pay for that spot.</p><p class="prg-6">However, ad network mediation uses a “weighted” system which prioritizes sites based on how much they are willing to pay for a particular spot, along with other factors such as a site’s geographic location and industry niche. Also, by connecting the publisher to a vast selection of ad networks, the mediation service is able to determine the best possible creative for the site.
