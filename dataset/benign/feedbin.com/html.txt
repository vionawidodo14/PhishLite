<section class="panel">
<div class="container">
<header class="panel-header">
<h2 class="with-accent">Organize, Manage &amp; Share</h2>
<div class="grid">
<p class="subhead-2 span-12 span-8-break-medium">Stay organized, share your faves, and find that really great article you read last month.</p>
</div>
<div class="grid feature-grab-bag">
<div class="feature icon-actions">
<h3>Actions</h3>
<p>Actions help you manage incoming articles. Using actions, you can automatically star, mark as read or send a push notification on the articles you want.</p>
</div>
<div class="feature icon-search">
<h3>Search</h3>
<p>Feedbin supports a powerful and expressive search syntax to find exactly what you're your looking for.</p>
</div>
<div class="feature icon-newsletters">
<h3>Newsletters</h3>
<p>Every account comes with a secret email address so you can unclutter your inbox by <a href="https://feedbin.com/blog/2016/02/03/subscribe-to-email-newsletters-in-feedbin/">receiving email newsletters</a> in Feedbin.</p>
</div>
<div class="feature icon-share">
<h3>Sharing</h3>
<p>Configurable sharing and read-it-later services let you decide what services you want to use. With built-in support for all of the most popular services out there.</p>
</div>
<div class="feature icon-full-text">
<h3>Full-Text</h3>
<p>Feedbin can help get full-text of an article for feeds that only offer partial-content. This way you can keep reading without leaving Feedbin.</p>
</div>
<div class="feature icon-podcasts">
<h3>Podcasts</h3>
<p>Listen to podcasts in Feedbin. Feedbin remembers your place so you can pick up where you left off.</p>
</div>
<div class="feature icon-tag">
<h3>Tags</h3>
<p>Tags are perfect for grouping related feeds. You can create as many tags as you need and even apply multiple tags to a single feed.</p>
</div>
<div class="feature icon-saved-search">
<h3>Saved Searches</h3>
<p>If there's something you're searching for frequently you can used saved searches and the results will always be a click away.</p>
</div>
<div class="feature icon-recently-read">
<h3>Recently Read</h3>
<p>Know you just read something but can't remember where it was from? Recently Read can help by maintaining a history of just the articles you've actually read.</p>
</div>
<div class="feature icon-updated">
<h3>Updated Articles</h3>
<p>Articles are updated whenever the original changes so you don't miss any important changes. You can even see the differences to know what changed.</p>
</div>
<div class="feature icon-format-toolbar">
<h3>Format Toolbar</h3>
<p>Customize Feedbin's appearance to your liking. Change the typeface, font size, theme and choose between a fluid layout or fixed width.</p>
</div>
<div class="feature icon-image-preview">
<h3>Image Preview</h3>
<p>The best quality images are shown alongside article summaries. Feedbin uses intelligent cropping to keep the subject front-and-center.</p>
</div>
</div>
