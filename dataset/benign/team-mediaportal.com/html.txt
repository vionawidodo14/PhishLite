<div class="next"></div><div class="prev"></div> <div class="caption" style="display: block;"><h3 style="color: #fff;margin-top: 0px;">Welcome to MediaPortal</h3>
<p><strong>MediaPortal</strong> is a free open source media center software, ideal for turning your PC into a very advanced multimedia center, or <strong>Home Theater PC</strong>, to view all  your digital media in your living room. </p></div><ul class="captions" style="display: none;"><li><h3 style="color: #fff;margin-top: 0px;">Welcome to MediaPortal</h3>
<p><strong>MediaPortal</strong> is a free open source media center software, ideal for turning your PC into a very advanced multimedia center, or <strong>Home Theater PC</strong>, to view all  your digital media in your living room. </p></li><li><h3 style="color: #fff;margin-top: 0px;">TV and PVR Support</h3>
<p>MediaPortal offers you the most powerful TV Engine and PVR (Personal Video Recorder) available today. You can do everything a TiVo can, and more, for free!</p></li><li><h3 style="color: #fff;margin-top: 0px;">Videos, DVD and Blu-Ray</h3>
<p>View or sort your movies however you wish: by title, actors, genre, year, or even sort by rating or file size. Choose from several display styles: List, Icons/Big Icons (Cover view), or Filmstrip</p></li><li><h3 style="color: #fff;margin-top: 0px;">It's Easy to Use</h3>
<p>The user interface is a snap to learn and use. Standard menus, layouts and views let you choose your style of display. Most remotes are supported and you can configure the keys however you want.</p></li><li><h3 style="color: #fff;margin-top: 0px;">A Tweaker's Dream</h3>
<p>As open source software licensed under the GNU/GPL agreement, <strong>MediaPortal is free!</strong> 
That means you can contribute to it, or tweak it to your heart's content. We don't just allow it, we count on it!</p></li><li><h3 style="color: #fff;margin-top: 0px;">Unleash the Power</h3>
<p>MediaPortal was designed to be extended. There are hundreds of amazing plugins available to do almost anything you can imagine, even stream web TV, Apple Trailers, Youtube videos or internet radio.</p></li><li><h3 style="color: #fff;margin-top: 0px;">For Music Lovers</h3>
<p>Audiophiles will love the DSP (Digital Signal Processing) and FX options for gain and compression as well as the ability to incorporate Winamp and VST plugins, not to mention all the visualizations supported.</p></li><li><h3 style="color: #fff;margin-top: 0px;">MediaPortal Does So Much More</h3>
<p>Connect all your PCs and stream music, video, live or recorded radio and TV throughout your home.  With MediaPortal you can do so much more!  Everything else is just a Media Center.</p></li></ul>

<h1>Welcome to the best media experience with MediaPortal!</h1>
<p><strong>MediaPortal</strong> transforms your PC into a complete media solution. <br/>It runs on basic hardware, connects directly to your TV and displays your TV Series, Movies, Photos and Music in a much more dynamic way. All in the comfort of your living room, on your big screen LCD or Plasma TV or projector!</p>
<div style="padding-left: 90px;">
<ul>
<li><span style="font-size: 95%;">Watch, schedule and record live <a href="/mediaportal-features/television" target="_self" title="TV">TV</a> - like a TiVo, but more, and for free!</span></li>
<li><span style="font-size: 95%;">Play <a href="/mediaportal-features/video-dvd" target="_self" title="Videos, Movies, DVDs">videos, movies, DVDs and Blu-ray discs </a></span></li>
<li><span style="font-size: 95%;">Listen to <a href="/mediaportal-features/music" target="_self" title="Music">music</a> and <a href="/mediaportal-features/radio" target="_self" title="Radio">radio</a></span></li>
<li><span style="font-size: 95%;">Enjoy <a href="/mediaportal-features/pictures" target="_self" title="Pictures">pictures</a>, home videos or create a slideshow</span></li>
<li><span style="font-size: 95%;"><a href="/mediaportal/about-mediaportal" target="_self" title="Stream over Network">Stream</a> media, radio and TV to any HTPC / PC connected to your network </span></li>
<li>Use a <a href="/mediaportal/about-mediaportal" target="_self" title="Remotes">remote</a> to control your HTPC from your couch</li>
<li><span style="font-size: 95%;">Check the <a href="/mediaportal-features/weather-news-info-and-more" target="_self" title="Weather">weather</a>, news, and more</span></li>
<li><span style="font-size: 95%;">Access MediaPortal from the <a href="/mediaportal-features/mediaportal-features-mobile" target="_blank" title="Web or Mobile Access">web or mobile device</a></span></li>
</ul>
</div>
<hr/>
<p>That’s only the basic features! By using the hundreds of <a href="/mediaportal-features/plugins" target="_self" title="Plugins">plugins</a> and <a href="/mediaportal-features/skins" target="_self" title="Skins">skins</a> available, you can do almost anything—like monitor your <strong>HTPC</strong>; view sports scores or local movie show times; stream or download web TV, Apple Trailers, YouTube videos or internet radio; even start up MediaPortal playing music from a predefined playlist.</p>
<p><strong>MediaPortal</strong> is quite simply a revolution in media control, it allows you to watch, listen, read and play your media as you want to, without getting in the way, or having to learn difficult navigations systems.</p>
<p>Just view our <a href="/mediaportal/features-overview">Features</a> pages , visit our <a href="/mediaportal-screenshots/skins-gallery">Skins Gallery</a> or check out all the <a href="/extensions">plugins</a> and see for yourself.<br/>MediaPortal is for free but  we would be thankful if you <a href="/support/donate-give-funds-or-gifts" target="_blank">support our team with a donation</a>. </p>
