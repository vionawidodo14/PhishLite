<p>Royal brothers was started in July 2015 with the intent to make bike rentals easier, affordable and convenient for everyone. But the same option wasn’t available when it came to all other cities in India. We started bike rental in Bangalore to begin with. We started with 5 Royal Enfields for rent within a small office in Indranagar. It was humble beginnings for us. We wanted to replicate the brotherhood that riders generally have with our service offerings. Hence the name Royal Brothers.
    </p><p>

      </p>
