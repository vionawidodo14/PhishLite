<h2>Important Notice:</h2>
<p class="text-lg">Marygrove College will close permanently, effective December 17, 2019.  For the complete public announcement of the closure, please <a href="https://graduate.marygrove.edu/campus-news/marygrove-college-to-close-after-92-years-in-detroit">click here</a>.</p>
<div style="text-align:left">
<ul type="disc">
<li>Questions of importance to       students can be addressed by the Registrar’s Office until December 17,       2019, at (313) 927-1260 or <a href="mailto:registrar@marygrove.edu">registrar@marygrove.edu</a>. </li>
</ul>
<ul type="disc">
<li>Questions related to Marygrove College buildings and facilities can be addressed to Jim Vetter of Beanstalk Real Estate Solutions, the campus property management company, at <a href="mailto:jimvetter@BeanstalkRES.com">jimvetter@BeanstalkRES.com</a> or (313) 218-3329 or (313) 927-1207.</li>
</ul>
<ul type="disc">
<li>On-campus activities of Marygrove’s new Center for Detroit Arts and Culture at Marygrove (CDAC@Marygrove), which includes the Institute of Music and Dance, Institute for Arts-Infused Education, Contemporary American Authors Lecture Series, Institute for Detroit Studies/Defining Detroit, and the Tuxedo Literary Arts Center, have been funded until December 2020. CDAC@Marygrove will inform the community of future programs and plans via various communications media.</li>
</ul>
<ul type="disc">
<li>Marygrove College announces the opening of its Online Logo Item Store now through December 23, 2019. <a href="https://shopmarygrove.merchorders.com/" target="new">Please click here to conveniently shop online.</a></li>
</ul>
<ul type="disc">
<li>Due to several changes on the Marygrove Campus, including the arrival of high school students, security procedures have been tightened for everyone's safety. Please make an appointment with a Marygrove staff or faculty member in advance of your visit to campus so that Campus Security can be informed. If you have questions, please call Campus Security at (313) 927-1411.</li>
</ul>
<ul type="disc"><li>Marygrove College office hours are Monday through Friday, 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. However, the Business Office, Registrar's Office and the Pop-Up Store have reduced hours as follows: Monday through Thursday, 9 a.m. to 1 p.m. and closed Friday.</li></ul>
