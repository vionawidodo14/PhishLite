But, of all the end-time themes revealed in the Bible—such as global disasters,<span class="Footnote-Reference"><span id="footnote-050-backlink"><a class="_idFootnoteLink _idGenColorInherit" href="#footnote-050"><sup>1</sup></a></span></span> the rise of a global super-state,<span class="Footnote-Reference"><span id="footnote-049-backlink"><a class="_idFootnoteLink _idGenColorInherit" href="#footnote-049"><sup>2</sup></a></span></span> the revelation of the Antichrist,<span class="Footnote-Reference"><span id="footnote-048-backlink"><a class="_idFootnoteLink _idGenColorInherit" href="#footnote-048"><sup>3</sup></a></span></span> the mark of the beast,<span class="Footnote-Reference"><span id="footnote-047-backlink"><a class="_idFootnoteLink _idGenColorInherit" href="#footnote-047"><sup>4</sup></a></span></span> and the Magog invasion<span class="Footnote-Reference"><span id="footnote-046-backlink"><a class="_idFootnoteLink _idGenColorInherit" href="#footnote-046"><sup>5</sup></a></span></span> — the topic of deception is referenced to more times than any other “sign of the end times.”</p>
<p class="Body-text">Jesus warned in His Olivet Discourse saying, “...Take heed that no man deceive you. For many shall come in my name, saying, I am Christ; and shall deceive many,”<span class="Footnote-Reference"><span id="footnote-045-backlink"><a class="_idFootnoteLink _idGenColorInherit" href="#footnote-045"><sup>6</sup></a></span></span> He then continued to warn, “And many false prophets shall rise, and shall deceive many. And because iniquity shall abound, the love of many shall wax cold.”<span class="Footnote-Reference"><span id="footnote-044-backlink"><a class="_idFootnoteLink _idGenColorInherit" href="#footnote-044"><sup>7</sup></a></span></span></p>
<p class="Body-text">In both his letter to Timothy and his letter to the Thessalonians the apostle Paul warns that “in latter times some will depart from the faith,”<span class="Footnote-Reference"><span id="footnote-043-backlink"><a class="_idFootnoteLink _idGenColorInherit" href="#footnote-043"><sup>8</sup></a></span></span> and “Let no man deceive you by any means: for [that day shall not come], except there come a falling away first, and that man of sin be revealed, the son of perdition.”<span class="Footnote-Reference"><span id="footnote-042-backlink"><a class="_idFootnoteLink _idGenColorInherit" href="#footnote-042"><sup>9</sup></a></span></span> </p>
<p class="Body-text">In both of these admonitions, Paul is writing about apostasy. <br/>In describing the “falling away” he uses the Greek term <span class="Italic">apostasia</span> which is a word that means to forsake, abandon or renounce a previously held belief. I believe that Paul is identifying two types of apostasy in these two letters. To Timothy, he warns of apostasy in the Church, and to the Thessalonian Church he warns of the apostasy in the world that will lead up to the revelation of the “man of sin.”</p>

By the end of World War II, knowledge was doubling every 25 years. Today things are not as simple as different types of knowledge have different rates of growth. For example, nanotechnology knowledge is doubling every two years and clinical knowledge every 18 months. But on average human knowledge is doubling every 13 months. According to IBM, the build-out of the ‘internet of things’ will lead to the doubling of knowledge every 12 hours.”<span class="Footnote-Reference"><span id="footnote-013-backlink"><a class="_idFootnoteLink _idGenColorInherit" href="#footnote-013"><sup>4</sup></a></span></span></p>

Let the chanting begin! — “Believe nothing! Challenge everything!” This disconnection from the moorings of truth leads us to today’s drift in critical thinking. Steven Spielberg is reported as saying:</p>

The Holocaust had been treated as just a footnote in so many textbooks or not mentioned at all. Millions knew little if anything about it. Others tried to deny it happened at all.”<span class="Footnote-Reference"><span id="footnote-012-backlink"><a class="_idFootnoteLink _idGenColorInherit" href="#footnote-012"><sup>5</sup></a></span></span></p>
