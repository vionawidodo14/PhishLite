<h2 class="A_r_seo_words_title">About Toyota</h2>
</div>
<div class="wrap_all visib_show"><div class="p_over_hidden" style="height: 68px; overflow-wrap: break-word;"><div class="A_r_seo_words_title mc_context">
Toyota Motor Corporation was established by Kiichiro Toyoda in 1937, as a spinoff from his father's company Toyota Industries to create automobiles. However, the company had begun its auto manufacturing three years earlier. In 1934, it created its first engine, the Type A engine, and, in 1936, its first passenger car, the Toyota AA. Today, the company has ranked among the world's largest automobile manufacturers.  <br/>  
It first entered the U.S. market in 1957 with its conservative Crown sedan. The Corolla, Toyota's iconic model, achieved the company a competitive rival against the lumbering American cars in the 1970s oil crisis. In an effort to cater to the U.S. consumers' tastes, the company began to produce sportier vehicles like the Celica and Supra. Besides, Toyota saw that what the consumer wanted is innovative technology. The company leads the forefront of green technology with its Prius, which is recognized as one of the world's best-selling car.  <br/>
