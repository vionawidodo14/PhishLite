<div class="heading parbase section"><h1 class="theme--default text-align-center">
    Welcome to Vintage Inns
</h1>
</div>
<div class="image parbase section">
<picture>
<source media="(max-width: 720px)" srcset="/content/dam/vintage-inn/images/content/theme/2018/elements/leaves-linebreak-m.png.asset/1553852165517.png"/>

<div class="parbase section text"><div class="text--only"><p style="text-align: center;"><b>We've got everything you need from a country pub near you.</b><br/>
<br/>
Burning log fires in the winter, beautiful <a href="/gardens">beer gardens</a> in the summer – this is just a taste of what you can expect at your local Vintage Inn.</p>
<p style="text-align: center;">With 186 country pubs dotted across the UK, your next country escape may be closer than you think.</p>
<p style="text-align: center;">Our <u><a href="/food">menus</a></u> are full of hearty favourites including steak &amp; mushroom pie, beer-battered fish and chips as well as seasonal surprises if you fancy something a little different.</p>
<p style="text-align: center;">When you visit expect a well-stocked <u><a href="/restaurants">bar</a></u> with cask ales, lagers, fine wines, British gin and soft drinks to suit every taste.</p>
<p style="text-align: center;">So, whether you’re looking for a quiet spot to relax after a countryside stroll, or a place to gather your loved ones for a traditional Sunday roast, you can’t go wrong with Vintage Inns.</p>
