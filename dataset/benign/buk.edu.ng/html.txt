<span class="rdf-meta element-hidden" content="BUK Programs Get International Accreditation" property="dc:title"></span><span class="rdf-meta element-hidden" content="0" datatype="xsd:integer" property="sioc:num_replies"></span> <span class="submitted"><span content="2019-09-12T23:55:27+02:00" datatype="xsd:dateTime" property="dc:date dc:created" rel="sioc:has_creator">Submitted by <span about="/?q=user/11" class="username" datatype="" property="foaf:name" typeof="sioc:UserAccount" xml:lang="">publication</span> on Thu, 09/12/2019 - 23:55</span></span>

<div class="content node-article">
<div class="field field-name-field-image field-type-image field-label-hidden"><div class="field-items"><div class="field-item even" rel="og:image rdfs:seeAlso" resource="https://buk.edu.ng/sites/default/files/styles/medium/public/field/image/HCERES%20Pic.jpg?itok=2zn4JwcF"><a href="/?q=node/243"><img alt="" height="165" src="https://buk.edu.ng/sites/default/files/styles/medium/public/field/image/HCERES%20Pic.jpg?itok=2zn4JwcF" typeof="foaf:Image" width="220"/></a></div></div></div><div class="field field-name-body field-type-text-with-summary field-label-hidden"><div class="field-items"><div class="field-item even" property="content:encoded"><p>French High Council for Evaluation of Research and Higher Education (HCERES) has granted a five-year unreserved international accreditation status on two programmes of the Centre for Dryland Agriculture (CDA), Bayero University, Kano (BUK). This came after a rigorous evaluation process involving self-evaluation, desk reviews, and an on-site visit.
