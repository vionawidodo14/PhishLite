<h3 style="font-size:34px; color:#BE5050;">Find keywords that convert based on what your audience is looking for</h3>
<p class="lead">Automatically categorize keywords based on audience intent. Categories available are -</p>
<p class="lead"><b>Buyer Intent</b>: Keyword phrases with words like buy, discount, deal and so on indicate that the user intends to purchase some time soon.
