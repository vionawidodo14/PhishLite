<div class="col-xs-12 col-md-3 full-width content-columns content-columns--e68eb442-1c34-4356-8794-6b2c9d0c614a" style=" ">
<h3 class="h2 small" style="text-align: center;"><img alt="Flexible" caption="false" height="171" src="https://az184419.vo.msecnd.net/ontario-colleges/Website/images/home-page/flexible.png" width="170"/></h3>
<h3 class="h2 small" style="text-align: center;">Flexible</h3>
<p style="text-align: center;">Ontario college program and delivery options let you choose how you want to learn. From prep and bridging courses to university transfer agreements, employee partnerships, hands-on work experience and more, there are more options than ever for finding your fit.</p>
</div>
<div class="col-xs-12 col-md-3 not-full-width content-columns content-columns--e06503ca-9378-4d48-8774-872fa6785fbd" style=" ">
<h3 class="h2 small" style="text-align: center;"><img alt="Economical" caption="false" height="171" src="https://az184419.vo.msecnd.net/ontario-colleges/Website/images/home-page/economical.png" width="170"/></h3>
<h3 class="h2 small" style="text-align: center;">Economical</h3>
<p style="text-align: center;">Higher education pays off. For every $1 invested in an Ontario college education, graduates receive a cumulative $3.20 more in their future wages. For number crunchers, that's an unprecedented 14.3 percent rate of return.</p>
</div>
<div class="col-xs-12 col-md-3 not-full-width content-columns content-columns--5ed77b70-70ff-43cd-8663-9d29cc39570b" style=" ">
<h3 class="h2 small" style="text-align: center;"><img alt="Innovative" caption="false" height="171" src="https://az184419.vo.msecnd.net/ontario-colleges/Website/images/home-page/innovative.png" width="170"/></h3>
<h3 class="h2 small" style="text-align: center;">Innovative</h3>
<p style="text-align: center;">Ontario's colleges and 750 industry partners are innovating together. To date, more than 10,000 students are involved in groundbreaking research and innovation projects, with an additional 1,000 students receiving support to pursue entrepreneurial ideas.</p>
</div>
<div class="col-xs-12 col-md-3 not-full-width content-columns content-columns--1420de05-51f1-4922-976a-f7494e10dd43" style=" ">
<h3 class="h2 small" style="text-align: center;"><img alt="Supportive" caption="false" height="171" src="https://az184419.vo.msecnd.net/ontario-colleges/Website/images/home-page/supportive-04.png" width="170"/></h3>
<h3 class="h2 small" style="text-align: center;">Supportive</h3>
<p style="text-align: center;">Ontario's colleges work with you to succeed. With financial assistance options, disability services, tutoring programs, onsite counselling and so much more, you'll have the support you need at every step as you work toward the future you've always wanted.</p>
</div>
