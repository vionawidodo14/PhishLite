<h1>Dungeons and Dragons <span class="accent-color">Spells</span> &amp; <span class="accent-color">Spellbooks</span> Just for you!</h1>

<h4>View and read up on the spells. Create and save your own spellbook and toggle all your prepared spells, you can even keep track of your spell slots.</h4>
<div class="hr5 margin-bottom"></div>
<p>
            Create spell cards based on your class or character, generate a PDF or print them. Sort the spells by level, alphabetical and many other ways. Spells and cantrips from all the classes, Sorcerer, Wizard, Druid, Bard, Paladin, Cleric, Ranger, Warlock.<br/>
            Customize the output. Two or three columns, you prefer the paper in portrait or landscape view? Filter and sort the spells based on class, level, casting time, ritual, concentration. Preview the printable PDF and save it for later or print it directly from your browser.<br/>
            Why not create a spellbook and save your spells so you easily can reach them on your mobile, tablet or computer. Now you can even toggle the prepared spells.
            </p>
<p>
<strong>Your DM want to take a look in your spell list? Did your RPs find a spellbook during the campaign? No problem, you can now share your spellbooks with others.</strong>
