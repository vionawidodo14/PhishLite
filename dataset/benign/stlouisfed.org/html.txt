</div>
<div class="">
<div class="col-lg-4 whoWeAreItem" style="float:left;">
<img class="whoWeAreImg" src="/~/media/HomePage/WhoWeAre/circle1.png"/>
<div class="whoWeAreHeadering">Decentralized <br/>by Design</div>
<div class="whoWeAreText">
<p style="text-align: left;">The Federal Reserve is a central bank system that includes the Board of Governors in Washington, D.C., and 12 independent regional Reserve banks.</p> <p>This decentralized structure ensures that the economic conditions of all areas of the country are taken into account in the making of monetary policy. </p>
<p style="text-align: left;">Learn more about the <a href="/timely-topics/the-feds-regional-structure">importance of the Fed’s regional structure</a>.</p>
</div>
</div><!-- /.col-lg-4 -->
<div class="col-lg-4 whoWeAreItem" style="float:left;">
<img class="whoWeAreImg" src="/~/media/HomePage/WhoWeAre/circle2.png"/>
<div class="whoWeAreHeadering">Our <br/>Mission</div>
<div class="whoWeAreText">
<p style="text-align: left;">The Federal Reserve promotes a healthy economy and financial stability by:</p>
<ul style="text-align: left; margin-left: -25px; color: #333333;">
<li>Pursuing maximum employment, stable prices and moderate long-term interest rates</li>
<li>Ensuring safety and soundness of the nation's banks and financial system and protecting consumer credit rights</li>
<li>Maintaining stability of the financial system and containing systemic risk</li>
<li>Providing services to depository institutions, the U.S. government and foreign official institutions</li>
</ul>
<p style="text-align: left;">Learn more about <a href="/about-us/what-we-do">what we do</a>.</p>
</div>
</div><!-- /.col-lg-4 -->
<div class="col-lg-4 whoWeAreItem" style="float:left;">

<div class="whoWeAreHeadering">Our <br/>Leadership</div>
<div class="whoWeAreText">
<p>The St. Louis Fed is overseen by a board of nine independent directors who are familiar with economic and credit conditions in the Eighth District.</p> <p>The Bank's president, first vice president and a team of officers manage the Bank's day-to-day operations. The Management Committee is the central policymaking body of the Bank. </p>
