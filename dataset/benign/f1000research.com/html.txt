<h3 class="c-highlight-list__title t-h2 u-mb--1" data-test-id="home_benefits_researchers_title">Benefits for Researchers</h3>
<ul class="c-highlight-list__list" data-test-id="home_benefits_researchers_list">
<li class="c-highlight-list__list-item t-h4">Enables authors, not editors, to decide what they wish to publish.</li>
<li class="c-highlight-list__list-item t-h4">Authors suggest peer reviewers and control the process.</li>
<li class="c-highlight-list__list-item t-h4">All types of research can be published rapidly: traditional articles, data sets, null results, protocols, case reports, incremental findings and more.</li>
</ul>
</div>
</div>
<div class="o-layout__item u-1/2@xsm u-1/3@md u-mb--4 u-left" data-test-id="home_benefits_research">
<div class="c-highlight-list__icon"><img class="c-highlight-list__icon-img" data-test-id="home_benefits_research_img" src="/img/research/home/benefits/benefits_research.jpg"/></div>
<div class="c-highlight-list">
<h3 "="" class="c-highlight-list__title t-h2 u-mb--1" data-test-id="home_benefits_research_title">Benefits for Research</h3>
<ul class="c-highlight-list__list" data-test-id="home_benefits_research_list">
<li class="c-highlight-list__list-item t-h4">Aims to shift the way research and researchers are evaluated.</li>
<li class="c-highlight-list__list-item t-h4">Moves away from journal-based measures towards direct assessment of individual outputs.</li>
<li class="c-highlight-list__list-item t-h4">Supports research assessment based on the intrinsic value of the research, not the venue of publication.</li>
</ul>
</div>
</div>
<div class="o-layout__item u-1/2@xsm u-1/3@md u-left" data-test-id="home_benefits_society">
<div class="c-highlight-list__icon"><img class="c-highlight-list__icon-img" data-test-id="home_benefits_society_img" src="/img/research/home/benefits/benefits_society.jpg"/></div>
<div class="c-highlight-list">
<h3 "="" class="c-highlight-list__title t-h2 u-mb--1" data-test-id="home_benefits_society_title">Benefits for Society</h3>
<ul class="c-highlight-list__list" data-test-id="home_benefits_society_list">
<li class="c-highlight-list__list-item t-h4">Reduces the barrier to collaborative research through data sharing, transparency and attribution.</li>
<li class="c-highlight-list__list-item t-h4">Reduces research waste and helps to remove the bias in our understanding of research.</li>
<li class="c-highlight-list__list-item t-h4">Enables others to build upon new ideas right away, wherever and whoever they are.</li>
</ul>
