<div class="author">BY <a href="/users/abrandon">Adam Brandon</a></div>
<div class="node-teaser-content"><p>On behalf of our activist community, I urge you to contact your representative and ask him or her to vote NO on the Corporate Transparency Act, H.R. 2513. Additionally, the bill may have long-term privacy implications.</p></div>
