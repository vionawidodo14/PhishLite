<h4>Zenodo in a nutshell</h4>
<ul>
<li><strong>Research. Shared.</strong> — all research outputs from across all fields of research are welcome! Sciences and Humanities, really!</li>
<li><strong>Citeable. Discoverable.</strong> — uploads gets a Digital Object Identifier (DOI) to make them easily and uniquely citeable.</li>
<li><strong>Communities</strong> — create and curate your own community for a workshop, project, department, journal, into which you can accept or reject uploads. Your own complete digital repository!</li>
<li><strong>Funding</strong> — identify grants, integrated in reporting lines for research funded by the European Commission via OpenAIRE.</li>
<li><strong>Flexible licensing</strong> — because not everything is under Creative Commons.</li>
<li><strong>Safe</strong> — your research output is stored safely for the future in the same cloud infrastructure as CERN's own LHC research data.</li>
</ul>
