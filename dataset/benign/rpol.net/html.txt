<h1 class="link-colour"><b>Welcome to RolePlay onLine!</b></h1><table class="info_box" style='width: 810px; max-width: 810px; width:expression(document.body.clientWidth &gt; 810? "810px": "auto");'><tbody><tr><td>
<p style="margin-top: 0;">RPoL is one of the oldest play-by-post roleplaying communities on the web, providing <b>free</b> membership for anyone to host and participate in <b>any</b> game system or communal story line.</p>
<p>With <b>unlimited</b> anonymous aliases (characters) in a game along with tools for hosting character sheets, descriptions, portraits (over 12,000), custom dice roller systems, game maps, private threads, private text, multiple groups, and <i>much</i> more, we're confident you'll find everything you need to manage and play your favourite characters in your favourite setting.</p>
<p>Or create and GM your own game and add to the <b>81,436</b> characters and <b>9,191,775</b> messages already on the system.</p>
<p>With <b>5,269</b> games on record, <b>1,380</b> highly active (<b>358</b> seeking players), we're sure you'll find a setting to suit you.</p>
Reliably providing <b>111,866</b> users with a place to get their gaming fix, since 2000; over <b>11</b> games created every day.</td></tr></tbody></table><br class="sm"/>
