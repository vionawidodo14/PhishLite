<div class="text">Adrack is an intuitive, cloud-based marketing platform that offers a variety of tools and solutions for just about every vertical. Adrack provides solutions for: <span>Advertisers</span>, <span>Publishers</span>, <span>Brokers</span>, <span>Agencies</span>, <span>Lead Generators</span>.<br/><br/>With Adrack, users can quickly and easily set up campaigns, capture and distribute prospects, and send and manage push notifications for customized re-engagement campaigns. Automatic segmentation makes it easy to analyze data and reach the right audiences with your messages. </div>
<div class="text">
<ul class="cols">
<li>
<div class="subtitle">Manage</div>

</li>
<li>
<div class="subtitle">Measure</div>

Plus, there are even options for exporting files and reports, allowing you to share the information with necessary parties.</div>
</li>
<li>
<div class="subtitle">Optimize</div>
<div class="text">With all of the information at your fingertips in the Adrack dashboard, it will be easy for you to optimize your marketing campaigns. With our built-in API, you can add data from other applications and systems, and then get a snapshot of all the information in one place. This makes it easy to optimize targeted campaigns and improve lead distribution efforts without going back and forth between various reports and applications.</div>
