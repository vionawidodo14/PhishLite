<h2 class="purple">Career Exploration</h2>
<p>Connecting students' interests to their potential career paths early ensures active participation in their own academic success and opens their eyes to the wide world of career opportunities. 

The career planning tools in Naviance allow students to understand how their strengths, goals, skills, and interests can lead to exciting careers. </p>
<hr class="endline"/>
</div>

<h2 class="fuchsia150">College and Career Preparation</h2>
<p>Each student’s path is unique. How can your school or district help all students reach their unique goals by connecting their passion to their future? 

Naviance allows students to create a personalized plan that helps them make the right decisions throughout their academic journey.</p>

<h2 class="teal150">Self-Discovery</h2>
<p>Students build confidence by trying new things, pursuing what they love, and learning from their missteps in a safe environment. Naviance is that safe place—by helping students focus on what’s strong, reflect on their interests and strengths, and explore a wide-variety of career options.</p>
