<div class="inner-section">
<div class="section-container">
<div class="title">
How It Works
</div>
<div class="description">
Swift Demand is an attempt at basic income. Each user who is signed up for the service receives 100 Swifts each day. No additional Swifts can be created with time or resources.
</div>
<a href="/faq"><div class="link">
Learn More
</div>
</a></div>
</div>
<div class="inner-section">
<div class="section-container">
<div class="title">
How Else Can I Get Swifts?
</div>
<div class="description">
Additional Swifts can temporarily be generated through referrals and confirming information. After enough initial users have signed up these bonuses will disappear and income generation will be the sole source of new Swifts.
</div>
<a href="/faq"><div class="link">
Learn More
</div>
</a></div>
<div class="section-container">
<div class="title">
Do Swifts Have Real Value?
</div>
<div class="description">
Swifts have no direct connection to any form of currency and are not backed by anything. Swifts are only worth what other people are willing to pay for them. There is no guarantee about the strength of Swifts in relation to any other currency.
</div>
<a href="/faq"><div class="link">
Learn More
</div>
</a></div>
<div class="section-container">
<div class="title">
Is This A Cryptocurrency?
</div>
<div class="description">
SwiftDemand is currently a digital currency and will soon become a full cryptocurrency. In mid-2018 all Swifts on SwiftDemand will be transferred at a 1:1 ratio onto the new Swift blockchain.
</div>
