<h1 class="h1">Learning brought to life</h1>
<div class="lead">
<p>Since our first school opened in 1955, we've created success stories for students from all over the world, transforming the lives of over 1 million people. We teach students throughout their learning journey, from young learners to preparing for a degree at a top university. </p>
</div>
<div class="field-wrapper field field-node--field-body-text field-name-field-body-text field-type-text-with-summary field-label-hidden">
<div class="field-items">
<div class="field-item"><p>We nurture and inspire <a href="https://www.bellenglish.com/young-learners/courses">young learners</a>, aged 7-17, with courses to suit all interests and abilities. We advance <a href="https://www.bellenglish.com/english-16">students aged 16+</a> with English courses that can all be personalised to suit the ambitions of the student. We prepare students for independent life at <a href="https://www.bellenglish.com/university-pathways">university in the UK</a> to achieve a top career. </p>
<p>Bell teaching experts also <a href="https://www.bellenglish.com/teacher-training">train teachers</a> all over the world. Our teacher trainers include world-class names such as our Head of Teacher Development Silvana Richardson.</p>
<p>Start your Bell story today #mybellstory</p>
