<h3 class="field heading field--name-field-title field--type-string field--label-hidden field__item" id="options-beyond-the-textbook">Options beyond the textbook</h3>
<div class="clearfix text-formatted field field--name-field-copy field--type-text-long field--label-hidden field__item"><p>This is a critical time in science education. Program choices made now will likely impact instruction for the next decade. Nationwide, curriculum leaders are moving away from the traditional science textbook as research repeatedly shows that this is not how students learn best.</p>
<p><a href="/5innovations" target="_blank"><strong>Learn how our programs differ</strong></a> and why you should be exclusively looking for research-based curricula. </p></div>
