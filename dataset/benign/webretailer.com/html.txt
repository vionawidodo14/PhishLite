<div class="mainpost"><a href="https://www.webretailer.com/lean-commerce/payability-ofx/"><h2>Selling to the USA? Here’s How To Get Paid Faster and Save on Currency Exchange</h2></a>
<p class="subtitle">Payability and OFX have teamed up to offer a streamlined global cash flow solution and preferential rates for cross-border marketplace sellers.</p>
<figure class="wp-block-image"><a data-wpel-link="internal" href="https://www.webretailer.com/lean-commerce/payability-ofx/"><img alt="Payability OFX Scale Your Cross Border Business" class="wp-image-12471" sizes="(max-width: 1200px) 100vw, 1200px" src="https://www.webretailer.com/lean-commerce/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/Payability-OFX-Scale-Your-Cross-Border-Business-1200x615.png" srcset="https://www.webretailer.com/lean-commerce/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/Payability-OFX-Scale-Your-Cross-Border-Business.png 1200w, https://www.webretailer.com/lean-commerce/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/Payability-OFX-Scale-Your-Cross-Border-Business-300x154.png 300w, https://www.webretailer.com/lean-commerce/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/Payability-OFX-Scale-Your-Cross-Border-Business-768x394.png 768w, https://www.webretailer.com/lean-commerce/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/Payability-OFX-Scale-Your-Cross-Border-Business-640x328.png 640w"/></a></figure>
<p>A new global partnership provides online sellers with next-day payments from their U.S. marketplace sales, and big savings on currency conversion when they bring these sales proceeds back home.</p>
<p>On one side of the partnership is Payability’s Instant Access service. This provides next-day payments from marketplace sales, covering Amazon, Walmart, Newegg, Tophatter, Jet.com and MoreCommerce. Instead of payments taking weeks, online sellers can receive payments each day.</p>
<p>On the other side is OFX, with their Global Currency Account. This provides local currency accounts in U.S. dollars, British pounds, Euros, Canadian dollars, Australian dollars and Hong Kong dollars. Sellers can pay international suppliers directly from their account balance, or transfer funds home at exchange rates which offer significant cost savings.</p>
<p>The two services work in tandem to help sellers accelerate their cash-flow and bring more money back home. They can make more frequent stock purchases, pay suppliers more efficiently, and just grow faster overall.</p>
