<h2 style="text-align: center;">Ready for Weekend <b>Services?</b></h2>
<h4 class="homepage-css">Planning worship is not easy, but there is a better way – worship<b>planning</b>.com<br/><br/>

    Chances are you already have a process for planning your services.  worship<b>planning</b>.com adds some <i>power</i> to your process, helping you organize and communicate better while allowing you to focus on what's important in your ministry.<br/><br/>
</h4><h3>How does it work?</h3>
<ol>
<li><b>Plan</b> your service details with an easy-to-use worship flow editor.  Add or import song details, upload files, and add notes for yourself or your team.</li>
<li><b>Schedule and Notify</b> team members one week at a time or for multiple months. Team members are notified via email, text, and even Facebook.  They can "accept" or "decline", and also access the service details you've set.</li>
<li><b>Update</b> the worship flow and find substitutes to serve as needed.  Since the plans are centrally stored in the cloud, you and your team can securely access them from anywhere, instantly.</li>
</ol>
<h4>You can relax with worship<b>planning</b>.com because everyone is on the same page!</h4>
