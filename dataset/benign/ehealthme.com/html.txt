<h1><span style="color:black;font-size:30pt;">Rethink how you pick a drug</span></h1>
<p class="tagline-secondary">A drug can treat a condition as prescribed, but at what costs to your overall health and quality of life? On eHealthMe, we use real-world data to find drugs that strike an optimal balance between effectiveness and side effects.</p>
<br/>
<p class="tagline-secondary">Why use real-world data? Real-world scenarios are not the same as the therapeutic experience within the controlled clinical trial protocols. As a result, drugs can behave differently after they are put in the market. The 21st Century Cures Act, passed in Congress in 2016, defines the use of real-world data to support regulatory decision making. The FDA has started to use real-world data to monitor postmarket safety and adverse events, and payers to support value-based payment program. </p>
<br/>
<p class="tagline-secondary">On eHealthMe, we have been monitoring drugs and supplements since 2008. We continuously gather data and improve our proprietary algorithms to analyze real-world drug outcomes. Our original studies have been referenced on <a href="http://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=ehealthme&amp;btnG=&amp;hl=en&amp;as_sdt=0%2C5" rel="nofollow" target="resultwindow">500+ </a>peer-reviewed medical publications. </p>
<br/>
<p class="tagline-secondary">eHealthMe has <a href="/health-tools/">public tools</a> and <a href="/solutions/">business solutions</a>.</p>
