<h2>QR CODES</h2><br/>
<img border="0" height="2" hspace="0" src="https://cdn.qrstuff.com/images/space.gif" vspace="0" width="1"/><br/>
A QR Code (it stands for "Quick Response") is a mobile phone readable bar code that can store website URL's, plain text, phone numbers, email addresses and pretty much any other alphanumeric data.<br/>
<img border="0" height="8" hspace="0" src="https://cdn.qrstuff.com/images/space.gif" vspace="0" width="1"/><br/>
The QR Stuff QR Code Generator allows you to create either <a href="https://blog.qrstuff.com/2012/08/12/dynamic-qr-codes" target="_blank">dynamic or static QR codes</a> and download them for immediate use.<br/>
<img border="0" height="8" hspace="0" src="https://cdn.qrstuff.com/images/space.gif" vspace="0" width="1"/><br/>
Storing up to 4296 characters they are internationally standardised under ISO 18004, so a QR code is a QR code all over the world - they've been big in Japan forever, broke into Europe and the UK a few years back, and are now popular in North America.<br/>
<img border="0" height="8" hspace="0" src="https://cdn.qrstuff.com/images/space.gif" vspace="0" width="1"/><br/>
Think "print-based hypertext links" and you'll get the idea.<br/>
<img border="0" height="8" hspace="0" src="https://cdn.qrstuff.com/images/space.gif" vspace="0" width="1"/><br/>
Click here for <a href="qr_codes.html">more information about QR codes</a> or vist the <a href="https://blog.qrstuff.com/" target="_blank">QRStuff QR Code Blog</a> for QR code articles, tips and tricks.<br/>
