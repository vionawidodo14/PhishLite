<h2 class="header-title">About SLA</h2>
</header>
<div class="content-item content-markdown" data-itemid="84104"><div class="well lead">
<p><strong>Science Leadership Academy</strong> is a partnership high school between the School District of Philadelphia and The Franklin Institute. SLA is an inquiry-driven, project-based high school focused on 21st-century learning that opened its doors on September 7, 2006.</p>
<p>You can use this site to access student blogs as well as log into our portal by clicking “Login” above.</p>
<p>More information can be found by accessing our District website below.</p>
<p><a class="button primary" href="https://sla.philasd.org/" title="">SLA - Center City Website</a></p>
<p>1482 Green St · Philadelphia, PA 19130<br/>
phone - (215) 400-7830<br/>
fax - (215) 400-7831</p>
</div>
</div>
