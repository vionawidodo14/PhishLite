<h2>We will guide you to the best of Costa Rica</h2><p>Sitting between the Caribbean Sea and the Pacific Ocean, Costa Rica’s landscape is painted with towering <a href="/travelInfo/volcanoes.asp">volcanoes</a> and mountains, <a href="/travelInfo/rainforests.asp">lush rainforests</a>, sparking coastal plains and amazing <a href="/travelInfo/beaches.asp">tropical beaches</a>. </p><p>The <a href="/travelInfo/weather.asp">weather</a> is indeed tropical and because of its diversity many <a href="/travelInfo/general/micro-climates.asp">micro climates</a> are found throughout the country. But a vacation offers more than a lovely landscape and beautiful weather - this tropical country is steeped in rich history dating back to the 1500's and boasts a community-centric lifestyle that is rare in much of the world.</p><p>In Costa Rica, you can wake up to the sound of howler monkeys or <a href="/travelInfo/photo-gallery.asp?tag=birds">toucans</a> chatting with one another, then spend the afternoon learning about the mysterious stone spheres that weigh up to 15 tons. </p><p>Nature enthusiasts, environmentalists, <a href="/travelInfo/surfers-guide.asp">surfers</a>, history buffs - no matter what your personality or hobby, a vacation to Costa Rica can provide something amazing to explore or uncover. Rest assured Costa Rica is considered one of the safest countries in Latin America, check out our <a href="/travelInfo/general/is-costa-rica-safe.asp">Safety Tips while traveling in Costa Rica</a>.</p><div class="clearFloats"></div><div class="HomeContentAnWrapper"></div><div class="HomeContentQuoteWrapper"><blockquote class="BodyTextQuote">It's no surprise Costa Rica is considered the "happiest place on earth."</blockquote></div><div class="clearFloats"></div>It's no surprise Costa Rica is considered the "happiest place on earth." <p></p><p>Once you land, you'll find it's easy to experience what Costa Ricans have been talking about since 1956: <i><a href="/travelInfo/general/meaning-of-pura-vida.asp">Pura Vida</a></i> - a phrase that truly encapsulates the friendly vibe of Costa Rica, and is used by locals and means everything from "Hello," to "This is the life!" If you're looking to fall into the "Pura Vida" lifestyle, check out some of the top activities and accolades:</p>

<h3><a href="/category/hotels/eco-hotels.asp">Eco-savvy</a></h3><p>Although this nation encapsulates a mere 51,100 square kilometers, Costa Rica's biodiversity could fill an entire continent. </p><p>Encompassing 12 distinct ecological zones, Ticos are well aware of their natural treasures: it is on its way to becoming the first carbon neutral nation by 2021. Costa Rica is home to 5 percent of the world's biodiversity, and locals are serious about protecting this magnificent environment.</p><p>Nature lovers can explore <a href="/travelInfo/nationalParks.asp">over 100 national parks, reserves, refuges and protected lands</a> - which makes up about 25 percent of the country's land. </p><p>Catch site of a scarlet macaw in <a href="/region/city.asp?cID=350">Corcovado National Park</a>, hang out with mantled howler monkeys at <a href="/region/city.asp?cID=426">Manuel Antonio National Park</a> or get lost in the beauty of native magnolias in <a href="/region/city.asp?cID=402">Monteverde Cloud Forest Reserve</a>.</p><h3><a href="/travelInfo/day-trips.asp">Wild thrill treks</a></h3><p>You say you're more of a thrill seeker? Costa Rica is a nature lover's playground, and no matter what your mood, you'll find a great adventure to suit it. </p><p>While here, you can fly through the canopy in Selvatura Park, hike to thunderous waterfalls or hot springs next to <a href="/region/city.asp?cID=454">Arenal Volcano National Park</a> or go <a href="/category/outdoorRecreation/whiteWaterRafting.asp">white water rafting</a> down the thrilling Pacuare River.</p><p>Not matter how far off the beaten path you choose to go, its hard to not be at the center of Costa Rica's premier ecological and geological features make sure there's never a dull moment.</p><h3><a href="/category/hotels/accommodations.asp">Timeless intrigue and beauty</a></h3><p>The <a href="/category/hotels/hotels.asp">hotels in Costa Rica</a> are set in some of the most beautiful places that are so remote being <a href="/category/hotels/allInclusiveResorts.asp">all inclusive</a> is a necessity. If you prefer urban jungles to green ones, the rich history and modern cultural accolades are sure to please.</p><p>Visitors seeking metropolis-aimed vacations can enjoy <a href="/region/sanJose/sanJose.asp">San Jose's</a> colonial-era architecture juxtaposed with the city's urban street art  - the dichotomy creates a dynamic beauty that no other city can quite encapsulate. </p><p>The sweet smells of Costa Rican bananas, fresh coffee beans and chocolate can be found at the Central Market, and if you'd like to pick up a souvenir, everything from artisan leather goods to handmade musical instruments can be found here.</p><p>What are you waiting for? </p><p>Our travel guide can help you plan your vacation today!</p>
