<p>Over two years ago, Cvent acquired RegOnline<sup>®</sup> (through its merger with Lanyon Solutions,
                Inc.), positioning Cvent to deliver world-class event management technology and customer support. Since
                then, we have been diligently building the best of RegOnline<sup>®</sup> into our Cvent event
              management platform.</p>
<h3>Why are we doing this?</h3>
<p>We know RegOnline<sup>®</sup> is a beloved product, but we’re excited about what lies ahead. We’ve
                worked to incorporate these two strong solutions into one comprehensive event management product – to
              support events both large and small.</p>
<h3>What does this mean for you?</h3>
<ul>
<li>RegOnline<sup>®</sup> will be retired on December 31, 2019, at 11:59pm Mountain Time</li>
<li>Effective April 2, 2019, RegOnline<sup>®</sup> will no longer set up new accounts or renew
                existing contracts</li>
<li>Customers can continue to use RegOnline<sup>®</sup> through December 31, 2019 – including
                  managing existing events already created or creating new RegOnline<sup>®</sup> events taking place
                in 2019</li>
<li>RegOnline<sup>®</sup> customers with existing events extending past 2019 should call us to
                discuss</li>
<li>Cvent shall continue to provide support services for RegOnline<sup>®</sup> through 11:59pm
                Mountain Time, December 31, 2019</li>
</ul>
