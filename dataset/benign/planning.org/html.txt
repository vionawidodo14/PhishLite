<h2>Featured APA Learn Courses</h2>
<div class="content-preview-list no-top-border no-top-spacing no-bottom-border">
<ul>
<!-- Featured -->
<li class="content-preview-item content-preview-item-featured">
<div class="content-preview-item-image-block">
<img alt="Featured Content" src="/static/newtheme/image/Events-2016-03-21.jpg" title="Featured Content"/>
</div>
<h6 class="content-preview-item-superheadline">Planning Methods and Tools | Public Participation | Social Justice and Equity</h6>
<h4 class="content-preview-item-headline"><a href="https://learn.planning.org/local/catalog/view/product.php?globalid=LRN_LAP19_02">Addressing Cultural Divides</a></h4>
<div class="content-preview-item-tags">
                        Lack of information in Spanish and cultural divides often keep Latinos away from the public planning process. Engagement with Latino communities is very weak or non-existent in many places. How can we better connect with Latino communities in the cities we work with to ensure they are an active voice in decision-making?
                    </div>
</li>
<li class="content-preview-item">
<h6 class="content-preview-item-superheadline">Planning Methods and Tools | Public Participation | Social Justice and Equity</h6>
<h4 class="content-preview-item-headline"><a href="https://learn.planning.org/local/catalog/view/product.php?globalid=LRN_LAP19_04">Engagement Techniques for Latino Communities</a></h4>
<div class="content-preview-item-tags">
                        Latino communities often take community development into their own hands with active placemaking often referred to as ‘Latino urbanism.’ This course will help planners learn how to tap into this energy to activate and engage Latinos in planning process.
                    </div>
</li>
<li class="content-preview-item">
<h6 class="content-preview-item-superheadline">Planning Methods and Tools | Public Participation | Social Justice and Equity</h6>
<h4 class="content-preview-item-headline"><a href="https://learn.planning.org/local/catalog/view/product.php?globalid=LRN_LAP19_03">What is a Diálogo?</a></h4>
<div class="content-preview-item-tags">
                        The original purpose behind all the Diálogos was to set a national agenda for planning for Latinos in the U.S. and to ultimately provide a tool for Latino planners. Diálogos is a bottom-up approach that provides a platform for those voices of communities to be heard, and pathway to bring them to the planning table.
                    </div>
</li>
<li class="content-preview-item">
<h6 class="content-preview-item-superheadline">Planning History and Theory</h6>
<h4 class="content-preview-item-headline"><a href="https://learn.planning.org/local/catalog/view/product.php?globalid=LRN_S466">Addressing Megaregions in Long-Range Plans</a></h4>
<div class="content-preview-item-tags">
                        Megaregional-scale forces and trends impact transportation, the economy, environmental quality, and many other issues that planners must address. Yet megaregional thinking is still the exception, not the norm, in long-range planning. This session provides guidelines for incorporating megaregional issues into local and regional long-range plans.
                    </div>
</li>
</ul>
