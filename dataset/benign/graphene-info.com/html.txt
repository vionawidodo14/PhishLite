<h2 class="entry-title"><a href="/2d-fab-collaborates-saab-and-blackwing-develop-graphene-enhanced-components">2D Fab collaborates with SAAB and Blackwing to develop graphene-enhanced components for the aviation industry </a></h2> <div class="entry-content content">
<div class="field field-name-body field-type-text-with-summary field-label-hidden"><div class="field-items"><div class="field-item even"><p><a href="https://www.graphene-info.com/2d-fab-ab"><span class="inline inline-right img-assist-img-rm"><img alt="2D Fab AB logo" src="https://www.graphene-info.com/files/graphene/2D-fab-AB-logo-img_assist-100x80.jpg" title="2D Fab AB logo"/></span>2D fab</a>, together with SAAB and Blackwing Sweden, <a href="https://www.graphene-info.com/2d-fab-saab-and-blackwing-sweden-develop-graphene-based-components-aviation-industry">developed new graphene-enhanced components for the aviation industry</a> that offer increased lightning strike protection and strength.</p><p>The project, called Multigraph, was launched in 2017 with the mission to create better components for the aviation industry. The aim was to use graphene’s multifunctional properties to increase the mechanical strength and electrical conductivity of the materials used, the latter reducing the amount of maintenance required due to lightning strikes.</p><p></p></div></div></div> </div>

<h2 class="entry-title"><a href="/graphene-market-research-tipping-point-discussion-idtechex-research-director-dr">The Graphene Market Research Tipping Point: A Discussion With The IDTechEx Research Director, Dr Khasha Ghaffarzadeh </a></h2> <div class="entry-content content">
<div class="field field-name-body field-type-text-with-summary field-label-hidden"><div class="field-items"><div class="field-item even"><p><em>This is a sponsored article by IDTechEx</em></p><p><strong>People often talk about the killer application for graphene. Has one yet been found?</strong><br/><br/>A killer application is one that has a large market and is uniquely enabled by that technology. Some technologies can become commercially successful over time without requiring a so-called killer application - and I believe this is the case for graphene.</p><p>In fact, one point of strength for graphene is that it has such a diverse and deep application pipeline. The image below demonstrates this. The image shows that (a) some applications beyond R&amp;D are already in the early phases of commercialization, (b) that many other applications are close to commercialization, and (c) that the application pipeline is deep. Of course, graphene cannot be expected to succeed in all of these applications, but equally it is unlikely to fail in all. As such, this diversity will give, on the aggregate, a strong long-term resilience to the market.</p><p></p></div></div></div> </div>

<h2 class="entry-title"><a href="/haydale-launches-functionalized-graphene-prepreg-lightning-strike-protection">Haydale launches functionalized graphene prepreg for lightning strike protection </a></h2> <div class="entry-content content">
<div class="field field-name-body field-type-text-with-summary field-label-hidden"><div class="field-items"><div class="field-item even"><p><a href="https://www.graphene-info.com/haydale"><span class="inline inline-right img-assist-img-rm"><img alt="Haydale logo" src="https://www.graphene-info.com/files/graphene/haydale-logo-img_assist-100x39.jpg" title="Haydale logo"/></span>Haydale</a> has launched a range of graphene-enhanced prepreg materials for lightning-strike protection, utilizing functionalized graphene to improve the electrical conductivity.</p><p>The material has been developed in collaboration with Airbus UK, BAE Systems, GE Aviation and Element Materials Technology Warwick, within the NATEP-supported GraCELs project where the first iterations of materials were developed and subjected to lighting strike tests. <h2 class="entry-title"><a href="/graphene-oxide-proposed-significant-component-interstellar-dust">Graphene oxide proposed as significant component of interstellar dust </a></h2> <div class="entry-content content">
<div class="field field-name-body field-type-text-with-summary field-label-hidden"><div class="field-items"><div class="field-item even"><p>Peter J Sarre, Professor of Chemistry and Molecular Astrophysics at the University of Nottingham in the UK, has released a fascinating work that infers, based on previously unassigned optical and infrared astronomical observations and comparison with laboratory data on <a href="https://www.graphene-info.com/reduced-graphene-oxide-introduction">graphene oxide (GO)</a>, that GO is a significant component of interstellar dust.</p><p><img alt="Interstellar dust image" src="https://www.graphene-info.com/files/graphene/Interstellar-suat-image-img_assist-400x300.jpg" title="Interstellar dust image"/></p><p style="font-size: 14px;">Dust particles play a major role in the formation, evolution and chemistry of interstellar clouds, stars, and planetary systems. Commonly identified forms include amorphous and crystalline carbon-rich particles and silicates. Also present in many astrophysical environments are polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons (PAHs), detected through their infrared emission, and which are essentially small flakes of graphene.</p><p style="font-size: 14px;"></p></div></div></div> </div>

<h2 class="entry-title"><a href="/cryorig-launches-graphene-enhanced-cooling-system-pcs">Cryorig launches graphene-enhanced cooling system for PCs  </a></h2> <div class="entry-content content">
<div class="field field-name-body field-type-text-with-summary field-label-hidden"><div class="field-items"><div class="field-item even"><p>PC gear company Cryorig has introduced its low-profile CPU <a href="https://www.graphene-info.com/graphene-thermal">graphene-enhanced cooling system</a> for small form-factor PCs that can dissipate up to 125 W. The Cryorig C7 G is among the smallest coolers for higher-end processors available today. To make C7 G's high performance possible, Cryorig applied <a href="https://www.graphene-info.com/graphene-coating">graphene coating </a>on the heatsink.</p><p><img alt="Graphene-based cooler fr CPUs available by Cryorig image" src="https://www.graphene-info.com/files/graphene/Cryorig-sells-graphene-cooling-system-for-CPUs-image-img_assist-400x272.jpg" title="Graphene-based cooler fr CPUs available by Cryorig image"/></p><p>As demands arise for higher-performance components, cooling designers are creating low-profile coolers rated for TDP levels of 95 W of higher. To maximize efficiency of such devices, manufacturers use copper for heatsinks, many heat pipes, and large fans. Cryorig decided to go one step further and applied graphene coating to the radiator’s fins. Thermal conductivity of graphene is considerably higher than thermal conductivity of aluminum or copper, so applying it on the fins could theoretically improve cooling performance.</p><p></p></div></div></div> </div>
</div>
