<h2>Great education doesn’t have to mean big debt...</h2>
<div class="wrap">
<div class="col">
<div class="title"><a href="/about/stlcc-by-the-numbers.aspx"><em class="ico icon-coin"></em><span class="txt">Save</span></a></div>
<div class="desc">
<p>Students save over <strong>$4,700</strong> per year by attending STLCC instead of a public 4-year institution. STLCC is <strong>1/3 the cost</strong> of other institutions in the area.
                           </p>
</div>
</div>
<div class="col">
<div class="title"><a href="/about/stlcc-by-the-numbers.aspx"><em class="ico icon-earn"></em><span class="txt">Earn</span></a></div>
<div class="desc">
<p>St. Louisans with an associates degree, on average earn <strong>$10,600</strong> more a year than someone with only a high school diploma.
                           </p>
</div>
</div>
<div class="col">
<div class="title"><a href="/admissions/transfer-planning/transferring-out-of-stlcc.aspx"><em class="ico icon-transfer"></em><span class="txt">Transfer</span></a></div>
<div class="desc">
<p>With the help of <a href="/admissions/office-of-the-registrar/core-42.aspx">Core 42</a>, more students transfer from STLCC to public 4-year institutions in Missouri than
                              <strong>any other</strong> Missouri community college.
                           </p>
</div>
</div>
<div class="col">
<div class="title"><a href="/campus-life-community/index.aspx"><em class="ico icon-sport"></em><span class="txt">Community</span></a></div>
<div class="desc">
<p><strong>7</strong> sports teams, <strong>3</strong> theaters, <strong>4</strong> art galleries and more than <strong>120</strong> student organizations adds up to one lively and thriving STLCC community.
                           </p>
</div>
</div>
<div class="col">
<div class="title"><a href="/programs-academics/online-education/index.aspx"><em class="ico icon-students"></em><span class="txt">Online</span></a></div>
<div class="desc">
<p>Take one class or complete a degree online with <strong>STLCC Online</strong>.<strong> </strong>6<strong> </strong>programs<strong> entirely online</strong> and over a dozen programs at least <strong>50% online</strong>.
                           </p>
</div>
</div>
<div class="col">
<div class="title"><a href="/college-policy-procedures/consumer-information/accreditation.aspx"><em class="ico icon-club"></em><span class="txt">Accredited</span></a></div>
<div class="desc">
<p>In addition to institutional accreditation by the <strong>HLC</strong>, many programs have been accredited or approved by recognized accrediting bodies.
                           </p>
</div>
