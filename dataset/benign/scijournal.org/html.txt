<p>Journal Impact Factor (IF) is a measure reflecting the average number of citations to articles published in science and social science journals. It is frequently used as a proxy for the relative importance of a journal within its field, with journals with higher impact factors deemed to be more important than those with lower ones. via Wikipedia.</p>
<p>Here you can check the <b>impact factor</b> of indexed journals <b>for 8+ years</b>, also you can check <b>h-index</b> and <b>SJR impact factor</b> of journals.</p>
