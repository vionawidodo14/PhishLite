<h2 id="an">Wiimmfi</h2>
The <b>Nintendo Wi-Fi Connection</b> (WFC) service was an online gaming service
that provided free online play in various Nintendo DS and Wii games.
Nintendo discontinued the service on May 20th, 2014,
primarily because the host of the service,
<a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GameSpy#Shutdown">GameSpy</a>,
was shutting down its servers.
Nintendo WFC was required for online play,
but the <b>Wiimmfi Project</b> has replicated much of its functionality
and allows for continued online play in Mario Kart Wii and other games.

<p>
<b>Wiimmfi</b> is available for the public since
<a href="/development#r-2014-05-10">May 10th, 2014</a> (10 days before WFC shutdown).
It started with <i>Mario Kart Wii</i> and supports now more than
<a href="stat?disab=hide&amp;all=1&amp;test=show">100 games</a>.
Read »<a href="/development">Development of Wiimmfi</a>« for details.

</p><p>
To use <b>Wiimmfi</b>, the games must be patched to use the new domains and
also to change some other settings.
See »<a href="http://wiiki.wii-homebrew.com/Wiimmfi_%28en%29/Patcher">Wiimmfi Patcher</a>«
for more details.
</p></div>

<div class="text"><h2 id="log">Log of last updates</h2>
<dl>
<h3 id="log-1">Sunday, November 11th, 2018</h3>
<dl>
<dt class="changelog">Required Wiimmfi Patcher update for Mario Kart Wii:</dt>
<dd>

	All Mario Kart Wii users have to update the game image
	to protect their hardware against cheat code that can brick it.
	→ <a href="/update">Details</a>
</dd></dl>
<h3 id="log-2">Saturday, March 31th, 2018</h3>
<dl>
<dt class="changelog">Custom Regions managed by a robot:</dt>
<dd>

	A robot detect regions, that were not used sinces 2 years
	or never used 1 year after assignment.
	The owner of the region gets a system message as warning.
	31 days after the warning message, the owner loses the control
	of the region, gets a second message and the region is disabled.
	
</dd></dl>
<h3 id="log-3">Sunday, March 18th, 2018</h3>
<dl>
<dt class="changelog">Custom Regions:</dt>
<dd>

	Until August 2017, users reservered custom regions by the
	<a href="https://wiimm.de/r/ctw/">Custom Track Wiiki</a>.
	Until today, these regions were reserved at Wiimmfi too
	and Wiiki users had an chance for re-applying then regions.
	Now most of these regions are free for anyone.
	
</dd></dl>
</dl>
