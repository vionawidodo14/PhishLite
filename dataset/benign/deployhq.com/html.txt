<div class="section-header">
<h2 class="section-header__title">Easy to get up and running</h2>
<div class="section-header__leads">
<p class="section-header__lead">DeployHQ handles all the hard work getting files from your repositories to your servers. This is how it works and the secrets behind the magic. It’s really as easy as 1, 2, 3.</p>
</div>
</div>
</div>
<div class="container">
<ol class="how__steps">
<li class="how__step how__step--1">
<h3 class="how__title">Connect your repository</h3>
<p class="how__text">Link your GitHub, Bitbucket, GitLab or self-hosted repositories directly to us.</p>
<ul class="how__logos">
<li class="how__logo">
<img alt="Github" class="how__logo-img" src="/assets/oauth/github-67f0171d1606e67c4d425ee2c41106c76822366a1a6b6e533c6bba5559fba9b3.svg"/>
</li>
<li class="how__logo">
<img alt="Bitbucket" class="how__logo-img" src="/assets/oauth/bitbucket-ea303c5abf42baa5804f09aeb323f310378cd3e403dfc9ee8737768029422701.svg"/>
</li>
<li class="how__logo">
<img alt="Gitlab" class="how__logo-img" src="/assets/oauth/gitlab-5071737c57356e5d880a4b1b64b6a2cabddd33ebdbe4a172d51bd863d00745cb.svg"/>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li class="how__step how__step--2">
<h3 class="how__title">Configure your recipe</h3>
<p class="how__text">Set up servers, config files, build commands and any other configuration.</p>
<p class="how__more"><a class="how__link" href="/features">Read more about our features</a></p>
</li>
<li class="how__step how__step--3">
<h3 class="how__title">Deploy your code!</h3>
<p class="how__text">Hit <strong>Deploy</strong> and watch your code get shipped out to your servers. You can also, optionally, configure code to be deployed automatically whenever you push to your repositories - very handy!</p>
</li>
</ol>
