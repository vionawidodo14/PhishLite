<h3><a href="https://blog.cartoonmovement.com/2019/10/blocked-in-china-iran-russia-and-turkey.html" target="_blank">Blocked</a></h3>
<p>If you can read this post, you are part of the group that still enjoys free access to the Internet (or using a VPN). An old saying about cartoonists states that a good cartoon always needs to piss off someone. We tend to agree, and it seems we are doing a good job pissing off those in power. How do we know this? Cartoon Movement is currently blocked in <strong>China</strong>, <strong>Iran</strong>, <strong>Russia</strong> and <strong>Turkey</strong>. (Source: <a href="https://www.comparitech.com/privacy-security-tools/">Comparitech</a>)</p>
