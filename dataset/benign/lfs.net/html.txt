<h1 class="m_0 m_l10">Discover the realism of racing simulation with Live for Speed.</h1>
<div class="ContentArticle2">
<p>You can join the action today! <a href="/downloadlfs">Download our simulator</a> and race online instantly with real-life competitors, offline with AI drivers or against the clock.  <a href="/contents">Your garage</a> includes club racing hatchbacks through to <a href="/cars/FZR">GT cars</a> and <a href="/cars/BF1">Formula 1</a>.</p>
<p>Set up your car to improve performance.  Make use of the setup sharing option to send your car setup to your friends.</p>
<p>Give your car a custom paint job using any art package.  Live for Speed makes your design visible to other online drivers.</p>
<p>Compete in online championships in races ranging from a few laps to 24-hour team events.</p>
<p>Start racing in the free Demo on the <a href="/tracks/bl">Blackwood</a> road and rallycross tracks with a choice of 3 cars.  Upgrade your license to access <a href="/contents">another 17 cars and 7 race track environments</a>.</p>
<p>
                    Live for Speed runs well even on older computers, so start racing now!
                </p>

                Graphics Progress Report: Lighting research
                <span class="block right f_12 m_r15">added <time class="DateField" datetime="2019-07-27 15:38Z" title="Sat, 27 Jul 2019 15:38 UTC">Sat 27 Jul 2019, 23:38</time></span>
</h2>
<div class="ContentArticle2">
<p>
                Hello Racers,<a class="FPostTextLink" href="https://www.lfs.net/static/newsattachments/1/pxbhBo42wZ7y.jpg" target="_blank"><img alt="" src="/static/newsattachments/1/pxbhBo42wZ7y.jpg" style="margin: 5px; max-height: 180px; float: right;" title="-41939122 12405054 809386 -29896 681 0.0 70.0.jpg"/></a><br/>
<br/>
Our previous progress report was about the Kyoto updates and the new lighting system. Since then, Scawen has done more work on the lighting which may be interesting for some readers. Eric continued with Kyoto for some time. He has now moved onto South City, which isn't yet ready to show but we can show you some more pictures from Kyoto.<br/>
<br/>
Originally the plan was to minimally update the existing track areas to make them work well with the new lighting and shadows. But some of the tracks were very old and Eric wanted to do extensive updates to bring them up to a more modern standard.<br/>
<br/>
