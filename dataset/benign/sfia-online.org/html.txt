<h3>SFIA 7 published </h3>
<p>The newest version of the world’s most widely adopted skills and competency framework is now <a href="/en/framework/sfia-7">available to use</a></p>
<p>New and revised skill definitions developed through global consultation with industry and business.</p>
<p>The previous version, <a href="/en/framework/sfia-6">SFIA 6</a>, will remain available.</p>
</div>
<div class="hp-col">
<h3>What's new?</h3>
<p>View and share our 2-minute introduction to SFIA.</p>
<iframe allow="autoplay; encrypted-media" allowfullscreen="" frameborder="0" height="140" src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/PmZMWV563JA?rel=0&amp;showinfo=0" width="250"></iframe>
</div>
<div class="hp-col">
<h3>Reference and learning for SFIA 7</h3>
<a href="#">
<img class="hp-preview" src="https://www.sfia-online.org/en/sfia-7/ref-guide-cover/" title="SFIA 7 complete reference cover preview"/>
</a>
<p>Logged-in users can access and use the <a href="/en/framework/sfia-7/documentation/sfia-7-the-complete-reference/view">print-friendly complete reference to SFIA 7</a>, the latest version of the <a href="/en/framework/sfia-7/documentation/sfia-7-summary-chart/view">famous SFIA chart</a>, a handy <a href="/en/framework/sfia-7/documentation/sfia-7-beta-3-april-2018-excel/view">Excel spreadsheet of all skill descriptions</a>, and the web-hosted <a href="/en/framework/sfia-7/moving-to-sfia-7">Moving to SFIA 7</a> guide to the changes.</p>
</div>

<p><a class="button" href="/en/get-help">Find help</a></p>

<div class="hp-col">
<h2>Software apps with SFIA</h2>
<p>Products to help you with effective management of skills – using SFIA.</p>
<p>Some SFIA Partners have built SFIA into their software products.
           Now you can get help with skills and resource management, with online skills assessment,
           and with immediate access to SFIA on your mobile phone.</p>
<p><a href="/en/get-help/software-products">Read more about software apps with SFIA</a></p>

<div class="hp-found">
<h2>The SFIA Foundation</h2>
<p>Our strategic direction is set by our Board of Directors. 
         Input to help define strategic direction is provided by the SFIA Council.
         More about the Governance of The SFIA Foundation.</p><p></p>
<p>Support for users of SFIA is provided by our accredited consultants and accredited partners.</p>
<p>There is also an online self-help group of users and consultants – the <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/groups/1934300/">SFIA User Forum</a> (external web site).</p>
