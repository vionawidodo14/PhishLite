<h2 class="pane-title">Discover What's Next!</h2>

<div class="field field--name-field-oc-text field--type-text-long field--label-hidden"><div class="field__items"><div class="field__item even"><p>Century College is a two-year community and technical college located in White Bear Lake, Minnesota. <p>Century College offers over 150 degrees, diplomas and certificates in a wide variety of high-demand program areas, including applied design, arts and humanities, business, health sciences, human services, science, technology, engineering and mathematics (STEM), social and behavioral sciences, trade and industry, and non-credit workforce training.</p>
<p>Dedicated to student success, Century College graduates more than 1,650 students each year, awarding over 2,000 degrees, diplomas and certificates.</p>
