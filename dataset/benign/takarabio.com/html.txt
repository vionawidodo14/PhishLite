</h2>
<p>Capturem Trypsin provides rapid, efficient, and complete digestion of protein samples, allowing an <a href="/learning-centers/protein-research/mass-spectrometry-digestion-reagents/protein-and-whole-cell-lysate-digestion-with-capturem-trypsin-technology">uninterrupted mass spectometry workflow</a> at room temperature for downstream protein analysis. This product utilizes our novel Capturem technology in a spin column format with membrane-immobilized trypsin.
