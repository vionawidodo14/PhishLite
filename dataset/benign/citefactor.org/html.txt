<div class="panel-body news">
<p>The CiteFactor server provides indexing of major international journals and proceedings. Author can get information about international journal <strong>impact factor</strong>, proceedings (research papers) and information on upcoming events. All the journal pages have pointers to Web pages of the publishers which are integrated into the CiteFactor stream pages.</p>
<p>The purpose is to increase the visibility and ease of use of open access scientific and scholarly journals. If your journal is indexed &amp; got validated stamp from Citefactor, you can request for the calculation of impact factor for your journal.</p>
<p>"In addition, CiteFactor is working on next major task to link publications with all the publications in their bibliographies, thereby making possible a wide variety of publication and citation reports."</p>
<p>Directory of International Reseach Journals in association with leading universities.</p>
<p> </p>
