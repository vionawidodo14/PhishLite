<h1 class="entry-title">Welcome</h1>
</header>
<div class="entry-content">
<p>Welcome to the Roughneck Blogs and ePortfolios site. We are proud to offer this resource to our staff and students. The goals of this site are:</p>
<p>Provide a stable, long term platform for students to display an electronic portfolio of work that they feel portrays their abilities</p>
<p>Provide a communication portal for staff to share information with students, parents, and the community</p>
<p>White Oak Blogs and ePortfolios are best viewed and edited using the latest free version of Firefox browser. You can <a href="https://www.mozilla.com/en-US/">download Firefox here</a> for free.</p>
