<h1 class="details-header">Eat fresh, local, and responsible. Join us in making cities more sustainable.</h1>
<div class="big-subheader">

<div class="section-content">
<h1>A customizable weekly food basket</h1>
<div class="jrm-truncate">
<p>Once you’re subscribed, we automatically build a base basket for you every week with $30-worth of our veggies. Customize it by adding or removing whatever you want - minimum order is only $20. Your basket is weekly by default, but you can suspend it with one click in your account settings, or suspend deliveries for longer if you’re travelling. And you can cancel anytime too by giving us a quick call. Signing up is free, so is pausing or cancelling your deliveries.</p>
</div>
</div>
</div>
</div>
<div class="section-about section2">
<div class="section-wrapper">
<div class="section-content">
<h1>An online farmer’s market home to the freshest foods in town</h1>
<div class="jrm-truncate">
<p>Every week, you’ll get an email when our online farmer’s market opens. This is your go to start customizing your basket with rooftop-grown, picked-to-order veggies and thousands of products from our hundreds of partners, like eggs, dairy, pasta, bread, meats, and more. You’ve got three full days to do so, until midnight the eve your delivery day. Our prices are competitive and everything on our Marketplace is fresh, local, and responsibly produced. </p>
</div>
</div>
</div>
</div>
<div class="section-about section3">
<div class="section-wrapper">
<div class="section-content">
<h1>A network of pick-up points for delivery across Quebec</h1>
<div class="jrm-truncate">
<p>Once your weekly order is finalized at midnight just before your delivery day, our team preps everything overnight. Your order is then delivered to the pick-up point of your choice (such as your local corner store, coffee shop, bookstore, you name it), or right to your door with one of our electric cars for an extra $5. Take a look at our pick-up points map <a href="/pups">here</a> to see which one’s most convenient for you.</p>
