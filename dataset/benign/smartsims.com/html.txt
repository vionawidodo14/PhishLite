Therefore, to truly grasp the impact strategy and management decision-making have on business outcomes, students need <a href="https://www.smartsims.com/case-study/real-world-experience-in-classroom/" title="real-world experience">real-world experience</a>.
<br/>
<br/>
<a href="https://www.smartsims.com/business-simulation/" title="Business Simulation Games">Business Simulation Games</a> bring business terminology and concepts to life. By giving students their own virtual company they become engaged with course content and bridge theory with practice. Professors report <a href="https://www.smartsims.com/experiential-learning/7-reasons-business-simulations-help-students-succeed/" title="learning outcomes">learning outcomes</a> such as student engagement, increased motivation, real management skills, workforce readiness and confidence in job interviews. Business simulators enable effective teaching methods such as <a href="https://www.smartsims.com/case-study/experiential-learning-drexel/" title="experiential learning">experiential learning</a>, reflective learning, action learning and the flipped classroom.</p>
