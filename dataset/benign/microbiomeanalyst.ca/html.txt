<td>
<h2 style="text-align: center; margin-top: 0px">MDP</h2>
<div style="padding-left: 10px;font-family: sans-serif">
<b>Marker Data Profiling (MDP)</b>: Comprehensive <font style="color:#2738aa">composition &amp; diversity analysis</font> 
                        supporting various methods of data overview, alpha diversity and beta-diversity; <font style="color:#2738aa">comparative analysis</font> 
                        supporting multiple differential abundance methods (metagenomeSeq, LEfSe, edgeR, DESeq2, <i>etc.</i>); as well as 
                        <font style="color:#2738aa">prediction of metabolic potentials</font>. 
                    </div></td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
<table class="panelColumns" style="padding: 20px; position:absolute;top:600px;left:50%;margin-left:-290px;width:280px;height: 300px;background-color: white">
<tbody>
<tr>
<td>
<h2 style="text-align: center; margin-top: 0px">SDP</h2>
<div style="padding-left: 10px;font-family: sans-serif">
<b>Shotgun Data Profiling (SDP)</b>: <font style="color:#2738aa">Functional diversity profiling</font> 
                        based on KEGG annotations (modules, pathways, metabolisms), EC numbers, or COG categories, direct <font style="color:#2738aa">functional association testing</font>, as well as <font style="color:#2738aa">differential 
                            abundance analysis</font> followed by functional enrichment analysis within a powerful <font style="color:#2738aa">metabolic network visualization</font> system. 
                    </div></td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
<table class="panelColumns" style="padding: 20px; position:absolute;top:600px;left:50%;margin-left:10px;width:280px;height: 300px;background-color: white">
<tbody>
<tr>
<td>
<h2 style="text-align: center; margin-top: 0px">TSEA</h2>
<div style="padding-left: 10px;font-family: sans-serif">
<b>Taxon Set Enrichment Analysis (TSEA)</b>: Enrichment analysis using ~2400 manually collected taxon sets organized into 11 different categories. They are defined based on their shared phenotypic traits or ecological niches, 
                        or their associations with host genetic variations, lifestyles, biochemistry, diseases, developmental stages, <i>etc</i>. 
                    </div></td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
<table class="panelColumns" style="padding: 20px; position:absolute;top:600px;left:50%;margin-left:310px;width:280px;height: 300px;background-color: white">
<tbody>
<tr>
<td>
<h2 style="text-align: center; margin-top: 0px">PPD</h2>
<div style="padding-left: 10px;font-family: sans-serif">
<b>Projection with Public Data (PPD)</b>: Co-processing your data together with a suitable public 16S rRNA data of interest 
                        and explore the results within an <font style="color:#2738aa">interactive 3D PCoA visualization system</font> to easily 
                        <font style="color:#2738aa">discover patterns of interest</font> as well as to associate these patterns with 
                        underlying taxonomic variations. 
                    </div></td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
