<div class="entry-content">
<h3>Fast Route Planning and Route Optimization</h3>
<p>Use our web services to add <a href="https://graphhopper.com/maps/">route planning</a>, navigation and <a href="https://www.graphhopper.com/blog/2017/11/06/time-dependent-optimization/">traffic aware</a> <a href="https://www.graphhopper.com/route-optimization/">route optimization</a> to your application in the food delivery industry or for traveling salesmen. We help you optimize the routes in your logistics, GIS, or real estate software.</p>
</div>
</div>
</div>
</div>
<div class="textGraphicsRow table">
<div class="tableRow">
<div class="tableCell imageCell mobileOnly">

</div>
<div class="tableCell textCell">
<div class="entry-content">
<h3>Cost-effective</h3>
<p>We offer fair, effort-based pricing with the use of a scalable architecture and resource-efficient routing software.</p>
</div>
</div>
<div class="tableCell imageCell desktopOnly">
<img alt="" src="https://www.graphhopper.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/01/GH-Illustration-Coin-1-300x300.png"/>

<div class="tableRow">

</div>
<div class="tableCell textCell">
<div class="entry-content">
<h3>Permissive Terms</h3>
<p>Use the GraphHopper Directions API for any use case (including commercial usage, vehicle tracking and navigation applications) on any map you like.</p>
</div>
</div>
</div>
</div>
<div class="textGraphicsRow table">
<div class="tableRow">
<div class="tableCell imageCell mobileOnly">

</div>
<div class="tableCell textCell">
<div class="entry-content">
<h3>Easy to integrate</h3>
<p>With our RESTful web service, you can integrate routing into your application without any knowledge about the complex algorithms behind it.</p>
<p>There is no end user application–instead, we help you build the end user applications in the industries and countries that you know best.</p>
</div>
</div>
<div class="tableCell imageCell desktopOnly">
<img alt="" src="https://www.graphhopper.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/01/GH-Illustration-Zahnraeder-300x300.png"/>

</div>
<div class="tableCell textCell">
<div class="entry-content">
<h3>World wide coverage</h3>
<p>All our routing and geocoding services are powered by OpenStreetMap data, and therefore cover the entire world. To support this effort, we are a proud member of the OpenStreetMap Foundation.</p>
</div>
</div>
</div>
</div>

</div>
<div class="tableCell textCell">
<div class="entry-content">
<h3>For Outdoor</h3>
<p>Our routing services come with elevation data which generates precise time and distance estimates. With many vehicle profiles– from mountain bikes to racing bikes–you’ll get a powerful tool for your outdoor application. You can even use these profiles to optimize routes for delivery bicycles. See our <a href="/showcases">showcases</a>.</p>
