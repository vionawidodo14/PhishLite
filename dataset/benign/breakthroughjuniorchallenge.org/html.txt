<h3>Welcome To This Year’s<br/><strong class="c">Breakthrough Junior Challenge</strong></h3>
<p>Explain a big scientific idea in Physics, Life Sciences or Mathematics with a short video.</p>
<p>You will have until <strong class="c">June 15, 2019 at 11:59 PM PDT</strong> to submit your video (3:00 minutes max).</p>
<p>Like our <a href="https://www.facebook.com/BreakthroughPrize" target="_blank">Breakthrough Facebook page</a> for updates.</p>
<h3><a href="/prize">Prizes</a></h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>$250,000</strong> post-secondary/college scholarship</li>
<li><strong>$50,000</strong> prize for your math<br/>or science teacher</li>
<li><strong>$100,000</strong> Breakthrough Science Lab</li>
</ul>

</div>
<div class="item text-center" data-period="Competition Period" data-period-length="">
<h3>Application Submission Deadline</h3>
<h6>June 15</h6>
<p>You must submit your application and video no later than June 15, 2019 at 11:59 PM PDT.</p>
</div>
<div class="item text-center" data-period="Review Period" data-period-length="">
<h3>Peer-To-Peer Review</h3>
<h6>June 18 – July 7</h6>
<p>As part of your entry, you must score at least 5 other submissions by July 7, 2019 at 11:59 PM PDT, during the <a href="/judging/peer-to-peer">Peer-to-Peer Review</a>. You may be asked to score additional submissions as well.</p>
</div>
<div class="item text-center active" data-period="Review Period" data-period-length="">
<h3>Evaluation Panel Review</h3>
<h6>July 15 – August 10</h6>
<p>In this period, the Evaluation Panel will score and judge the top 75 submissions in accordance with the criteria set forth in the Rules.</p>
</div>
<div class="item text-center" data-period="Review Period" data-period-length="">
<h3>Verification</h3>
<h6>August 11 – September 4</h6>
<p>In this period, Challenge staff will review and verify the submissions (up to 30) selected by the Evaluation Panel.</p>
</div>
<div class="item text-center" data-period="Review Period" data-period-length="">
<h3>Popular Vote</h3>
<h6>September 5 – 20</h6>
<p>The Popular Vote is a chance for the world to participate in the voting for their favorite submission to make it to the final phase of the judging of the Challenge. As a result of the Popular Vote, 7 Regional Champions will also be selected.</p>
</div>
<div class="item text-center" data-period="Celebration" data-period-length="">
<h3>Announcement of Finalists, Popular Vote Top Scorer And Regional Champions</h3>
<h6>September 21</h6>
<p></p>
</div>
<div class="item text-center" data-period="Celebration" data-period-length="">
<h3>Announcement of Winner And Award Ceremony</h3>
<h6>November 2019 (Date to be announced)</h6>
<p>The Challenge winner will be announced at the televised Breakthrough Prize Ceremony.</p>
