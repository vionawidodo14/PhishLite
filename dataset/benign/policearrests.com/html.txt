<h3 class="hiw-title">How Police Arrests Work?</h3>
<h4 class="hiw-title">PoliceArrests.com receives live recent arrest records from various US law enforcement agencies.</h4>
</div>
<div class="block hiw cf">
<div class="row cf">
<div class="cell hiw-01" style="height: 201px;">
<div class="hiw-header"><i>1</i> Arrest</div>
<div class="hiw-body">Once a Police Officer has probable cause they can arrest someone</div>
</div>
<div class="cell hiw-02" style="height: 201px;">
<div class="hiw-header"><i>2</i> Booking</div>
<div class="hiw-body">After arrest, the person is booked as a suspect</div>
</div>
<div class="cell hiw-03" style="height: 201px;">
<div class="hiw-header"><i>3</i> Information</div>
<div class="hiw-body">Suspect’s information and the crime details are recorded</div>
</div>
<div class="cell hiw-04" style="height: 201px;">
<div class="hiw-header"><i>4</i> Mugshot</div>
<div class="hiw-body">Mugshot of the suspect is taken</div>
</div>
<div class="cell hiw-05" style="height: 221px;">
<div class="hiw-header"><i>5</i> Fingerprints</div>
<div class="hiw-body">Police then record the suspect's fingerprints</div>
</div>
<div class="cell hiw-06" style="height: 221px;">
<div class="hiw-header"><i>6</i> Warrants</div>
<div class="hiw-body">Suspect is investigated for warrants</div>
</div>
<div class="cell hiw-07" style="height: 221px;">
<div class="hiw-header"><i>7</i> Health</div>
<div class="hiw-body">A health screening of the suspect is conducted to protect the jail officials and inmates</div>
</div>
<div class="cell hiw-08" style="height: 221px;">
<div class="hiw-header"><i>8</i> Distribution</div>
<div class="hiw-body">Booking information is distributed to other law enforcement agencies, media and web</div>
</div>
</div>
</div>
</div>
</div>
