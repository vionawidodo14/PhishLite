<div class="col-md-12 text-justify">
<p class="lead">
<small> In the recent past, the computer vision community has relied on several centralized benchmarks for performance evaluation of numerous
 tasks including object detection, 
		    pedestrian detection, 3D reconstruction, optical flow, single-object 
		    short-term tracking, and stereo estimation. 


Despite potential pitfalls 
		    of such benchmarks, they have proved to be extremely helpful to advance 
		    the state-of-the-art in the respective research fields. Interestingly, 
		    there has been rather limited work on the standardization of multiple 
		    target tracking evaluation. One of the few exceptions is the well-known 
		    <a href="http://www.cvg.reading.ac.uk/PETS2009/" target="_blank">PETS</a> dataset, targeted primarily at 				surveillance applications. Even for 
		    this widely used benchmark, a common technique for presenting tracking 
		    results to date involves using different subsets of the available data, 
		    inconsistent model training and varying evaluation scripts.
		    <br/>
		    With this benchmark we would like to pave the way for a 
		    unified framework towards more meaningful quantification of multi-target 
		    tracking.</small>
</p>
</div>
</div><!-- /.row -->
<div class="row">
<div class="col-md-12 text-justify">
<h2>What do we provide?</h2>
<hr style="height:1px;margin-left: 0;width:25%;margin-top: 0.5em;color:#FF8000;background-color:#FF8000;"/>
<p class="lead"><small>
          	We have created a framework for the fair evaluation of multiple people tracking algorithms. In this framework we provide: 
</small>
</p>
<big>
<ul>
<li> A large collection of datasets, some already in use and some new challenging sequences! </li>
<li> Detections for all the sequences. </li>
<li> A common evaluation tool providing several measures, from recall to precision to running time. </li>
<li> An easy way to compare the performance of state-of-the-art tracking methods. </li>
<li> Several challenges with subsets of data for specific tasks such as 3D tracking, surveillance, sports analysis (updates coming soon). </li>
</ul>
</big>
<br/>
<p class="lead"><small>
We rely on the spirit of <i>crowdsourcing</i>, and we encourage researchers to submit their sequences to our benchmark, so the quality of multiple object tracking systems can keep increasing and tackling more challenging scenarios.</small></p>
