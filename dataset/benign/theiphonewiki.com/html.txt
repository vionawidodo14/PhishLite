<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Welcome_to_the_iPhone_Wiki">Welcome to the iPhone Wiki</span></h2>
<p>This is a conglomerate work of everything done by everyone on Apple's amazing <a href="/wiki/IDevice" title="IDevice">iDevices</a>. <i>Anyone</i> can contribute here, just <a href="/wiki/The_iPhone_Wiki:Account_creation" title="The iPhone Wiki:Account creation">ask an administrator</a> for an account. Currently there are 4,405 users that contribute to this wiki, with 8,924 articles.
</p>
<ul><li>Check out the most <a href="/wiki/Special:RecentChanges" title="Special:RecentChanges">recent article changes</a>.</li>
<li>Get <a href="/wiki/Up_to_Speed" title="Up to Speed">up to speed</a> in the community (and learn about how jailbreaks work).</li>
<li>Read (and edit) the <a href="/wiki/Constitution" title="Constitution">constitution</a> to understand what purpose this wiki serves.</li>
<li>Read the <a href="/wiki/Timeline" title="Timeline">timeline</a> to see where we are.</li>
<li>Read the <a href="/wiki/The_iPhone_Wiki:Community_portal" title="The iPhone Wiki:Community portal">community portal</a> to find out what people request to be added/changed.</li>
<li>Read the <a href="/wiki/The_iPhone_Wiki:Ground_rules" title="The iPhone Wiki:Ground rules">ground rules</a> to know what you should and should not post in this wiki.</li>
<li>If you have notes on something you did, post them here, no matter how ugly.</li>
<li>If you see something ugly, work to make it pretty.</li>
<li>If you have a fix for a problem people are having, post it here.</li></ul>
</div>
<div>
<h2><span id="What_are_we_about?"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="What_are_we_about.3F">What are we about?</span></h2>
<p>The iPhone Wiki is an unofficial wiki dedicated to collecting, storing and providing information on the internals of Apple's amazing <a href="/wiki/IDevice" title="IDevice">iDevices</a>. We hope to pass this information on to the next generation of hackers so that they can go forth into their forebears' footsteps and break the ridiculous bonds Apple has put on their amazing mobile devices.
</p>
</div>
