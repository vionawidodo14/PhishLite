<h3>New to flight simulation?</h3>
<div class="pad story">
<p>Welcome to <strong>Fly Away Simulation</strong>. We cover many aspects of flight simulation on this website including the popular series released by Microsoft: Microsoft Flight, Flight Simulator X (including Steam Edition), FS2004 and of course X-Plane by Laminar Research.</p>
<h4>Getting Started</h4>
<p>Before you are able to become a virtual aviator, you need a package for your <strong>PC</strong> or <strong>Mac</strong>. We feature some great <a href="https://flyawaysimulation.com/downloads/98/free-flight-simulators/">free flight simulator packages</a> that users may download including <em>X-Plane</em>, <em>FlightGear</em>, and <em>Orbiter</em>. However, for a true simulation experience with maximum features and expansion options, we currently recommend Microsoft Flight Simulator X: Steam Edition or X-Plane (the latest is version 11) from Laminar Research; both can be purchased from our store, SimShack with worldwide delivery.</p>
<p>Before you can enjoy virtual aviation, you will need to get yourself a flight sim package.  There are many out there, but currently the best and most expandable are <em>Microsoft Flight Simulator X </em>or <em>X-Plane</em>.  If you are reading this, it's likely you are already set up for simmin'!</p>
<h4 style="clear:both"><img alt="FSX: Steam Edition logo." src="https://flyawaysimulation.com/media/images14/images/fsx-se-logo.jpg" style="width: 200px; height: 78px; float: right;"/>Microsoft Flight Simulator X: Steam Edition</h4>
<p>Microsoft's flight simulation packages have always been the most popular, and not by chance.  They feature complete world scenery and many stunning aircraft out of the box.  Microsoft <abbr title="Flight Simulator X">FSX</abbr> is the latest of their simulation series that offers "full world" simulation with a complete experience.  This differs from the newer 2012 <em>"Microsoft Flight"</em> game, as this is not a complete package out of the box; it's more of a quick, fun game with no third-party add-on support - so for those who want the full experience, we recommend FSX.  </p>
<p>The most recent version is the Steam Edition re-released by Dovetail Games.  You can <a href="https://flyawaysimulation.com/news/4728/">read our review of the Steam Edition here</a>.  While it's slightly dated (and not up to date in terms of bug fixes and features), you can also <a href="https://flyawaysimulation.com/downloads/files/1304/microsoft-flight-simulator-x-demo/">download the original FSX demo from our file library here</a>.</p>
<h4 style="clear:both"><img alt="X-Plane 11 logo." src="https://flyawaysimulation.com/media/images14/images/xp11-logo.jpg" style="width: 200px; height: 92px; float: right;"/>X-Plane 11</h4>
<p>X-Plane has always been the professional's choice, it features many advanced functions and ultimate realism.  Just released is X-Plane version 11, which is quite possibly the ultimate and most complete package available.  X-Plane runs on Windows, Mac and even Linux, so you don't have to worry about what operating system you run. </p>
<p>Out of the box, over 20 aircraft are included and highly detailed world scenery with thousands of airports and airfields.  You can <a href="https://flyawaysimulation.com/news/4889/">read our review here</a> and of course, <a href="https://flyawaysimulation.com/downloads/files/3276/x-plane-for-windows-mac-linux-demo/">download a demo from our file library here</a>.</p>
