<section aria-labelledby="whoHeading" id="whoWeAre" role="region">
<div class="content"><div class="wrap"><h2 id="whoHeading" role="heading">Who We Are</h2><p>KIPP Austin is a public charter school network of 10 award-winning elementary, middle, and high schools. With exceptional teachers, high-quality facilities and a nurturing environment, our focus is on getting students to and through college. KIPP Austin’s tuition-free schools educate more than 5,000 students from underserved communities.</p>
<p>KIPP Austin is part of a national network of 200 college-preparatory public charter schools.</p>
