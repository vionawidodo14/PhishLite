<h1>Play all your games on Linux</h1>
<p>
        Lutris is an Open Source gaming platform for Linux. It installs and
        launches games so you can start playing without the hassle of setting
        up your games. Get your games from GOG, Steam, Battle.net, Origin, Uplay
        and many other sources running on any Linux powered gaming machine.
        </p>
</div>
</div>
<div class="row">
<div class="col-sm-4">
<p class="intro-text">
        Lutris uses community contributed installation scripts to provide an
        optimal experience and setup. Once installed, games are launched with
        programs called runners. Those runners include RetroArch, Dosbox,
        customized Wine versions and many more!<br/>
<a class="action-button" href="/downloads">Download Lutris</a>
</p>
</div>
<div class="col-sm-4">
<p class="intro-text">
        We are a fully independent project and Lutris will always remain free of charge.
        In order to keep the project alive, we need your support! Donations fund the
        entirety of the development and hosting costs, please consider becoming one of
        our supporters!<br/>
<a class="action-button" href="/donate">Support us</a>
</p>
</div>
<div class="col-sm-4">
<p class="intro-text">
        We want to provide a powerful yet simple platform for everyone to enjoy all
        their games, old and new. This is possible thanks to our amazing community.
        Join us and help us make Linux a top-tier gaming platform!<br/><br/>
<a class="action-button" href="https://github.com/lutris">Contribute</a>
</p>
</div>
</div>
