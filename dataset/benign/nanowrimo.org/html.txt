<h1 class="headline">Every story matters. <br/>Let's start writing yours.</h1>
<p class="copy">Writing a novel alone can be difficult, even for seasoned writers. NaNoWriMo helps you track your progress, set milestones, connect with other writers in a vast community, and participate in events that are designed to make sure you finish your novel. Oh, and best of all, it’s free!</p>

<div class="subtitle">What's the Young Writers Program about?</div>
<h3 class="headline">The Young Writers Program offers <strong>tools, resources, and community access</strong> to help young writers and educators set ambitious creative goals and tackle projects year-round!</h3>
<p class="copy">Each year, over 100,000 young writers under 18 enjoy our <strong>youth-friendly writing space, progress tracking tools,</strong> and <strong>Young Novelist Workbooks</strong>. Educators can support student skill development with our free Common Core–aligned curricula, online classroom management tools, and motivational classroom materials.</p>
