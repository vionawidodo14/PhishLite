</h3>

<div class="box-body">
<div class="feed_item_title"> RTI RULES</div>
<div class="feed_item_subtitle"> The Ministry of Personnel notified the Right to Information (Term of Office, Salaries, Allowances and Other Terms and Conditions of Service of Chief Information Commissioner, Information Commissioners and State Information Commissioners) Rules, 2019. </div>
<div class="long_text_container">
<div class="long_text_main_image">
<img alt="current affairs image" src="https://vajiramandravi.s3.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/media/2019/10/26/11/29/43/RTIII.jpg" style="min-height:200px;"/>
</div>
<div class="feed_item_content">
<p><strong>About:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>The rules have reduced the tenure of Information Commissioners from five years to three.<br/><br/></li>
<li>It give the government the discretion to decide on “conditions of service” for which no express provisions are made in the rules.<br/><br/></li>
<li>The Chief Information Commissioner’s salary has been fixed at ₹2.5 lakh and an Information Commissioner’s at ₹2.25 lakh.<br/><br/></li>
</ul>
<div>
<div class="feed_item_important_info_box alert alert-info">
<div><strong>Important Info : </strong></div>
<p><strong>Opposition to rules: </strong></p>
<ul>
<li>According to activists, the rules had been drafted and promulgated in violation of the procedures laid down in the Pre-Legislative Consultation Policy of 2014.</li>
<li>The policy requires all draft rules to be placed in the public domain for comments/suggestions of people. The draft was not available in the public domain and no consultations were held with members of the public.</li>
<li>The changes would affect the independence of ‘Information Commissions who will function like caged parrots.’</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div class="box-body">
<div class="feed_item_title"> LEBANON</div>
<div class="feed_item_subtitle"> On October 17, the Lebanese government proposed a plan to tax calls made through the internet and other similar services. </div>
<div class="long_text_container hiding">
<div class="long_text_main_image">
<img alt="current affairs image" src="https://vajiramandravi.s3.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/media/2019/10/26/11/24/50/LEAO.jpg" style="min-height:200px;"/>
</div>
<div class="feed_item_content">
<p><strong>About:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Voice Over IP (VoIP) calls in Lebanon would be charged at 20 cents everyday, from January 2020 onwards, which totals to $6 per month. These VoIP services include calls made through Facebook, WhatsApp, Skype and Viber.<br/><br/></li>
<li>While the proposal has since then been withdrawn, if it were to pass, Lebanon would be the first country in the world to do so. The move, however, has triggered mass anti-government protests in the West Asian country.<br/><br/></li>
</ul>
<div>
<div class="feed_item_important_info_box alert alert-info">
<div><strong>Important Info : </strong></div>
<p><strong>Lebanon?</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Lebanon is a country in Western Asia. It is bordered by Syria to the north and east and Israel to the south, while Cyprus is west across the Mediterranean Sea.</li>
<li><strong>Capital:</strong> Beirut.</li>
</ul>

<img alt="current affairs image" src="https://vajiramandravi.s3.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/media/2019/10/26/10/59/51/SUPERCOMPUTERRR.jpg" style="min-height:200px;"/>

<ul>

<li>Google's quantum computer consists of microscopic circuits of superconducting metal that entangle 53 qubits in a complex superposition state.<br/><br/></li>

<div class="feed_item_important_info_box alert alert-info">
<div><strong>Important Info : </strong></div>
<p><strong>Quantum supremacy?</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Quantum supremacy, or “quantum advantage” is the point at which quantum computers can solve problems that are practically unsolvable for “classical” (non-quantum) computers to complete in any reasonable timeframe.</li>
<li>It is generally believed that at least 49 qubits are required to cross the quantum supremacy line.</li>
<li>Qubits behave very differently to bits in classical computers. Bits represent either a “1” or a “0”. In contrast, qubits can represent a combination state made up of both “1” and “0,” due to the peculiar quantum effects in which properties like particle position, direction, and momentum are not well-defined. </li>
</ul>

<div class="text-bold"> How to take UPSC Online Prelims Test Series on VajiramIAS App and Website</div>
<div>
<p><strong>This Announcement is Only for ONLINE mode of Test Series.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Method 1 : VajiramIAS App</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Login to VajiramIAS App (<a href="https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.vajiramandravi.iasprep" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Click here to Download</a>)<br/><br/></li>
<li>Make Sure that you are using the updated version of VajiramIAS App<br/><br/></li>
<li>Register on the App<br/><br/></li>
<li>Click on More on the bottom Menu<br/><br/></li>
<li>Click on Prelims Test Series<br/><br/></li>
<li>Buy the GS+CSAT Package (recommended) or buy the GS or CSAT Test Series individually.<br/><br/></li>
<li>Once you buy the test series, the tests will get unlocked and you will be able to attempt the tests.<br/><br/></li>
<li><strong>IMPORTANT : You do no need any new credentials, you automatically get access to the test series with the account through which you bought the test series.</strong><br/><br/></li>
<li>Once you take the test, you will be get a Result Release Time. You will be able to see the test solution at that time.<br/><br/></li>
<li>You will get your All India Rank in the form of a pdf result which will be uploaded on the app and website 3 days after the test. <br/><br/></li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Method 2 : VajiramIAS Website</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Visit <a href="https://vajiramias.com/" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">https://vajiramias.com</a><br/><br/></li>
<li>Register on the site<br/><br/></li>
<li>Click on Test Series menu<br/><br/></li>
<li>Buy the GS+CSAT Package (recommended) or buy the GS or CSAT Test Series individually.<br/><br/></li>
<li>Once you buy the test series, the tests will get unlocked and you will be able to attempt the tests.<br/><br/></li>
<li><strong>IMPORTANT : You do no need any new credentials, you automatically get access to the test series with the account through which you bought the test series.</strong><br/><br/></li>
<li>Once you take the test, you will be get a Result Release Time. You will be able to see the test solution at that time.<br/><br/></li>
<li>You will get your All India Rank in the form of a pdf result which will be uploaded on the app and website 3 days after the test.<br/><br/></li>
</ul>
