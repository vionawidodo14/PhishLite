<hr/>
<br/>I'm David J. Bruton Centennial Professor of <a href="https://login.cs.utexas.edu/">Computer Science</a> at <a href="http://www.utexas.edu/">The University of Texas at Austin</a>, and director of its <a href="http://www.cs.utexas.edu/~qic/">Quantum Information Center</a>.  Prior to coming here, I taught for nine years in Electrical Engineering and Computer Science at MIT.  My research interests center around the capabilities and limits of quantum computers, and computational complexity theory more generally.
