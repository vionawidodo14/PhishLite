					Frequently Asked Questions

<div class="faq-text col8">

<div class="faq-subtitle col8">
                    What is Tingles?
                </div>
<div class="faq-text col8">
                    Tingles is a video platform built for ASMR. It's available for both iOS and Android.
                    <ul>
<li><b><a href="https://itunes.apple.com/app/tingles-asmr-player-listen/id1181126709" target="_blank">Download for iOS</a></b></li>
<li><b><a href="https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.designs1290.tingles" target="_blank">Download for Android</a></b></li>
</ul>
</div>
<div class="faq-subtitle col8">
					What do I get by joining Tingles?
				</div>
<div class="faq-text col8">
				    By joining Tingles you get:
                    <ul>
<li>Earn revenue. Artists on Tingles earn 4x more per video view than on YouTube.</li>
<li>Get the best ASMR experience for your fans.</li>
<li>Get new fans on Tingles.</li>
<li>Get a 3Dio microphone for free when you reach 50 supporters.</li>
</ul>
</div>
<div class="faq-subtitle col8">
					How is Tingles different than YouTube?
				</div>
<div class="faq-text col8">
<p>Tingles is built for ASMR. It offers features like background play, sleep timer, video download for offline viewing, looping, top video charts, curated trigger playlists, search by trigger, audio only mode and more. On Tingles there are also no loud ads.</p>
</div>
<div class="faq-subtitle col8">
					How does monetization on Tingles work?
				</div>
<div class="faq-text col8">
<p>Instead of loud ads, there are three ways to make money on Tingles:</p>
<p><b><a id="minutes-watched">From Minutes Watched</a></b></p>
<p>As a baseline we pay for every minute your videos are watched on Tingles. Tingles users can buy Tingles Premium subscription that gets them access to premium app features. That money is split between artists based on minutes watched. The more people watch your videos, the more you earn. Current rate is already higher than YouTube average.</p>
<p><b><a id="direct-support">Direct Support</a></b></p>
<p>In addition to that, Tingles users can support you directly with a $4.99 monthly subscription. This is similar to Patreon. Your supporters get:</p>
<ul>
<li>download your videos for offline viewing</li>
<li>access your exclusive supporter-only chatroom</li>
<li>watch your exclusive videos and get early access to new videos (optional)</li>
</ul>
<p>You receive 70% of the net-revenue, Tingles keeps 30%. As part of our partnership with 3Dio we also offer our partners 3Dio microphones for free when they reach 50 supporters (or more for more premium models).</p>
<p><b><a id="tipping">Tipping</a></b></p>
<p>In addition to that users can also tip your videos with a 💖 sticker for $1, $2, $5 or $10. You can watch a quick demo here: <b><a href="https://youtu.be/lPjYHC3Z0Vk" target="_blank">https://youtu.be/lPjYHC3Z0Vk</a></b>.</p>
</div>
<div class="faq-right">
<div class="faq-subtitle col8">
                        How much work will I have to do?
                    </div>
<div class="faq-text col8">
                        Being on Tingles is no extra work for you, your videos show on Tingles automatically. We encourage artists to create some exclusive videos for their Tingles supporters, but it's not a requirement. The main rewards for your supporters are provided by us.

                    </div>
<div class="faq-subtitle col8">
                        Will Tingles affect my YouTube earnings?
                    </div>
<div class="faq-text col8">
                        No. Tingles is a completely separate platform from YouTube so it doesn't affect your YouTube earnings in any way. Anything you make on Tingles is in addition to what you already make on YouTube.
                    </div>
<div class="faq-subtitle col8">
<a id="3dio-faq">How do I get a 3Dio microphone for free?</a>
</div>
<div class="faq-text col8">
<p>Tingles is partnering with 3Dio. As a Tingles partner you can get one 3Dio microphone for free when you reach enough supporters on Tingles:</p>
<ul>
<li>For 50 Tingles supporters - get Base model <b><a href="https://3diosound.com/products/free-space-binaural-microphone" target="_blank">Free Space</a></b> for free (regular price: $499)</li>
<li>For 100 Tingles supporters - get <b><a href="https://3diosound.com/products/free-space-xlr-binaural-microphone" target="_blank">Free Space XLR</a></b> for free (regular price: $799)</li>
<li>For 200 Tingles supporters - get <b><a href="https://3diosound.com/products/free-space-pro-binaural-microphone" target="_blank">Free Space Pro II</a></b> for free (regular price: $1,999)</li>
</ul>
</div>
<div class="faq-subtitle col8">
                        How does Tingles help me grow my audience?
					</div>
<div class="faq-text col8">
<p>Tingles is a community of the most dedicated ASMR fans. Our average user watches 2 hours of ASMR a day. We will promote your videos to our users who don't know you yet:</p>
<ul>
<li>Your profile will show on Artists tab where users can discover new artists</li>
<li>Your profile will show under Related Artists on profiles of artists similar to you</li>
<li>Your videos can get featured on Discover Weekly and Best of the Week playlists</li>
<li>Your videos can get featured in trigger playlists</li>
<li>Your profile can get featured on top of Home or Artists tab</li>
</ul>
</div>
<div class="faq-subtitle col8">
                        How is Tingles different than Patreon?
					</div>
<div class="faq-text col8">
<p>Tingles has three main advantages over Patreon: it offers a better experience for your fans, it's less work for you and it gets you more supporters:</p>
<ul>
<li>
Tingles is actively encouraging users to become your supporters so you don't have to. On Patreon all the promotion efforts are on you.
	</li>
<li>
Tingles provides most of the rewards for your paying supporters. On Patreon you have to provide all the rewards for your supporters yourself.
	</li>
<li>
Becoming a supporter on Tingles is as easy as one tap. This is so much easier than Patreon where users have to go to a different site, create a new account, add their credit card and so on.
	</li>
<li>
Tingles offers better rewards for paying supporters. Being a supporter on Patreon doesn't unlock any benefits on YouTube where most of the users still watch your videos. Because Tingles is one unified platform it can offer much better benefits to your paying supporters.
	</li>
</ul>
</div>

What are exclusive videos?

				</div>
<div class="faq-text col8">
Exclusive videos mean videos that are only available on Tingles and not available on other platforms. They are locked for free users and only accessible to your paying supporters. It's your choice if you want to have them, but they're a great way to convince more of your fans to become paying supporters.

What is early access to new videos?

				</div>
<div class="faq-text col8">
Early access to new videos means new videos become available on Tingles some time before they become available on other platforms. You can think of these videos as temporarily exclusive. It's advised to leave them exclusive for one week before making them available on other platforms. Early access videos are locked for free users and only accessible to your paying supporters. It's your choice if you want to have them, but they're a great way to convince more of your fans to become paying supporters.

				</div>
